Verified architecture

Nodal and police machinery

The State page identifies the Special Inspector General of Police, SC/ST, and the senior Social Justice & Empowerment Secretary as nodal officers. It describes a State Police Protection Cell and district-level DySP posts for investigation and supervision.

Committees

State, District, City and Taluka vigilance/monitoring structures are described, with the Chief Minister chairing the State V&MC and Collectors chairing District V&MCs.

Courts and prosecutors

The current BCK-64 page states 19 functional Special Courts. The State page describes District Government Pleaders as Special Public Prosecutors. A 31-01-1990 Legal Department notification provides primary historical appointment evidence.

Relief and rehabilitation

State-published Rule 12(4) relief norms and the Veer Megh Maya Contingency Scheme provide offence-linked compensation and additional rehabilitation assistance. Current payment workflow remains open.

Manual scavenging

A 24-06-2024 State resolution establishes State, District and Municipal Corporation survey committees. A 29-02-2020 notification assigns enforcement duties to local authorities and District Magistrates.

Data and administration

The N-1 branch handles atrocity data, annual reports, Central assistance and statistics. Directorate pages also identify computerisation and administrative strengthening functions.

Open verification questions

Current primary Rule 9 appointment and office-holder, Protection Cell staffing, Rule 10 orders, sensitive-area notifications, complete court notification matrix, current SPP/ESPP register, relief/DBT workflow, PoA-specific SOPs, digital workflow, bonded-labour instruments and later Manual Scavengers instruments remain open.
