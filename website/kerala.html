Kerala

Phase 1 State implementation inventory. Status: inventoried with qualifications. Inventory date: 5 September 2026.

Verified current/recent evidence

  • Current Kerala State Planning Board and Scheduled Tribes Development Department publications describe Special Courts/benches, a Scheduled Castes Protection Cell and Special Police Stations as part of the continuing implementation architecture.
  • Current Kerala Police statistics report 976, 1,081, 1,222, 1,313, 1,269 and 1,059 SC/ST PoA-related cognizable cases for 2020 through 2025, plus 609 through July 2026. These are police crime-category figures, not prosecution or conviction figures.
  • Kerala Police provides a 10-day service standard for enquiry of petitions regarding atrocities on SC/ST persons and maintains a dedicated SC/ST petition register under cited police directives.
  • The 2025 Economic Review records 154 atrocity victims receiving compensation grant in 2024-25 and describes district legal-councillor support plus State-level legal and call-centre support.
  • Current LSGD material states all 14 districts were declared manual-scavenging free in 2023, records one-time cash assistance to 137 workers and states District/Sub-Divisional Vigilance Committees were constituted in all districts.
  • Current State legal and labour repositories establish the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act and district/sub-district labour administration, while current bonded-labour committee instruments remain to be frozen.
  • Current Kerala State Election Commission material publishes the Kerala Panchayat Raj Act 1994, providing the local-government legislative interface.

Open verification

Current Rule 9 designation, Protection Cell establishment and staffing, complete police-unit matrix, Rule 10 Special Officers, atrocity-prone-area notifications, V&MC matrix, complete Special/Exclusive Special Court notification and jurisdiction matrix, SPP/ESPP appointments, relief-payment workflow, PoA-specific executive instruments, digital case workflow, related-law instruments and post-BNSS instructions remain open.

Historical Central reports are retained only for their reporting periods and are not treated as a 2026 census.
