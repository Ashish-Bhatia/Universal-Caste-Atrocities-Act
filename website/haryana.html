Verified architecture

Nodal and police machinery

Official implementation reporting identifies an Additional Chief Secretary as Rule 9 nodal officer and reports a State SC/ST Protection Cell at Police Headquarters with district Special Cells. Current 2026 appointments, staffing and operative orders remain open.

Committees

Haryana publishes State Monitoring Committee, District Level V&MC and Sub-Divisional V&MC instruments. Official reporting records State, District and Sub-Divisional committee structures. Current membership and meeting records remain open.

Courts and prosecutors

Gazette Notification S.O.63/C.A.33/1989/S.14/2017 dated 08-09-2017 specified first Additional Sessions Judges in every district as Special Courts. The current Prosecution Department maintains a dedicated PoA Special Courts publication. A current statewide court and prosecutor matrix remains open.

Relief and rehabilitation

The current State relief page publishes assistance of Rs.85,000 to Rs.8,25,000, bank-account payment and Deputy Commissioner sanction. The RTS page provides a seven-working-day service target. Current treasury/DBT and case-level workflow remains open.

Police data

Haryana Police's current 2025 crime chart records 34 cases under the SC/ST (PoA) Act 1989/2015 category. This is a police crime-count field, not a conviction, prosecution, pendency or victim count.

Related-law interfaces

Current State sources publish the Manual Scavengers Act and a 28-07-2021 implementation-review record, identify manual-scavenging functions within Urban Local Bodies, and publish the Haryana State Commission for Scheduled Castes Act 2018 and Rules 2019.

Open verification questions

Current Rule 9 appointment and office-holder, Protection Cell staffing, Rule 10 orders, atrocity-prone-area notifications, complete court and SPP/ESPP matrices, relief/DBT workflow, PoA-specific SOPs, digital workflow, current V&MC records, bonded-labour instruments and later Manual Scavengers instruments remain open.
