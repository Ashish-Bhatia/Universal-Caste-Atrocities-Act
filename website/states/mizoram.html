Mizoram

Mizoram

Phase 1 State/UT Inventory

Date: 05-09-2026

Scope and method

This inventory is jurisdiction-specific. It covers the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, as implemented/reported for Mizoram. Primary Government and Gazette material is preferred. Historical reports are separated from the latest verified State Annual Report material. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

Executive position

Mizoram has a State-level SC/ST Protection Cell and two notified Special Courts covering the State's judicial districts. The latest located Mizoram State Annual Report, for calendar year 2023 and transmitted to the Union Government on 19 March 2024, reports no registered PoA cases, no identified atrocity-prone areas, no State-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee, no Sub-Divisional V&MCs, no Special Officers for identified areas, no specified Special Public Prosecutors, no Special Police Stations, no relief/legal-aid payments, no periodic survey, and no State plan under Rule 15. It also reports that the SC/ST Protection Cell exists, with an ADGP/IGP as Chair and senior CID/Aizawl police members, but the report does not identify a current named Rule 9 Nodal Officer. These findings are evidence of the position reported for 2023, not a verified census of every arrangement in September 2026.

The 2013 Gazette notification remains the identified primary instrument for the two Special Courts. The 2000 Gazette instrument identified the Additional Secretary, Home Department as Rule 9 Nodal Officer, but no later authoritative appointment instrument was located in the present research pass. Accordingly, the current office-holder remains UNVERIFIED.

Rule 9 Nodal Officer

  • Historical primary instrument: Notification No. A-14017/2/90-LJD/234 dated 04-08-2000 appointed the Additional Secretary, Home Department as Nodal Officer for the whole of Mizoram.
  • Current named office-holder: not verified.
  • 2023 State Annual Report: reports the Nodal Officer reporting mechanism but does not identify the office-holder by name and states that quarterly progress reports were not received from the SC/ST Protection Cell and review was not undertaken.
  • Status: HISTORICAL INSTRUMENT VERIFIED; CURRENT OFFICE-HOLDER UNVERIFIED.

Rule 8 Protection Cell

The 2023 State Annual Report states that the SC/ST Protection Cell exists. It records the composition as ADGP/IGP, Chairman; DIG (NR), Member; DIG (CID), Member; SP CID (Crime), Member; and SP Aizawl, Member. The report's activity fields are largely NIL/blank, with a review indicator for registered cases, consistent with no cases being reported for the year.

  • State headquarters Cell: YES, reported for 2023.
  • Command/composition: ADGP/IGP Chair, with DIG (NR), DIG (CID), SP CID (Crime) and SP Aizawl as members.
  • Additional non-Rule-8 responsibility: NO, according to the 2023 report.
  • Current 2026 continuity: not independently verified after the 2023 reporting period.

Special Police Station / SC-ST police machinery

The 2023 State Annual Report states that no Special Police Station had been set up and lists all 11 districts as districts without a Special Police Station. Separately, official Police Organisation data for 01-01-2023 reports no dedicated Crime Against SCs/STs/Weaker Sections police station for Mizoram. The State therefore appears to rely on ordinary police stations plus the State Protection Cell and designated investigation/prosecution machinery rather than dedicated PoA Special Police Stations.

  • Special Police Stations: NONE reported for 2023.
  • State Protection Cell: YES.
  • Current dedicated Special Police Station status in 2026: not independently verified beyond the latest located State report and 2023 police-organisation data.

Rule 10 Special Officers and atrocity-prone/identified areas

The 2023 State Annual Report records no identified atrocity-prone area across the 11 districts and no Special Officer appointed for any identified district. The report therefore supplies no operative Rule 10 Special Officer matrix for 2023.

  • Identified/atrocity-prone areas: NONE reported for 2023.
  • Special Officers: NONE reported for 2023.
  • Current 2026 status: not independently verified.
  • Caution: absence of identified areas in a reporting table is not evidence that no vulnerability exists; it is only the State's reported implementation status for that year.

Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2023 State Annual Report states that the State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee was not constituted and held no meetings. The Sub-Divisional Level Committee table reports NIL. The District Level table does not provide a reliable complete constitution-date matrix in the retrieved copy and records no meaningful meeting activity. Therefore the existence and operational status of each District V&MC should be treated as unresolved rather than inferred.

The 2012 Mizoram Gazette separately records constitution of a State Level V&MC under Rule 16, chaired by the Chief Minister, with Ministers, MPs/MLAs, Chief Secretary, Home Secretary, DGP and Social Welfare Secretary among the members. The 2016 Rules changed the statutory Rule 16 composition and meeting requirement. The 2023 State Annual Report's statement that the State Level Committee was not constituted therefore establishes a later reported implementation position, not that the 2012 notification never existed.

  • State V&MC: 2012 historical constitution verified; 2023 State Annual Report says NOT CONSTITUTED.
  • State meetings: NIL reported in 2023.
  • District V&MCs: current constitution/meeting matrix not adequately verified from the 2023 return.
  • Sub-Divisional V&MCs: NIL reported in 2023.

Special / Exclusive Special Courts

Primary Gazette Notification No. A.45011/1/2008-LJE dated 29-01-2013, published 08-02-2013, specifies two Courts of Sessions as Special Courts under section 14 of the PoA Act:

  • 3rd Court of Additional District and Sessions Judge, Aizawl, for the Aizawl Judicial District comprising Aizawl, Kolasib, Mamit, Champhai and Serchhip administrative districts.
  • Court of Additional District and Sessions Judge, Lunglei, for Lunglei, Lawngtlai and Saiha.

The 2023 State Annual Report repeats the two-court structure and reports no Exclusive Special Court.

  • Special Courts: 2.
  • Exclusive Special Courts: NONE reported.
  • Territorial coverage: all 11 districts through two judicial districts.
  • Current 2026 continuation of the 2013 notification: strongly supported by the 2023 State return, but a later superseding Gazette instrument was not located in this research pass.

Special Public Prosecutor / Exclusive Public Prosecutor

The 2023 State Annual Report records NIL Special Public Prosecutors and NIL Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors for both Aizawl and Lunglei Special Courts. No later Gazette appointment instrument was located in the present pass.

This creates an implementation issue because section 15 and Rule 4 require specification/appointment of Special Public Prosecutors for Special Courts. The present inventory records the State's 2023 reported NIL position without treating it as proof that no prosecutor ever acted in such capacity or that the courts were legally inoperative.

  • SPP specified for Aizawl Special Court: NIL reported in 2023.
  • SPP specified for Lunglei Special Court: NIL reported in 2023.
  • ESPP: NIL, with no Exclusive Special Courts reported.
  • Current named SPP/ESPP: UNVERIFIED.

Relief and payment workflow

The 2016 Rules as republished in the Mizoram Gazette require the District Magistrate/Sub-Divisional Magistrate/Executive Magistrate to provide relief within seven days, permit State authorization for immediate treasury withdrawal, require State budgetary provision and twice-yearly review, and integrate victim/witness rights into the State plan. The 2023 Mizoram State Annual Report reports NIL relief payments and NIL travelling/maintenance expenses because no PoA cases were reported. It also reports no State plan under Rule 15.

  • Statutory workflow: Rule 12(4), as amended, provides relief within seven days; Rule 14 requires annual budget provision and twice-yearly State review; Rule 15 requires an implementation plan.
  • 2023 actual relief paid: NIL reported.
  • 2023 travel/maintenance expenses: NIL reported.
  • State authorization for immediate treasury withdrawal: NIL reported in the State return.
  • State plan under Rule 15: NO plan reported.
  • Current 2026 payment workflow: UNVERIFIED.

Annual reporting

The Mizoram Home Department transmitted its annual PoA implementation material for calendar year 2023 to the Union Ministry on 19-03-2024. An RTI response dated 18-09-2024 states that the 2023 State Annual Report was sent on 19-03-2024 and enclosed the State submission. This is strong provenance for the 2023 implementation data.

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment lists the Union annual report under section 21(4) for 2022 as published on 17-09-2024. The 2022 national report is therefore a separate Union compilation and should not be substituted for the State's own 2023 return.

  • 2023 State annual report: VERIFIED SENT 19-03-2024.
  • 2023 State report source: Mizoram Home Department submission reproduced in RTI disclosure.
  • Post-2023 annual reporting: not verified.

State PoA/PCR instruments

Identified State-specific instruments include:

  • 2000 Rule 9 Nodal Officer notification.
  • 2012 State Level V&MC constitution notification.
  • 2013 Special Court notification under section 14.
  • 2016 Mizoram Gazette republication of the central SC/ST PoA Amendment Rules, 2016.
  • State administrative allocation instruments assign enforcement of the SC/ST PoA Act and Rules to the relevant State departments.

No separate Mizoram-specific PoA rules replacing the central Rules were identified. No current State Rule 15 plan was identified; the 2023 State return expressly states that no plan had been formulated.

Current/recent judicial and police corroboration

A targeted search of the Gauhati High Court's jurisdiction did not locate a clearly Mizoram-specific SC/ST PoA merits decision sufficient to establish a current State implementation practice. The search results largely concern Assam or other States despite the shared High Court jurisdiction. Accordingly, no case-law proposition is inferred for Mizoram from those results.

The 2023 Mizoram State Annual Report itself references the DGP's communication dated 23-02-2024 and is therefore direct administrative/police corroboration for the 2023 machinery. Police Organisation data as on 01-01-2023 separately reports no dedicated Crime Against SCs/STs/Weaker Sections police station in Mizoram.

Related-law interfaces

  • Community-status verification: The Mizoram Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Castes (Regulation of Issuance and Verification of) Community Certificates Act, 2014 regulates issuance and verification of SC/ST community certificates. This is relevant where an accused disputes the victim's statutory community status.
  • Manual scavenging: Mizoram republished the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013 in the State Gazette in 2014. The SC/ST PoA Act also contains an offence concerning manual scavenging, creating an overlapping enforcement interface.
  • PCR law: the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 remains a separate central statute. The State administrative allocation records enforcement responsibility for SC/ST protection and related welfare functions.
  • General criminal law transition: PoA offences interact with the general criminal law framework. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition is a separate national Phase 1 closure workstream and is not re-researched here.

Evidence limitations

  • The strongest current Mizoram-specific implementation source located is the State's 2023 annual return, transmitted in March 2024 and disclosed under RTI in September 2024. It is not a September 2026 census.
  • The current Rule 9 Nodal Officer name/designation after the 2000 appointment was not verified.
  • Current 2026 Special Public Prosecutor appointments were not verified.
  • Current 2026 Rule 10 Special Officer status and identified-area status were not verified beyond the 2023 return.
  • District and Sub-Divisional V&MC constitution/meeting records require further primary retrieval.
  • No current Rule 15 plan was located; the 2023 return states none existed for that reporting year.
  • The 2023 State return reports no PoA cases, while national crime statistics/search extracts show some SC/ST crime figures for Mizoram in other contexts/years. Those datasets must not be conflated. The State return is an implementation-reporting source; NCRB crime tables require separate reconciliation of offence definitions, year and reporting basis.
