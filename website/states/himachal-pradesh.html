Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map Inventory date: 2026-09-05

Scope and evidence rule

This record maps Himachal Pradesh-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and related civil-rights/atrocity interfaces. It is not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion. Currentness is distinguished from historical reporting. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

1. Departmental and Rule 9 nodal architecture

The current Department of Social Justice & Empowerment site identifies the Directorate for the Empowerment of SCs, OBCs, Minorities and the Specially Abled (ESOMSA), identifies its Director and publishes annual reports, notifications and office orders. The 2024 State PoA annual report identifies the Director, ESOMSA as the Rule 9 nodal officer and records receipt of quarterly Protection Cell reports, but no review of those reports. A current 2026 Rule 9 appointment/designation instrument was not located. [HP-STATE-002, HP-STATE-004]

2. Protection Cell and police machinery

The 2024 State report records a SC/ST Protection Cell at State CID Headquarters, Shimla, under DGP supervision, with DGP/HP, ADGP/CID/HP and ADGP (Law & Order) identified in its composition. No Special Police Station was reported in any of the 12 districts. PoA investigations were reported to be handled by police officers not below DSP rank, with district SPs directed to complete investigation within 60 days and higher officers monitoring progress. Current 2026 establishment/staffing orders and any district-unit structure remain open. [HP-STATE-005, HP-STATE-006, HP-STATE-007]

3. Rule 10 Special Officers and atrocity-prone areas

For 2024, all 12 districts reported Additional District Magistrates of the concerned districts as Rule 10 Special Officers. No atrocity-prone area was identified in the 2024 report. Current 2026 appointment instruments and any operative atrocity-prone-area notification remain open. The HP Police statement that no particular area is notorious for SC/ST offences is descriptive and is not treated as a legal notification. [HP-STATE-008, HP-STATE-009, HP-STATE-017]

4. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2024 State report states that a State Level V&MC under the Chief Minister was constituted, but held no meeting in 2024 and reconstitution was under process. Twelve District Level V&MCs were reported, with 32 meetings during 2024. Sub-Divisional V&MCs were also listed, with meeting activity in some sub-divisions. Current constitution orders, membership and meeting records remain open. [HP-STATE-010]

5. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

The 2024 State report states that District and Sessions Judge courts in all districts had been designated Special Courts under section 14. It also describes specified Family Court judicial locations as designated Special Courts. The report's tabulation is partly difficult to parse and is not a substitute for underlying State/High Court notifications. A complete current court/jurisdiction matrix remains open. Recent Himachal Pradesh High Court decisions confirm functioning of PoA Special Judges in individual districts, but do not establish a statewide notification census. [HP-STATE-011]

6. SPP/ESPP and prosecution machinery

The 2024 State report states that all District Attorneys-cum-Public Prosecutors were designated Special Public Prosecutors for PoA cases within their jurisdictions. No Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors were specified. Current individual appointment orders, replacements, additional SPPs and ESPP appointments remain open. [HP-STATE-011, HP-STATE-012]

7. Relief, rehabilitation, treasury and payment workflow

The 2024 State report records Rs.2,70,80,639 as immediate relief provided to 415 atrocity victims during 2024. It states that relief is to be provided within seven days and that State authorization is provided to District Magistrates for immediate treasury withdrawal. Annexure VII records 415 relief cases and states that the majority were paid within the stipulated period. Current rate/order, DBT mechanism, sanction workflow and financial-control documents remain open. [HP-STATE-013]

8. Rule 15 contingency plan and PoA-specific instruments

The 2024 report states that a State contingency plan had been formulated under Rule 15, based on the Model Contingency Plan, covering departmental/officer responsibilities, local bodies, NGOs and relief measures. The operative Gazette/SOP/manual/circular set was not independently frozen. [HP-STATE-014]

9. Digital/FIR/case-management workflow

The current HP Police CCTNS citizen portal provides online complaint, citizen tip, FIR viewing and FIR/complaint status searches. The retrieved public material does not establish a PoA-specific e-FIR or PoA case-management workflow, so no such workflow is inferred. The current Crime Review 2024 provides district-wise SC/ST PoA and PCR crime reporting. [HP-STATE-015, HP-STATE-016]

10. PCR interface

The 2024 State annual report records PCR implementation review through State and District V&MCs and reports three PCR cases in 2024, two put in court, one pending investigation and one pending court. This establishes an administrative PCR/PoA interface without resolving all current operational details. [HP-STATE-001, HP-STATE-026]

11. Manual scavenging interface

Historical official Central material records Himachal Pradesh as having its own manual-scavenging legislation before the 2013 Central framework. Current ESOMSA material links to Manual Scavengers resources. The National Commission for Safai Karamcharis reported the State Government's nil manual-scavenger figure for 2021-22. A current Himachal Pradesh High Court judgment applies section 5 of the 2013 Act. Current State-law and district/local implementation instruments remain open. [HP-STATE-022, HP-STATE-023, HP-STATE-024, HP-STATE-025]

12. Bonded labour interface

The Himachal Pradesh Economics & Statistics Department Economic Survey 2018-19 reports District and Sub-Division Vigilance Committees and a State Level Standing Committee under the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976. This is historical official evidence. Current committee orders, labour instructions and post-2018 records remain open. [HP-STATE-018]

13. Land and local-government interfaces

The Himachal Pradesh Transfer of Land (Regulation) Act, 1968 and Rules 1969 remain relevant State-law interfaces. The official Land Code compilation records the Act as updated through 2023 and section 3 restrictions on transfer of land interests by Scheduled Tribes without prior State permission, including consultation with Gram Sabha/Panchayats in the stated cases. [HP-STATE-021]

The current State Panchayati Raj repository publishes the HP Panchayati Raj Act 1994 and later 2025/2026 instruments. The Urban Development Department publishes the HP Municipal Act 1994 and Municipal Corporation Act 1994 with later amendments. [HP-STATE-019, HP-STATE-020]

14. Currentness assessment

Himachal Pradesh is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. The 2024 State annual report provides detailed official reporting on Rule 9, Rule 10, Protection Cell, investigation rank, Special Courts, SPPs, V&MCs, relief and contingency planning. Current 2026 primary instruments remain incomplete, especially individual appointments, operative orders and complete court/prosecutor matrices.

Open closure items

  • Current 2026 Rule 9 designation/appointment instrument and individual office-holder.
  • Current Protection Cell establishment/order, staffing and district-unit structure.
  • Current Rule 10 Special Officer appointment orders.
  • Current atrocity-prone-area notifications, if any, and operative status.
  • Complete current Special/Exclusive Special Court notification and jurisdiction matrix.
  • Current PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment orders/register and replacements.
  • Current relief-rate/order, treasury authorization, payment/DBT and case-processing workflow.
  • Current PoA-specific Gazette/SOP/circular/manual set and operative contingency-plan instrument.
  • Current PoA-specific digital/FIR/case-management workflow.
  • Current State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC constitution orders, membership and meeting records.
  • Current Manual Scavengers State/District/local implementation instruments and post-2013 State-law status.
  • Current bonded-labour implementation orders and vigilance-committee records.
