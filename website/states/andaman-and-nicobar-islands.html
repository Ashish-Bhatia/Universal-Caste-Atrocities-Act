Andaman and Nicobar Islands

Andaman and Nicobar Islands

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

Scope

This jurisdiction inventory records Union Territory-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, related tribal-protection interfaces, police machinery, courts/prosecutors, relief and monitoring. It is an existing-law inventory. It does not assess policy superiority, necessity or constitutional validity.

1. Beneficiary scope and territorial context

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment's current published State/UT Scheduled Caste list records zero notified Scheduled Castes for Andaman and Nicobar Islands as of the Ministry's listed date. [A: AN-STATE-001]

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs' current published Scheduled Tribe list identifies six notified tribal entries for Andaman and Nicobar Islands: Andamanese and related listed communities, Jarawas, Nicobarese, Onges, Sentinelese and Shom Pens. [A: AN-STATE-002]

Accordingly, this inventory is principally concerned with implementation concerning Scheduled Tribes. The historical absence of notified Scheduled Castes in the UT does not support any broader conclusion about caste-based harm or future statutory scope.

2. Rule 9 Nodal Officer

A 2014 Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment reply lists Rule 9 Nodal Officers as appointed in a number of States/UTs but does not list Andaman and Nicobar Islands. [A: AN-STATE-003]

The 2017-18 Ministry PoA implementation material likewise lists Nodal Officers in specified jurisdictions and does not identify Andaman and Nicobar Islands in that list. [A/B: AN-STATE-004]

No current Andaman and Nicobar Administration appointment order naming a Rule 9 Nodal Officer was independently retrieved. This is a controlled evidence gap, not an absence finding.

3. Rule 8 Protection Cell and investigation machinery

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) report states that a Special Cell consisting of the Director General of Police, Inspector General of Police, Superintendent of Police of all districts and Director (Tribal Welfare) had been constituted and was functioning to address atrocities against members of Scheduled Tribes. [A: AN-STATE-005]

Historical Ministry reporting records the same Protection Cell architecture, and a 2002 report identifies an order dated 19-10-2001 constituting a Special Cell under Rule 8. [A: AN-STATE-006, AN-STATE-007]

The 2022 report does not establish a current 2026 notification, sanctioned strength, staff deployment, reporting matrix or operational workload. Those remain open.

The 2022 report states that PoA cases were investigated by a police officer of the rank of Deputy Superintendent of Police and above. [A: AN-STATE-008]

4. Special Police Stations

The Ministry's historical implementation material identifies Special Police Stations for the PoA Act in only a limited group of States and does not identify Andaman and Nicobar Islands as having dedicated Special Police Stations. [A: AN-STATE-004]

No current Andaman and Nicobar Police notification independently establishing or enumerating dedicated Special Police Stations was located. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

5. Rule 7 investigation and charge-sheet timing

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records three Andaman and Nicobar cases in which investigation and filing of the charge-sheet were completed within sixty days, and no case in which completion occurred later than sixty days. [A: AN-STATE-005]

These are reporting-period figures only. They do not establish current 2026 Rule 7 compliance, reasons for delay, or post-BNSS investigation instructions.

6. Rule 10 / identified and atrocity-prone areas

The 2022 Ministry report records the Additional District Magistrate, Port Blair and Project Officer, ITDP Car Nicobar as Special Officers for Andaman and Nicobar District respectively. [A: AN-STATE-008]

The same report identifies specific atrocity-prone areas in North & Middle Andaman District: Kadamtala, Rangat and Baratang. [A: AN-STATE-008]

A 2022 PIB release independently reproduces the same identified-area information. [A: AN-STATE-009]

This establishes identified-area reporting for the relevant period. Current 2026 Rule 3/Rule 10 notifications, survey criteria, present-area status and operative Special Officer instruments remain open.

7. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2022 Ministry report states that a State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee under the Lieutenant Governor reviewed PoA implementation. It records that no State-level meeting was held during 2022. [A: AN-STATE-005]

District Level V&MCs were constituted and one district-level meeting was held during 2022. Sub-Divisional Level V&MCs were constituted in Diglipur, Mayabunder, Nancowry and Car Nicobar sub-divisions, except South Andaman. [A: AN-STATE-005]

A 2024 Lok Sabha document also records that UT and District level V&MCs had been constituted. [A: AN-STATE-010]

Current membership, reconstitution orders, mandatory meeting calendar, minutes and compliance remain unverified.

8. Special Court

Historical and recent Ministry material establishes the Court of District and Session Judge, Andaman and Nicobar Islands as the designated Special Court for offences of atrocities against Scheduled Tribes. [A: AN-STATE-011, AN-STATE-008]

The 2014 Ministry reply also records Andaman and Nicobar Islands among UTs that had designated District Session Courts as Special Courts. [A: AN-STATE-012]

Current judicial evidence confirms continuing operation. A February 2025 Calcutta High Court Circuit Bench at Port Blair judgment records Special Case No. 4 of 2019 before the Special Judge, Port Blair, involving Section 3(1)(u) of the PoA Act. [A: AN-STATE-013]

A July 2026 Calcutta High Court Circuit Bench at Port Blair order records Aberdeen Police Station Case No. 121 of 2026 under Sections 3(r), 3(u) and 3(2)(vii) of the PoA Act and proceedings before the Sessions Judge, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Port Blair. [A: AN-STATE-014]

These sources corroborate continuing PoA-specific judicial machinery but do not establish a complete 2026 designation instrument or separate Exclusive Special Court.

9. SPP / ESPP

Historical Ministry material records the Public Prosecutor as specified as Special Public Prosecutor for PoA cases in the Special Court. [A: AN-STATE-011, AN-STATE-015]

The 2022 report states that a Special Public Prosecutor had been specified for conducting atrocity cases in the Special Court. [A: AN-STATE-008]

A current named SPP appointment instrument, tenure, qualifications, court allocation and any Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor arrangement were not independently verified. These remain open.

10. Relief, legal aid and rehabilitation

Historical Ministry reporting records that legal aid and other facilities were provided by the Judicial Court/Administration as required, and that travelling and maintenance expenses were provided as required. [A: AN-STATE-008]

A 2014 Ministry report records the same general assistance architecture and historical Special Officer arrangements. [A: AN-STATE-015]

The current Ministry CSS archive confirms that central assistance for implementation of the PCR/PoA scheme is maintained as a Government scheme, but a current Andaman-specific 2020-21 through 2024-25 relief-recipient/payment series was not independently extracted in this pass. [A: AN-STATE-016]

Current statutory relief rates, payment workflow, seven-day compliance, victim/witness travel and maintenance, case-level sanction records and payment auditability remain open.

11. Annual reporting and statistical context

The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal recent UT-specific institutional snapshot located. It records the State/District V&MC structure, Rule 8 Protection Cell, Special Officers, identified areas and charge-sheet timing. [A: AN-STATE-005, AN-STATE-008]

The Ministry's public archive lists Annual Report 2021 and Annual Report 2021-22 among its PoA reporting materials. [A: AN-STATE-016]

Later Section 21(4) reports after the 2022 reporting period were not independently retrieved as direct primary Andaman-specific material. This is a retrieval limitation, not an absence finding.

12. State/UT PoA, PCR and tribal-protection instruments

India Code records the Andaman and Nicobar Islands Protection of Aboriginal Tribal Regulation, 1956, administered through the Department of Tribal Welfare, together with the Protection of Aboriginal Tribal Rules, 1957 and numerous later notifications and amendments. [A: AN-STATE-017]

The 2017 PIB release records use of the SC/ST PoA Act alongside the Andaman and Nicobar Islands Protection of Aboriginal Tribes framework in a case concerning objectionable videos of aboriginal tribes. [A: AN-STATE-018]

The 2025 Calcutta High Court decision in Shri Rupin Dang records FIR No. 219/2017 under the PoA Act and the Protection of Aboriginal Tribes Regulation, 1956, arising from the same institutional context. [A: AN-STATE-019]

The interaction between the PoA Act and the UT's special tribal-protection regime is therefore an important jurisdiction-specific interface. A complete current Gazette/notification/circular corpus remains open.

13. Current/recent judicial and police corroboration

The February 2025 Calcutta High Court Circuit Bench at Port Blair decision in Smt. Puspha Nelson records a PoA Special Case before the Special Judge, Port Blair. [A: AN-STATE-013]

The November 2025 Calcutta High Court Circuit Bench at Port Blair decision in Shri Rupin Dang records a PoA FIR registered by Port Blair police in 2017 following a complaint from the Directorate of Tribal Welfare. [A: AN-STATE-019]

The July 2026 Calcutta High Court Circuit Bench at Port Blair decision in The State v. Aziz-Ur-Rehman records a current 2026 Aberdeen Police Station case under PoA provisions and a challenge concerning anticipatory bail. [A: AN-STATE-014]

These are institutional corroboration only. Phase 2 merits synthesis was not undertaken.

14. Related-law interfaces

The PoA Act operates alongside the Andaman and Nicobar Islands Protection of Aboriginal Tribes Regulation, 1956 in documented enforcement involving protected tribal communities. [A: AN-STATE-018, AN-STATE-019]

The 2026 Aziz-Ur-Rehman proceeding also provides current evidence of PoA litigation in the UT after the commencement of the BNS/BNSS/BSA regime. This does not establish complete UT police/prosecution transition instructions. [A: AN-STATE-014]

BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains a separate national workstream.

15. Currentness limitations

The following remain unverified or incompletely verified as of 2026-09-05:

  • current named Rule 9 Nodal Officer and operative appointment instrument;
  • current Rule 8 Protection Cell notification, command, staffing and reporting matrix;
  • current district investigation staffing and Special Police Station status;
  • current Rule 3/Rule 10 Special Officer instruments and identified-area notifications;
  • current State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC membership, calendars, minutes and compliance;
  • complete 2026 Special Court designation instrument and any Exclusive Special Court status;
  • current named SPP/ESPP roster, appointment instruments, tenure and court allocation;
  • current relief rates and payment workflow;
  • current victim/witness travel, maintenance and legal-aid workflow;
  • current contingency and prevention plan;
  • later Section 21(4) reports after the principal 2022 snapshot;
  • complete current UT Gazette/notification/circular corpus for PoA/PCR implementation;
  • current post-BNS/BNSS/BSA police and prosecution transition instructions;
  • current court-wise prosecutor allocation and performance records;
  • current case-level relief/payment audit trail.
