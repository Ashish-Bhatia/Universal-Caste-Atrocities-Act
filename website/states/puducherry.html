Puducherry

Puducherry Phase 1 State/UT Implementation Inventory

Date: 2026-09-06 Disposition: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS

1. Jurisdiction

Puducherry is a Union Territory administered under the Constitution and the Government of Union Territories Act framework. The PoA Act applies as Central legislation. Puducherry has a distinct constitutional ST instrument from 2016.

2. Beneficiary baseline

Census 2011 records 196,325 Scheduled Caste residents in the UT, 15.7% of the population. The Constitution (Puducherry) Scheduled Tribes Order, 2016 specifies Irular, including Villi and Vettaikaran, as the ST for Puducherry. The 2011 census therefore predates formal ST recognition in the UT and must not be used to infer current ST absence.

3. Nodal / Special Officer architecture

Official Puducherry records identify Rule 9 Nodal Officer appointments in 2013 and 2018 and a Rule 10 Special Officer appointment in 2013. A current 2026 holder/notification was not established in the targeted official-source search. This remains open.

4. Protection Cell / policing

Puducherry Police states that PCR Cells operate in Puducherry, Karaikal and Yanam. The PCR Cell registers and investigates cases under the PCR Act and SC/ST PoA Act. Its published structure lists one SP and one Inspector in Puducherry, and SI/ASI/HC/PC staffing in the outlying units. The page states that PoA cases are investigated by the SP, PCR Cell and prosecution is conducted before the II Additional District Sessions Judge.

A 03-07-2014 police press note separately records registration by SHOs and investigation by SDPOs. The relationship between the 2014 allocation and the later/current PCR Cell description is unresolved. No silent reconciliation is made.

5. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

A State Level V&MC was reconstituted on 28-06-2022 under Rule 16, chaired by the Chief Minister. A 06-08-2025 notification constituted DLVMCs for Puducherry District, comprising Puducherry, Mahe and Yanam, and for Karaikal District. The 2025 notification requires quarterly meetings to review implementation, relief and rehabilitation, registration/disposal of cases and performance of officials/agencies. Annual nominations are specified for non-official and specified official members.

6. Special Court

The Puducherry Police's current PCR Cell profile reproduces G.O. Ms. No.24/90-LLD dated 30-03-1990, under which the II Additional Sessions Judge, Puducherry was specified as Special Judge for the whole UT for PoA cases. No later superseding Special Judge notification was located in the targeted search. Current court-allocation verification remains open.

7. Prosecution

The same police profile records prosecution under the PoA Act before the II Additional District Sessions Judge. G.O. Ms. No.14/2023-LD dated 28-04-2023 appointed P. Kumarasan as Special Public Prosecutor for the SC/ST (POA) Act in II ADJ. The current 2026 prosecution roster was not established.

8. Victim legal aid / services

The 2025 Law Department notification appointed the Executive Chairman of the Union Territory of Puducherry Legal Services Authority. The Police PCR profile states that victim facilities and legal aid are provided through the Adi Dravidar Welfare Department. Current victim-service workflow and dedicated PoA legal-aid referral arrangements require further verification.

9. Relief / rehabilitation

A 2020-21 official Adi Dravidar Welfare note reports 13 PoA cases registered, 31 victims compensated and Rs.69,21,696 spent. It also reports five deceased-victim families receiving pension then fixed at Rs.5,000 plus DA. A 02-12-2025 Gazette order revised the applicable atrocity pension to Rs.17,600 per month, comprising Rs.5,000 basic pension plus 252% DA, with directions for immediate implementation.

10. Statistical baseline

Official Census 2011 data records UT population of 1,247,953 and SC population of 196,325. NCRB/PoA statistical coverage exists through official Central reporting, but a current 2023-2025 Puducherry-specific PoA case/disposal/relief series was not established in this workstream. The project therefore does not infer current incidence or performance from the historical 2020-21 figures.

11. Current criminal-law transition

Puducherry Police's 2024 Manual and current Police materials recognise BNS/BNSS/BSA implementation. The precise PoA-specific local investigation/prosecution workflow under the new criminal laws remains a verification item. No numerical CrPC-to-BNSS substitution is assumed.

12. Related-law interfaces

Relevant interfaces include the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955; BNS; BNSS; BSA; Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987; Puducherry Police framework; and the constitutional SC/ST identity instruments. The PCR Cell operationally covers both PCR and PoA matters.

13. Phase 1 residuals

  • Current Rule 9 Nodal Officer instrument/holder.
  • Current Rule 10 Special Officer instrument/holder.
  • Current PoA-specific investigation allocation after the 2014 SHO/SDPO instruction and later PCR Cell description.
  • Current Special Judge notification and court-allocation matrix.
  • Current 2026 Special Public Prosecutor roster.
  • Current State V&MC meeting record and compliance frequency after the 2022 reconstitution.
  • Current DLVMC meeting records and annual nomination status after the 2025 constitution.
  • Current PoA-specific legal-aid referral workflow.
  • Current annual PoA case registration, charge-sheet, disposal and pendency data.
  • Current relief disbursement and rehabilitation audit after the 2025 pension revision.
  • Current Rule 7 investigation and 60-day charge-sheet compliance data.
  • Current PoA-specific Gazette/SOP corpus after BNS/BNSS/BSA transition.
  • Current digital FIR/case-management interface for PCR/PoA cases.
  • Current State/UT annual report under the PoA framework and latest Section 21 reporting.
  • Current SC/ST population baseline after recognition of Irular as ST in 2016.
  • Current Special Court functioning and case allocation across Puducherry and Karaikal.

14. Conclusion

Puducherry has a documented PoA implementation architecture, including a dedicated PCR Cell, State and District V&MC structures, a specified Special Judge framework, prosecution arrangements and relief mechanisms. The evidence is sufficient for Phase 1 closure with limitations, but several current-holder, current-roster, current-case-flow and post-2024 criminal-law transition questions remain open. This inventory does not support any policy-superiority, necessity or constitutional-validity conclusion about a proposed replacement law.
