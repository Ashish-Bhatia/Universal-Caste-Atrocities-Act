West Bengal

West Bengal

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

Scope

West Bengal-specific existing-law implementation inventory for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, PCR interfaces, State machinery, police, courts/prosecutors, relief and monitoring. No policy-superiority, necessity or constitutional-validity analysis.

Rule 9

The 2022 Ministry Section 21(4) report records West Bengal as having a Rule 9 Nodal Officer. [A: WB-STATE-001] The West Bengal Backward Classes Welfare Department's official archive contains "Appointment of Nodal Officer under PoA Act", memo 1333-BCW. [A: WB-STATE-003] Historical Ministry reports identify the Secretary/Principal Secretary, Backward Classes Welfare Department as Nodal Officer. [A: WB-STATE-004, WB-STATE-005] Current officer, operative instrument and tenure remain unverified.

Rule 8 Protection Cell / investigation

The 2022 Ministry report records a State SC/ST Protection Cell under DGP/IGP supervision. [A: WB-STATE-001] Historical reports record the same architecture. [A: WB-STATE-004, WB-STATE-005] West Bengal Police Order No. 7 of 2015 requires CID month-wise monitoring of PoA/PCR cases, including pending investigations and cases exceeding the Rule 7 period, with IGP-level monitoring. [A: WB-STATE-006] Current Rule 8 command, staffing and reporting matrix remain open.

Special Police Stations

The Ministry's 2021-22 implementation table records no dedicated Special Police Stations for West Bengal. [A: WB-STATE-002] A separate Ministry PCR report lists dedicated SC/ST Special Police Stations only for Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Kerala and Madhya Pradesh. [A: WB-STATE-007] No current West Bengal dedicated-station census was independently retrieved. Search silence is not treated as absence.

Rule 7 / charge-sheet timing

The 2022 Section 21(4) dataset records 118 West Bengal charge-sheets within 60 days and no reported figure in the more-than-60-days field. [A/B: WB-STATE-001, WB-STATE-008] Police Order No. 7 of 2015 requires monitoring of delays beyond the Rule 7 period. [A: WB-STATE-006] These are reporting-period/historical findings, not current 2026 compliance conclusions.

Rule 10 / atrocity-prone areas

The 2022 report records zero atrocity-prone districts. [A: WB-STATE-001] Historical Ministry reporting recorded no identified atrocity-prone district in 2014 but recorded Rule 10 Special Officers in Howrah, Malda, Dakshin Dinajpur and Nadia. [A: WB-STATE-011] The current departmental website states West Bengal is not an atrocity-prone area while also stating atrocities occur and implementation machinery exists. [A: WB-STATE-012] Current Rule 3/10 instruments and Special Officer appointments remain open. No present absence conclusion is drawn.

V&MCs

The 2022 Ministry report records a functioning State V&MC with no State meeting during 2022, 39 District V&MC meetings and 17 Sub-Divisional meetings. [A: WB-STATE-001] The official departmental archive contains the 29 November 2011 State V&MC constitution notification under the Chief Minister, with the Secretary, Backward Classes Welfare Department as Convener and January/July meeting schedule. [A: WB-STATE-013] The department also maintains an order concerning Sub-Divisional V&MC formation. [A: WB-STATE-014] Current membership, reconstitution, calendars, minutes and compliance remain open.

Special / Exclusive Special Courts

Historical Ministry material records designated Special Courts in West Bengal. [A: WB-STATE-004] A 2024 Rajya Sabha answer states West Bengal has 22 designated Special Courts under Section 14. [A: WB-STATE-015] 2026 Calcutta High Court decisions corroborate active PoA Special Courts in Howrah and Raiganj, while a 2025 decision records a Special Court in Cooch Behar. [A: WB-STATE-016, WB-STATE-017, WB-STATE-018] These do not establish a complete 2026 court census or current designation notifications. Current Exclusive Special Court status remains open.

SPP / ESPP

The 2021-22 Ministry implementation table records Special Public Prosecutors for West Bengal and no Exclusive Special Courts in that table. [A: WB-STATE-002] The current State department states Special Public Prosecutors have been appointed and maintains an instrument concerning the name of the Public Prosecutor. [A: WB-STATE-012, WB-STATE-019] Current named roster, appointment instruments, tenure, qualifications and allocation remain open.

Relief / payment

Current Ministry CSS data records West Bengal relief recipients of 107, 556, 319, 347 and 430 for 2020-21 through 2024-25, with central assistance of Rs. 0.37 crore, Rs. 0.87 crore, Rs. 0.24 crore, Rs. 3.69 crore and Rs. 4.64 crore. [A: WB-STATE-020] The State department describes PoA/PCR implementation machinery and the State budget contains relevant relief/protection/enforcement scheme heads. [A: WB-STATE-012, WB-STATE-021, WB-STATE-022] Individual payment timing, current amended-rule rates, seven-day compliance, victim/witness expenses and case-level auditability remain open.

Annual reporting / statistical context

The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal recent institutional snapshot located. Later Section 21(4) reports after 2022 were not independently retrieved as direct primary State-specific material. This is a retrieval limitation, not an absence finding. The 2024 Rajya Sabha answer reports 22 designated Special Courts and reproduces NCRB conviction data for 2018-2022. [A: WB-STATE-015]

State PoA/PCR instruments

The current West Bengal Backward Classes Welfare Department Acts & Rules archive contains the PoA Act, PoA Rules, PCR Act, PoA amendments, police investigation guidelines and instruments concerning Special Courts and Public Prosecutors. [A: WB-STATE-003] A complete current Gazette/GO/circular corpus remains unverified.

Current judicial / police corroboration

2026 Calcutta High Court decisions show active PoA Special Court proceedings in Howrah and Raiganj; a 2025 decision records a PoA Special Court in Cooch Behar. [A: WB-STATE-016, WB-STATE-017, WB-STATE-018] A March 2026 Paschim Medinipur Police report records a case combining BNS provisions with PoA sections 3(1)(r), 3(1)(s) and 3(1)(za)(C). [A: WB-STATE-023] This is institutional corroboration only, not Phase 2 case-law research.

Related-law interface

The State department publishes PCR and PoA instruments and police guidelines covering both. [A: WB-STATE-003] Police Order No. 7 of 2015 expressly covers both PoA and PCR cases. [A: WB-STATE-006] Current police records show PoA charges alongside BNS provisions. Complete BNSS/BSA transition instructions remain a separate national workstream.

Currentness limitations

Open: current Rule 9 officer/instrument; Rule 8 command/staffing; district investigation machinery; dedicated Special Police Stations; Rule 3/10 instruments; current V&MC membership and compliance; complete 2026 court census; SPP/ESPP roster; current relief rates/payment workflow; contingency plan; post-2022 Section 21(4) reports; complete State GO/Gazette/circular corpus; post-BNS/BNSS/BSA transition instructions; PoA-specific legal-aid/victim-witness workflow; court-wise prosecutor allocation and performance records.
