Haryana

Haryana State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map Inventory date: 2026-09-05

Scope and evidence rule

This record maps Haryana-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and related civil-rights/atrocity interfaces. It is not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion. Currentness is distinguished from historical reporting. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

1. Departmental and nodal architecture

The current Government of Haryana Social Justice, Empowerment, Welfare of SC & BC and Antyodaya Department identifies administration of the Protection of Civil Rights Act and SC/ST PoA Rules among the functions of the Welfare of SCs/BCs wing. An official Government of India implementation report for Haryana states that an Additional Chief Secretary in Government of Haryana was appointed as the Rule 9 nodal officer. This establishes the historical/official architecture but does not freeze the current 2026 office-holder or appointment instrument. [HRY-STATE-001, HRY-STATE-004]

The Haryana Home Department's current work-distribution record identifies a Home-II branch function covering complaints of SC/ST/BC matters and monthly/annual reports, confirming a continuing administrative interface at the Home Department level. [HRY-STATE-010]

2. Protection Cell and police implementation

Official Government of India reporting for Haryana states that a Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Protection Cell under the direct supervision of an Additional Director General of Police/Inspector General of Police was established at Police Headquarters, with Special Cells in every district under the Superintendent of Police for speedy investigation of atrocity cases. Earlier official reporting also records a Special Cell at Police Headquarters, Panchkula. These sources establish the reported institutional architecture for their reporting periods. The current 2026 staffing, exact unit structure, present office-holder and operative order were not frozen by the targeted search. [HRY-STATE-004, HRY-STATE-005]

The current Haryana Police site maintains a State Crime Records Bureau and current police organisational structure. It also publishes 2025 crime figures and lists the SC/ST (PoA) Act among electronically available crime information. These establish current police data/publication capability but do not themselves establish a dedicated current PoA Protection Cell structure. [HRY-STATE-006, HRY-STATE-007]

3. Special Officers and atrocity-prone areas

An official Government of India implementation report states that, although no atrocity-prone area was identified in Haryana at the reporting point, all District Magistrates were designated Special Officers under Rule 10. The same report states that no particular pocket had been identified where such incidents occurred repeatedly. A later 2022 Haryana State Annual Report similarly states that no atrocity-prone area was identified in the State. These are reporting-period findings, not a 2026 notification census. [HRY-STATE-004, HRY-STATE-008]

No current 2026 primary Rule 10 designation order or current atrocity-prone-area notification was retrieved in this pass. No absence conclusion is drawn.

4. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The current Haryana Social Justice Department documents repository publishes State Monitoring Committee and District/Sub-Divisional Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee instruments dated 21-10-2014, together with later State/District and Sub-Division committee notifications dated July 2015. The documents are official State implementation instruments and establish that the State has maintained a formal committee framework. [HRY-STATE-002]

A Government of India/Central implementation record for Haryana states that a State Level Monitoring and Vigilance Committee under the Chief Minister, District Level Vigilance Committees under District Magistrates and Sub-Division Level Vigilance Committees under Sub-Divisional Magistrates had been set up to review implementation and monetary relief. The 2022 State Annual Report also records this architecture. Current 2026 constitution orders, membership and meeting records remain to be frozen. [HRY-STATE-008]

5. Special Courts

A primary Haryana Gazette notification dated 08-09-2017, published 12-09-2017, superseded the 2011 notification and specified the Courts of all first Additional Sessions Judges in every district of Haryana as Special Courts to try offences under the PoA Act, with concurrence of the Punjab and Haryana High Court. [HRY-STATE-003]

The current Haryana Prosecution Department maintains a specific publication titled “Special Courts under SC and ST (POA) Act”, indicating continuing departmental maintenance of this subject. The current contents and a complete 2026 court-notification matrix were not retrieved in this pass. [HRY-STATE-009]

Because Haryana's district/court structure has changed since 2017, the 2017 notification is retained as a verified primary historical instrument and is not treated as a complete 2026 court census.

6. SPP/ESPP and prosecution machinery

The official Government of India implementation report states that the Public Prosecutor posted in each Session Court specified as a Special Court was designated as Special Public Prosecutor for PoA cases. The current Haryana Prosecution Department continues to publish a dedicated Special Courts under SC/ST PoA Act document. The current individual SPP/ESPP appointment register and any later replacements or Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor appointments were not frozen in this pass. [HRY-STATE-004, HRY-STATE-009]

The 2022 State Annual Report states that the first Additional Sessions Judge in each district had been designated as Special Court under the 2017 notification. It does not establish current individual prosecutorial appointments. [HRY-STATE-008]

7. Relief, rehabilitation and financial administration

The current Haryana Social Justice Department scheme page states that monetary relief is available to Scheduled Caste victims of specified atrocities where an FIR is registered under the PoA Act. It states a published assistance range of Rs.85,000 to Rs.8,25,000, references Annexure-I of the PoA Rules, and states that payment is made into the bank account of the victim or family/dependant. The Deputy Commissioner is identified as sanctioning authority and the District Welfare Officer as DDO. [HRY-STATE-011]

The same State service-rights page gives a seven-working-day service time for “Monetary Relief to the Victim of Atrocities”, with the Deputy Commissioner as first grievance authority and Divisional Commissioner as second grievance authority, and directs online applications through the SARAL Haryana portal. This is evidence of a current administrative service workflow, but it does not establish case-level compliance or the complete treasury/DBT process. [HRY-STATE-012]

The 2023-24 Haryana budget material located through an official government-hosted document provides an outlay of Rs.28 crore, split equally between State and Central share, for financial assistance to victims of atrocities and states that assistance is transferred to victims' bank accounts immediately on registration of an FIR under sections 3(1) or 3(2) of the PoA Act. This is a budget/reporting-period source and should not be substituted for a current 2026 payment-order census. [HRY-STATE-013]

8. Awareness, legal aid and administrative functions

The current Haryana department objectives state that the Welfare of SCs/BCs wing administers the PCR Act, handles SC/ST-related welfare matters and provides legal-aid-related welfare support. Haryana State Legal Services Authority material also identifies rights under the SC/ST PoA Act among subjects for rural legal literacy/awareness camps. [HRY-STATE-001, HRY-STATE-014]

9. Police data and digital interface

Haryana Police's current RTI/public-information page provides 2025 crime figures and reports 34 cases under “The SC/ST (POA) Act, 1989/2015”. The same current page provides BNS and IPC cross-reference labels for other offence categories. This is a current State police data source, but the 34 figure is a police crime-count field and should not be interpreted as a conviction, prosecution, pendency or victim count. [HRY-STATE-006]

The current Haryana Police public information portal supports district selection and date-range reporting. The current Police site also provides FIR download and citizen-facing police services. No dedicated PoA-specific current e-FIR or PoA case-management workflow was established from the targeted official material. [HRY-STATE-007]

10. Protection of Civil Rights Act interface

The current Social Justice Department Acts & Rules page publishes the PCR Act, 1955 and PCR Rules, 1977 alongside the PoA Act and Rules. The department's stated functions expressly include eradication of untouchability and administration of the PCR Act. This establishes the State administrative interface; detailed clause-level interaction remains governed by the completed Central PCR comparison and final Phase 1 closure work. [HRY-STATE-001, HRY-STATE-002]

11. Manual scavenging interface

The current Haryana Social Justice Department Acts of WSCBC page publishes the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013 and a 28-07-2021 review/implementation document. The current department Acts & Rules index also publishes the Act. Haryana's Urban Local Bodies Department identifies Manual Scavengers, compensation for sewer/open-drain death cases, the National Commission for Safai Karamcharis and Social Justice/SC matters within its sanitation-wing functions. These sources establish a current administrative interface, but a complete 2026 State instrument census was not completed in this pass. [HRY-STATE-015, HRY-STATE-016]

12. Bonded labour interface

Haryana State Legal Services Authority material identifies the Bonded Labour (Abolition) Act, 1976 as a legal-literacy subject. The targeted official Haryana search did not freeze a distinct current Haryana bonded-labour implementation order or rule set. This is an evidence gap, not an absence finding. [HRY-STATE-014]

13. Inter-caste marriage and related social-protection interface

The current Haryana Social Justice Department publishes the Mukhya Mantri Samajik Samrasta Antarjatiya Vivah Shagun Yojana. The scheme provides an incentive to qualifying inter-caste couples where one spouse is from a Scheduled Caste and provides for online application through SARAL Haryana. This is a related social-protection interface, not an atrocity-law enforcement instrument. [HRY-STATE-017]

14. State institutional interface

The current Haryana Acts of WSCBC page publishes the Haryana State Commission for Scheduled Castes Act, 2018 and Haryana State Commission for Scheduled Castes Rules, 2019. These instruments provide a State-level institutional interface for Scheduled Caste safeguards and complaints. Their detailed relationship with PoA enforcement should be crosswalked only where material during later Phase 1 closure. [HRY-STATE-018]

15. Currentness assessment

Haryana is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. The official source base establishes:

  • a State departmental mandate covering PCR and PoA implementation;
  • historical/official Rule 9 nodal-officer architecture;
  • reported State and district SC/ST Protection Cell architecture;
  • Rule 10 Special Officer architecture in official reporting;
  • formal State, District and Sub-Divisional Vigilance and Monitoring Committee instruments;
  • a verified 08-09-2017 Special Court notification covering first Additional Sessions Judges in every district at that time;
  • continuing Prosecution Department publication of a dedicated SC/ST PoA Special Courts document;
  • a current published victim-relief service with identified authorities and a seven-working-day service target;
  • current State police publication of 2025 SC/ST PoA crime figures;
  • current PCR, Manual Scavengers and Scheduled Caste Commission statutory interfaces; and
  • a current inter-caste marriage social-protection scheme.

The following remain open for final Phase 1 closure:

  • Current primary Rule 9 designation/appointment instrument and individual office-holder.
  • Current Protection Cell establishment/order, staffing and district unit matrix.
  • Current Rule 10 Special Officer orders.
  • Current atrocity-prone-area notifications, if any, and their operative status.
  • Complete current Special/Exclusive Special Court notification matrix after district reorganisation.
  • Current PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment register.
  • Current relief-rate/order, treasury/DBT and case-processing workflow.
  • Current PoA-specific Gazette/SOP/circular/manual set.
  • Current PoA-specific digital/FIR/case-management workflow.
  • Current V&MC constitution orders, membership and meeting records.
  • Current bonded-labour implementation instruments.
  • Current Manual Scavengers State/District/local implementation instruments beyond the 28-07-2021 review record.
