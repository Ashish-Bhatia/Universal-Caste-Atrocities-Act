Rajasthan

Rajasthan

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

Scope

This jurisdiction inventory covers Rajasthan-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, related Protection of Civil Rights interfaces, State institutional arrangements, police machinery, courts/prosecutors, relief, monitoring committees and recent official corroboration. It is an existing-law inventory, not a policy-superiority or constitutional-validity conclusion.

1. Governing instruments and State publication

The Rajasthan Department of Social Justice and Empowerment (SJED) maintains a current Acts & Rules catalogue listing the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989, SC/ST Prevention of Atrocities Rules, 1995, Protection of Civil Rights and related atrocity documents. The site was last updated in August 2026. [A: RJ-STATE-001, RJ-STATE-002]

The Central Act applies in Rajasthan. India Code identifies the Act as applicable to Rajasthan and records the 30 January 1990 enforcement date. [A: RJ-STATE-003]

The Rajasthan SJED repository also hosts the post-2015/2016 amended PoA material and the 2016 amended Rules. The Rules page confirms State publication of the Rules. [A: RJ-STATE-002, RJ-STATE-004]

2. Rule 9 nodal architecture

The current 2026 Rule 9 appointment order was not located in this first-pass inventory. Historical Section 21(4) material and the State implementation architecture establish an SJED/police coordination structure, but a current named Rule 9 Nodal Officer must not be inferred from historical records. [B: RJ-STATE-005]

Current Rule 9 appointment and order number remain open residuals.

3. Rule 8 Protection Cell and police machinery

The 2022 Section 21(4) State report records a Civil Rights Cell at Police Headquarters. It was headed by a Superintendent of Police under the supervision of the Additional Director General of Police (Civil Rights), with a Deputy Inspector General of Police (Civil Rights) and other staff. The report also records 37 SC/ST Cells in 33 districts, headed by Deputy Superintendents of Police and entrusted with atrocity-related work. [A: RJ-STATE-005]

Rajasthan Police's current online district list continues to expose district-level SC/ST Cell entries in multiple districts, corroborating that SC/ST Cell machinery exists in the current police structure. This is not treated as proof of the complete current statewide staffing or posting matrix. [A: RJ-STATE-006]

Current officer-service records also show recent Civil Rights and SC/ST Cell postings, including an Additional SP Civil Rights post at Police Headquarters through July 2026 and a Deputy SP SC/ST Cell posting in Pali in November 2025. These records corroborate continuing Civil Rights/SC-ST specialist functions but do not establish the full current establishment order. [A: RJ-STATE-007, RJ-STATE-008]

A 2022 Government of India PIB release lists Rajasthan among States/UTs with a State-level SC/ST Protection Cell under Rule 8. [A: RJ-STATE-009]

4. Rule 7 investigation and charge-sheet timing

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records 4,195 cases charge-sheeted within 60 days and 5,445 cases charge-sheeted later than 60 days. These are 2022 reporting-period figures and are not 2026 compliance measurements. [A: RJ-STATE-005]

The 2022 data therefore establishes a substantial historical late-charge-sheet volume, while the current rate and current investigation instructions remain open.

5. Rule 10 / atrocity-prone areas

The 2022 State report identifies specific areas requiring preventive measures. It records Bharatpur, Alwar, Bhiwadi and other district entries, including a specific declaration concerning Khasara No. 18/875, Village Dhindhor, Behror, Bhiwadi, and preventive action in Sri Ganganagar. The searchable report should be treated as a historical reporting-period snapshot. [A: RJ-STATE-010]

An older official/secondary reproduction records 18 atrocity-prone districts in an earlier reporting period. This is historical and is not substituted for the 2022 or current position. [C: RJ-STATE-011]

Current 2026 Rule 10 Special Officer orders and a current statewide atrocity-prone-area notification were not located in the first-pass search. This remains open.

6. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records a State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee chaired by the Chief Minister, with no State-level meeting during 2022. It records 123 District-level meetings across 33 districts and six Sub-Divisional meetings in four sub-divisions during 2022. [A: RJ-STATE-005]

The Rajasthan SJED repository contains an order concerning constitution of Sub-Divisional Vigilance and Monitoring Committees dated 8 August 2018 and a 2019 State-level monitoring committee order. [A: RJ-STATE-012, RJ-STATE-013]

A 2023 Rajasthan Government/officially reported meeting of the State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee occurred under the Chief Minister. The event is useful corroboration of post-2022 activity but does not establish compliance with every statutory meeting cycle. [B: RJ-STATE-014]

Current 2024-2026 State-level and district/sub-divisional meeting calendars/minutes were not fully located. This remains open.

7. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records 35 districts and Exclusive Special Courts in 31 districts: Ajmer, Alwar, Barmer, Baran, Bharatpur, Bhilwara, Bikaner, Bundi, Chittorgarh, Churu, Dausa, Dholpur, Ganganagar, Hanumangarh, Jaipur, Jaisalmer, Jalore, Jhalawar, Jhunjhunu, Jodhpur City, Karauli, Kota, Nagaur, Pali, Pratapgarh, Rajsamand, Sawai Madhopur, Sikar, Sirohi, Tonk and Udaipur. It records District and Sessions Courts designated as Special Courts in Banswara, Dungarpur, Jaipur Metro-II and Jaipur District. [A: RJ-STATE-015]

The 2025 Rajasthan High Court administrative record confirms continuing Special Court, SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) posts, including Dausa and Jhalawar. [A: RJ-STATE-016, RJ-STATE-017]

The 2022 report records 31 Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors for the 31 Exclusive Special Courts and three Special Public Prosecutors for the four designated Special Courts. [A: RJ-STATE-015]

A current statewide 2026 court notification and current SPP/ESPP roster were not located. The 2022 structure therefore remains a historical implementation snapshot, corroborated by selected 2025 judicial postings.

8. Relief, rehabilitation and victim/witness expenses

The 2022 report records travel and maintenance expenses for 32 persons under Rule 11 and relief to 15,474 atrocity victims. [A: RJ-STATE-015]

The Rajasthan SJED site continues to expose an Atrocity Scheme service entry and a published relief framework. The current online service presence does not by itself establish the current payment workflow, sanction authority, processing times or reconciliation against the Rule 12 seven-day requirement. [A: RJ-STATE-018, RJ-STATE-019]

The amended Rules require immediate relief within seven days and require State budgetary provisions and twice-yearly review of implementation performance. Rajasthan's current compliance against these requirements remains open. [A: RJ-STATE-004]

9. Current/recent police statistics

Rajasthan Police publishes monthly comparative SC/ST atrocity statistics. The December 2024 SC table records 7,007 total registered cases in the category reported as SC atrocity for 2024, compared with 8,291 in 2022 and 8,212 in 2023. The same table records 2,613 cases under the SC/ST Act category in 2024, compared with 3,091 in 2022 and 2,979 in 2023. [A: RJ-STATE-020]

The November 2024 ST table records 2,194 total registered ST-atrocity cases through November 2024, compared with 2,667 through November 2022 and 2,537 through November 2023. It records 860 cases under the SC/ST Act category through November 2024. These are police reporting-period figures, not the Central annual Section 21(4) series. [A: RJ-STATE-021]

The Central 2022 Section 21(4) report records 8,651 SC atrocity cases and 2,498 ST atrocity cases in Rajasthan for 2022 in its State-wide tables. These figures should not be mechanically substituted for NCRB/State Police series without documenting the source definitions. [A: RJ-STATE-022, RJ-STATE-023]

10. State-law and institutional interfaces

The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly records the Rajasthan State Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Development Fund (Planning, Allocation and Utilization of Financial Resources) Bill, 2022, and the Assembly Bill text describes the institutional and budgetary framework for SC/ST development. This is not an atrocity-law instrument but is relevant to the broader State institutional context. [A: RJ-STATE-024, RJ-STATE-025]

The Rajasthan SJED website separately identifies Protection of Civil Rights as a State-administered legal interface. The first-pass inventory does not establish a distinct Rajasthan substantive anti-discrimination offence code beyond the Central PCR framework. [A: RJ-STATE-002]

11. Legal-aid interface

Rajasthan State Legal Services Authority material records statewide legal-services infrastructure, and the national judiciary annual-report material records LADCS functioning across Rajasthan's DLSAs and campaigns concerning freedom from untouchability and prevention of atrocities. Current case-specific referral and victim-assistance workflow for PoA matters was not independently verified in this first pass. [B: RJ-STATE-026]

12. Recent judicial/police corroboration

Rajasthan High Court records in 2025 continue to identify Special Courts for SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Cases. The High Court also maintains recent case material involving the PoA Act. One 2025 judgment, Roop Lal v State of Rajasthan, discusses Section 3(1)(x) and the operation of a Special Judge, SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Cases, and ultimately sets aside the PoA conviction while maintaining convictions for other offences. It is recorded here only as jurisdictional judicial corroboration, not as a Phase 2 case-law analysis. [A: RJ-STATE-016, RJ-STATE-017, RJ-STATE-027]

13. Currentness limitations

The following remain unverified as of 2026-09-05:

  • current Rule 9 named Nodal Officer and appointment order;
  • current Rule 10 Special Officer orders;
  • current statewide atrocity-prone-area notification/list;
  • current Civil Rights Cell establishment order and staffing matrix;
  • current district SC/ST Cell establishment and staffing matrix;
  • current Special/Exclusive Special Court notifications after subsequent district reorganisation;
  • current SPP/ESPP roster and appointment orders;
  • current State, District and Sub-Divisional V&MC meeting compliance and minutes;
  • current relief sanction/payment workflow and seven-day compliance;
  • current annual State reports for later years where not independently located;
  • current post-BNS/BNSS/BSA instructions and State police/prosecution transition orders;
  • full current legal-aid referral and victim/witness support workflow.
