Madhya Pradesh

Madhya Pradesh, State Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map Inventory date: 2026-09-05

Scope and evidence discipline

This is a State implementation inventory, not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion. Current State sources are preferred. Historical Ministry reports are retained only for their reporting periods. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

Current State administrative/service layer

The Madhya Pradesh CM Helpline scheme directory, last updated 20-08-2026, currently lists “अत्याचार निवारण अधिनियम” under the Tribal Affairs Department and separately lists an ST relief scheme. The State Social Justice and Disabled Persons Welfare Department maintains a current 2026 publication, circular and archive system. These sources establish current public-facing administrative/service architecture, but the underlying operative PoA orders and case-processing workflow remain to be retrieved. [MP-STATE-002, MP-STATE-003, MP-STATE-020]

Protection Cell and police machinery

Official Central State-specific reporting records a State SC/ST Protection Cell under an Additional Director General of Police and 51 Scheduled Caste Welfare Special Police Stations in the 2020 and 2022 reporting periods. Historical reports also record a Directorate-level Protection of Civil Rights Cell. Current district-level MP Police material independently identifies an “अजा/अजाक” police station and DSP Ajak post in Balaghat. A current statewide establishment, staffing and territorial matrix has not been frozen. [MP-STATE-004, MP-STATE-010, MP-STATE-013, MP-STATE-014]

Investigation and Rule 7

The 2011 official PoA implementation report expressly records Deputy Superintendents of Police in all districts as Investigating Officers under Rule 7. The 2022 official report records 6,083 investigations/charge-sheets completed within 60 days and 4,038 later than 60 days. These figures are reporting-period evidence and do not establish the current 2026 investigation workflow. [MP-STATE-005, MP-STATE-007, MP-STATE-013]

Rule 9 nodal architecture

Official Central reports identify a Secretary-level State nodal officer under Rule 9 for Madhya Pradesh. The 2016 and later official reports identify Madhya Pradesh among States with a Rule 9 nodal officer. A current 2026 primary designation/appointment instrument and individual office-holder have not yet been retrieved. [MP-STATE-004, MP-STATE-005, MP-STATE-018]

Rule 10 and atrocity-prone/sensitive areas

Historical official reporting identifies Madhya Pradesh as having atrocity-prone/sensitive areas and records Special Officer arrangements in identified districts. A 09-07-2024 Madhya Pradesh Legislative Assembly record specifically concerns district, police-station and area lists of notified atrocity-sensitive areas and historical SC/ST offence data. The detailed current annexure and current primary notifications remain open. [MP-STATE-012, MP-STATE-018, MP-STATE-019]

Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

Official reports record a State Level V&MC chaired by the Chief Minister and District Level V&MCs chaired by District Magistrates. In 2022, 118 DLVMC meetings were reported across 52 districts, while no State-level V&MC meeting was reported for that year. Earlier reports record 167 meetings in 50 districts in 2011 and 154 meetings in 51 districts in 2015. These are reporting-period figures. The current State/District/Sub-Divisional committee constitution and meeting matrix remains open. [MP-STATE-004, MP-STATE-005, MP-STATE-006, MP-STATE-013, MP-STATE-014]

Special Courts and prosecutors

The High Court of Madhya Pradesh current Judicial Information directory, as on 15-05-2026, identifies Special Judge SC/ST (P.A.) Act postings in multiple districts, including Indore, Satna, Dhar, Jhabua, Seoni, Vidisha, Guna, Rewa, Narmadapuram, Damoh, Neemuch, Shivpuri, Harda, Datia, Mandla and Narsinghpur. The High Court Transfer/Posting archive also records 16-03-2026 communication 248-Confdl-2026 concerning posting of Special Judges SC/ST PA Act. This establishes current judicial operation at identified locations but not a complete statewide notification and jurisdiction matrix. Current PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment orders/register remain open. [MP-STATE-008, MP-STATE-009]

Relief, rehabilitation and funding

Current Central scheme data records 9,951, 10,081, 6,582, 10,834 and 19,359 Madhya Pradesh persons provided relief in 2020-21 through 2024-25 respectively. Central assistance released was Rs.83.49 crore, Rs.103.41 crore, Rs.31.82 crore, Rs.63.49 crore and Rs.88.12 crore respectively. These figures are scheme-level reported activity and do not establish individual entitlement or payment compliance. A secondary 2025-26 budget report states a Rs.25 crore State provision for the Scheduled Caste Atrocities Prevention Act, but the underlying State budget document has not been retrieved and the figure is retained only as a lead. [MP-STATE-001, MP-STATE-017]

Current training and post-BNSS interface

Datia Police reported a 15-03-2026 district workshop on the SC/ST Atrocities Act and cyber-forensics. Police, prosecution and judicial participants covered mandatory PoA investigation/evidence requirements, BNS/BNSS timing, electronic evidence and forensic workflow. This demonstrates current implementation/training activity but is not a statewide SOP. Current post-BNSS State police/prosecution instructions remain open. [MP-STATE-011]

Related-law interfaces

The M.P. CODE State legal repository provides the current State legal publication environment. Targeted retrieval confirms the continuing Land Revenue Code framework and other State land-law instruments. Current Panchayat Raj, bonded-labour and Manual Scavengers implementation instruments have not yet been comprehensively frozen in this first-pass inventory. No absence conclusion is drawn from the search gaps. [MP-STATE-016]

Current digital/service evidence

The CM Helpline directory provides current public scheme lookup/status architecture for the PoA scheme. This should not be equated with a PoA-specific FIR or case-management system. A dedicated current PoA digital workflow remains an open verification item. [MP-STATE-002, MP-STATE-020]

Residual closure items

  • Current Rule 9 appointment/designation and office-holder.
  • Current Protection Cell order, staffing and command structure.
  • Complete current 51-plus Special Police Station/SC-ST police-unit census and legal basis.
  • Current Rule 10 Special Officer orders and atrocity-prone-area notifications.
  • Current State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC constitution, membership and meeting matrix.
  • Current Special/Exclusive Special Court notification and jurisdiction matrix.
  • Current SPP/ESPP appointment orders/register.
  • Current relief-rate, sanction, treasury/DBT and case-processing workflow.
  • Current PoA-specific Gazette/SOP/circular/manual and Rule 15 contingency-plan set.
  • Current PoA-specific digital/FIR/case-management workflow.
  • Current PCR implementation orders and operational interface.
  • Current Manual Scavengers State/District/local-body implementation instruments.
  • Current bonded-labour vigilance committee orders and records.
  • Current land/local-government interface matrix, including Land Revenue Code and Panchayat Raj instruments.
  • Current post-BNSS police/prosecution instructions.
  • Primary retrieval of the 2025-26 State budget provision attributed to the PoA Act.
  • Primary retrieval of current atrocity-prone/sensitive-area notifications and detailed 09-07-2024 Assembly annexure.

Status determination

Madhya Pradesh is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. The record establishes current public-facing PoA service architecture, current Special Judge postings and current PoA-related police/prosecution training. Official Central reporting establishes a substantial historical implementation architecture, including an ADGP-led Protection Cell, 51 Special Police Stations, State/District monitoring committees, Secretary-level Rule 9 nodal architecture and DSP-level Rule 7 investigation. The current primary-instrument census is incomplete. No historical figure is treated as a 2026 census, and no absence is inferred from search silence.
