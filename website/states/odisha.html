Odisha

Odisha: Phase 1 Existing-Law Baseline

Date: 2026-09-05 Disposition: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS

Scope

This jurisdiction pass inventories Odisha-specific implementation of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, using primary or official sources where available. It does not assess the desirability, necessity or constitutional validity of a proposed Universal Caste Atrocities Act.

1. State institutional machinery

Rule 8 Protection Cell

Odisha has a Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Protection Cell under the Additional Director General of Police, Human Rights Protection Cell, at State Police Headquarters, Cuttack. The 2022 Section 21(4) report records the State Protection Cell and District Human Rights Protection Cells. The Odisha Police currently describes HRPC as a specialised State Police wing monitoring investigations involving atrocities against SC/ST communities. Evidence: A.

Rule 9 Nodal Officer

The 2022 Section 21(4) report identifies the Commissioner-cum-Secretary, ST & SC Development, Minorities & Backward Classes Welfare Department, Government of Odisha, as the Rule 9 Nodal Officer. Evidence: A.

Rule 10 Special Officer / atrocity-prone areas

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records Additional District Magistrates of concerned districts as Special Officers and states that the Home Department identified atrocity-prone areas. Older official reports identify a State list of atrocity-prone areas and appointment of Additional District Magistrates as Rule 10 Special Officers. Current district-level list and current appointment orders require fresh verification. Evidence: A for the 2022 report; B for older State reports.

Special police stations / investigation

The historical Odisha State record stated there was no separate SC/ST police station and that existing police stations handled SC/ST cases. Current Odisha Police instead documents the HRPC as the specialised monitoring mechanism and district HRPC structures. A current statewide dedicated-special-police-station matrix was not established in this pass. Do not treat absence of a current matrix as proof that no special police station exists. Evidence: B, with currentness limitation.

The 2019 official State report stated atrocity cases were treated as special reported cases and investigation was entrusted to a Deputy Superintendent of Police or officer above that rank under District SP/Addl. SP supervision. Current post-2023 investigation arrangements require verification. Evidence: B.

2. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records:

  • State Level High Power V&MC under the Chief Minister. No State-level meeting was held during 2022.
  • District V&MCs under District Magistrates in all districts. 18 meetings were held in 30 districts during 2022.
  • Sub-Divisional V&MCs constituted in 30 districts. One meeting was held during 2022.

Older State reports record constitution of district committees in all 30 districts and instructions for quarterly meetings. Current meeting compliance after 2022 requires verification. Evidence: A.

3. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

Official Odisha reports establish a history of designated Special Courts and three Exclusive Special Courts in Cuttack, Balangir and Balasore. The 2019 State report records 105 designated courts and three Exclusive Special Courts, with 43 Special/Additional Special Public Prosecutors. A 2015-16 report records 92 designated District and Sessions/Addl. Sessions Judges and the same three Exclusive Special Courts. A 2024-25 State activity report confirms continuing implementation of the PoA Act but does not, in the extracted text, provide a current court-by-court matrix.

Current 2026 court designations, whether the three Exclusive Special Courts remain operational in the same form, and current SPP/ESPP appointments remain open verification items. Evidence: B for State reports, A for the State department activity report as to its stated implementation status.

4. Relief, rehabilitation and legal aid

The 2024-25 State activity report states that social rehabilitation and monetary relief to ST/SC victims of atrocity, legal aid to ST/SC litigants, and travelling/diet expenses for witnesses and victims are implemented under the relevant framework. The 2022 Section 21(4) report records that relief was provided within seven days to 0 persons and later than seven days to 2,288 persons. This is a reporting-period statistic, not a finding of non-compliance in 2026.

Older State reports describe a model contingency plan for immediate relief following an FIR and legal-aid arrangements. Current payment workflow, funding, district-wise implementation and 2026 compliance require verification. Evidence: A for 2022 and 2024-25 statements; B for older operational details.

5. Annual reporting and statistics

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) report records Odisha-specific institutional and implementation data. It reports 835 cases in which investigation and charge-sheet filing occurred within 60 days and 1,315 cases in which it occurred later than 60 days. It also reports 2,288 persons receiving relief later than seven days and none within seven days. These figures must be kept separate from current-year performance.

The same report records 773 cases of atrocities against members of STs registered under the PoA Act in conjunction with the IPC in Odisha in 2022. The Ministry's publication index confirms the official 2022 annual report was published on 17 September 2024. Evidence: A.

The Odisha Home Department's 2021-22 activity report states that State HRPC monitors SC/ST atrocity investigations and that 3,003 SC/ST cases were registered in 2021, of which 2,034 were disposed of and 969 remained under investigation. This is a broader SC/ST case count and must not be equated with PoA-only cases without category reconciliation. Evidence: A.

6. State-specific related legislation

Relevant Odisha legislation includes the Odisha Protection of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Interest in Trees) Act, 1981 and the Odisha Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Backward Classes (Regulation of Issuance and Verification of Caste Certificates) Act, 2011. These instruments should be considered in later duplication, evidence, status-certification and consequential-amendment analysis. No conclusion of conflict with the PoA Act is made in Phase 1. Evidence: A.

7. Currentness limitations

The strongest Odisha-specific implementation detail located in this pass is the Ministry's official Section 21(4) report for calendar year 2022, supplemented by Odisha Government annual/activity reports and current Odisha Police institutional material. Current 2026 appointment orders, court notifications, atrocity-prone-area notifications, current SPP/ESPP roster, current V&MC meeting compliance, current special-police-station matrix and current district relief workflow were not fully verified.

Search silence is not treated as evidence of absence.
