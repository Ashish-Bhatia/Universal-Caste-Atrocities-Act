Assam

Assam State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

Scope and source discipline

This is an existing-law inventory only. It does not assess necessity, superiority, desirability, constitutionality, or policy merits of any proposed replacement. Assam was researched independently and no implementation arrangement was inferred from Andhra Pradesh or Arunachal Pradesh.

Primary State sources were preferred. Central Government reports are used as official corroboration or historical implementation records. Third-party mirrors are identified where used and do not override a primary State record. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence. Currentness is expressly qualified where the latest located State-specific instrument is historical.

1. State legislation and Rules relevant to SC/ST atrocity implementation

The Government of Assam Social Justice & Empowerment Department's current Acts/Rules index lists the central SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, 1995, and the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955. The page was last updated 07-08-2026. The Department's current functions page states that it is responsible for implementation of the SC/ST PoA Act. These are State administrative publication/mandate records, not State amendments to the central Act.

No separate Assam statute specifically amending the central SC/ST PoA Act or Rules was located in the targeted State-source search. State executive and administrative implementation material therefore remains the principal State-specific layer identified.

The current Department/Citizen Charter also identifies the Assam SC/ST Reservation Act, 1978, as amended in 2012, as a State implementation responsibility. This is an employment/reservation interface rather than an atrocity offence instrument.

2. Protection Cell and special investigation machinery

Official Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment implementation reports record a State-level SC/ST Protection Cell in Assam functioning under the supervision of the Director General of Police. The 2017 PoA implementation report records the Cell as consisting of ADGP (CID), IGP (A), DIG (Range) and all Superintendents of Police. Earlier official reports similarly record State-level Cell operation under the DGP.

A current 2026 Assam Police website establishes the wider CCTNS and Assam Police Seva Setu digital police infrastructure, but the current public Police pages located do not identify a separately named SC/ST Protection Cell or current Cell staffing list. Accordingly, the historical Cell structure is verified, while current 2026 Cell composition/staffing remains UNVERIFIED.

3. Rule 9 nodal officer

The Government of Assam Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Castes Annual Administrative Report 2015-16 states that the Director of Welfare of Scheduled Castes was notified by the Government as the nodal officer for implementation of the PoA Act and PCR Act and was responsible for coordination and annual reporting.

Official Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment PoA implementation reports for 2010, 2013 and 2017 likewise identify the Director, Welfare of Scheduled Castes, Assam as the State Nodal Officer.

The current Assam Social Justice & Empowerment Department states that it is responsible for implementation of the PoA Act, but the current 2026 page does not itself name an individual Rule 9 office-holder. Current 2026 appointment/office-holder status is therefore QUALIFIED, with the Director, Welfare of Scheduled Castes as the latest directly identified State nodal office-holder in the located official record.

4. Rule 10 Special Officers and atrocity-prone areas

Official Ministry PoA reports record the Additional District Magistrate in each district as the Rule 10 Special Officer. The 2017 report records this designation and states that no area had been specified as atrocity-prone at that reporting point. The 2010 report similarly records Additional District Magistrates as Special Officers and no atrocity-prone area.

The 2017 report records District Magistrates as the chairpersons of District Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committees. The current 2026 State sources located do not establish a complete, current Rule 10 appointment list or current atrocity-prone-area notification.

Current 2026 Rule 10 status: HISTORICALLY VERIFIED, CURRENT LIST/NOTIFICATION UNVERIFIED.

Current 2026 atrocity-prone-area status: UNVERIFIED. Historical reports saying no area was specified are not treated as a current absence finding.

5. Special/Exclusive Special Courts and current judicial operation

Official Ministry records provide several historical State-specific court counts. The 2013 PoA implementation report records 18 Special Courts covering 22 of 27 districts. The 2017 PoA implementation report records 33 designated Special Courts functioning in Assam for PoA cases. A 2014 PIB release states that Assam had designated District Sessions Courts as Special Courts and was among the States with such courts.

A 2025 Ministry PCR report records 33 designated Special Courts functioning in Assam for PCR trials. A 2024 official Assam district-court monthly statement from Kamrup (Metro), hosted on an official court-government document server, includes a dedicated row for SC/ST PoA Act cases, demonstrating that the category remains part of current judicial reporting, although the extracted row contains no numerical figures in the located statement.

No current 2026 Assam notification establishing or listing the complete State-wide Special/Exclusive Special Court matrix was located in the targeted public State judicial search. Current judicial operation is therefore PARTIALLY CORROBORATED but the State-wide 2026 court/designation census remains UNVERIFIED.

No current Assam Exclusive Special Court list was established from a primary State notification in the targeted search. Historical sources located for Assam refer to designated Special Courts rather than establishing a current Exclusive Special Court network.

6. Special Public Prosecutor and Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor appointments

Official Ministry implementation material records that Special Public Prosecutors had been appointed in Assam to conduct PoA cases. The 2013 official report states that Special Public Prosecutors were appointed for PoA trials. A 2016/2017 official PCR implementation compilation records Special Public Prosecutors associated with Assam's Special Courts.

The current Assam Judicial Department notification archive contains current Special Public Prosecutor appointment material for other special statutes and a general appointment-notification system, but the targeted search did not locate a current 2026 Assam-specific appointment order naming Special Public Prosecutors or Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors for PoA courts.

Current 2026 PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment status: UNVERIFIED.

7. State, District and Sub-divisional Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

A 21-03-2018 Assam State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee meeting record, hosted by CVMC as a third-party mirror of the underlying State document, records the State Level V&MC meeting at the Chief Minister's Secretariat. The minutes state that 13 remaining districts were to constitute District Level V&MCs immediately, that District V&MC meetings should be held quarterly and minutes sent to the State Nodal Officer and nodal department, and that the District Magistrate could declare atrocity-prone areas. The minutes also assign action on monthly/quarterly/annual case reporting and victim compensation proposals.

The 2017 official Ministry PoA report records a State Level V&MC chaired by the Chief Minister and District V&MCs in 20 districts. A later official Ministry report records District V&MCs in 21 districts and a State Level V&MC chaired by the Chief Minister. These are historical official implementation records and show evolution in district coverage.

Current 2026 State-level committee constitution and meeting record was not located in the targeted State-source search. A 2026 Cachar District Commissioner public communication, retrieved through a third-party social-media page, reports a District Level V&MC meeting on 27-07-2026 concerning the PoA Act. This is retained only as a secondary current lead and does not replace primary minutes/order retrieval.

Current 2026 State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC status: PARTIALLY INDICATED, NOT FROZEN. No reliable current State-wide SDVMC matrix was located.

8. Relief and rehabilitation orders and payment mechanisms

The Government of Assam budget documents establish a State-funded Prevention of Atrocities Act scheme for relief and rehabilitation. The 2022-23 outcome budget identifies a Prevention of Atrocities Act scheme with a budget estimate of Rs.30 lakh, targeting five victims and describing the outcome as relief and rehabilitation of SC/ST victims/dependants of atrocities. The 2023-24 gender budget identifies a State-share Prevention of Atrocities Act programme under the Directorate of Welfare of Scheduled Caste, with the stated objective of providing relief and rehabilitation to SC/ST victims/dependants of atrocities, including women in sexual-harassment cases. The 2025-26 revenue estimates include Head 5939/106, “Strengthening of Machinery for Enforcement of Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1995 & Prevention of Atrocities Act 1989”, with a Budget Estimate of Rs.65.80 lakh in the located schedule.

The current State SC welfare scheme page describes prevention of atrocities as a centrally sponsored scheme aimed at awareness and monitoring of atrocities. It does not publish a current State-specific relief-rate schedule or victim-payment workflow.

The 21-03-2018 State V&MC minutes direct Finance/WPT&BC/nodal authorities to provide budget for witness travelling/maintenance, economic and social rehabilitation, legal aid and victim compensation. They also require District Magistrates/ADMs to assess victim-compensation needs from police case data and submit proposals to the nodal officer.

Current 2026 payment mechanism, rate schedule, treasury/DBT workflow and case-level payment records: UNVERIFIED. The existence of State budget heads establishes a funded administrative mechanism, but does not by itself establish current payment performance or a specific digital transfer workflow.

9. PoA-specific SOPs, circulars, manuals and digital workflows

The 21-03-2018 State V&MC minutes constitute an operational coordination record. They direct monthly, quarterly and annual case reporting from police, committee review, victim-compensation assessment, budget provision, police threat assessment/protection, awareness training, and constitution of Special Courts in new districts.

Assam Police currently operates CCTNS and Assam Police Seva Setu. The current Police complaint service states that online complaints generate an acknowledgement/reference number and alert the concerned police station. The Police website also states that an e-Book compiling advisories on human trafficking, crimes against women, children, senior citizens and SC/STs was uploaded into CCTNS application software 4.0 for district police use. This is the strongest current digital-workflow evidence located that expressly includes SC/ST material.

The current e-FIR service is limited to crime against women, POCSO and vehicle theft. It should not be treated as a dedicated PoA e-FIR channel. The current general e-Complaint/CCTNS service is broader and is not PoA-specific.

No consolidated Assam PoA-specific SOP/manual or dedicated PoA case-tracking portal was located in the targeted search. Status: SPECIFIC OPERATIONAL/DIGITAL ELEMENTS VERIFIED, CONSOLIDATED POA-SPECIFIC SOP NOT LOCATED.

10. State Gazette material

The Directorate of Printing & Stationery states that it publishes the Assam Gazette weekly and Extraordinary Gazettes, and maintains Gazette publication material. The targeted search did not locate a current Assam Gazette notification specifically establishing the PoA Protection Cell, Rule 9 nodal officer, Rule 10 Special Officers, or a current State-wide Special/Exclusive Special Court matrix.

The 21-03-2018 V&MC minutes are an underlying State administrative record but are not treated as a Gazette notification. Historical implementation reports identify executive designations and court arrangements but do not substitute for primary Gazette retrieval where the Act requires Gazette notification.

Gazette status: PRIMARY REPOSITORY VERIFIED; POA-SPECIFIC CURRENT GAZETTE INSTRUMENTS NOT YET RETRIEVED.

11. State-specific PCR and related atrocity-interface legislation

The current Assam Social Justice & Empowerment Acts/Rules index lists the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955. The current Department mandate includes implementation of the PCR Act and PoA Act. The current official PCR text hosted by Assam reproduces the State's central-law publication and includes section 15A measures concerning legal aid, prosecution supervision, special courts and committees.

Official Ministry PCR implementation reports record Assam's State Level V&MC, State SC/ST Protection Cell, Special Courts and awareness/legal-aid arrangements. A 2025 Ministry PCR report records 33 designated Special Courts functioning in Assam for PCR cases. This is current-ish official corroboration but not a 2026 State Gazette census.

No separate Assam PCR statute materially altering the central PCR Act was located in the targeted search.

12. Land, bonded labour, manual scavenging, local government and public-access interfaces

Land: The Assam Land and Revenue Regulation, 1886, as amended, is published by the State Legislative Department and other State repositories. Chapter X regulates protected tribal belts and blocks. The State Directorate of Land Records and Surveys states that Chapter X was added in 1947 and that protected belts/blocks are administered under the Regulation and related rules. The official Land Revenue handbook reproduces Government instructions dated 30-12-1982 requiring prevention of illegal transfer/alienation and restoration of alienated land in tribal belts and blocks. These provisions are relevant to land dispossession, alienation and protected tribal land issues that may intersect with atrocity allegations.

Local government: The Assam Panchayat Act, 1994 and Rules 2008 are published by the State Legislative Department and Panchayat & Rural Development Department. The P&RD Acts page records a 2025 Assam Panchayat (Amendment) Act and other recent amendments/rules. This establishes a current local-government interface; no PoA-specific panchayat offence/procedure was established in the targeted inventory.

Bonded labour: The Assam Labour Commissionerate's official labour-law page lists the central Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976 among labour laws administered in Assam. The current Assam labour rules page does not list a separate Assam bonded-labour Rule in the located index. No State-specific bonded-labour instrument creating a PoA-specific interface was located.

Manual scavenging: The current Assam Social Justice & Empowerment Acts/Rules index lists the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013. State sanitation guidance also expressly applies the 2013 Act's definition/prohibition to rural sanitation programmes. No separate Assam manual-scavenging statute or PoA-specific implementing rule was located in the targeted search.

Public access/untouchability: PCR Act remains the central public-access/untouchability interface. Current Assam State publication of the PCR Act is verified. No separate Assam untouchability statute was located.

14. Inventory status

Assam is sufficiently inventoried for progression, with qualifications.

Verified or strongly corroborated architecture includes:

  • State SC/ST Protection Cell historically functioning under the DGP, with CID/range/SP participation recorded in official reports.
  • Director, Welfare of Scheduled Castes historically identified as Rule 9 Nodal Officer.
  • Additional District Magistrates historically designated as Rule 10 Special Officers.
  • Historical reports state no atrocity-prone area had been specified at the relevant reporting periods, but current status is not established.
  • Special Courts historically operated at 18 courts/22 districts in 2013 and 33 designated courts in the 2017 report.
  • Special Public Prosecutors historically appointed.
  • State V&MC chaired by the Chief Minister and District V&MC structures historically established, with 2018 State minutes documenting quarterly review and reporting directions.
  • State budgetary provision for relief/rehabilitation and machinery strengthening is evidenced through multiple Assam Finance documents.
  • Assam Police's current CCTNS environment contains an SC/ST advisory compendium for district police and a general online complaint workflow.
  • Current State departments expressly retain responsibility for PoA and PCR implementation.
  • Assam's land-protection, panchayat, bonded-labour and manual-scavenging administrative interfaces are identifiable from current State sources.

The inventory does not establish a complete 2026 census of nodal officer, Protection Cell staffing, Rule 10 appointments, atrocity-prone areas, Special/Exclusive Special Courts, PoA-specific prosecutors, V&MC constitution/meetings, or victim-level payment workflows.
