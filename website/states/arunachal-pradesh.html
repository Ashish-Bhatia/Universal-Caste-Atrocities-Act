Arunachal Pradesh

Arunachal Pradesh State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

Scope and source discipline

This is an existing-law inventory only. It does not assess necessity, superiority, desirability, constitutionality, or policy merits of any proposed replacement. The principal State-specific record located is the Government of Arunachal Pradesh Social Justice & Empowerment and Tribal Affairs Department Annual Report material for calendar year 2023, transmitted in May 2024, with Police Headquarters material dated 05-04-2024. The located copy is hosted by CVMC, a third-party mirror, but the underlying document identifies itself as a Government of Arunachal Pradesh record and carries State file numbers/signatures. It is therefore treated as an A-grade underlying State record, with hosting provenance separately noted.

Search silence is not treated as proof of absence. Currentness is qualified where the latest State-specific record is historical or where only secondary/central evidence was located.

1. State legislation and Rules

No separate Arunachal Pradesh statute or Rule specifically amending the central SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act or Rules was located in the targeted official-source search. The State implementation record instead operates through the central Act/Rules and State executive/police instruments.

The 2023 State Annual Report identifies SC & ST Cell order No. HMB(B) 25-2002 dated 30-09-2015. The primary copy was not located.

Relevant State interfaces identified:

  • Arunachal Pradesh (Land Settlement and Records) Act, 2000, as amended.
  • Arunachal Pradesh Panchayati Raj Act, 1997 and related Rules/Manual.

2. Protection Cell and special investigation machinery

The 2023 State Annual Report records that an SC & ST Cell exists at Police Headquarters, Itanagar. It states that IGP (Crime), SIT, PHQ, Itanagar is designated as the SC & ST Cell under order No. HMB(B) 25-2002 dated 30-09-2015. The report records nil activity for the listed supervision functions in the reporting period.

The current Arunachal Pradesh Police contact directory lists IGP (Crime/Sec.) and SIT/Crime functions, but does not identify a current SC/ST Cell office-holder. Current Cell staffing therefore remains unverified.

3. Rule 9 nodal officer

The 2023 State Annual Report expressly states that there was no State Nodal Officer "till date". It records monthly SC & ST Cell reports being sent to the Commissioner (Home), and states that review was not undertaken.

A 2024 secondary handbook lists the Department of Social Justice and Empowerment and Tribal Affairs as the Arunachal Pradesh State nodal contact. This conflicts with the 2023 State submission and is retained only as a lead because no primary appointment order was located.

Current 2026 Rule 9 appointment status: UNVERIFIED.

4. Rule 10 special officers and atrocity-prone areas

The 2023 State Annual Report records no Special Officers appointed for identified areas under Rule 10. Its Annexure XI records no identified atrocity-prone district/specific area for the reporting period. No later primary State notification designating Rule 10 Special Officers or atrocity-prone areas was located.

5. Special/Exclusive Special Courts and judicial operation

The 2023 State Annual Report records nil Special Courts and nil Exclusive Special Courts. Older Government of India reports likewise state that Arunachal Pradesh had not specified a Special Court. Those records are historical context only.

A targeted search of current publicly indexed judicial material did not locate an Arunachal Pradesh-specific Gazette/court notification establishing a PoA Special Court or Exclusive Special Court, nor a current official Arunachal judicial directory entry establishing one. This does not establish absence. Current judicial designation and operation remain OPEN for final Phase 1 closure.

6. Special Public Prosecutors

The 2023 State Annual Report records nil Special Public Prosecutors and nil Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors. No primary State appointment notification was located. Current appointment status remains UNVERIFIED and no absence conclusion is drawn.

7. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2023 State Annual Report records that the State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee was not constituted and held no meetings during 2023.

It separately records that District Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committees had been formed. Several extracted entries show last constitution in January 2022 and nil meetings during 2023. OCR extraction of the district table is incomplete, so the full district-by-district matrix is not frozen from this copy.

The report records no Sub-Divisional Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee meetings and provides no reliable current constitution matrix.

Current 2026 State, District and Sub-Divisional committee status remains UNVERIFIED.

8. Relief, rehabilitation and payment mechanisms

For 2023 the State Annual Report records nil legal-aid/other-facility recipients, nil travelling/maintenance payments, nil relief/rehabilitation cases and nil section 3 registrations. Under Rule 12(4)/(4A), it records nil cases paid within seven days and nil paid later than seven days. It also records that authorization for District Magistrates to make immediate treasury withdrawals had "Not done".

These entries establish the 2023 reporting position, not absence of a current relief mechanism. No later Arunachal-specific relief-rate/payment order was located. A 2024 NPCI DBT list contains a Tawang entry for the centrally sponsored PCR/PoA implementation scheme. This is treated as payment/infrastructure evidence only, not proof of a State-specific relief workflow or rate.

9. PoA-specific SOPs, circulars, manuals and digital workflows

The 2023 State Annual Report establishes a monthly reporting workflow from the SC & ST Cell to the Commissioner (Home). It does not identify a consolidated PoA-specific SOP, investigation manual, victim-relief digital workflow or public case-tracking platform.

No primary State PoA-specific SOP/manual/circular/digital workflow was located. Status: NO SPECIFIC PRIMARY INSTRUMENT LOCATED, NOT AN ABSENCE CONCLUSION.

10. State Gazette material

The 2023 State Annual Report identifies SC & ST Cell order No. HMB(B) 25-2002 dated 30-09-2015. The primary order/Gazette copy was not located. The State Portal confirms that it maintains Government orders, notifications, circulars, Acts and Rules, but the targeted search did not locate the specific instrument or a later PoA-specific Gazette notification.

Primary retrieval remains a closure item.

11. PCR and related civil-rights interface

The Government of Arunachal Pradesh 2022 PCR Annual Report states there was no reported instance of untouchability during calendar year 2022 and therefore section 15A of the PCR Act was not applied. It submitted a NIL report. This is a State-reported 2022 position and does not establish absence in later periods.

No separate Arunachal Pradesh PCR implementing statute or Rule was located in the targeted search.

12. Land, bonded labour, manual scavenging, local government and public access

Land: The Arunachal Pradesh (Land Settlement and Records) Act, 2000, as amended, is the principal State land-administration interface identified. It incorporates customary land rights and governs land revenue/records. It is relevant to allegations involving possession, dispossession, land records, allotment and related administration. A State eODB copy identifies publication in Arunachal Pradesh Gazette No. 80, Vol. X dated 17-11-2000. India Code separately lists the amended Act.

Local government: The Arunachal Pradesh Panchayati Raj Act, 1997 and related Rules/Manual are identified by the official State Election Commission and Ministry of Panchayati Raj. No PoA-specific provision was established in this inventory.

Bonded labour: No Arunachal-specific bonded-labour statute, Rule or PoA-interface notification was located in the targeted search. The central Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act/Rules remain the separate Phase 1 baseline.

Manual scavenging: No Arunachal-specific enactment, Rule or PoA-interface notification under the 2013 Manual Scavengers Act was located in the targeted search. This is not an absence conclusion.

Public access/untouchability: PCR Act remains the central public-access/untouchability interface; the 2022 State PCR report is NIL.

13. Currentness and evidence assessment

A-grade underlying State record: Government of Arunachal Pradesh Social Justice & Empowerment and Tribal Affairs Department Annual Report for 2023, transmitted May 2024, with Police Headquarters material dated 05-04-2024. Retrieved from third-party mirror; underlying document is a State record.

A-grade current State portal evidence: Arunachal Pradesh State Portal and Police contact directory, used only for current portal/organizational information, not to infer PoA-specific arrangements absent a specific instrument.

Official central corroboration: Parliamentary/Ministry records provide historical confirmation of State/District vigilance machinery and historical non-designation of Special Courts. They do not establish 2026 status.

14. Inventory status

Arunachal Pradesh is sufficiently inventoried for progression, with qualifications. The record establishes a State SC/ST Protection Cell and cited 2015 designation instrument; no State Nodal Officer in the 2023 State report; no Rule 10 Special Officers; no identified atrocity-prone areas; nil Special/Exclusive Special Courts and prosecutors in the 2023 report; District-level committee formation with nil meetings; no State-level committee in 2023; nil relief/payment cases; and a 2022 NIL PCR report.

The inventory does not establish a complete 2026 implementation census.

15. Closure items

  • Retrieve primary copy of HMB(B) 25-2002 dated 30-09-2015.
  • Verify current 2026 Rule 9 State Nodal Officer appointment and resolve the conflict with the later secondary handbook listing.
  • Verify current SC/ST Protection Cell structure and staffing.
  • Search for later Special Court/Exclusive Special Court notifications and confirm current judicial operation.
  • Search for Special Public Prosecutor/Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor appointments.
  • Verify current State, District and Sub-Divisional Vigilance and Monitoring Committee constitution and meetings.
  • Search for State-specific relief/payment orders and later contingency plans.
  • Search for PoA-specific SOPs, circulars, manuals and digital workflows.
  • Retrieve later State Gazette instruments affecting PoA implementation.
  • Complete targeted State-specific bonded-labour/manual-scavenging interface verification if new instruments emerge.

These are Phase 1 closure items. They do not block progression to the next jurisdiction unless new evidence materially changes the recorded position.
