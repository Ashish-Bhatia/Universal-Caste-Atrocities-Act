Phase 1 State-by-State Official-Source Inventory First pass: 2026-09-05 Targeted verification: 2026-09-05

Scope

This record inventories Andhra Pradesh legislation, Rules, notifications/orders, schemes, SOPs, Special Courts, nodal/protection officers, prosecutors, monitoring committees and implementation machinery relevant to the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989, the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 and closely connected atrocity-interface laws.

This is a source inventory only. It does not assess necessity, superiority, policy desirability or constitutional validity.

2. Notifications and Government Orders

Relief and rehabilitation

Government of Andhra Pradesh, Social Welfare Department, G.O.Ms.No.95, dated 29-08-2016, revises compensation/relief for SC/ST atrocity victims and dependents and states that the State adopted the 2016 Central PoA Rules relief changes. The order traces earlier State orders including G.O.Ms.No.3 dated 16-01-1996, G.O.Ms.No.22 dated 16-06-2012 and G.O.Ms.No.76 dated 28-11-2014. [A] citeturn8search22

The 2023 State Annual Report states that relief sanction proceedings are sent to victims/dependents and the amount is directly credited to their accounts. It records use of the SNA account/payment route, with BRO/ASO orders followed by release and withdrawal by the Collector and District Magistrate for payment. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn12view0

A February 2026 Andhra Pradesh High Court judgment records a State order dated 23-10-2025 cancelling a monthly compensation arrangement in an individual case and confirms continuing litigation concerning G.O.Ms.No.76. This is evidence of later case-specific implementation activity, not a statewide replacement of G.O.Ms.No.95. [B, judicial source] citeturn16search0

State implementation plan / Gazette

The 2023 State Annual Report states that a Model Contingency Plan was prepared and notified through A.P. Gazette Notification No.731, Part-I Extraordinary, dated 22-12-2021, under G.O.Ms.No.41, SW(CV.POA), dated 21-12-2021. The underlying Gazette issue should remain a primary-source retrieval target, but the State's own 2024 submission establishes the instrument identity. [A for underlying State report] citeturn13search0

3. Protection Cell / Special Investigation machinery

The 2023 State Annual Report, prepared by the Social Welfare Department from reports of the ADGP, PCR Cell, Director of Prosecution and all District Collectors, records a State Headquarters PCR Cell under the control of the Additional Director General of Police, CID. It gives sanctioned strength as one DIG, seven DSPs, nine Inspectors, thirteen Sub-Inspectors, thirteen Head Constables and twenty-three Police Constables. One DSP is assigned to each of seven regions. It records seven Regional Crime Investigation offices at Nellore, Kurnool, Tirupati, Vijayawada, Guntur, Rajahmundry and Visakhapatnam. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn6view1turn12view0

The same report states that regional officers investigate important atrocity cases and conduct enquiries into complaints of perfunctory investigation referred by the DGP CID, Social Welfare Department, State SC/ST Commission and NCSC. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn6view1

Current district official pages independently evidence district SC/ST Cells, including a DSP SC ST Cell in Prakasam and an SC ST Cell in YSR Kadapa. [A] Existing first-pass sources remain relevant.

4. Nodal / Protection Officers

The 2023 State Annual Report states that the Commissioner of Social Welfare / Director of Social Welfare is appointed as the Nodal Officer under Rule 9. It also states that the Nodal Officer reviews reports from the SC/ST Protection Cell, district committees, Special Public Prosecutors and Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn6view1

The 2022 State Annual Report similarly states that the Commissioner of Social Welfare / Director of Social Welfare is the nominated Nodal Officer and describes periodic review of monthly case positions, quarterly district committee reports, half-yearly prosecutor performance and law-and-order conditions. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn5search0

No separate 2025/2026 appointment order was located. The designation is therefore verified as the latest located State-reported position, but current office-holder identity and a later appointment instrument remain open.

5. Special Courts

The 2023 State Annual Report records 14 Special Courts and one Exclusive Special Court at Lakshmipeta, Srikakulam. It identifies the relevant courts by district and states that Special Public Prosecutors were appointed for the Special Courts and an Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor for the Exclusive Special Court. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn12view0

The current Andhra Pradesh High Court judicial directory independently lists Special Judges for SC/ST PoA matters in multiple districts, including Guntur, Kurnool, East Godavari, Visakhapatnam, Srikakulam, Nellore, Ananthapur and YSR Cuddapah. [A] citeturn11search1 The High Court's 2025 telephone directory also lists SC/ST Special Judge posts in Srikakulam, Chittoor and Kurnool. [A] citeturn11search2turn11search3turn11search13 A January 2026 High Court proceeding records a Special Judge for SC/ST PoA cases-cum-X Additional District and Sessions Judge at Machilipatnam. [A] citeturn11search9

The current judiciary evidence establishes ongoing Special Court functionality but does not replace the need to retrieve every underlying State/Gazette court-specification notification. The 2023 State report's 14 + 1 count must not be treated as the 2026 statewide count.

6. Special Public Prosecutors / prosecution machinery

The 2023 State Annual Report records 14 Special Courts and one Exclusive Special Court, with 14 Special Public Prosecutors and one Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor specified. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn12view0

A 2025 Andhra Pradesh High Court judgment confirms the operation of an individual appointment instrument, G.O.Rt.No.1214 Home (Courts.A) Department dated 27-10-2023, appointing a Public Prosecutor as Special Public Prosecutor for specified Srikakulam Sessions Cases. The Court set aside that order on 19-08-2025 and directed a fresh panel/appointment process. This demonstrates that appointment instruments are case/district specific and subject to judicial review. [B, judicial source] citeturn2search0

The 2023 State report also states that prosecutor performance is reviewed periodically by District Collectors, the Director of Prosecution and the Nodal Officer, with half-yearly reports. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn6view1

7. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2023 State Annual Report states that the State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee was constituted. It identifies G.O.Ms.No.2, Social Welfare (POA) Department, dated 01-02-2021, as the reconstitution instrument. It states that the High Power Committee meets once every six months under the Chief Minister, with four Ministers, elected and sitting SC/ST Members of Parliament and State Legislature, and seven nominated official members. The report records nil State-level meetings during calendar year 2023. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn12view0

The same report records District Level Committees as constituted under G.O.Ms.No.232 SW(H) Department dated 26-01-1991 and provides district and sub-divisional meeting data for 2023. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn12view0

Current district Social Welfare material continues to state that District and Sub-divisional Vigilance & Monitoring Committees are constituted and periodically review implementation. [A] citeturn0search0

8. Relief, rehabilitation and scheme machinery

G.O.Ms.No.95 remains the principal State relief enhancement order located in the official AP source set. The State's 2023 report confirms an operational payment workflow through SNA-based processing and direct beneficiary credit. [A] citeturn8search22turn12view0

The current AP Social Welfare Department scheme page continues to describe monetary relief/legal aid under the PoA framework and cites G.O.Ms.No.95 as the State enhancement order. It also records the PCR Cell and Special Sessions Court machinery. [A] citeturn10search0

No later statewide replacement/consolidation order governing PoA relief rates was located in the targeted search. Individual later orders and litigation exist, including the 2025 order recorded by the High Court in 2026. Those are not treated as a statewide rate replacement. [B] citeturn16search0

9. SOPs, digital workflows and implementation records

The 2023 State Annual Report records timely circulars with guidelines sent to SDPOs, a handbook of Government Orders, memoranda and circular instructions on PCR and PoA Acts supplied to Commissioners/Superintendents of Police for investigating-officer guidance, and State-level training/workshop activity. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn6view1

The same report records that FIR copies are available through the AP Police citizen portal and AP Police Seva mobile application for registered users; victims receive FIR copies from the concerned police officer. It also states that relief sanction proceedings are sent to victims/dependents, amounts are directly credited, investigation/charge-sheet status is communicated, trial/investigation dates are to be provided, and Special Public Prosecutors receive case briefings. [A for underlying Government report] citeturn12view0

A separate statewide PoA-specific SOP instrument was not located. The evidence therefore establishes circular/manual/digital workflow elements, not a single consolidated PoA SOP.

11. State-specific relevant interfaces

The Andhra Pradesh Social Welfare Department's current scheme page identifies PCR and PoA implementation machinery together. [A] citeturn10search0

The Andhra Pradesh State Commission for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Act, 2003 is a directly relevant institutional interface. Its legislative history states that it was enacted to safeguard SC/ST interests and address untouchability and atrocities. [A for underlying statutory source where available; India Kanoon reproduction retained only as source lead] citeturn14search7

The Andhra Pradesh State Commission for Scheduled Tribes Act, 2020 is an additional State institutional interface. India Code identifies it as Act 30 of 2020 under the Social Welfare Department. [A] citeturn14search1

The Andhra Pradesh community certificate framework also directs the Protection of Civil Rights/Vigilance Cell to conduct social-status enquiries, making it relevant to SC/ST identity verification and atrocity-interface administration. [B/C, statutory reproduction with source-verification limitation] citeturn10search2

No separate State criminal enactment equivalent to the central SC/ST PoA Act was established. The State-specific interface is principally institutional, procedural, administrative and welfare-related.

14. Verification conclusion

Andhra Pradesh is sufficiently verified for progression in the State/UT inventory, but not frozen as a complete 2026 implementation census. The targeted verification established strong State-level evidence for the Rule 9 nodal designation, State Protection Cell structure, State-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee constitution, Special Court operation, prosecutor architecture, relief/payment workflow, implementation circulars and digital workflows. It also identified the 2021 Model Contingency Plan Gazette instrument and relevant State institutional legislation.

The following remain explicit qualified/open items and must be carried into Phase 1 closure rather than blocking progression: independent 2026 staffing confirmation for the nodal/Protection Cell structure; consolidation of every current Special/Exclusive Special Court notification; a complete current Special Public Prosecutor appointment register; 2026 State-level committee composition/meeting confirmation; retrieval of the primary 2021 Gazette issue; a later statewide relief consolidation if one exists; a consolidated PoA-specific SOP if one exists; and primary verification of the 2025 amendment to the State Commission for Scheduled Tribes legislation.

No absence claim is made for any unlocated instrument. The 2023 State submission is treated as a high-value official State record, but its calendar-year scope is preserved. Current 2026 High Court and State-government material is used where available to establish continuing operation, without back-projecting current evidence into earlier periods.

Next jurisdiction: Arunachal Pradesh, processed independently under the State/UT inventory methodology.
