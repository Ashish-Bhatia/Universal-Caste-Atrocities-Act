Ladakh

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map Currentness reference date: 2026-09-06

1. Territorial and legal transition

The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 created the Union territory of Ladakh without a legislature, with the appointed day of 31 October 2019. Sections 65 and 66 applied the Constitution (Jammu and Kashmir) Scheduled Castes Order, 1956 and the Constitution (Jammu and Kashmir) Scheduled Tribes Order, 1989 to both successor Union territories from the appointed day. Sections 95-98 provide for territorial application, adaptation, construction and designation of authorities. [A: LAD-001, LAD-017]

The central PoA Act is an Act of Parliament with nationwide extent. Its current India Code text provides the statutory framework for Special Courts/Exclusive Special Courts, Special Public Prosecutors/Exclusive Public Prosecutors, victim and witness rights, relief and implementation duties. [A: LAD-002]

The Ladakh-specific 2020 adaptation-order catalogue confirms separate Ladakh adaptation instruments for Central and State laws. The exact interaction of every later criminal-law transition instrument remains part of the national BNS/BNSS/BSA workstream and is not frozen here. [A: LAD-017]

2. Beneficiary scope

The current Constitution (Jammu and Kashmir) Scheduled Castes Order applies to Ladakh through section 65 of the Reorganisation Act. The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment's current SC-list page, updated 31 August 2026, maintains a Ladakh-specific list link. The underlying linked PDF was not independently retrieved because the connector timed out. [A: LAD-006, LAD-016]

The 2024 Constitution (Jammu and Kashmir) Scheduled Tribes Order (Amendment) Act creates a separate Ladakh ST list containing Bakarwal, Balti, Beda, Bot/Boto, Brokpa/Drokpa/Dard/Shin, Changpa, Gaddi, Garra, Gujjar, Mon, Purigpa and Sippi. [A: LAD-007]

Ladakh's 2025 reservation framework provides 1% reservation for Scheduled Castes and 80% for Scheduled Tribes in direct recruitment. This is a current reservation measure, not evidence about PoA case incidence or implementation performance. [A: LAD-008]

Historical Census 2011 tables record SC population in the former J&K districts of Leh and Kargil and caste-specific data. These are retained as historical demographic evidence only. [A: LAD-015]

3. Rule 8 Protection Cell

UT Ladakh Order No. 228-LA(GAD) of 2021 dated 11 December 2021 constituted the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Protection Cell under the charge of the Head of Police, Ladakh, pursuant to Rule 8. The order assigns functions including survey of identified areas, public order and tranquillity, recommendations for special police force/posts, investigation into probable causes, security, reporting to the nodal and special officers, enquiry into investigation and police-station refusal/negligence matters, review of registered cases, and monthly reporting to the nodal officer. [A: LAD-003]

The 2021 order is the located primary instrument. A later 2022-2026 replacement, reconstitution or current staffing order was not located in the targeted official-source search. Search non-location is not treated as proof that the 2021 arrangement remains unchanged in every operational detail. [A: LAD-003, LAD-019, LAD-020]

4. Rule 9 Nodal Officer

UT Ladakh Order No. 230-LA(GAD) of 2021 dated 11 December 2021 designated the Secretary, Social & Tribal Welfare Department as Nodal Officer under Rule 9. The order assigns coordination of District Magistrates, Superintendents of Police, investigating officers and other implementing officers. It requires quarterly review of reports under Rules 4, 6 and 8, case status, law-and-order conditions in identified areas, relief measures, immediate facilities including rationing, clothing, shelter, legal aid, travel/daily allowance and transport, and performance of NGOs, the Protection Cell, committees and public servants. The order also directs the District Magistrates/Deputy Commissioners/CEOs of LAHDCs Leh and Kargil to constitute district V&MCs under Rule 17. [A: LAD-005]

No current 2026 named incumbent or replacement instrument was located. The current Social & Tribal Welfare Department continues to publish active departmental orders and staffing material, but those publications do not themselves establish continuation of the specific Rule 9 designation. [A: LAD-005, LAD-019]

5. Rule 10 and identified areas

The Rule 9 order expressly directs the District Magistrates/Deputy Commissioners/CEOs of LAHDCs Leh and Kargil to appoint Special Officers in identified areas under Rule 10. [A: LAD-005]

A current 2026 primary Rule 3 identified-area notification and corresponding current Rule 10 Special Officer appointment matrix were not located in the targeted official-source search. This remains OPEN. Search non-location is not treated as proof that no identified area or Special Officer exists. [A: LAD-005, LAD-020]

6. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

Order No. 229-LA(GAD) of 2021 dated 11 December 2021 constituted a UT-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee under Rule 16 and the 2016 amendment Rules. It is chaired by the Lieutenant Governor and includes the Chairpersons/CECs of LAHDC Kargil and Leh, the Member of Parliament, Advisor to the Lieutenant Governor, Head of Police, Administrative Secretary Social & Tribal Welfare as Convener, representatives of the NCSC and NCST, and Additional Secretaries for Home and Finance. The order requires meetings at least twice a calendar year, in January and July, and review of implementation, victim/witness rights and access to justice, relief/rehabilitation, prosecution and reports from implementing officers. [A: LAD-004]

The same order directs district authorities to constitute district-level V&MCs under Rule 17. [A: LAD-004]

A current 18 March 2026 Kargil district V&MC meeting is direct evidence that the district committee was functioning in Kargil in 2026. The meeting involved the Deputy Commissioner, Additional SSP, DSWO and other officials, reviewed the PoA Act and directed awareness and identification of cases requiring relief and rehabilitation. [A: LAD-009]

A complete current 2026 UT-level and district/sub-divisional committee membership, reconstitution and meeting matrix was not located. The current local evidence is therefore sufficient for progression but not for an unqualified 2026 committee census. [A: LAD-004, LAD-009]

7. Investigation

The 2021 Protection Cell order places PoA-related investigation review and enquiries within the Protection Cell's functions. The Rule 9 order establishes coordination across District Magistrates, Superintendents of Police, investigating officers and other implementing officers. [A: LAD-003, LAD-005]

No current 2026 district-wise investigating-officer roster, Rule 7 compliance audit or complete investigation-performance dataset was located. BNS/BNSS transition verification remains separate. This is OPEN.

8. Special Courts and Exclusive Special Courts

The PoA Act's current statutory framework provides for Special Courts and Exclusive Special Courts. [A: LAD-002]

No current Ladakh-specific 2026 notification designating a PoA Special Court or Exclusive Special Court was located in the targeted official UT/Government and judicial-source search. The absence of a located notification is recorded as a controlled negative result, not as proof that no court is functioning under the Act. The complete current court designation/jurisdiction matrix remains OPEN.

9. Special Public Prosecutor / Exclusive Public Prosecutor

The current PoA Act provides for Special Public Prosecutors and Exclusive Public Prosecutors. [A: LAD-002]

The UT Home Department confirms that Prosecution is under the Home Department and current Ladakh Gazette material recognises the Director of Prosecution's authority over Public Prosecutors and Special Public Prosecutors. These are general prosecution-architecture sources, not proof of a current PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment. [A: LAD-018]

No current Ladakh-specific PoA SPP/ESPP appointment order or roster was located in the targeted search. This remains OPEN.

10. Relief and payment

The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment's current Centrally Sponsored Scheme data record zero persons provided relief under the Scheme in Ladakh in each of 2020-21, 2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24 and 2024-25. This is evidence about beneficiaries recorded under the Central scheme and is not proof that no relief was provided from UT resources. [A: LAD-012]

The same current Central scheme records Central Assistance to Ladakh of 0.00 crore in 2020-21 through 2023-24 and 0.08 crore in 2024-25. Receipt/release of Central Assistance does not establish expenditure, case-level payment or the source of individual victim relief. [A: LAD-013]

The 18 March 2026 Kargil V&MC meeting specifically directed Police and Social Welfare officials to identify cases requiring relief and rehabilitation outside the state, providing current evidence of an active relief/rehabilitation review function. [A: LAD-009]

A current case-level relief register, payment workflow, rate schedule, expenditure reconciliation and rehabilitation outcome audit was not located. These remain OPEN.

11. Victim/witness rights and legal aid

The current PoA Act provides statutory victim and witness rights under section 15A. [A: LAD-002]

The 2021 UT-level V&MC order expressly requires review of victim and witness rights and access to justice. The 2026 Kargil V&MC meeting demonstrates continuing local review and awareness activity. [A: LAD-004, LAD-009]

The August 2026 Kargil awareness programme was organised by Social Welfare with the District Legal Services Authority and included an Assistant Legal Aid Defence Counsel. It is evidence of current awareness/legal-services interface, not a complete legal-aid utilization census. [A: LAD-010]

A current UT-wide section 15A implementation scheme, legal-aid caseload and travel/maintenance payment audit were not located. These remain OPEN.

12. Section 21(4) reporting

The 2021 Rule 9/Rule 8 instruments establish internal reporting and review mechanisms. A current Ladakh-specific Section 21(4) annual implementation report was not located in the targeted official-source search. This remains OPEN. Search non-location is not treated as proof of non-submission.

13. PoA/PCR instruments

The PoA Act applies to Ladakh as a Central Act. The Reorganisation Act and Ladakh-specific adaptation-order catalogue establish the post-2019 transition architecture. [A: LAD-001, LAD-002, LAD-017]

No separate Ladakh-specific PCR instrument materially altering the Central framework was located in the targeted search. The national PCR/PoA instrument corpus remains a separate central-law completeness workstream.

14. Related-law interfaces

The 2019 Reorganisation Act's SC/ST applicability provisions and the 2024 separate Ladakh ST list are the principal jurisdiction-specific beneficiary-scope interfaces. [A: LAD-001, LAD-007]

The 2025 Ladakh reservation framework provides 1% SC and 80% ST reservation in direct recruitment. [A: LAD-008]

The 2021 Ladakh Forest Rights Act committee material located in the UT document corpus provides a related ST-rights interface, but implementation effectiveness is outside this Phase 1 PoA inventory. [A: LAD-019]

No Fifth Schedule territorial regime is asserted for Ladakh from the sources reviewed. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

15. Current/recent corroboration

Current 2026 evidence demonstrates active PoA-related local administration in Kargil through a District V&MC meeting and a Social Welfare/DLSA awareness programme. [A: LAD-009, LAD-010]

A September 2026 NCST review with the UT Chief Secretary, DGP and Social & Tribal Welfare leadership demonstrates active current constitutional-safeguard and ST-welfare governance, but it is not treated as PoA-specific machinery evidence. [A: LAD-011]

The official Central scheme data provide the latest located quantitative relief/assistance evidence for Ladakh through 2024-25. [A: LAD-012, LAD-013]

Historical NCRB/PIB data record zero PoA cases for Ladakh in 2020, while 2018-2019 figures were not separately available because Ladakh was included within the former J&K data. Historical figures are not projected to 2026. [A: LAD-014]
