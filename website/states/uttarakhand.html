Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

Scope

This jurisdiction inventory records Uttarakhand-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, related PCR interfaces, State institutional arrangements, police machinery, courts/prosecutors, relief, monitoring and recent official corroboration. It is an existing-law inventory. It does not assess policy superiority, necessity or constitutional validity.

1. Rule 9 nodal architecture

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) report states that the Director, Social Welfare, Uttarakhand was appointed as the Nodal Officer for coordinating the functioning of District Magistrates, Superintendents of Police and other authorised officers implementing the PoA Act. [A: UK-STATE-001]

A 2020 Ministry report recorded the Secretary, Social Welfare Department as the Nodal Officer. [A: UK-STATE-002]

The current Uttarakhand Social Welfare Department website identifies the current Director as Shri Sanjay Kumar, IAS, but the webpage does not itself establish that he is the current Rule 9 Nodal Officer. [A: UK-STATE-009]

Current appointment order/Gazette, effective date, tenure and exact current Rule 9 designation remain unverified. Historical officer identities are not treated as current without an instrument.

2. Rule 8 Protection Cell and SC/ST investigation machinery

The 2022 Ministry Section 21(4) report states that a Special Inquiry Cell had been set up in each district under the supervision of the Superintendent of Police for prompt action, that atrocity cases were regularly reviewed and registered under the PoA Act when reported, and that the cases were investigated by Deputy Superintendents of Police. [A: UK-STATE-001]

Earlier Ministry reporting likewise records a Special Inquiry Cell in each district under the Superintendent of Police. [A: UK-STATE-002]

This establishes a district Special Inquiry Cell / DySP investigation arrangement for the relevant reporting periods. It does not establish the current Rule 8 State-headquarters Protection Cell notification, command, sanctioned strength, staffing, reporting chain or current district staffing establishment. Those remain open.

3. Special Police Station / police machinery

The retrieved Ministry material describes Special Inquiry Cells but does not establish a current dedicated Special Police Station census for Uttarakhand. Search results did not independently identify a current Uttarakhand Police notification establishing or enumerating dedicated Special Police Stations.

Search silence is not treated as absence. Current police orders, Special Police Station notifications, SC/ST Protection Cell staffing and post-reorganisation arrangements remain open.

4. Rule 7 investigation and charge-sheet timing

The 2022 Section 21(4) data records 77 charge-sheets/investigations completed within 60 days and 16 completed after 60 days for Uttarakhand. [A: UK-STATE-001]

The 2022 Ministry-derived table reproduced by NCDHR records the same 77/16 figures and records zero identified atrocity-prone districts and 30 persons receiving legal aid. This is secondary corroboration, not a substitute for the Ministry report. [B/C: UK-STATE-006]

These figures are reporting-period data. They do not establish current Rule 7 compliance, written reasons for delay under the Rules, or post-BNSS investigation instructions.

5. Rule 10 / identified and atrocity-prone areas

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) reporting table records zero atrocity-prone districts for Uttarakhand. [A: UK-STATE-001]

Historical Ministry reporting also stated that no atrocity-prone areas had been identified in Uttarakhand. [A: UK-STATE-003]

The 2024 POA Handbook, using 2021 government data, lists Uttarakhand among States without appointed Special Officers and discusses the absence of identified areas. This is secondary corroboration and is not a 2026 absence finding. [C: UK-STATE-007]

Current Rule 3/Rule 10 surveys, criteria, notifications and Special Officer appointments remain unverified. No present absence conclusion is drawn.

6. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) report states that the State Level V&MC was constituted under the Chief Minister and that no State-level meeting was held during 2022. It records District Level V&MCs under District Magistrates, with 41 meetings across 13 districts during 2022. It also records Sub-Divisional Level V&MCs constituted in all sub-divisions, with 5 meetings in the sub-divisions of 13 districts during 2022. [A: UK-STATE-001]

The 2024 POA Handbook's summary of 2022 reporting likewise records 2 mandatory State meetings and 0 conducted, and 52 mandatory District meetings with 41 conducted. [C: UK-STATE-008]

Current State, District and Sub-Divisional V&MC membership, notification instruments, meeting calendars, minutes and compliance remain unverified.

7. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

Historical Ministry reporting establishes two Exclusive Special Courts at Nainital and Haridwar, with District and Sessions Courts designated as Special Courts in the remaining districts. Uttarakhand was recorded with 13 districts and 2 Exclusive Special Courts in Ministry reporting. [A: UK-STATE-003, UK-STATE-004]

The 2013 Ministry report also recorded two Exclusive Special Courts at Nainital and Haridwar and designated District and Sessions Courts in the remaining districts. [A: UK-STATE-004]

Current judicial corroboration is strong. The Supreme Court's 27 May 2026 decision in Mehtab v. State of Uttarakhand records trial before the Special Judge (SC/ST Act), Dehradun, in Special Sessions Trial No. 3 of 2013. [A: UK-STATE-010]

The Uttarakhand High Court's 2026 decision in Shekhar Singh Rautela records proceedings pending before the Special Judge (SC/ST Act)/5th Additional District and Sessions Judge, Dehradun. [A: UK-STATE-011]

A 2026 Uttarakhand High Court decision in Pankaj Kumar records proceedings before the District & Sessions Judge/Special Judge (SC/ST Act), Nainital. [A: UK-STATE-012]

These sources corroborate continuing PoA-specific Special Judge arrangements in Dehradun and Nainital but do not establish a complete 2026 district-by-district court census or current Exclusive Special Court notifications.

8. SPP/ESPP

Ministry reporting states that Special Public Prosecutors are associated with Special Courts and records Uttarakhand's designated Special Court structure. The NCSC 2017-18 annual report records 0 districts with Exclusive Special Courts, 13 districts with designated Special Courts, and 0/13 in the Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor / Special Public Prosecutor column, based on State-furnished information. [A/B: UK-STATE-004, UK-STATE-013]

The 2024 POA Handbook lists Uttarakhand among States non-compliant with the one-Special-Prosecutor-per-Special-Court / one-Exclusive-Special-Prosecutor-per-Exclusive-Special-Court framework in its audit methodology. This is secondary evidence and is not treated as a current legal finding without current appointment records. [C: UK-STATE-007]

A current named SPP/ESPP roster, appointment instruments, tenure, qualifications and court allocation were not independently verified. This remains a high-priority residual.

9. Relief, rehabilitation and related assistance

The current Ministry CSS dataset records persons provided relief in Uttarakhand as 119 in 2020-21, 103 in 2021-22, 116 in 2022-23, 154 in 2023-24 and 229 in 2024-25. Central assistance released is recorded as Rs. 0.95 crore, Rs. 0.78 crore, Rs. 0.00 crore, Rs. 1.01 crore and Rs. 1.18 crore respectively. [A: UK-STATE-005]

The Uttarakhand Social Welfare Department's current PoA page states that financial assistance is available to persecuted SC/ST persons at different stages and describes an FIR-stage payment process. The page, however, displays relief figures and stage rules attributed to the 2011 Rules, while the Central Rules were amended subsequently. It therefore establishes a live State departmental implementation page but does not establish that its displayed rates are the current statutory rates. [A: UK-STATE-014]

The State's DBT portal lists the centrally sponsored PCR/PoA implementation scheme as a current Social Welfare Department DBT-applicable scheme. [A: UK-STATE-015]

These sources do not establish individual statutory payment timing, current amended-rule rates, victim/witness travel and maintenance compliance, dietary allowance workflow, sanction controls or case-level payment auditability. These remain open.

10. Annual reporting and statistical context

The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal detailed institutional snapshot located for Uttarakhand. It records V&MC structures, Special Inquiry Cells, Rule 9 Nodal Officer designation, Special Court arrangements and charge-sheet timing. [A: UK-STATE-001]

For 2022, the Ministry-derived charge-sheet table records 77 cases completed within 60 days and 16 after 60 days. [A: UK-STATE-001]

Current Ministry scheme data provides the 2020-21 through 2024-25 relief series and central-assistance series. [A: UK-STATE-005]

Later Section 21(4) reports after 2022 were not independently retrieved as direct primary State-specific material. This is a retrieval limitation, not an absence finding.

11. State PoA/PCR instruments and related-law interfaces

The Uttarakhand Social Welfare Department currently publishes the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 in its Acts & Rules section. [A: UK-STATE-016]

The Department also identifies the Centrally Sponsored Scheme for implementation of the PCR Act and PoA Act as an active DBT-applicable scheme. [A: UK-STATE-015]

The State budget records a budget head for implementation of the SC/ST PoA Act and PCR Act scheme. [A: UK-STATE-017]

The Department's current PoA webpage contains relief information, but its displayed figures are expressly tied to the 2011 Rules and therefore require reconciliation with later central amendments. [A: UK-STATE-014]

A complete current Uttarakhand Gazette/GO set for Rule 8, Rule 9, Rule 10, V&MC composition, court designations, SPP/ESPP appointments, relief rates, contingency planning and PCR implementation was not independently retrieved. This is a retrieval limitation.

12. Current/recent judicial and police corroboration

The Supreme Court's 2026 Mehtab decision confirms that a Special Judge (SC/ST Act), Dehradun, continues to exercise jurisdiction over PoA proceedings. [A: UK-STATE-010]

The Uttarakhand High Court's May 2026 Shekhar Singh Rautela decision records proceedings before the Special Judge (SC/ST Act)/5th Additional District and Sessions Judge, Dehradun. [A: UK-STATE-011]

The Uttarakhand High Court's July 2026 Pankaj Kumar decision records proceedings before the District & Sessions Judge/Special Judge (SC/ST Act), Nainital. [A: UK-STATE-012]

A March 2026 Uttarakhand High Court decision in Govind Singh records a Sessions Trial in Almora arising from a 2022 FIR under Section 3(2)(v) of the PoA Act and identifies the trial as pending before the competent court. It is used only as institutional corroboration. [A: UK-STATE-018]

Current Uttarakhand Police material sufficient to establish a 2026 Rule 8 Protection Cell / Special Police Station census was not independently located. Search silence is not treated as absence.

13. Related-law interfaces

The Uttarakhand Social Welfare Department separately publishes the PCR Act and the PoA Act and administers the combined centrally sponsored implementation scheme. [A: UK-STATE-016, UK-STATE-015]

Current judicial records show post-2023 BNS provisions appearing alongside PoA charges in Uttarakhand proceedings, including the 2026 Shekhar Singh Rautela decision. [A: UK-STATE-011]

This corroborates operational coexistence of the PoA Act with the BNS in current litigation. It does not establish complete BNSS/BSA transition instructions or a Statewide prosecution/police mapping. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains a separate national workstream.

14. Currentness limitations

The following remain unverified or incompletely verified as of 2026-09-05:

  • current named Rule 9 Nodal Officer and appointment instrument;
  • current Rule 8 State Protection Cell notification, command, staffing and reporting matrix;
  • current district Special Inquiry Cell staffing and operational records;
  • current dedicated Special Police Station census and notification status;
  • current Rule 3/Rule 10 Special Officer appointments and atrocity-prone-area notifications;
  • current State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC membership, notifications, calendars and minutes;
  • complete 2026 district-by-district Special/Exclusive Special Court census;
  • current SPP/ESPP roster, appointment instruments, tenure and court allocation;
  • reconciliation of the State's current relief webpage with the later amended Central Rules and current statutory rates;
  • current sanction/payment workflow, seven-day compliance and victim/witness expense workflow;
  • current contingency plan and prevention measures;
  • later Section 21(4) reports after 2022 as directly verified primary material;
  • complete current Uttar Pradesh-style State GO/Gazette/circular corpus for PoA/PCR implementation;
  • current post-BNS/BNSS/BSA police and prosecution transition instructions;
  • current PoA-specific legal-aid and victim/witness referral workflow;
  • current court-wise SPP allocation and case-load records.
