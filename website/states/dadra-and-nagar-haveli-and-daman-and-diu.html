Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu

Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

Scope

This jurisdiction inventory records UT-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules. It does not assess policy superiority, necessity or constitutional validity.

1. Territorial and beneficiary context

The Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (Merger of Union Territories) Act, 2019 formed the present UT from the former UTs with effect from 26 January 2020 and provides for extension/adaptation of laws and continuation of legal proceedings. [A: DDD-STATE-001]

Census 2011 provides separate SC and ST datasets for Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. The Census catalogue identifies ST-specific and SC-specific tables for both legacy territories. [A: DDD-STATE-002, DDD-STATE-003]

The Constitution (Dadra and Nagar Haveli) Scheduled Tribes Order, 1962 identifies notified ST communities for Dadra and Nagar Haveli. [A: DDD-STATE-004]

The UT therefore requires a combined SC/ST implementation baseline while preserving legacy territorial distinctions where historical instruments or data remain territory-specific.

2. Rule 9 Nodal Officer

The Ministry's Section 21(4) reporting for 2021 states that, under Rule 9, the Secretary, Social Welfare Department was appointed Nodal Officer to coordinate the District Collector and Superintendent of Police. [A: DDD-STATE-005]

The current UT directory and 2026 officer postings establish an active Social Welfare/Tribal Welfare administrative structure, including a Director-cum-Deputy Secretary-cum-Deputy Director for Social Welfare and Women & Child Development, with additional Tribal Welfare charge. These current postings do not independently establish the named Rule 9 incumbent. [A: DDD-STATE-006]

Current Rule 9 instrument and named incumbent remain open.

3. Rule 8 Protection Cell and investigation

The 2020-21 Ministry reporting framework recorded an SC/ST Protection Cell for the legacy Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu territories. [B: DDD-STATE-007]

The UT's 2022 annual-return material states that Police register cases under the PoA Act and that officers of Dy.SP/SDPO rank are appointed to investigate PoA cases. [A/B: DDD-STATE-008]

The 2021 Ministry report separately identifies a functioning SC/ST Protection Cell in the UT. [A: DDD-STATE-005]

Current Protection Cell composition, chair, staffing, deployment, review frequency and reporting chain remain open.

4. Special Police Station and investigation machinery

No current dedicated SC/ST Special Police Station was independently verified in this pass. Historical Ministry tables cannot establish 2026 status. [B: DDD-STATE-007]

The 2021 UT annual return confirms Dy.SP/SDPO-level investigation for PoA cases. [A/B: DDD-STATE-008]

Current station designation and investigating-officer roster remain open. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

5. Rule 10 Special Officer and atrocity-prone areas

The 2021 Ministry report states that no atrocity-prone area had been identified in the UT and that a Rule 10 Special Officer of Dy.SP/SDPO rank had been nominated. [A: DDD-STATE-005]

The 2022 Ministry report again records no atrocity-prone area. [A: DDD-STATE-009]

These are reporting-period findings. Current 2026 Rule 3/Rule 10 review, notification and present-area determination remain open.

6. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2021 Ministry report records a UT-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee functioning to review PoA implementation and District-level V&MCs under District Collector chairmanship. [A: DDD-STATE-005]

The 2022 Ministry report states that the UT-level committee was functioning but no meeting was held during 2022. District-level committees under District Collectors were reported as reviewing implementation. [A: DDD-STATE-009]

Current committee constitution, membership, reconstitution, meeting compliance and territory-specific coverage remain open.

7. Special Courts

The 2007 Ministry report records a Session Court of Dadra and Nagar Haveli designated as a Special Court under the Act. [A: DDD-STATE-010]

The 2022 Ministry report states that the Session Court, Dadra & Nagar Haveli had been designated as a Special Court for PoA trials. [A: DDD-STATE-009]

Because the 2019 merger created a single UT and section 8 of the Merger Act extended Bombay High Court jurisdiction, the current trial-court designation for both DNH and Daman/Diu requires current instrument-level verification. [A: DDD-STATE-001]

No current Exclusive Special Court designation was independently verified.

8. Special Public Prosecutor / Exclusive SPP

The 2007 Ministry report records appointment of the Assistant Public Prosecutor of Dadra and Nagar Haveli as Special Public Prosecutor. [A: DDD-STATE-010]

The 2022 Ministry report states that one Public Prosecutor was designated as Special Public Prosecutor for PoA cases. [A: DDD-STATE-009]

The current named SPP, notification date, tenure, court allocation and any ESPP arrangement remain open.

9. Relief, legal aid and rehabilitation

Historical Ministry reporting records legal-aid arrangements and relief-related expenditure for atrocity victims in the legacy UT. [A: DDD-STATE-011]

The 2022 Ministry reporting does not establish a complete current case-level relief/payment workflow. Current relief rates, sanction authority, payment timing, victim/witness travel and maintenance, legal-aid referral, rehabilitation and audit trail remain open.

Current Ministry PFMS material confirms central scheme releases to the present UT in 2026 and earlier years, but these releases must not be equated with case-level victim relief without reconciliation. [A: DDD-STATE-012]

10. Annual reporting and statistical context

The UT supplied annual PoA returns for 2020, 2021 and 2022 through its Social Welfare Department. The 2022 return confirms PoA registration and Dy.SP/SDPO investigation. [A/B: DDD-STATE-008, DDD-STATE-013]

The Ministry's 2021 and 2022 reports provide institutional and case-reporting snapshots. In the 2021-22 reporting annexure for the PCR Act, no PCR case was registered in the UT during 2020. [A: DDD-STATE-014]

PoA and PCR statistics must be kept separate. Historical no-case entries do not establish present absence of offences.

11. Current/recent judicial and police corroboration

Bombay High Court proceedings in 2023 concerning Dadra and Nagar Haveli included allegations under section 3(1)(x) of the PoA Act involving alleged caste abuse of members of a Scheduled Tribe community. The Court's judgment arose primarily from preventive-detention proceedings and is used here as current judicial corroboration of PoA litigation in the UT, not as a Phase 2 merits conclusion. [B/C: DDD-STATE-015]

The 2023 Bombay High Court record also identifies a Special Public Prosecutor appearing for the UT in the proceedings, but this litigation appearance does not independently establish the current statutory SPP roster. [B/C: DDD-STATE-015]

12. Related-law and tribal-regime interfaces

The present UT contains notified ST communities, particularly in the former Dadra and Nagar Haveli territory. The 2019 Merger Act preserved and adapted the pre-merger legal corpus. [A: DDD-STATE-001, DDD-STATE-004]

The Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Orders applicable to the former territories also require verification against the post-merger territorial framework. A 2015 Parliament record confirms amendment of the Constitution (Dadra and Nagar Haveli) Scheduled Castes Order, 1962. [A: DDD-STATE-016]

The Bombay High Court has jurisdiction over the merged UT under the Merger Act. [A: DDD-STATE-001]

No special tribal-protection regulation equivalent to the Andaman and Nicobar Islands interface was established in this pass. This is not a negative legal conclusion. Relevant ST Orders, land/forest/customary interfaces and local protective instruments require controlled second-level search if they materially affect PoA implementation.

13. Currentness limitations

  • current Rule 9 instrument and named incumbent;
  • current Rule 8 Protection Cell composition, chair and deployment;
  • current investigating-officer roster and case-supervision records;
  • current dedicated Special Police Station status;
  • current Rule 10 Special Officer instrument and atrocity-prone-area determination;
  • current UT and District V&MC constitution, membership and meeting compliance;
  • current Special Court designation for the merged UT, including Daman/Diu coverage;
  • current SPP/ESPP notification, named roster, tenure and allocation;
  • current relief-rate/payment workflow and case-level audit trail;
  • current victim/witness and legal-aid workflow;
  • current contingency/prevention plan;
  • later Section 21(4) reports beyond the 2022 snapshot;
  • complete post-merger Gazette/notification corpus;
  • BNS/BNSS/BSA transition instructions;
  • current court-wise prosecutor workload/performance;
  • reconciliation of central assistance with case-level relief;
  • post-merger SC/ST territorial notification and local-rule interface.
