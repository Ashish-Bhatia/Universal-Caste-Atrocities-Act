Karnataka

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map. Inventoried 5 September 2026.

Status

Karnataka is inventoried with qualifications using independent current/recent official State and Government of India sources. This page does not state a conclusion on necessity, superiority, constitutionality or policy desirability.

Verified architecture

  • Directorate of Civil Rights Enforcement (DCRE) is the State protection/investigation structure identified in official reporting.
  • The 2025 Karnataka Assembly/Budget record states that 33 special police stations had been started under DCRE.
  • Official reporting records State, District and Sub-Divisional Vigilance and Monitoring Committee structures.
  • The 2023 State Annual Report records 23 district-level Special Courts and 8 Exclusive Special Courts for the 31-district reporting structure.
  • Current Government of India scheme data records 5,167 persons provided relief in Karnataka in 2024-25 and Rs.48.66 crore in Central assistance.
  • The Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act contains bonded-labour reporting and anti-untouchability/social-exclusion interfaces.

Qualifications

  • Current Rule 9 appointment and office-holder require a 2026 primary instrument.
  • The underlying establishment and staffing instruments for the 33 DCRE stations require retrieval.
  • Current V&MC, Special/Exclusive Special Court and SPP/ESPP matrices are not fully frozen.
  • Current atrocity-prone-area notifications require primary verification.
  • Current relief payment, treasury/DBT, digital workflow and PoA-specific SOP set require further sourcing.
  • Current PCR, Manual Scavengers and Bonded Labour State instruments remain partly open.
  • Post-BNSS DCRE procedural instructions remain a separate transition-verification task.
