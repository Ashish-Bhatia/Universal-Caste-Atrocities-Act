Verified architecture

Nodal and police machinery

The 2024 State report identifies the Director ESOMSA as Rule 9 nodal officer and a State CID Protection Cell under DGP supervision. No Special Police Station was reported in the 12 districts. Current 2026 appointments and staffing remain open.

Special Officers and geography

All 12 districts reported Additional District Magistrates as Rule 10 Special Officers. No atrocity-prone area was identified in the 2024 report. Current notification status remains open.

Committees

The State V&MC was reported constituted but held no meeting in 2024, while 12 District V&MCs held 32 meetings. Sub-Divisional committees were also listed. Current constitution and membership records remain open.

Courts and prosecutors

The 2024 State report states that District and Sessions Judge courts were designated Special Courts and District Attorneys-cum-Public Prosecutors were designated SPPs. No ESPPs were specified. A current notification matrix remains open.

Relief and contingency plan

The 2024 report records seven-day relief and State authorization for District Magistrates to withdraw money from the treasury. A Rule 15 contingency plan was reported as formulated. Current rate, DBT and operative instruments remain open.

Digital and related-law interfaces

HP Police CCTNS provides online complaint, FIR viewing and status services. Related State-law interfaces include PCR, bonded labour, manual scavenging, land-transfer regulation, Panchayati Raj and municipal law.

Open verification questions

Current Rule 9 instrument, Protection Cell staffing, Rule 10 orders, atrocity-prone-area notifications, complete Special Court and SPP/ESPP matrices, relief/DBT workflow, PoA-specific SOPs, digital workflow, current V&MC records, bonded-labour instruments and Manual Scavengers instruments remain open.
