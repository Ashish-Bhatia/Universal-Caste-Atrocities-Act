Project status

The project does not presume that replacement of existing law is necessary, constitutional, desirable or superior. Research conclusions remain subject to verification.

Research areas

  • Existing Central legislation and Rules
  • State-by-State implementation machinery
  • Case-law corpus
  • Statistical and empirical evidence
  • Constitutional competence and risk
  • Comparative and policy analysis
  • Legislative drafting and verification, after prerequisite phases

Source discipline

Primary authoritative sources are preferred. Material claims are recorded with provenance and evidence grades. Search silence is not treated as proof of legal completeness.
