Project status

Phase 1 is active. State-by-State implementation research is underway. The repository is the authoritative project record. The public site is a publication layer.

Research is source-controlled and evidence graded. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence. Bill drafting, policy conclusions and constitutional conclusions have not begun.

Method

Primary authoritative sources are preferred. Evidence is graded A through D. Material claims require traceable provenance. Historical implementation reports are separated from current State practice. Contradictions and unresolved source gaps remain visible rather than being silently resolved.
