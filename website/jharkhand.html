Jharkhand

Phase 1 State implementation inventory. Status: inventoried with qualifications. Inventory date: 5 September 2026.

Verified current/recent evidence

  • Current Jharkhand Police district profiles list dedicated SC/ST Police Stations or SC/ST units in multiple districts.
  • A 2023 State Cabinet decision authorized police officers below DSP rank, including Inspector/Sub-Inspector level officers, to investigate PoA cases. Its interaction with Rule 7 remains an open legal-status verification item.
  • A 15 April 2026 State Cabinet decision approved three District Judge-level posts for Special Courts in Ranchi, Dhanbad and Daltonganj.
  • Current Jharkhand High Court records show SC/ST Act Special Court assignments at multiple locations.
  • Current Ministry data records Jharkhand relief coverage and central assistance through 2024-25.
  • Current State DBT, treasury and UDDP infrastructure exists, but a PoA-specific payment/case-management workflow has not been established from the retrieved public material.
  • Current Labour Department material identifies Bonded Labour vigilance-committee resources. Manual-scavenging State instruments remain incompletely sourced.
  • Revenue and local-government material identifies land administration, CNT/SPT/PESA and Panchayati Raj interfaces requiring further instrument-level verification.

Open verification

Current Rule 9 and Rule 10 instruments, Protection Cell order and staffing, statewide SC/ST Police-unit census, V&MC matrix, complete Special/Exclusive Special Court notification matrix, SPP/ESPP appointments, relief workflow, PoA-specific executive instruments, digital workflow and related-law implementation instruments remain open.
