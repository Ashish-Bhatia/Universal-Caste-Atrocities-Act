Section 3 conduct and Schedule correspondence with BNS.

SC/ST Act Section 3 Conduct-by-Conduct BNS Crosswalk

Date: 2026-09-05

Scope and method

This artifact extends the completed Act extraction. It does not reproduce the Act or Rules. It maps every conduct in section 3 of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 against the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS), and separately maps every IPC reference in the Schedule under section 3(2)(va).

Method: compare enacted BNS text with the legacy IPC offence or definition; apply Ministry of Law and Justice S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, issued under General Clauses Act section 8; apply BNS section 358 repeal and savings; treat merged/split provisions as correspondence clusters; preserve residual differences and unresolved questions. No constitutional, policy, necessity, superiority or desirability conclusion is made.

Transition basis

BNS Act 45 of 2023 came into force on 01-07-2024. BNS section 358 repeals the IPC and preserves previous operation, accrued rights/liabilities, penalties and punishments, and proceedings/investigations/remedies relating to offences committed under the repealed IPC. S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, directs that references to IPC provisions in Acts, rules, notifications and similar instruments for the time being in force are read as references to BNS and corresponding provisions construed accordingly. General Clauses Act section 8 is the stated basis of the notification.

The date of conduct remains material. The crosswalk does not retrospectively convert a pre-01-07-2024 IPC offence into a BNS offence.

Schedule structural findings

  • The Schedule is not a clean list of independently punishable provisions. It includes definitions such as IPC 120A, 141, 142, 146, 319, 320 and 359 alongside punishment provisions. Post-01-07-2024 application therefore requires reading the BNS provision cluster.
  • BNS s.189 consolidates the unlawful-assembly cluster represented by IPC 141-145 in the Schedule. BNS s.191 consolidates the rioting cluster represented by IPC 146-148.
  • BNS s.115, s.117 and s.118 consolidate several hurt/grievous-hurt provisions. IPC 324 therefore maps to s.118(1), while IPC 325 maps to s.117(2).
  • BNS s.124 materially restructures the acid-offence provisions. IPC 326B maps to s.124(2), but the BNS conduct should be read in full rather than treated as a numerical substitution.
  • IPC 506 maps into BNS s.351(2)-(3), while the IPC definition in s.503 is also consolidated into BNS s.351(1). The applicable BNS subsection must be identified from the facts.
  • BNS changes in punishment ceilings and fines matter because section 3(2)(va) expressly adopts the punishment specified for the underlying scheduled offence.

Rule 2(ga), IPC section 39

Rule 2(ga) of the SC/ST Rules refers to IPC section 39 for “voluntarily”. BNS section 2(33) contains the corresponding definition. The enacted BNS text retains the core intention/knowledge test and the familiar illustration. S.O. 2790(E) supplies the transition reading of the Rules' legacy reference. This does not amend the Rules text, which should retain its historical IPC citation until a controlling source is formally updated.

Verification and limitations

Primary/official sources verified for this workstream include the BNS enacted text and current India Code material, BPRD Government of India BNS reference material, S.O. 2790(E), the General Clauses Act section 8 framework, and the SC/ST Act Schedule/2015 Amendment insertion. Secondary comparison tables were used only to locate or sanity-check mappings, not as controlling evidence where primary text was available.

Remaining verification:

  • Final Gazette/Legislative Department completeness check for later Act/Rules instruments remains open.
  • Exact current BNS punishment text should be captured directly from the final primary text for every merged Schedule cluster before declaring the crosswalk frozen.
  • The statutory Schedule count should be reconciled against the project's existing Act extraction. Present enumeration identifies 32 IPC references, distinct from the Rules' 47 relief contingencies.
  • State-law overlays remain outside this workstream.
  • BNSS and BSA crosswalks remain separate workstreams.
