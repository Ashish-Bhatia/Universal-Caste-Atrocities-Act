SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Rules, 1995, Consolidated Clause Extraction

Date: 2026-09-05

Scope and source discipline

This artifact does not recreate the previously completed Rules amendment-history matrix. It extends that work by recording the operative structure of the Rules as amended through G.S.R. 588(E), dated 27-06-2018.

Primary source chain:

  • India Code / Government of India record for the principal Rules, G.S.R. 316(E), dated 31-03-1995.
  • Government Gazette amendment instruments: G.S.R. 896(E), 23-12-2011; G.S.R. 725(E), 08-11-2013; G.S.R. 416(E), 23-06-2014; G.S.R. 774(E), 05-11-2014; G.S.R. 424(E), 14-04-2016; G.S.R. 588(E), 27-06-2018.
  • Department of Social Justice and Empowerment current publication index, which presently lists the 1995 Rules and separate 2016 and 2018 PoA Rules documents.
  • The Government-hosted India Code/eCourts transcription was used as a consolidated text aid. It expressly states that the Department's own PDF/Gazette remains authoritative. Evidence grade A applies to the underlying Government instruments; transcription-only propositions are treated as verification aids, not substitutes for the Gazette.
  • Ministry of Law and Justice S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, BNS s.358, BNSS s.531 and General Clauses Act s.8 are transition sources for legacy criminal-law references. The exact correspondence is not presumed from section numbers.

The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment's current site lists "The Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (PoA) Rules, 2018" and "The Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (PoA) Rules, 2016" as active publications, and separately lists the 1995 Rules. This is useful current-source evidence but is not treated as a declaration that no later instrument exists. [Source: Department of Social Justice and Empowerment current archive, 2026.]

Version status

The consolidated operative version used for this extraction is the Rules as amended through 27-06-2018. The 2018 amendment itself states that the amendments come into force on publication and records several substitutions with retrospective effect from 31-03-1995. Later-instrument completeness remains provisional pending the final Gazette/Legislative Department index pass already recorded as an open Phase 1 issue.

Rule-by-rule extraction

Rule 1. Short title and commencement

Operative content:

  • Rule 1(1): short title, Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Rules, 1995.
  • Rule 1(2): commencement on publication in the Official Gazette.

Provenance: Principal Rules, G.S.R. 316(E), 31-03-1995. No identified amendment.

Act interaction: made under Act s.23(1).

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: none material identified.

Verification: Verified against Government/India Code record and consolidated transcription. Grade A.

Rule 2. Definitions

Rule 2 supplies definitions used throughout the Rules.

(a) "Act": the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989.

(b) "dependent": current text substituted by G.S.R. 424(E), 14-04-2016, effective from 31-03-1995. Current formulation covers spouse, children, parents, brother and sister of the victim who are dependent wholly or mainly on the victim for support and maintenance.

(c) "identified area": area where the State Government has reason to believe an atrocity may take place, there is apprehension of recurrence of an offence under the Act, or the area is prone to atrocities. The historical phrase deleted by G.S.R. 604(E), 29-08-1995 is retained in provenance notes because it is visible in consolidated historical material.

(d) "Non-Government Organisation": voluntary organisation engaged in welfare activities relating to SCs/STs and registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 or under a law for registration of documents/organisations then in force.

(e) "schedule": the Schedule annexed to the Rules.

(f) "section": a section of the Act.

(g) "State Government": for a Union territory, the Administrator appointed by the President under Article 239 of the Constitution.

(ga) "voluntarily": inserted by G.S.R. 588(E), 27-06-2018, effective from 31-03-1995; same meaning as IPC s.39.

(h) words/expressions not defined in the Rules but defined in the Act carry the Act meaning.

Act interaction: definitions control interpretation of the implementation machinery and relief provisions.

BNS interaction: Rule 2(ga) is a direct legacy IPC reference. IPC s.39 was repealed on BNS commencement. S.O. 2790(E), BNS s.358 and GCA s.8 must be applied before stating the current corresponding BNS provision. Direct numerical substitution is prohibited by project methodology.

BNSS/BSA interaction: no direct rule 2 reference identified.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: the term "voluntarily" is a high-priority transition item. Current correspondence must be source-mapped separately.

Verification: Rule text and amendment provenance verified. Grade A for amendment instrument, consolidated text verified as aid.

Rule 3. Precautionary and preventive measures

Rule 3(1)(i)-(xi) requires State preventive machinery including:

  • identification of areas at risk of atrocity or recurrence;
  • direction to District Magistrate/Superintendent of Police or other officer to visit and review law and order;
  • where considered necessary, cancellation/deposit of specified arms licences and seizure of illegal firearms;
  • possible provision of arms licences to SC/ST members for safety;
  • State, district, divisional and other committees;
  • vigilance and monitoring committee;
  • awareness centres and workshops;
  • encouragement and assistance for NGOs conducting awareness work;
  • special police force deployment;
  • quarterly review of law-and-order conditions, committees, prosecutors, investigating officers and other responsible officers, and registered cases.

Provenance: Principal Rules, with structural numbering and subsequent implementation references preserved through later amendments.

Act interaction: operationalises the preventive and implementation obligations contemplated by Act s.21 and related provisions.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: no direct statutory reference in Rule 3. Firearms measures operate alongside the general criminal/arms law framework and require separate crosswalk if used in later implementation analysis.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: the Rule uses administrative duties without an express consequence within the Rule for non-performance. Any legal consequence requires separate case-law/statutory analysis and is not inferred here.

Verification: Verified at rule/sub-clause level from consolidated text and Government descriptions. Grade A/B depending on specific consolidated wording; Gazette clause verification retained as preferred source.

Rule 4. Supervision of prosecution and submission of report

Rule 4(1), 4(1A), 4(1B):

  • State Government, on District Magistrate recommendation, prepares district panels of eminent senior advocates with at least seven years' practice for Special Courts and Exclusive Special Courts.
  • State Government, in consultation with Director Prosecution/person in charge, specifies panels of Public Prosecutors and Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors.
  • Both panels are notified in the State Official Gazette and remain in force for three years.

Rule 4(2): District Magistrate and Director Prosecution/person in charge review at least twice yearly, January and July, performance of Special Public Prosecutors and Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors and report to State Government.

Rule 4(3): State Government may de-notify a prosecutor for inadequate ability/care/caution, with reasons recorded in writing.

Rule 4(4)(a)-(b): district-level review of registered cases and implementation of victim/witness rights under Chapter IVA of the Act, with monthly report by the 20th day of the succeeding month.

Rule 4(5): District Magistrate/Sub-Divisional Magistrate may, if necessary or desired by victims, engage an eminent senior advocate for cases in Special/Exclusive Special Courts on appropriate fee.

Rule 4(6): State Government fixes fees for Special/Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors at a scale higher than other panel advocates in the State.

Provenance: major restructuring by G.S.R. 424(E), 14-04-2016, effective from 31-03-1995; terminology for Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors subsequently reflected in 2018 amendment material where applicable.

Act interaction: directly implements Act ss.14, 15 and 15A interfaces.

BNSS interaction: prosecution, filing and trial operate within the general criminal procedure framework subject to the special Act. No one-to-one procedural substitution is made in this extraction.

BSA interaction: no direct evidence-law reference.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: panel terminology and the relationship between statutory appointment under Act s.15 and Rules panel mechanisms require a later section-by-section crosswalk.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 5. Information to police officer in charge of a police station

Rule 5(1): oral information relating to an offence under the Act must be reduced to writing, read over to the informant, signed, and entered in the station record.

Rule 5(2): free copy to informant forthwith.

Rule 5(3): refusal route to Superintendent of Police, with investigation by SP or police officer not below DSP and written direction to enter the information.

Provenance: Principal Rules. Operative wording predates BNSS.

Act interaction: complements Act offence-registration machinery.

BNSS interaction: the Rule contains a CrPC-era recording/refusal architecture. Current operation must be read with BNSS and the transition instruments, particularly BNSS s.531 and S.O. 2790(E). The project will not assume direct CrPC-to-BNSS section substitution.

BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: interaction with BNSS registration provisions requires exact crosswalk.

Verification: Verified. Grade A for principal instrument; current-law interface open.

Rule 6. Spot inspection by officers

Rule 6(1): on information or own knowledge of an atrocity within jurisdiction, District Magistrate, Sub-Divisional Magistrate, other Executive Magistrate, or police officer not below DSP must immediately visit the place, assess atrocity, loss of life and property damage, and report forthwith to State Government.

Rule 6(2)(i)-(v): on-site officers must draw victim/family/dependent list, prepare damage report, order intensive patrolling, protect witnesses/sympathisers, and provide immediate relief.

Act interaction: operationalises immediate administrative response and relief.

BNSS interaction: officer powers and police response sit alongside BNSS; no direct cross-reference in Rule 6.

BSA interaction: inspection/reporting is evidentiary in effect but no special BSA rule is expressly incorporated.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: evidentiary status of administrative reports requires later BSA/case-law analysis.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 7. Investigating Officer

Rule 7(1): offence under the Act must be investigated by a police officer not below DSP rank. Appointment is by State Government/DGP/SP after considering experience, ability, justice and ability to perceive implications and investigate promptly.

Rule 7(2): investigation must receive top priority; current amended rule requires completion and reporting to SP, forwarding to DGP/Commissioner of Police, and filing of charge sheet by the officer in charge of the police station in the Special Court/Exclusive Special Court within 60 days, inclusive of investigation and filing.

Rule 7(2A): delay in investigation or charge-sheet filing must be explained in writing by the investigating officer.

Rule 7(3): quarterly review by Home Department and SC/ST Development Department secretaries or corresponding UT officers, Director Prosecution, prosecution officer and DGP/Commissioner of Police.

Provenance: Rule 7(2)-(3) substantially substituted by G.S.R. 424(E), 14-04-2016, effective from 31-03-1995.

Act interaction: directly interfaces with Act s.4 and investigation/prosecution machinery.

BNSS interaction: Rule 7 contains procedure, investigation and charge-sheet timing concepts. Current procedure requires crosswalk to BNSS, including investigation and police-report provisions. The 60-day special Rule requirement must not be equated with any BNSS default period without analysis.

BSA interaction: no direct reference.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: potential interface between Rule 7's 60-day period and BNSS investigation/remand/statutory-bail architecture requires later detailed procedural crosswalk.

Verification: Verified against consolidated text and 2016 amendment instrument. Grade A.

Rule 8. Setting up of the SC/ST Protection Cell

State Government must establish a State-headquarters Protection Cell under DGP/IGP charge.

Functions include survey of identified areas; public order; recommendation of special police force/posts; investigation into probable causes; restoration of security; information to nodal/special officers; victim/witness rights implementation; enquiries into investigation and spot inspection; enquiries into refusal to record information; wilful negligence by public servants; review of registered cases; monthly reporting by the 20th to State Government/nodal officer.

Provenance: Principal Rule with victim/witness additions by G.S.R. 424(E), 14-04-2016, effective from 31-03-1995.

Act interaction: supports Act s.21 implementation and Chapter IVA rights.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: no direct section reference; police functions require general-code interface.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: overlap with general police supervisory structures and Act s.4/21 is operational rather than resolved here.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 9. Nomination of Nodal Officer

State Government nominates a nodal officer at Secretary level, preferably from SC/ST, to coordinate District Magistrates, SPs/authorised officers, investigating officers and other implementation officers.

Quarterly review covers Rule 4 reports, Rule 6, Rule 8(xi), registered cases, law-and-order conditions, relief measures, adequacy of ration/clothing/shelter/legal aid/travel/daily allowance/transport, NGO and Protection Cell performance, committees/public servants, and victim/witness rights implementation.

Provenance: Principal Rule, with victim/witness rights addition by G.S.R. 424(E), 2016.

Act interaction: implementation and monitoring under Act s.21 and Chapter IVA.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: reporting hierarchy overlaps with Rules 8, 10, 14, 16 and 17; no conflict conclusion drawn.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 10. Appointment of a Special Officer

In an identified area, State Government appoints Special Officer not below Additional District Magistrate to coordinate with District Magistrate, SP/other officers, committees and Protection Cell.

Functions: immediate relief and recurrence prevention; awareness centre/workshops; NGO coordination and assistance; implementation of victim/witness rights in identified areas.

Provenance: Principal Rule, with Chapter IVA addition by G.S.R. 424(E), 2016.

Act interaction: preventive and implementation machinery under Act s.21.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: no direct references.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: coordination role overlaps with nodal officer and Protection Cell. Allocation of responsibility requires later operational analysis.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 11. Travelling allowance, daily allowance, maintenance expenses and transport facilities

Rule 11(1): victim, dependent and witnesses receive return rail fare by second class in express/mail/passenger train or actual bus/taxi fare from residence/stay to investigation/trial location.

Rule 11(2): District/Sub-Divisional/Executive Magistrate arranges transport or reimburses full transport for visits to investigating and administrative officers.

Rule 11(3): specified women, minors, persons over 60 and persons with 40% or more disability may be accompanied by an attendant of choice, whose travel/maintenance expenses are payable.

Rule 11(4): daily maintenance expenses during absence for investigation/hearing/trial at rates not less than State minimum wages for agricultural labourers.

Rule 11(5): diet expenses at State-fixed rates.

Rule 11(6): payments immediately or within three days for qualifying visits.

Rule 11(7): reimbursement of medicines, special medical consultation, blood transfusion, replacement clothing, meals and fruits for victims where an offence under Act s.3 has been committed.

Provenance: Principal Rule.

Act interaction: victim support and relief machinery; interacts with Rule 12 and Act s.15A.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct, though trial/investigation terminology requires BNSS interface mapping.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: interaction with current disability law and current minimum-wage framework requires later verification if operational amounts are assessed.

Verification: Verified. Grade A for Rule text.

Rule 12. Measures to be taken by District Administration

Rule 12(1): DM/SP visit place/area, assess loss of life/property and identify victims/family/dependents entitled to relief.

Rule 12(2): SP ensures FIR registration and measures to apprehend accused.

Rule 12(3): SP after spot inspection appoints investigating officer and deploys police/preventive measures.

Rule 12(4): immediate relief in cash/kind/both within seven days according to Schedule Annexure-I read with Annexure-II; includes food, water, clothing, shelter, medical aid, transport and essential items.

Rule 12(4A): State/UT may authorise DM to withdraw treasury money immediately for relief.

Rule 12(4B): Special/Exclusive Special Court may order socio-economic rehabilitation during investigation, inquiry and trial under Act s.15A(6)(c).

Rule 12(5): as amended in 2018, specified relief for death, injury, rape, gang rape, unnatural offences, voluntarily causing grievous hurt by acid, voluntarily throwing/attempting to throw acid, etc., and property damage is additional to compensation rights under other laws.

Rule 12(6): relief/rehabilitation according to Schedule.

Rule 12(7): relief report to Special/Exclusive Special Court; court may order full/part payment or other assistance if relief was late, insufficient or partial.

Provenance: Rule 12(4), (4A), (4B) and related provisions substantially substituted by G.S.R. 424(E), 2016; Rule 12(5) amended by G.S.R. 588(E), 2018.

Act interaction: directly interfaces with Act s.15A, especially victim/witness rights and socio-economic rehabilitation.

BNSS interaction: "investigation, inquiry and trial" are procedural stages under current BNSS. No automatic CrPC terminology substitution is used.

BSA interaction: relief eligibility may depend on medical/post-mortem/other reports in the Schedule, but the Rule itself does not create a special evidence code.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: relationship between statutory compensation, relief under the Rules and other compensation statutes must be analysed later. Current Rule 12(5) expressly preserves other-law compensation rights.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 13. Selection of officers and other staff

Rule 13(1): officers/staff appointed in atrocity-prone areas must have appropriate aptitude and understanding of SC/ST problems.

Rule 13(2): State Government must ensure adequate SC/ST representation in administration and police at all levels, particularly police posts/stations.

Provenance: Principal Rule.

Act interaction: implementation duty under Act s.21.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: "adequately represented" is not quantified by the Rule. No policy conclusion is made.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 14. Specific responsibility of State Government

Rule 14(1): annual budget provisions for relief/rehabilitation and an appropriate scheme for victim/witness rights and entitlements under Act s.15A(11).

Rule 14(2): review at least twice yearly, January and July, of Special/Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors, reports, investigations, preventive steps, relief/rehabilitation and lapses by responsible officers.

Provenance: G.S.R. 424(E), 2016 substitution, effective from 31-03-1995.

Act interaction: Act s.15A and s.21 implementation.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: overlaps with Rules 4 and 16/17 monitoring. Operational duplication is recorded, not resolved.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 15. Contingency plan by State Government

Rule 15(1): State Government must frame and implement a plan to effectively implement the Act and notify it in State Official Gazette. It specifies roles/responsibilities of departments/officers, rural/urban local bodies and NGOs.

Minimum package includes: (a) immediate relief scheme; (aa) victim/witness rights and entitlements scheme under Act s.15A(11); (b) agricultural land and house-sites; (c) rehabilitation packages; (d) employment in Government/Government undertaking for dependent/family member; (e) pension scheme for widows, dependent children, handicapped/old-age victims; (f) mandatory compensation; (g) socio-economic strengthening; (h) brick/stone masonry houses; (i) healthcare, essential commodities, electrification, drinking water, burial/cremation ground and link roads to SC/ST habitats.

Rule 15(2): plan/summary and scheme to Central Government Department of Social Justice and Empowerment and specified district/police authorities.

Provenance: Rule 15(1) and (2) amended/substituted by G.S.R. 424(E), 2016; victim/witness rights item added in 2016.

Act interaction: Act s.21 and s.15A.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: the Rule creates a broad administrative package. Its relationship with State schemes and statutory entitlements requires later mapping; no duplication conclusion is made.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 16. State-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee

Rule 16(1): State Government constitutes a high-power vigilance and monitoring committee. The 2016 version substituted the composition and the 2018 amendment removed the former 25-member ceiling by leaving the member-count field blank/omitted. Composition includes: (i) Chief Minister/Administrator as Chair, with Governor as Chair in President's Rule; (ii) Home, Finance and SC/ST welfare/development Ministers, or Advisors during President's Rule; (iii) all elected MPs and State Legislative Assembly/Council members from the State belonging to SC/ST; (iv) Chief Secretary, Home Secretary, DGP, and Directors/Deputy Directors of NCSC and NCST; (v) Secretary in charge of SC/ST welfare/development as Convener.

Rule 16(2): committee meets at least twice yearly, January and July, to review Act implementation, victim/witness rights scheme, relief/rehabilitation, prosecution, responsible officers/agencies and reports including nodal and special officer reports.

Provenance: composition substituted by G.S.R. 424(E), 2016; former numerical ceiling altered by G.S.R. 588(E), 2018.

Act interaction: Act s.21 and Chapter IVA.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: post-2018 omission of the numerical ceiling is an important version-control item. The exact legislative effect of the deletion/asterisk in consolidated text should be retained against the 2018 Gazette, not inferred from formatting alone.

Verification: Verified against 2016 and 2018 amendment instruments. Grade A.

Rule 17. District-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee

Rule 17(1): in each district, DM sets up committee to review Act implementation, victim/witness rights scheme, relief/rehabilitation, prosecution, responsible officers/agencies and reports.

Rule 17(2): composition includes elected MPs/State Assembly/Council members, SP, three Group A/Gazetted SC/ST State officers, up to five non-official SC/ST members and up to three members from other categories associated with NGOs. DM is Chair and District Social Welfare Officer is Member-Secretary.

Rule 17(2A): inserted by G.S.R. 725(E), 2013, provision for up to three Central-Government-nominated social workers as members. Omitted by G.S.R. 774(E), 05-11-2014.

Rule 17(3): committee meets at least once every three months.

Provenance: 2013 insertion and 2014 omission verified from amendment instruments; 2016 victim/witness language added in committee review provisions.

Act interaction: Act s.21 and Chapter IVA.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: committee composition and appointment rules are administrative. No constitutional or policy conclusion is drawn in Phase 1.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 17A. Sub-Division Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee

Rule 17A(1): in each Sub-Division, SDM sets up committee to review Act implementation, victim/witness rights scheme, relief/rehabilitation, prosecution, responsible officers/agencies and reports.

Rule 17A(2): current composition includes State Legislative Assembly/Council members from the sub-division, elected Panchayati Raj members belonging to SC/ST, DSP, Tehsildar, BDO, up to two non-official SC/ST members and up to two members from other categories associated with NGOs. SDM is Chair and BDO Member-Secretary.

Rule 17A(3): up to three Central-Government-nominated social workers as members.

Provenance: inserted by G.S.R. 725(E), 08-11-2013; Rule 17A(2) substituted by G.S.R. 774(E), 05-11-2014; victim/witness rights language reflected in later consolidated text.

Act interaction: Act s.21 and Chapter IVA.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: none direct.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: interaction with district/state committees and local-body governance remains an operational mapping issue.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Rule 18. Material for annual report

State Government must every year before 1 July forward to Central Government a report on measures taken to implement the Act and schemes/plans during the previous calendar year.

Provenance: Principal Rule; current text retained.

Act interaction: reporting/monitoring under Act s.21.

BNS/BNSS/BSA interaction: no direct reference.

Duplication/conflict/gap/ambiguity: annual reporting interface with statutory annual reports and Department formats requires later source inventory.

Verification: Verified. Grade A.

Schedule and Annexures

The Schedule is annexed to the Rules and is expressly linked to Rule 12(4). The current consolidated structure contains Annexure-I, "Norms for Relief Amount", and Annexure-II, a disability-related Government notification/material with multiple appendices.

  • Rape, unnatural offences or gang rape. 2018 amendment expanded the item to include unnatural offences and identifies IPC ss.375, 376, 376A, 376E and 377, with gang rape under s.376D. The 2018 Gazette is the controlling source for the exact wording. Relief in the consolidated text includes Rs.5,00,000 for rape/related category and Rs.8,25,000 for gang rape, subject to specified staging.
  • Murder or death. Rs.8,25,000, with payment stages tied to post-mortem report and charge-sheet filing.
  • Additional relief for murder, death, massacre, rape, gang rape, permanent incapacitation and dacoity. Possible pension, employment, agricultural land/house, education and maintenance of children, and three months' utensils/food provisions.
  • Complete destruction or burnt houses. Brick or stone masonry house constructed/provided at Government cost.

BNS / BNSS / BSA interface inventory

BNS

  • Rule 2(ga) expressly refers to IPC s.39 for "voluntarily".
  • Schedule items 24-32 and 40-41 use IPC offence terminology.
  • Schedule item 44 uses IPC ss.375, 376, 376A, 376D, 376E and 377.
  • The project must map each conduct to the corresponding BNS provision, where one exists, using conduct elements rather than section-number substitution.
  • S.O. 2790(E), BNS s.358 and GCA s.8 are transition sources. The transition question is distinct from substantive equivalence and must be recorded separately.

BNSS

  • Rules 5, 7 and 12 use legacy criminal-procedure concepts including police information recording, investigation, charge-sheet filing and trial.
  • The 60-day Rule 7(2) requirement is a special Rules requirement and must be compared against, not replaced by, BNSS procedure.
  • The Rules do not themselves provide a complete BNSS cross-reference table.
  • BNSS s.531 and S.O. 2790(E) are transition sources.

BSA

  • No Rules provision identified in the present extraction as expressly citing the Indian Evidence Act.
  • Medical reports, post-mortem reports, investigation reports and disability certification are operationally relevant to relief/payment but their evidentiary treatment is not specified by the Rules.
  • A separate BSA crosswalk remains required and is not completed by this artifact.
