## Research rules

- Prefer primary authoritative sources.
- Grade evidence A/B/C/D.
- Do not treat search silence as proof of absence.
- Do not infer currentness from historical reporting.
- Do not presume replacement, reform, necessity, superiority or constitutionality.
- Preserve source IDs, issue IDs and decision IDs.
- Do not begin a new State/UT workstream without explicit authorization in authoritative project state.
