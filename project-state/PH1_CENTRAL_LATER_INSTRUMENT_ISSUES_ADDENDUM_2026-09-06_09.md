## PH1-ISSUE-008, Representation of the People / Conduct of Elections Rules

Status: OPEN, ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION.

1. The authorized next Central later-instrument dependency after PCA Extension 08 was the Representation of the People Act, 1951 / Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 class.
2. Primary and official parliamentary evidence expands the identified post-2018 amendment sequence to nine instruments, including S.O.1023(E), 26-02-2019 and S.O.3667(E), 19-10-2020, which were not represented by the India Code Rules-row dates alone.
3. Official Gazette evidence confirms S.O.3778(E), 23-08-2023, G.S.R.995(E), 01-03-2024 and S.O.5517(E), 20-12-2024.
4. Official parliamentary records confirm laying for the identified 2020, 2022, 2023 and 2024 instruments reviewed in this extension.
5. Targeted official Gazette and parliamentary searches for 2025 and 2026 did not retrieve a qualifying Conduct of Elections Rules amendment instrument. This is retrieval silence, not proof of legal absence.
6. The India Code Rules table is treated as a registry/discovery source and not as an exhaustive later-instrument register.
7. RPA-related election-result notifications and other instruments are excluded from the Rules amendment sequence where they do not amend the Conduct of Elections Rules.
