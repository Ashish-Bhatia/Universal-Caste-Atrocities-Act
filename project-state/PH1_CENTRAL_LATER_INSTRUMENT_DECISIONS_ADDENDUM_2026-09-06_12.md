# Phase 1 Central Later-Instrument Decisions Addendum 12

Date: 2026-09-06

## Decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-030

The next authorized Central later-instrument workstream after Forest Rights is the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 and Central Rules/instrument class. The workstream is advanced with qualification after verification of Act 34 of 2019, G.S.R. 644(E), S.O. 1838(E), and current parliamentary legislative-activity records.

## Classification control

Act 34 of 2019, Schedule V item 87 is the controlling post-2018 Central amendment record for PWDVA territorial extent.

G.S.R. 644(E), 17-10-2006 remains the identified Central Rules instrument and qualified Rules endpoint.

S.O. 1838(E), 26-10-2007 is classified as a Central Article 239(1) administrative delegation instrument, not an amendment to the Act or Rules.

## Parliamentary activity control

The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence (Amendment) Bill, 2025, Bill No. 90 of 2025, and the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence (Amendment) Bill, 2024 introduced in Rajya Sabha on 07-02-2025 are legislative-history records only. They are not enacted amendments and must not be incorporated into the current-law baseline.

## Registry control

India Code and the MWCD legislation index remain discovery/current-publication sources and are not treated as exhaustive Gazette registers. No later Rules amendment retrieved in the defined search is recorded as a legal-absence conclusion.

## Acceptance effect

This disposition advances PH1-ISSUE-008 but does not close broader Central later-instrument completeness or Phase 1 acceptance.

## Next sequence
