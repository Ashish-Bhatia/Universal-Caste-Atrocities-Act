## 4. Juvenile Justice Model Rules, 2016, later-instrument class

The Government of India Savitribai Phule National Institute of Women and Child Development legislation index was checked on 06-09-2026. The current legislation page was last updated on 04-09-2026 and lists the `Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Model Amendment Rules, 2022` as the current Central Model Rules amendment record for the JJ framework. It separately lists the 2021 Amendment Act and Adoption Rules/Regulations.

The Ministry-hosted Gazette text confirms G.S.R. 678(E), dated 01-09-2022, as the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Model Amendment Rules, 2022, made under the proviso to section 110(1) of the JJ Act. The instrument amends the 2016 Model Rules.

Targeted official-source searches did not identify a later Central amendment instrument to the JJ Model Rules after G.S.R. 678(E). This is a qualified search result only. It does not establish legal absence.

Important classification control: State-specific JJ Rules and State implementation notifications are not counted as amendments to the Central Model Rules. Delhi Gazette notifications using the Model Rules as an operative reference are likewise implementation instruments, not Central Model Rules amendments.
