# Phase 1 PCA Rules Primary-Source Advance

Date: 2026-09-06
Issue: PH1-ISSUE-008
Status: OPEN, ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION
Evidence grade: A for underlying official sources

## Finding

The Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 section 29A expressly authorises the Central Government to make rules by Official Gazette notification. The provision also contemplates rules concerning section 9 commercial-organisation guidelines, section 19 prosecution-sanction guidelines and other prescribed matters, with parliamentary laying requirements.

A targeted primary-source pass of DoPT, Legislative Department and official eGazette material did not retrieve a qualifying Central instrument expressly made under section 29A. The same pass did retrieve official DoPT evidence of administrative prosecution-sanction guidance, including O.M. No. 372/19/2012-AVD-III dated 03-05-2012, described by the DoPT Vigilance Handbook and annual reports as guidance rather than as a statutory rule.

## Classification

The project will not treat DoPT OMs, CVC guidance, Lokpal rules, service rules or State vigilance rules as PCA section 29A rules unless their parent-power citation establishes that status.

## Negative-search control

The absence of a qualifying instrument in the searched official indexes is an indexed/retrieval result only. It is not proof that no section 29A instrument exists. PH1-ISSUE-008 therefore remains OPEN.

## Next step

Perform a parliamentary-laying and archival notification-number search for section 29A rules, with special attention to section 9 commercial-organisation guidelines and section 19 prosecution-sanction guidelines. If an instrument is found, verify its Gazette text, parent power, commencement, amendments/repeal and laying history.
