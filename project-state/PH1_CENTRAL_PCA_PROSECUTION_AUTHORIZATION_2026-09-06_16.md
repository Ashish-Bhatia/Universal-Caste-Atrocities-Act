# Phase 1 Central PCA Prosecution-Machinery Later-Instrument Authorization 16

Date: 2026-09-06
Phase: Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Issue: PH1-ISSUE-008
Status: SUBSTANTIVE RESEARCH AUTHORIZED, NOT YET PERFORMED

## Authorization decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-036

A separate controlled Phase 1 workstream, Extension 16, is authorized to examine post-2018 Central Government instruments materially connected to the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 prosecution machinery under sections 17A and 19, including Central prior-approval and prosecution-sanction arrangements, but excluding the completed PCA section 29A Rules work, the completed section 3 Special Judge census, and the Extension 14 matters.

This authorization is a research permission only. It does not imply that any qualifying instrument exists, that an identified administrative instruction has statutory force, or that the resulting search will establish completeness.

## Authorized scope

1. Search for Central Government instruments issued from 01-01-2019 through 06-09-2026 that expressly invoke, implement, materially operationalise, amend by valid legal authority, or otherwise directly concern PCA section 17A prior-approval machinery or section 19 prosecution-sanction machinery.
2. Include Central notifications, orders, rules, regulations, statutory instruments, formal schemes or instructions only where their legal relationship to sections 17A or 19 is identifiable from the instrument or an authoritative parent source.
3. Record Central prosecution-sanction arrangements, including competent-authority designations, delegation arrangements, approval/sanction procedures, and formal instructions, while separately classifying whether each item is statutory, delegated, executive/administrative, judicial, or merely informational.
4. Identify later instruments that amend, supersede, withdraw, replace, extend, clarify or materially alter a located Central instrument.
5. Verify each material legal proposition from the strongest available primary source, preferably Gazette/eGazette, India Code, Legislative Department, DoPT, CBI, Department of Personnel and Training, Ministry of Home Affairs, Department of Legal Affairs, or another official Government repository reproducing the instrument.
6. Distinguish instruments having direct legal effect from administrative guidance. An administrative instruction must not be described as a statutory amendment, rule or notification unless its legal basis supports that characterization.
7. Keep Central instruments separate from State/UT sanction, approval, prosecution or court arrangements. State/UT material is outside the substantive scope except as clearly labelled corroborative context.
8. Preserve the distinction between a search result, a located instrument, and proof of legal absence. Retrieval silence remains non-proof of absence.
9. Do not reopen the Extension 15 section 3 census. A section 3 instrument is outside this workstream unless a narrowly necessary cross-reference is required to identify the legal boundary of an instrument.
10. Do not reopen PCA Extensions 06-08 section 29A Rules research or Extension 14 except for narrowly necessary cross-reference to statutory structure or previously recorded provenance.

## Date boundary

Primary substantive search boundary: 01-01-2019 through 06-09-2026 inclusive.

Earlier instruments may be recorded only when necessary to establish the continuing legal status, amendment chain, delegation chain or provenance of a post-2018 instrument. Earlier instruments must remain clearly identified as historical context and must not be counted as post-2018 findings.

## Repositories and source classes

Mandatory targeted repositories/source classes:

1. Official Gazette of India / eGazette.
2. India Code.
3. Legislative Department and Ministry of Law and Justice repositories.
4. Department of Personnel and Training repositories, including formal orders, office memoranda and sanction-related material.
5. Central Bureau of Investigation official material where it reproduces or identifies competent-authority/sanction arrangements.
6. Ministry of Home Affairs official material where Central prosecution/sanction arrangements are issued or reproduced.
7. Department of Legal Affairs and other Central administrative repositories where the statutory relationship is explicit.
8. Parliament/Digital Sansad/eParlib records for instruments or questions specifically identifying the operation of sections 17A or 19.
9. Official judicial records only as corroboration of the existence or continuing operation of an arrangement, not as a substitute for an underlying statutory or executive instrument where the latter is material.

Searches must use statutory-section terms, instrument terms, authority terms, and relevant PCA/CBI terminology. Repository/index silence must be recorded as a retrieval limitation.

## Evidence controls

- Evidence grades remain A = primary authoritative source; B = strong official/credible source; C = reliable secondary source; D = weak/unverified source.
- D-grade evidence must not be the sole basis of a major conclusion.
- A source's existence is not proof of every proposition attributed to it.
- Exact statutory enabling provisions must be verified before characterising an instrument as made under section 17A, section 19, or another PCA provision.
- Do not infer legal force from an office memorandum, circular, webpage, appointment list or prosecution manual without verifying its legal status.
- Preserve the instrument's own terminology where it distinguishes sanction, approval, recommendation, delegation or administrative processing.
- Record unresolved retrieval failures and inaccessible primary documents explicitly.

## Required output

The research record must contain:

- search universe and date boundary;
- repositories and source classes searched;
- every located Central post-2018 instrument materially within scope;
- identifier, date, issuing authority and formal title where verified;
- exact statutory provision invoked or verified legal basis;

- amendment/supersession/delegation chain where identified;
- evidence grade and verification status;
- retrieval failures and limitations;
- explicit exclusions and reasons;
- whether a complete Central census was established.

A separate research ledger entry set must be created for the substantive extension. No substantive findings are entered in this authorization file.
