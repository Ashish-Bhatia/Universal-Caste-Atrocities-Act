## 1. SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989

Status: VERIFIED CURRENT TEXT, QUALIFIED FOR FINAL CENTRAL FREEZE.

Primary source: India Code consolidated Act No. 33 of 1989, PDF headed "As on the 21st September, 2025". The consolidation identifies the 2015 Amendment Act, 2018 Amendment Act and Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 as incorporated amendments.

Additional official control: India Code's Act record identifies enforcement on 30-01-1990 and lists the Act's Rules, amendments and notifications through the 2018 Rules publication. The 2023 Repealing and Amending Act lists the 2018 SC/ST Amendment Act among repealed amending Acts, consistent with standard repeal-of-amending-enactment treatment and not a substantive repeal of the amendments incorporated into the principal Act.

Targeted 2026 search outcome: no later Central Act amending the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act was located through the defined India Code, Legislative Department and Gazette-oriented searches. This is a controlled negative result for a later amending Act, not proof that no later administrative instrument exists.

Disposition: PH1-ISSUE-001 is closed with qualification. PH1-ISSUE-008 remains open for the broader later-instrument search, including subordinate instruments and non-amending Central directions.

## 2. SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Rules, 1995, Annexure-II

Status: VERIFIED PRIMARY SOURCE; PRIOR APPARENT DISCREPANCY RESOLVED.

The official Ministry of Tribal Affairs-hosted Rules PDF contains Annexure-II and identifies it as a Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment notification dated 1 June 2001, concerning guidelines for evaluation of disabilities and certification. The text expressly explains that the 2001 guidelines were issued to review the earlier Ministry of Welfare O.M. No. 4-2/83-HW-III dated 6 August 1986.

Therefore, the appearance of the 1986 instrument within the Annexure-II historical explanation does not establish a source conflict with the 2001 notification. The controlling attached Annexure-II document is the 2001 notification, while the 1986 O.M. is historical source/provenance material referred to within it.

Disposition: PH1-ISSUE-014 is closed. PH1-ISSUE-002 remains CLOSED WITH QUALIFICATION pending the broader final Gazette/source freeze for the Rules corpus.
