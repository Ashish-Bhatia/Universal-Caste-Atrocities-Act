## SC/ST Rules Gazette endpoint control

The official India Code-hosted Gazette PDF for G.S.R. 588(E), dated 27 June 2018, confirms that the Central Government amended the 1995 Rules under section 23(1) of the SC/ST Act. The instrument inserts Rule 2(ga), defining “voluntarily” by reference to IPC section 39, and makes further Schedule changes. This independently confirms the latest identified Rules amendment instrument already recorded in the project.

The official India Code-hosted 2016 Gazette material confirms G.S.R. 424(E), dated 14 April 2016, as the 2016 amendment to the 1995 Rules. The 2013 Gazette material confirms G.S.R. 725(E), dated 8 November 2013. The 2014 Gazette material confirms G.S.R. 416(E), dated 23 June 2014. These instruments corroborate the existing Rules sequence and do not introduce a later post-2018 amendment.

## PCR current-source control

India Code's current PCR Act record lists the Protection of Civil Rights Rules, 1977 as the subordinate-legislation record, dated 15 September 1977. The official India Code-hosted Rules text identifies S.O. 3006 dated 15 September 1977. The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment current archive also lists the 1977 Rules.

No later PCR Rules amendment instrument was identified in the defined official-source search. This remains controlled search evidence only and is not a legal-absence conclusion.
