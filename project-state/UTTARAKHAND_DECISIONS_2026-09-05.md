# Uttarakhand Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-UK-0001: Uttarakhand was processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after Uttar Pradesh in the controlled sequence.
- DEC-UK-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Uttarakhand-specific jurisdiction inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. Generic/master files were not treated as jurisdiction-specific artifacts.
- DEC-UK-0003: Uttarakhand is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-UK-0004: The 2022 MoSJ&E Section 21(4) Uttarakhand report is treated as the principal detailed institutional snapshot.
- DEC-UK-0005: The 2022 report's Director, Social Welfare Rule 9 designation is accepted as established for the reporting period. The current named officer and appointment instrument remain open.
- DEC-UK-0006: The district Special Inquiry Cell and DySP investigation arrangement is accepted as documented for the reporting period. Current Rule 8 State Protection Cell staffing and district operational establishment remain open.
- DEC-UK-0007: Uttarakhand is not treated as currently lacking Special Police Stations solely because no current dedicated-station census was located.
- DEC-UK-0008: Zero atrocity-prone districts in the 2022 report is retained as a reporting-period finding only. No current 2026 absence conclusion is made.
- DEC-UK-0009: The 2022 V&MC figures are retained as reporting-period data. Current membership and meeting compliance remain open.
- DEC-UK-0010: The historical two Exclusive Special Courts at Nainital and Haridwar, with designated Special Courts elsewhere, are accepted as historical institutional evidence. Current 2026 judicial decisions corroborate continuing Special Judge arrangements but do not constitute a complete court census.
- DEC-UK-0011: Current named SPP/ESPP appointments are not accepted without direct appointment evidence. Historical NCSC and Ministry material is retained as contextual evidence only.
- DEC-UK-0012: Current Ministry relief and central-assistance figures are retained as scheme statistics and are not treated as proof of individual statutory payment compliance.
- DEC-UK-0013: The current Uttarakhand Social Welfare PoA webpage is treated as administrative corroboration, but its displayed relief amounts are expressly tied to the 2011 Rules and require reconciliation with later amendments before current-rate conclusions are drawn.
- DEC-UK-0014: Current judicial decisions from the Supreme Court and Uttarakhand High Court are used only as institutional corroboration. Phase 2 case-law research is not commenced.
- DEC-UK-0015: Current Government Orders/Gazettes, later Section 21(4) reports, contingency plan, prosecutor roster, payment workflow and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition instructions remain separate retrieval workstreams.
- DEC-UK-0016: Central later-instrument/current-law completeness and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remain separate national workstreams. No blanket numerical substitution is permitted.
- DEC-UK-0017: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis or policy-superiority/necessity analysis was undertaken.
