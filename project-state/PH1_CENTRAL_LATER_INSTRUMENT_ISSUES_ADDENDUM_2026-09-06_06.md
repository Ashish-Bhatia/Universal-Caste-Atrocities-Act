## PH1-ISSUE-008 extension

### Prevention of Corruption Rules

Status: OPEN, ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION.

1. PCA section 29A is an express Central rule-making power and therefore India Code silence cannot close the issue.
2. Targeted DoPT, Legislative Department and official eGazette searches did not retrieve a qualifying Central section 29A Rules instrument.
3. DoPT primary material establishes administrative guidance on prosecution sanction, including O.M. No. 372/19/2012-AVD-III dated 03-05-2012, but does not establish that the OM is a section 29A rule.
4. Lokpal rules, service rules, CVC guidance and State vigilance rules remain separate source classes unless their legal authority expressly connects them to section 29A.
5. The remaining closure dependency is a parliamentary-laying/archive search and, if necessary, a notification-number search for section 9 and section 19 rule-making instruments.
