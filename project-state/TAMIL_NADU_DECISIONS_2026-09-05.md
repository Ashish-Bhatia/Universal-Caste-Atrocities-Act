# Tamil Nadu Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-TN-0001: Tamil Nadu is processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after Sikkim in the repository sequence.
- DEC-TN-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Tamil Nadu-specific jurisdiction inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. The master State inventory was not modified.
- DEC-TN-0003: Tamil Nadu is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-TN-0004: The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal recent implementation snapshot for committees, Protection Cell, courts, SPPs, relief, travel, legal aid and awareness.
- DEC-TN-0005: Tamil Nadu's documented Rule 9 architecture traces to G.O.(Ms.) No.18 dated 20-01-2003, but current named-office verification remains open.
- DEC-TN-0006: The 2024 status source is treated as strong recent evidence that Tamil Nadu has no dedicated Special Police Stations and that all police stations register PoA cases; this is not treated as a 2026 instrument census.
- DEC-TN-0007: Rule 10 Special Officer architecture is recorded from the cited 2006 order and 2017 instructions; current appointments remain open.
- DEC-TN-0008: The 2022 State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC figures are retained as historical reporting-period evidence only.
- DEC-TN-0009: 2022 and 2023 State atrocity-prone village counts are retained separately from the 2020 PIB snapshot because their reporting bases differ.
- DEC-TN-0010: The 2022 report's 14 Exclusive Special Court districts and later secondary 17-district count are not merged; current primary census remains open.
- DEC-TN-0011: 2025-2026 Gazette notices establish continuing Special Court and SPP designation activity but not a complete statewide current roster.
- DEC-TN-0012: The 2022 relief, travel/maintenance, legal-aid and charge-sheet figures are reporting-period evidence and are not converted into 2026 compliance rates.
- DEC-TN-0013: 2026 Madras High Court references are retained only as institutional/jurisdictional corroboration. Phase 2 case-law research is not opened.
- DEC-TN-0014: The master State inventory and master State source ledger remain untouched.
- DEC-TN-0015: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis or policy-superiority/necessity analysis was undertaken.
