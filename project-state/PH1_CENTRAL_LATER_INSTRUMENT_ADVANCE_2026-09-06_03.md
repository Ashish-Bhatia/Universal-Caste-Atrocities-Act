## 4. Section 3(2)(v) threshold work

The controlled matrix is stored at `legislation/SCST_ACT_SECTION3_2V_BNS_THRESHOLD_MATRIX.md`.

Rule: identify the exact BNS provision/subsection first, then apply its enacted punishment. Numerical IPC-to-BNS substitution is not permitted.

The material threshold-control classes are:

1. IPC 120B -> BNS s.61(2)(a), conditional because punishment tracks the abetment of the conspired offence when the statutory serious-offence condition is met.
2. IPC 332 -> BNS s.121(1), maximum five years, below the threshold. BNS s.121(2) is the separate grievous-hurt limb corresponding to former IPC 333 and is separately threshold-positive where its own elements are proved. It must not be substituted for IPC 332 merely because BNS consolidates the provisions.
3. IPC 376C -> BNS s.68, five to ten years plus fine, so the maximum reaches the ten-year threshold. The changed authority/fiduciary/public-servant/custody/hospital structure remains controlled.

The matrix also records the remaining Schedule correspondences as threshold-negative, definition-only or fact-dependent.
