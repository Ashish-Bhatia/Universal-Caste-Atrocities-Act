# Telangana Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-TG-0001: Telangana is processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after Tamil Nadu in the repository master sequence.
- DEC-TG-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Telangana-specific jurisdiction inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. The master State inventory was not modified.
- DEC-TG-0003: Telangana is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-TG-0004: The 2020 Ministry Section 21(4) Telangana section is treated as the principal directly retrievable detailed institutional snapshot for the first-pass inventory.
- DEC-TG-0005: Rule 9 architecture is recorded from the Ministry report as the Commissioner, Scheduled Castes Development Department serving as Nodal Officer. Current named-office verification remains open.
- DEC-TG-0006: Rule 8 architecture is recorded from the Ministry report as a State SC/ST Protection Cell under IG CID and ADGP CID supervision, with police-unit and regional-CID structure. Current staffing remains open.
- DEC-TG-0007: No conclusion of absence of dedicated Special Police Stations is drawn because the located evidence does not provide a sufficiently current explicit State census.
- DEC-TG-0008: Rule 10 architecture is recorded as Joint Collectors serving as Special Officers in districts with atrocity-prone areas. Current orders remain open.
- DEC-TG-0009: The 66 identified atrocity-prone villages are retained as an official historical snapshot and are not treated as a 2026 census.
- DEC-TG-0010: V&MC figures from 2020 and 2022 are kept as separate reporting periods; 2022 figures remain C grade pending direct Ministry file verification.
- DEC-TG-0011: Ten Special Sessions Courts and related prosecutor arrangements are recorded from departmental material; the 2022 secondary 10-district Exclusive Special Court count is not treated as a final current census.
- DEC-TG-0012: The State monetary relief/legal-aid scheme is treated as an established administrative layer, with current district-level corroboration from Warangal and current central scheme data.
- DEC-TG-0013: The 2024 Rachakonda Police report is used as post-BNS operational corroboration only. It does not resolve the national BNS/BNSS/BSA transition workstream.
- DEC-TG-0014: Current court/prosecutor rosters, payment workflow, V&MC compliance, Rule 8/9/10 current instruments and later annual reports remain open.
- DEC-TG-0015: The master State inventory and master State source ledger remain untouched.
- DEC-TG-0016: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was undertaken.
