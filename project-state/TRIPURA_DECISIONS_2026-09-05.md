# Tripura Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-TR-0001: Tripura is processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after Telangana in the controlled repository sequence.
- DEC-TR-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Tripura-specific jurisdiction inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. Existing generic/master files were not treated as Tripura-specific artifacts and were not modified.
- DEC-TR-0003: Tripura is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-TR-0004: The 2022 Ministry Section 21(4) Tripura report is treated as the principal detailed institutional snapshot.
- DEC-TR-0005: Historical Rule 9 Nodal Officer appointment is accepted as established for the relevant reporting periods, but current named officer and instrument remain open.
- DEC-TR-0006: The 2022 Ministry narrative saying an SC/ST Protection Cell was constituted is not treated as conclusive because the certified state annual-report annexure contemporaneously records No to the Rule 8 setup question.
- DEC-TR-0007: The Rule 8 contradiction is preserved as a material evidence conflict and assigned a Critical open issue. No inference is made from search silence.
- DEC-TR-0008: The certified 2022 state annexure is used to record the historical absence of Special Police Stations and the stated DSP-or-above investigation arrangement, with current status left open.
- DEC-TR-0009: The 2022 statement that Tripura had no specific atrocity-prone area is retained as a historical reporting snapshot, not a 2026 absence finding.
- DEC-TR-0010: The 2022 Special Court designations and eight SPPs are accepted as historical institutional evidence; current court/prosecutor rosters remain open.
- DEC-TR-0011: The 2022 relief and legal-aid figures are retained as reporting-period statistics and not treated as proof of statutory payment compliance.
- DEC-TR-0012: Current Government of Tripura directories and 2025-26 budget material establish continuing SC Welfare/PCR/PoA administrative and budgetary layers, but not current Rule 9/8/10 instruments.
- DEC-TR-0013: The 2025 Tripura High Court PoA-related order is used only for current judicial/institutional corroboration. Phase 2 case-law research is not commenced.
- DEC-TR-0014: Current eCourts district listings are treated as judiciary infrastructure corroboration only, not as proof of PoA-specific Special Court designation.
- DEC-TR-0015: Central later-instrument/current-law completeness and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remain separate national workstreams.
- DEC-TR-0016: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis or policy-superiority/necessity analysis was undertaken.
