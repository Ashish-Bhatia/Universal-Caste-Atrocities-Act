# Punjab Phase 1 Decisions

Date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-PB-0001: Punjab is treated as the next jurisdictional Phase 1 inventory after Odisha. Completed State research is not reopened.
- DEC-PB-0002: Punjab is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS.
- DEC-PB-0003: The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) PoA report is the principal Punjab implementation snapshot for this pass, supplemented by Punjab Government, Punjab Police and Punjab & Haryana High Court material.
- DEC-PB-0004: Historical 2017 and 2020 Punjab reports are retained to show institutional continuity and change, but historical counts are not substituted for 2026 status.
- DEC-PB-0005: The 2022 statement that Punjab had no atrocity-prone area is treated as reporting-period evidence only. It is not a 2026 finding without a current survey/notification.
- DEC-PB-0006: The 2022 figures of 17 charge-sheets within 60 days and 28 later than 60 days are retained as historical reporting-period evidence.
- DEC-PB-0007: The 2022 figures of 27 Special Courts, 2 Exclusive Special Courts, 26 SPPs and 2 ESPPs are retained with explicit date/currentness limitations.
- DEC-PB-0008: The 2022 relief figures of 0 within seven days and 9 later than seven days are not combined with the separate figure of 26 victims receiving relief because the reporting fields are not established as equivalent.
- DEC-PB-0009: The 2022 Punjab ST statistical row is treated as a data-integrity residual because the searchable table presents internally inconsistent fields. No substantive ST conclusion is based on that row.
- DEC-PB-0010: Punjab State Commission for Scheduled Castes Act, 2004 and other State SC-related instruments are recorded for later crosswalk work only. No conflict, duplication or superiority conclusion is made in Phase 1.
- DEC-PB-0011: Search silence is not treated as evidence of absence, especially for dedicated police stations, current appointments and current court/prosecutor notifications.
- DEC-PB-0012: Punjab residuals are recorded in PH1-ISSUE-PB-001 through PH1-ISSUE-PB-016.
- DEC-PB-0013: No Bill drafting, policy-superiority conclusion, constitutional-validity conclusion or Phase 2 case-law corpus is initiated by this inventory.
