## 1. Forest Rights Rules, 2007, as amended

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs current Acts-and-Rules knowledge hub lists the Forest Rights Act, 2006 together with the Rules, including the 2012 Amendment, as the current Central rules publication. The Ministry's current FRA page also identifies the Rules/Guidelines resource and continues to host the consolidated Rules booklet. The Government-hosted booklet expressly records G.S.R. 669(E), dated 06-09-2012, as the Forest Rights Amendment Rules, 2012 and footnotes multiple operative insertions/substitutions to the Rules. The Ministry's earlier official PIB material likewise identifies the principal Rules as notified on 01-01-2008 and the Amendment Rules on 06-09-2012.

Disposition: current official publication endpoint advanced to a controlled 2012 amended Rules text. No later Central Rules amendment was identified in this targeted official-source pass. This remains a qualified endpoint, not a legal-absence conclusion.

## 2. Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Rules, 2006

The Ministry of Women and Child Development's current Government-hosted Gazette reproduction contains G.S.R. 644(E), dated 17-10-2006, the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Rules, 2006. India Code's current Act record lists the 2006 Rules and a single 17-10-2006 notification. No later Central amendment Rules instrument was identified in the targeted official-source pass.

Disposition: Central Rules endpoint advanced to the 2006 principal Rules instrument. The absence of a later identified instrument remains qualified.

## 3. POCSO Rules, 2020

The current Savitribai Phule National Institute of Women and Child Development legislation index, last updated 04-09-2026, lists the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Rules, 2020. The identified primary instrument is G.S.R. 165(E), dated 09-03-2020. The Rules expressly repeal the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Rules, 2012, subject to the savings stated in rule 13. The 2020 Rules therefore constitute the controlled Central Rules endpoint for this class.

The Ministry-hosted legislation index is the current official registry evidence. The Gazette text is independently corroborated by an official judicial publication and the identified Gazette instrument number/date. No later Central POCSO Rules amendment was identified in the targeted official-source pass. This remains qualified.

## 4. Child and Adolescent Labour Rules, 1988

India Code's current Child and Adolescent Labour Act record lists the principal Rules dated 01-01-1988 and the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Amendment Rules, 2017 dated 02-06-2017. The Ministry of Labour and Employment Government-hosted Gazette PDF contains G.S.R. 543(E), dated 02-06-2017, and records that the draft was published as G.S.R. 392(E), dated 20-04-2017. The final instrument amends the 1988 Rules and renamed them as the Child and Adolescent Labour Rules framework.

Disposition: the Central Rules sequence is advanced to G.S.R. 543(E), 02-06-2017. No later Central amendment Rules instrument was identified in this targeted official-source pass. This is not a legal-absence conclusion.

## 5. Prevention of Corruption Rules class

India Code's current Prevention of Corruption Act record, maintained for the Central Act, displays no Central Rules entry in its Rules table, while the Act's section 29A expressly confers Central rule-making power. This creates a source-classification residual rather than a negative legal conclusion. The current record also identifies the 2018 Amendment Act commencement notification. No Central Rules instrument was established in this pass from a primary source.

Disposition: do not close the Prevention of Corruption Rules class on India Code silence. Retain it as an unresolved PH1-ISSUE-008 source-classification/search residual requiring a targeted Legislative Department/DoPT/eGazette pass.
