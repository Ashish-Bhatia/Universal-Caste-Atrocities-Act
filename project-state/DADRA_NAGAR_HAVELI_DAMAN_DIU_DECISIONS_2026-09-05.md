# Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu Phase 1 Decisions

- DEC-DD-0001: Treat the merged UT, effective 26-01-2020, as the jurisdiction baseline while retaining legacy territorial distinctions where source instruments/data require them.
- DEC-DD-0002: Classify the jurisdiction PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS because core PoA machinery is documented but current instrument-level verification is incomplete.

- DEC-DD-0004: Do not infer current absence of Special Police Stations, atrocity-prone areas or committees from search silence or old reporting.
- DEC-DD-0005: Treat Dy.SP/SDPO investigation as verified for the 2022 reporting period; current roster remains open.
- DEC-DD-0006: Treat the 2022 statement that one Public Prosecutor was designated SPP as historical/current-to-that-reporting-period evidence only.
- DEC-DD-0007: Treat the 2022 Special Court statement as historical evidence requiring post-merger current designation verification.
- DEC-DD-0008: Keep PCR evidence separate from PoA evidence.
- DEC-DD-0009: Treat PFMS releases as central assistance evidence, not case-level victim-relief evidence.
- DEC-DD-0010: Use the 2023 Bombay High Court proceedings only as recent judicial corroboration; do not conduct Phase 2 merits synthesis.
- DEC-DD-0011: Preserve the controlled post-merger SC/ST Orders and local-instrument reconciliation residual.
- DEC-DD-0012: No Bill drafting, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or constitutional-validity analysis undertaken.
