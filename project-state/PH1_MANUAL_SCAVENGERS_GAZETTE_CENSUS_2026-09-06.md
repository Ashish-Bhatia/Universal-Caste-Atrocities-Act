# Phase 1 Manual Scavengers Gazette and Section 39 Instrument Census

Date: 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Final targeted primary-source census for the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013, the Central 2013 Rules, Central instruments under section 29, and Central section 39 exemption instruments.

## Census result

The census corrected the earlier India Code-only instrument boundary. India Code currently displays the Act, 2013 Rules and Central notification chain through S.O. 888(E), 01-03-2018, but Gazette records establish additional section 29 Central Monitoring Committee amendments in 2023-2025.

- Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Rules, 2013, dated 12-12-2013.
- S.O. 89(E), 13-01-2014, Central Monitoring Committee.
- S.O. 605(E), 28-02-2014, CMC amendment.
- S.O. 2008(E), 07-08-2014, CMC amendment.

- S.O. 4379(E), 06-10-2023, CMC amendment, published 09-10-2023.
- S.O. 1510(E), 21-03-2024, CMC member removal.
- S.O. 93(E), 03-01-2025, CMC State/UT entry amendment.
- S.O. 1352(E), 21-03-2025, CMC Member of Parliament replacements.
- S.O. 3793(E), 13-08-2025, CMC nominee replacement.

The Gazette records expressly identify the 2023-2025 instruments as amendments to the S.O. 89(E) CMC notification under section 29. They do not amend the 2013 Rules and do not operate under section 39.

## Rules census

No Central amendment to the 12-12-2013 Rules was identified in the defined Gazette and Legislative Department searches. The 14-11-2017 Central Monitoring Committee proposal to amend/simplify the Rules is retained as proposal evidence only.

## Section 39 census

No Central section 39 exemption order was identified in the defined Gazette, Legislative Department, India Code, Department of Social Justice and Empowerment or National Commission for Safai Karamcharis search set.

This is a controlled negative result. It is not a legal-absence conclusion. Section 39 itself requires publication of a general or special exemption order in the Official Gazette and limits an exemption to six months at a time.

The 2014 Railway notifications G.S.R. 376(E) and G.S.R. 726(E) are excluded from the section 39 set. The parliamentary record describes their statutory basis as the Act's rule-making framework, and the Supreme Court recorded their withdrawal in 2023.
