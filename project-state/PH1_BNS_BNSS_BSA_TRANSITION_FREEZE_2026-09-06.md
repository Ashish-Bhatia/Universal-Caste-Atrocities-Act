Date: 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Freeze the primary transition framework for the existing-law baseline without reopening completed crosswalks or beginning Phase 2 case-law research.

## Primary transition rule

Legislative Department Notification S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, issued under section 8 of the General Clauses Act, provides that references in Acts, State Acts, Ordinances, Article 240 regulations, Presidential orders, and rules/regulations/orders/notifications made under them to the IPC, CrPC or Indian Evidence Act are to be read respectively as references to BNS, BNSS or BSA and corresponding provisions are to be construed accordingly.

This is a construction-of-references rule. It does not justify blanket numerical substitution or erase special-law provisions.

## General current-law framework

- BNS, Act No. 45 of 2023, is the current general substantive criminal law from 01-07-2024, subject to its commencement notification and statutory exceptions.
- BNSS, Act No. 46 of 2023, is the current general criminal procedure law from 01-07-2024, subject to its savings and transition provisions.
- BSA, Act No. 47 of 2023, is the current general evidence law from 01-07-2024.
- BNSS sections 3-5 and section 531 are controlling for construction, special-law preservation and pending-proceeding transition where procedure is concerned.
- Special statutes remain special statutes. A corresponding BNS/BNSS/BSA provision does not displace a special statutory command merely because the subject matter is similar.

## Freeze rules

1. Do not replace legacy statutory numbers by arithmetic substitution.
2. Determine whether the referenced provision is a construction-of-reference matter, a special-law provision, or a provision requiring a substantive correspondence analysis.
3. Preserve special jurisdiction, special procedure, special offence definitions, special presumptions and special rights where the special statute controls.
4. For procedural matters, distinguish pending proceedings saved by BNSS section 531 from proceedings commenced after 01-07-2024.
5. Record unresolved State-specific empowerment or notification questions separately from the Central transition rule.
6. Do not treat current judicial interpretation as frozen in Phase 1 where the repository deliberately reserves case-law analysis for Phase 2.
