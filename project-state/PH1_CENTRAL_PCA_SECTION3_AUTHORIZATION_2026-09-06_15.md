# Phase 1 Central PCA Section 3 Special Judge Census Authorization 15

Date: 2026-09-06
Phase: Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Issue: PH1-ISSUE-008
Status: SUBSTANTIVE RESEARCH AUTHORIZED, NOT YET PERFORMED

## Authorization decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-034

A separate controlled Phase 1 workstream is authorized to perform a Central-only census of notifications under section 3 of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 appointing Special Judges, limited to post-2018 instruments and their relevant continuing/current effect.

This authorization exists because Extension 14 expressly did not establish a complete Central section 3 notification register, and the cumulative acceptance audit identifies Central later-instrument completeness as an unmet Phase 1 dependency. The authorization does not imply that any particular notification exists or that the census will establish completeness.

## Authorized scope

1. Identify Central Government notifications issued under PCA section 3 appointing Special Judges for specified areas or cases from 01-01-2019 through 06-09-2026.
2. Identify later Central instruments that amend, supersede, extend, withdraw, transfer or otherwise alter the legal effect of an identified Central section 3 appointment.
3. Verify each located instrument from the strongest available primary source, preferably Gazette/eGazette, India Code, Legislative Department or another official Government repository reproducing the instrument.
4. Record, for each material instrument: identifier, formal title, notification number, date, issuing authority, statutory enabling provision, appointed court/judge/designation, territorial or subject-matter scope, commencement/effective status, amendment/supersession status, source, evidence grade and verification status.
5. Distinguish Central Government section 3 notifications from State Government notifications. State instruments are outside this workstream and must not be incorporated into a Central census except as clearly labelled corroborative leads.
6. Where a complete Central register cannot be established, report the result as a qualified retrieval endpoint and preserve retrieval silence as non-proof of legal absence.

## Mandatory source and evidence controls

- Prefer primary authoritative sources.
- Preserve evidence grades A-D.
- Do not treat search-engine silence, India Code index silence, Ministry-page silence or an incomplete repository as proof that a notification does not exist.
- Do not infer a notification number, title, enabling provision, court, judge, territory or operative effect from sequence, secondary reporting or a related parent instrument.
- Do not treat appointment lists or current court webpages as substitutes for notification text where the notification itself is material and available.
- Preserve historical instruments separately from current-status conclusions.
- Record unresolved retrieval failures explicitly.

## Mandatory exclusions

Do not repeat:

- PCA Extensions 06-08 section 29A Rules research.
- PCA Extension 14's completed Act 34 of 2019 and S.O. 1123(E) analysis except where needed as contextual background.
- The 31-05-2025 eGazette PDF retrieval assessment already completed under Extension 14.
- State/UT Special Judge research as a substantive workstream.
- POCSO Extension 13, PWDVA Extension 12, Forest Rights Extension 11, Child and Adolescent Labour Extension 10 or RPA Extension 09.
- State/UT inventories.
- BNS/BNSS/BSA controlled freeze.
- Website verification.
- Bill drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research.

## Controlled output requirements

The resulting research record must state:

- the search universe and date boundary;
- official repositories searched;
- every located Central section 3 notification materially within scope;
- any amendment/supersession chain identified;
- gaps and retrieval failures;
- evidence grade for each material proposition;
- whether a complete Central census was established.

PH1-ISSUE-008 remains OPEN during this workstream.
Phase 1 acceptance remains NOT YET SATISFIED unless separately closed through the cumulative acceptance controls.

## Authorization effect

This record authorizes substantive research under the scope above. It contains no substantive section 3 census findings. The research must begin from the verified Extension 14 endpoint and must not repeat completed work.
