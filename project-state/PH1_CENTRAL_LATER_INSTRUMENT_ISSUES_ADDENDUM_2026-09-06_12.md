## PH1-ISSUE-008, Protection of Women from Domestic Violence correction and extension

Status: OPEN, ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION.

1. The authorized next Central later-instrument dependency after Forest Rights is the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 and Central Rules/instrument class.
2. Act 34 of 2019, Schedule V item 87, is verified as a post-2018 Central amendment removing the former Jammu and Kashmir exclusion from PWDVA section 1(2), effective 31-10-2019.
3. G.S.R. 644(E), 17-10-2006 remains the identified Central Rules instrument and qualified Rules endpoint.
4. S.O. 1838(E), 26-10-2007 is verified as an Article 239(1) delegation instrument for UT Administrators. It is not a Rules amendment.
5. Targeted official-source searches did not retrieve a later Central PWDVA Rules amendment. This remains retrieval silence, not legal absence.
6. Bill No. 90 of 2025 and the 2024 PWDVA Amendment Bill introduced in 2025 are retained as parliamentary legislative activity only. Neither is treated as enacted law.
7. PH1-ISSUE-008 remains open because broader Central later-instrument completeness remains unresolved.
