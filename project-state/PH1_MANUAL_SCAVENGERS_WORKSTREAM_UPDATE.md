- A 2022 amendment Bill was located but is not enacted law and is excluded from the current-law baseline.

## Key crosswalk findings

- MS Act ss.5-7 overlap with SC/ST Act s.3(1)(h)-(j) in defined circumstances, but the MS Act is sanitation-specific and does not depend on SC/ST status of offender or affected person.
- MS Act rehabilitation is identification/status based; SC/ST Act relief and rehabilitation are atrocity-incident based.
- MS Act ss.20-23 establish distinct inspector, trial and corporate-liability architecture.
- MS Act s.22 makes MS Act offences cognizable and non-bailable. This is not identical to the SC/ST Act's anticipatory-bail exclusion and Special Court regime.
- MS Act ss.24-30 and Rules 10-14 create substantial administrative overlap with SC/ST monitoring/reporting machinery, but the monitored subject matter differs.
- MS Rules 3-8 create a detailed technical occupational-safety and emergency-response regime with no direct SC/ST Rules equivalent.
- MS Rule 13 permits inspection evidence collection, including photographs, video, samples, records and electronic-device material, subject to the rule. This is a later evidence-provenance interface, not a special SC/ST presumption.
