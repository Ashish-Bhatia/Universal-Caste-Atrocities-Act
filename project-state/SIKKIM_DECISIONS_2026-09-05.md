# Sikkim Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-SK-0001: Sikkim is processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after Rajasthan in the repository sequence.
- DEC-SK-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Sikkim-specific legislation inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum before research began. The only Sikkim search hit was the master State inventory, which was not modified.
- DEC-SK-0003: Sikkim is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-SK-0004: The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal recent implementation snapshot for committees, Protection Cell, identified areas, Special Courts, SPPs and charge-sheet timing.
- DEC-SK-0005: The 2022 report records no State-level V&MC meeting, five District V&MC meetings across six districts, a Protection Cell under SSP/CID, no identified atrocity-prone area, District and Session Courts as Special Courts, eight SPPs, one charge-sheet within 60 days and none after 60 days.
- DEC-SK-0006: Historical Rule 10 Special Officer appointments for four districts are retained as historical evidence only and are not treated as current 2026 appointments.
- DEC-SK-0007: The current State Acts and Rules page is treated as primary evidence of a State publication layer, but individual linked instruments are not treated as fully verified where the underlying file could not be directly retrieved.
- DEC-SK-0008: The current departmental generic nodal-officer entry is not treated as proof of a Rule 9 PoA Nodal Officer appointment.
- DEC-SK-0009: Sikkim Protection Cell evidence is treated as established at the institutional level, while current staffing and any dedicated Special Police Station remain open.
- DEC-SK-0010: Special Court evidence is recorded from the 2022 report and State publication index, with four district Sessions locations corroborated by official government reporting. A current 2026 PoA court census remains open.
- DEC-SK-0011: Eight SPPs recorded in the 2022 report are retained as historical implementation evidence. The current named SPP/ESPP roster remains open.
- DEC-SK-0012: FY 2021-22 and FY 2022-23 relief tables recording zero relief beneficiaries are not interpreted as proof that no qualifying atrocity occurred.
- DEC-SK-0013: NCRB/parliamentary crime statistics, Section 21(4) implementation data and State administrative material remain separate reporting series.
- DEC-SK-0014: Naresh Kumar Agarwal v. State of Sikkim is recorded only as jurisdictional judicial corroboration. No Phase 2 case-law corpus or merits synthesis is opened.
- DEC-SK-0015: Master State inventory and master State source ledger are intentionally left untouched.
- DEC-SK-0016: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research is undertaken in this workstream.
