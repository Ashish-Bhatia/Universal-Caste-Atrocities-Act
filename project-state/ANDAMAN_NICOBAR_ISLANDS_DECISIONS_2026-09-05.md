# Andaman and Nicobar Islands Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-AN-0001: Andaman and Nicobar Islands was processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after West Bengal in the controlled repository sequence.
- DEC-AN-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Andaman and Nicobar-specific inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum.
- DEC-AN-0003: The jurisdiction is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-AN-0004: The principal beneficiary focus in the available evidence is Scheduled Tribes. The Ministry's current published SC list records zero notified Scheduled Castes for the UT.
- DEC-AN-0005: The 2022 MoSJ&E Section 21(4) report is treated as the principal recent institutional snapshot located.
- DEC-AN-0006: The 2022 Protection Cell architecture is accepted as documented for the reporting period, but current Rule 8 staffing and command remain open.
- DEC-AN-0007: No current Rule 9 Nodal Officer instrument was independently retrieved. Historical Ministry lists are not treated as proof of present appointment or absence.
- DEC-AN-0008: Search silence for dedicated Special Police Stations is not treated as proof of absence.
- DEC-AN-0009: Kadamtala, Rangat and Baratang in North & Middle Andaman are retained as 2022 identified atrocity-prone areas. No current 2026 status conclusion is made.
- DEC-AN-0010: State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC structures are documented for the reporting period, with current membership and compliance remaining open.
- DEC-AN-0011: The District and Session Judge, Andaman and Nicobar Islands is treated as the documented PoA Special Court. Current judicial decisions corroborate continuing operation.
- DEC-AN-0012: Historical and 2022 material supports a Special Public Prosecutor arrangement, but the current named roster and appointment instruments remain open.
- DEC-AN-0013: Current relief/payment statistics and workflow were not sufficiently extracted to support a current compliance conclusion.
- DEC-AN-0014: The Andaman and Nicobar Islands Protection of Aboriginal Tribes Regulation, 1956 is recorded as a material related-law interface because official and judicial sources document enforcement alongside the PoA Act.
- DEC-AN-0015: The 2025 and 2026 judicial sources are used only for institutional corroboration. Phase 2 case-law research was not commenced.
- DEC-AN-0016: BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification and Central later-instrument/current-law completeness remain separate national workstreams. No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis or policy-superiority/necessity analysis was undertaken.
