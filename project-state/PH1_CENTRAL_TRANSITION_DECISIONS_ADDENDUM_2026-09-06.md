# Phase 1 Central Transition Decisions Addendum

Date: 2026-09-06

This addendum extends the existing `DECISIONS_LOG.md` without renumbering prior decisions.

## Decisions

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-001

The current Ministry of Women and Child Development legislation index is accepted as primary/current publication-index evidence for the POCSO 2019 Amendment Act, POCSO Rules 2020, JJ Amendment Act 2021 and JJ Model Amendment Rules 2022. Underlying Gazette instruments are preferred where available and have been located for the Rules instruments.

Reason: India Code's current subordinate-record displays do not always expose the latest Central instrument, so current Ministry publication indexes and Gazette instruments must be reconciled rather than treated as interchangeable.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-002

The identified RPwD, RPA, FRA, POCSO, JJ, PCA, PWDVA and Child and Adolescent Labour instrument sequences are treated as controlled later-instrument advances, not exhaustive 2026 Gazette censuses.

Reason: project methodology prohibits converting registry/search silence into legal absence.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-003

BNS section 116's 15-day grievous-hurt threshold and BNS section 115's current simple-hurt punishment are material correspondence differences for SC/ST Act Schedule analysis. BNS section 146 is a general unlawful-compulsory-labour provision and is not treated as an equivalent to SC/ST Act section 3(1)(h). BNS section 358 governs repeal and savings for the IPC.

Reason: the SC/ST Act imports underlying-code offences and punishment thresholds, so correspondence must preserve substantive differences.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-004

BNSS sections 3-5, 173-193, 283-289 and 531 are retained as primary structural controls for the SC/ST Act procedural transition. Rule 7 consequences remain unresolved and are not closed by structural section-number correspondence alone.

Reason: special-law procedure and the Rule 7 investigation framework require subject-matter correspondence rather than arithmetic renumbering.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-005

BSA sections 104-109 and 115 are treated as general evidence/burden/presumption architecture only. They do not establish an equivalent to SC/ST Act section 8. Judicial operation remains outside this Phase 1 advance.

Reason: the project reserves judicial-operation analysis for Phase 2/4 and avoids premature equivalence between special and general statutory presumptions.
