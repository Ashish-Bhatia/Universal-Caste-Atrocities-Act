## Decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-029

The next authorized Central later-instrument workstream after Child and Adolescent Labour is the Forest Rights Act, 2006 and Central Rules/instrument class. The workstream is advanced with qualification after verification of Act 34 of 2019, S.O. 2631(E), and the controlled 2012 Rules endpoint.

## Correction decision

Act 34 of 2019, section 95 and the Fifth Schedule is now the controlling post-2018 Central amendment record for FRA territorial extent. The former Extension 05 proposition that no post-2018 Central amendment Act had been located is withdrawn.

## Classification control

S.O. 2631(E), 07-06-2022 is classified as a Central constitutional/administrative delegation instrument under Article 239(1), not as an amendment to the Act or Central Rules.

G.S.R. 669(E), 06-09-2012 remains the identified Central Rules amendment endpoint. The absence of a later retrieved Rules instrument remains qualified retrieval silence.

## Registry control

India Code remains a discovery/current-text source and is not treated as an exhaustive Gazette register. Primary Gazette and statutory evidence controls the later-instrument classification.

## Acceptance effect

This disposition advances and corrects the PH1-ISSUE-008 evidence record but does not close broader Central later-instrument completeness or Phase 1 acceptance.
