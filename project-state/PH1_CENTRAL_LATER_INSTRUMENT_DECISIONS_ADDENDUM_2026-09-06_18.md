A new discrete Central later-instrument workstream, Extension 16, is authorized for post-2018 Central Government instruments materially connected to Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 sections 17A and 19 prosecution machinery, subject to the dedicated authorization record.

- State/UT prosecution-sanction, prior-approval and related arrangements are outside substantive scope.

- Primary-source verification is required for material legal propositions.
- Retrieval silence is not legal absence.
- PH1-ISSUE-008 remains OPEN.
- Phase 1 acceptance remains NOT YET SATISFIED.
