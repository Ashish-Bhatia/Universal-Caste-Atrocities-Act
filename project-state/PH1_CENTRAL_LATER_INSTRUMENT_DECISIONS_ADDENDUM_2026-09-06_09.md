## Decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-027

## Classification control

Targeted 2025-2026 searches did not retrieve a qualifying Conduct of Elections Rules amendment instrument. This is a qualified negative-source finding only.

No State/UT research, PCA Extension 06-08 searches, BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze, section 3(2)(v) threshold work, RPwD/JJ residuals, website verification, drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was reopened.
