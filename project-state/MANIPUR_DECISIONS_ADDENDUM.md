# Manipur Phase 1 Decision Addendum

| Decision | Decision record |
|---|---|
| DEC-0107 | Manipur begins only after successful Maharashtra master-ledger integration. The integration was completed and verified before Manipur files were created. |
| DEC-0108 | Manipur current status will be separated from historical Central reporting periods. Historical Rule 9 and Special Court records are not treated as current 2026 status. |
| DEC-0109 | Current Manipur Police FIR publications are accepted as primary case-level evidence of continuing PoA invocation and post-BNS coexistence, but not as a substitute for statewide annual implementation reporting or current institutional orders. |
| DEC-0110 | The 17-01-2019 Manipur Law Department SPP order is treated as a primary historical/recent instrument. Current continuation or replacement after 2019 remains unresolved. |
| DEC-0111 | The FY 2022-23 to FY 2024-25 Parliament table showing zero Central assistance and no listed relief beneficiaries is treated as evidence about the Centrally Sponsored Scheme only. It is not treated as proof that Manipur provided no State-funded relief. |
| DEC-0112 | Current High Court listings that do not identify an SC/ST Special Court are treated as an unresolved search result, not proof of absence. Historical Central Special Court designations remain separately recorded. |
| DEC-0113 | Manipur post-BNS evidence is kept separate from the general BNS/BNSS/BSA transition workstream. Current Police FIRs establish coexistence of BNS and PoA provisions but do not establish a complete State SOP. |
| DEC-0114 | Current official searches did not locate a Manual Scavengers-specific State instrument. This is recorded as an open research issue and not as an absence conclusion. |
| DEC-0115 | Manipur land, local-government, FRA and bonded-labour material is recorded as an interface layer. It is not treated as PoA-specific implementation machinery unless the underlying instrument expressly connects the functions. |
| DEC-0116 | BPRD Data on Police Organizations 2024 is used as a dated official corroboration that Manipur had zero actual special-purpose police stations classified for crimes against SC/ST/weaker sections as on 01-01-2024. This does not establish the position on 05-09-2026. |
| DEC-0117 | The 2024-25 Standing Committee report's statement that Manipur had not established an SC/ST Protection Cell at the 21-11-2023 review is retained as dated official Central evidence. It narrows the Protection Cell residual but does not establish the current 2026 position. |
| DEC-0118 | The Ministry's 2025-26 Annual Report is treated as the current Central baseline for national PoA machinery counts: 217 Exclusive Special Courts in 15 States/UTs and 181 Special Police Stations in 7 States/UTs. The report is not used to infer a Manipur-specific absence where it does not identify the State. |
| DEC-0119 | The Standing Committee record that Manipur's 2022 PCR/PoA annual report was pending at the 21-11-2023 review is retained as a reporting-compliance lead. It does not establish that later reports were not submitted. |
