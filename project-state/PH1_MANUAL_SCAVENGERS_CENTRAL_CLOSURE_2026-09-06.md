## Rules position

No Central amendment instrument to the 12-12-2013 Rules was identified in the defined Gazette/Legislative Department search. The 14-11-2017 Central Monitoring Committee record proposing amendment/simplification of the Rules is retained as proposal evidence only. Current Department of Social Justice and Empowerment material continues to publish the 2013 Rules as the Central Rules source.

The India Code subordinate-notification display is therefore not a complete current instrument index by itself. The Gazette corpus supplies the later section 29 amendments through August 2025.

## Section 39 position

Section 39 remains an operative statutory power. The defined search identified no Central general or special exemption order under section 39 in the Gazette, Legislative Department, India Code, Department of Social Justice and Empowerment or NCSK material reviewed.

This is a controlled negative instrument-identification result. It is not a claim that no exemption order exists anywhere. Search silence is not treated as legal absence.

G.S.R. 376(E), dated 04-06-2014, and G.S.R. 726(E), dated 13-10-2014, are excluded from the section 39 category. The parliamentary record describes them as Railway notifications under the Act's rule-making framework, and the Supreme Court's 20-10-2023 judgment records their withdrawal by Railway order dated 26-04-2023.
