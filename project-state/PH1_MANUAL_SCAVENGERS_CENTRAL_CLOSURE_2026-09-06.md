# Phase 1 Manual Scavengers Central-Law Closure Addendum

Date: 2026-09-06
Phase: Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION; acceptance dependency remains OPEN

## Scope

Controlled primary-source closure work for the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013 and Rules, with focus on later Central instruments and section 39 exemption instruments. This record does not reopen completed State/UT inventories and does not close the broader Central-law or BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze.

## Verified findings

1. India Code currently lists the 2013 Act as Act No. 25 of 2013, enforced from 06-12-2013. Its subordinate-record view lists the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Rules, 2013 dated 12-12-2013 and Central notifications dated 01-10-2013, 13-01-2014, 28-02-2014, 07-08-2014, 16-02-2017, 02-05-2017 and 01-03-2018. No later Central amendment or subordinate instrument is displayed in the India Code record located in this search.

2. The Department of Social Justice and Empowerment current archive continues to publish the Act and the 2013 Rules. The National Commission for Safai Karamcharis archive also continues to identify the Act, Rules and the 2014 Ministry of Railways notifications as the principal Central source set.

3. The 14-11-2017 Central Monitoring Committee record proposed amendment of the 2013 Rules for simplification. The located current India Code and NCSK Act/Rules indexes do not identify a subsequent Central Rules amendment instrument. This is a controlled search result, not proof that no unpublished or differently indexed instrument exists.

4. The section 39 exemption search did not locate a Central general or special exemption order under section 39 in the India Code, Legislative Department, Department of Social Justice and Empowerment or NCSK material reviewed. Section 39 itself remains an operative statutory power. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

5. The Ministry of Railways notifications G.S.R. 376(E) dated 04-06-2014 and G.S.R. 726(E) dated 13-10-2014 are not treated as section 39 exemption orders. The available parliamentary record describes G.S.R. 376(E) as a notification under the Act's rule-making framework concerning protective gear/devices, and G.S.R. 726(E) as an amendment to it. The Supreme Court's 20-10-2023 judgment records that Railways withdrew the two notifications by order dated 26-04-2023.

6. The Supreme Court's judgment in Dr. Balram Singh v. Union of India, 2023 INSC 950, is used here only for this direct Phase 1 instrument-status point. The Court recorded withdrawal of the 2014 Railway notifications and noted Railways' mechanized-cleaning/model-contract measures. No Phase 2 case-law programme is opened by this addendum.
