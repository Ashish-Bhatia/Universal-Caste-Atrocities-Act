# Phase 1 Central Later-Instrument Decisions Addendum 04

Date: 2026-09-06

## Decisions

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-015

The direct eGazette endpoint for RPwD G.S.R. 175(E), dated 08-03-2024, remains technically unresolved after a fresh retrieval attempt returned HTTP 502. Independent subject-matter corroboration does not replace the requested direct endpoint retrieval.

Reason: preserve the distinction between instrument identity, corroborated operative text and direct endpoint retrieval success.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-016

RPwD G.S.R. 343(E), dated 20-06-2024, is treated as primary-text verified through an accessible Government-hosted Gazette mirror bearing registration CG-DL-E-24062024-254893, while the direct eGazette endpoint remains technically unavailable.

Reason: a Government-hosted Gazette reproduction provides primary text, but the project's direct-endpoint residual must remain explicit rather than being silently closed.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-017

The Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Model Rules later-instrument class is advanced to a qualified 2022 endpoint. The Ministry of Women and Child Development's current legislation index, last updated 04-09-2026, lists the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Model Amendment Rules, 2022. G.S.R. 678(E), dated 01-09-2022, is confirmed by Ministry-hosted Gazette text. No later Central amendment was identified in the targeted official-source search.

Reason: current Government indexing plus the identified primary Gazette instrument materially strengthens the endpoint, but search silence is not treated as legal absence.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-018

State-specific Juvenile Justice Rules and implementation notifications are excluded from the Central Model Rules amendment sequence unless they amend the Central instrument itself.

Reason: Central later-instrument completeness must distinguish Central subordinate legislation from State implementation architecture.
