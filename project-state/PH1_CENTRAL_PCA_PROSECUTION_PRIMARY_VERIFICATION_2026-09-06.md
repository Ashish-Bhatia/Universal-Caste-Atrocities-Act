## 2. CVC Master Circular No. 05/MC/2026

### 2.1 Instrument identity

Verified from a contemporaneous reproduction of the CVC Office Memorandum and the attached circular, and independently corroborated by an official Indian Railways/MRVC page listing the instrument:

- Issuing authority: Central Vigilance Commission.
- Office Memorandum number: 024/VGL/017-5.
- Date: 27-07-2026.
- Master Circular number: 05/MC/2026.
- Subject: Procedure for Processing request for Sanction for Prosecution under Section 19 of Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
- Signed by: Trishaljit Sethi, Secretary, CVC.

The attached PDF reproduces CVC letterhead, emblem, CVC identification and the signed forwarding memorandum. The first page states that the circular is available on the CVC website under Publications > Master Circulars. The official Indian Railways/MRVC page separately lists 27-07-2026, 024-VGL/017-5 and the same subject under CVC circulars.

### 2.2 Legal classification

The instrument is classified as an executive/administrative CVC master circular. It is not treated as a statutory amendment to the Prevention of Corruption Act and not treated as delegated legislation merely because it concerns section 19.

The instrument itself describes the CVC's mandate as superintendence over vigilance administration of organisations within its advisory jurisdiction and states that the circular consolidates, updates and revises earlier CVC guidelines/circulars on prosecution-sanction processing.

Section 19(1) of the Prevention of Corruption Act separately contains the Central Government guideline power concerning sanction for prosecution. No proposition in this record treats the CVC circular itself as an exercise of that statutory rule-making power without further legal analysis.

### 2.3 Applicability

The forwarding memorandum is addressed to:

- Secretaries of all Ministries/Departments of Government of India;
- Chief Executives of Central Public Sector Enterprises, Public Sector Banks, Public Sector Insurance Companies and Autonomous Bodies;
- Chief Vigilance Officers of Ministries/Departments, CPSEs, Public Sector Banks, Public Sector Insurance Companies and Autonomous Bodies;
- the CVC website.

The circular's reproduced secondary description further identifies the covered organisations as those within the CVC's advisory jurisdiction. The project records this as the operative applicability boundary, subject to direct CVC primary-source confirmation of the full applicability clause.

### 2.4 Operative subject and provisions verified from the reproduced circular

The attached circular's contents establish the following operative sections:

- Short title, applicability and commencement.
- Purpose.
- Prior sanction for prosecution under the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
- Guidelines for competent authority for sanction under section 19.
- Cases requiring CVC advice for grant of sanction for prosecution.
- Acronyms/abbreviations.

The reproduced full-text account identifies, among other matters, the section 19 sanction framework, the competent authority's administrative consideration, statutory timelines, CVC-advice situations, and documentary requirements for prosecution-sanction proposals. These detailed propositions remain secondary-source-derived until the official CVC PDF is directly retrieved.

### 2.5 Supersession

Paragraph 2 of the forwarding memorandum expressly states that the Commission has consolidated, updated and revised its earlier guidelines/circulars on the subject and that, with issuance of Master Circular No. 05/MC/2026, all earlier CVC guidelines/circulars issued on the subject stand superseded.

This is verified at the reproduced instrument level. The exact list of superseded CVC instrument numbers and dates is not yet frozen because the direct CVC primary PDF has not been retrieved and the reproduced copy's detailed supersession references require page-level extraction.

### 2.6 Direct CVC primary retrieval result

The official CVC website was opened at `https://www.cvc.gov.in/`, but the available retrieval interface returned no page text. Targeted searches for the exact circular number, file number, title and date did not return an independently retrievable CVC PDF endpoint. The CVC website itself is therefore verified as the issuing authority's official publication domain, but the underlying 27-07-2026 PDF was not directly retrieved from that domain in this session.

The CVC primary-source requirement therefore remains OPEN. The circular is not upgraded from Grade B to Grade A on the basis of the reproduction alone.

## 5. Amendment/supersession chain

The Extension 16 search did not establish an exhaustive post-2018 amendment or supersession chain for the two DoPT instruments. The 15-03-2021 OM expressly refers back to the 24-02-2014 DoPT OM and 28-07-2014 DoPT letter establishing the Single Window System. The 03-09-2021 SOP is separately confirmed as circulated by the 27-12-2021 DoPT covering letter.

The 2026 CVC Master Circular expressly supersedes earlier CVC guidance on the subject of section 19 prosecution-sanction processing. It does not, on the evidence presently retrieved, establish blanket supersession of DoPT instruments.

No legal conclusion is drawn from the absence of a located later DoPT instrument.
