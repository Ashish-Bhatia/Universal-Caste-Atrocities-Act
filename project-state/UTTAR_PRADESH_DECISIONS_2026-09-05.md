# Uttar Pradesh Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-UP-0001: Uttar Pradesh was processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after Tripura in the controlled sequence.
- DEC-UP-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Uttar Pradesh-specific jurisdiction inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. Generic/master files were not treated as Uttar Pradesh-specific artifacts.
- DEC-UP-0003: Uttar Pradesh is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-UP-0004: The 2022 MoSJ&E Section 21(4) Uttar Pradesh report is treated as the principal detailed institutional snapshot.
- DEC-UP-0005: Historical Rule 9 Nodal Officer appointment is accepted as established for the relevant reporting periods, while the current named officer and appointment instrument remain open.
- DEC-UP-0006: The 2022 State/district Special Investigation Cell structure is accepted as documented. Current Rule 8 staffing and command remain open.
- DEC-UP-0007: The Ministry Special Police Station census is retained as historical evidence only. Uttar Pradesh is not treated as currently lacking Special Police Stations solely because of historical reporting or search silence.
- DEC-UP-0008: Historical no-identified-area and Special Officer findings are retained as reporting snapshots, not 2026 absence findings.
- DEC-UP-0009: The 2022 V&MC figures are retained as reporting-period data. Current membership and meeting compliance remain open.
- DEC-UP-0010: The historical 65 Exclusive Special Court figure and remaining Special Court framework are accepted as institutional evidence. 2026 High Court notifications are used as current corroboration but not as a complete court census.
- DEC-UP-0011: A current named SPP/ESPP roster is not accepted without direct appointment evidence. The official prosecution portal is used only as administrative corroboration.
- DEC-UP-0012: Current Ministry relief and central-assistance figures are retained as scheme statistics and are not treated as proof of individual statutory payment compliance.
- DEC-UP-0013: The 2025 Supreme Court and 2026 Allahabad High Court material is used only for current institutional corroboration. Phase 2 case-law research is not commenced.
- DEC-UP-0014: Current Uttar Pradesh Government Orders/Gazettes, later Section 21(4) reports, contingency plan, relief workflow and transition instructions remain separate retrieval workstreams.
- DEC-UP-0015: Central later-instrument/current-law completeness and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remain separate national workstreams.
- DEC-UP-0016: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis or policy-superiority/necessity analysis was undertaken.
