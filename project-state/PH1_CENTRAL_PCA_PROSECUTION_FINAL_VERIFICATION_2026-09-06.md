## 4. DoPT 18-10-2024 OM: controlled finding

Instrument: No. 428/10/2021-AVD-IV(B), dated 18-10-2024.

Subject: Checking delays in grant of sanction for prosecution in respect of a public servant under section 19 of the PCA, as amended in 2018.

The retrieved instrument text identifies consolidated components covering investigating-agency action points, investigation-report format, single-window procedure, section 19 checklist, competent-authority processing stages, legal consultation, speaking orders and requests by private persons for prosecution sanction. It states that the instructions review, consolidate at one place and reiterate instructions issued so far on the subject, and requests compliance by Central and State administrative authorities and investigating agencies.

Legal classification: executive/administrative instruction. It is not treated as a statutory amendment or delegated legislation merely because it implements section 19 processing.

Evidence grade: B for the present endpoint because the accessible full text is reproduced by a credible non-government repository. The Government identity is explicit, but direct DoPT document-host retrieval was not achieved in this session. It is therefore not silently upgraded to A.

A 2025 judicial record also refers to this 18-10-2024 DoPT letter as the revised checklist governing resubmission of a Central prosecution-sanction proposal under the single-window system. This is corroborative only and does not replace the underlying DoPT instrument.
