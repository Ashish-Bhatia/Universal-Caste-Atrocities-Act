### 2.2 Official eGazette indexed primary-source result

The official eGazette PDF is independently indexed by the web search system as the Government of India Gazette source. The indexed result identifies the issue as Gazette of India, Part II, Section 3(ii), dated 31 May 2025, page 3521, and reproduces the operative amendment language.

The indexed primary-source text verifies that the notification:

- concerns appointment/continuation of a Special Public Prosecutor for appearing for the CBI in trial courts in Madhya Pradesh;
- concerns cases relating to offences specified under section 3 of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988;
- concerns examinations conducted by Vyapam and investigated by the CBI under the Delhi Special Police Establishment Act, 1946; and
- amends Government of India, Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, Department of Personnel and Training notification S.O. 8 dated 28-12-2015, published on 02-01-2016.

The indexed text states the operative amendment exactly as follows in substance: in the 2015 notification's Table, serial number 4 and the entry relating to it are omitted.

The indexed result also verifies file number F. No. 225/22/2015-AVD-II and the signatory Satyam Srivastava, Under Secretary.
