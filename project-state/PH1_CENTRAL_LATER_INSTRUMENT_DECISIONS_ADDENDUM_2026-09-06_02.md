# Phase 1 Central Later-Instrument Decisions Addendum 02

Date: 2026-09-06

## Decisions

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-008

The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Rules, 2017 later-instrument sequence is extended through the identified 2024 amendment series: G.S.R. 07(E), 114(E), 175(E), 334(E), 343(E), 358(E), 361(E) and 649(E). G.S.R. 175(E) and G.S.R. 343(E) remain sequence-confirmed through the current consolidated Rules record but their direct Gazette endpoints are not yet separately retrieved. G.S.R. 649(E) is directly retrieved and is the latest identified instrument in this extension.

Reason: official Gazette/current Department evidence establishes operative 2024 amendments. The project preserves each instrument separately and does not infer absence of later instruments.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-009

The Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 later-instrument sequence is extended through S.O. 995(E), dated 01-03-2024, and S.O. 5517(E), dated 20-12-2024.

Reason: both are operative Central Gazette amendments to the Rules. Election declarations and administrative instructions that merely cite the Rules are not counted as Rules amendments.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-010

2024 and 2025 Bills proposing amendments to the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act are not treated as enacted amendments or current-law instruments.

Reason: the project distinguishes legislative proposals from enacted Central law and subordinate legislation.
