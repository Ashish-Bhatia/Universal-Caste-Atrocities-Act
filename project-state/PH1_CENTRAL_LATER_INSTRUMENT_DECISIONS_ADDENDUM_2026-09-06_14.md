## Decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-032

Preserve POCSO Extension 13 as ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION. Do not reopen it absent a qualifying primary-source trigger.

The next eligible unresolved Central later-instrument dependency within the existing Priority 2 screening record is the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, but only for unresolved instrument classes outside the section 29A Rules question already exhausted by PCA Extensions 06-08.

## Selection rationale

The Priority 2 screening record classifies PCA as an implementation-integrity interface. Forest Rights, RPA, Child and Adolescent Labour, PWDVA and POCSO have been advanced in the controlled later-instrument sequence. RPwD and Juvenile Justice residuals are excluded by the current handoff instruction. PCA therefore remains the eligible unresolved Priority 2 item for a new, non-duplicative later-instrument extension.

## Non-repetition control

Extensions 06-08 are complete for the PCA section 29A Rules residual. Extension 14 must not repeat their parliamentary laying searches, Gazette notification-number census, DoPT prosecution-sanction guidance search, or final section 29A negative-source formulation.

## Scope boundary

The authorized Extension 14 workstream is limited to post-2018 PCA amendment Acts and other Central statutory instruments affecting PCA that were not covered by Extensions 06-08, with parliamentary legislative-history verification where relevant. Administrative guidance is to remain separately classified unless primary evidence establishes statutory legal effect.

## Evidence controls

Preserve evidence grades. Prefer primary authoritative sources. Distinguish retrieval silence from legal absence. Do not treat India Code, Ministry indexes or parliamentary search silence as proof that an instrument does not exist. Do not treat Bills or draft instruments as enacted law.
