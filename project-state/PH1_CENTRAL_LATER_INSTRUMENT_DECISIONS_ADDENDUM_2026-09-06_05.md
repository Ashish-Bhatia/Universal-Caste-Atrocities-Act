# Phase 1 Central Later-Instrument Decisions Addendum 05

Date: 2026-09-06

## Decisions

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-019

Forest Rights Rules are advanced to the Government-hosted 2012 amended Rules endpoint. G.S.R. 669(E), dated 06-09-2012, is treated as the identified Central amendment instrument for the controlled endpoint, while later-instrument completeness remains qualified.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-020

Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Rules are advanced to G.S.R. 644(E), dated 17-10-2006. The current Ministry-hosted Gazette text and India Code record support the principal Rules endpoint. Search silence is not legal absence.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-021

POCSO Rules are advanced to G.S.R. 165(E), dated 09-03-2020. Rule 13's repeal of the 2012 Rules is recorded as an operative transition control, subject to the savings stated in that rule.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-022

Child and Adolescent Labour Rules are advanced to G.S.R. 543(E), dated 02-06-2017. The final instrument is distinguished from the earlier draft G.S.R. 392(E), dated 20-04-2017.

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-023

The Prevention of Corruption Rules class remains open. India Code's current Rules table contains no Central Rules entry, but section 29A expressly authorises Central rules. The absence of a displayed Rules record is therefore insufficient for a negative legal finding.
