## 4. Completed-state residuals

Completed State inventories are not equivalent to full 2026 implementation censuses. They carry jurisdiction-specific limitations and open residuals. These include, depending on the jurisdiction, current Rule 9 office-holder/designation, Protection Cell structure, Special Police Station or SC/ST police-unit matrix, Rule 10 officers/atrocity-prone areas, V&MC constitution/meetings, Special/Exclusive Special Court jurisdiction matrix, SPP/ESPP register, relief/payment workflow, PoA-specific SOP/digital systems, related-law interfaces and post-2022 annual-report status.

The completed-state residuals remain separate and must not be collapsed into a generic national assumption. Meghalaya's residuals are specifically PH1-ISSUE-256 through PH1-ISSUE-265. Manipur's residuals remain PH1-ISSUE-246 through PH1-ISSUE-255 plus earlier Manipur open issues already recorded in the repository.
