# Odisha Phase 1 Decisions

Date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-OD-0001: Odisha is treated as the next jurisdictional Phase 1 inventory after Nagaland. Completed State research is not reopened.
- DEC-OD-0002: Odisha is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS.
- DEC-OD-0003: Official Government sources are preferred. The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal Odisha implementation snapshot for this pass.
- DEC-OD-0004: The 2022 institutional and statistical figures are treated as historical reporting-period evidence, not as proof of 2026 status.
- DEC-OD-0005: The current Odisha Police HRPC page is used as current institutional corroboration for specialised SC/ST atrocity monitoring, but not as proof of every Rule 8 statutory element.
- DEC-OD-0006: Historical figures for Special Courts, Exclusive Special Courts, SPPs and atrocity-prone areas are retained with explicit dates and currentness limitations.
- DEC-OD-0007: Search silence is not treated as evidence of absence.
- DEC-OD-0008: Odisha-specific related legislation is recorded for later crosswalk work but no conflict or duplication conclusion is made in Phase 1.
- DEC-OD-0009: Odisha residuals are recorded in PH1-ISSUE-OD-001 through PH1-ISSUE-OD-018.
- DEC-OD-0010: No Bill drafting, policy-superiority conclusion, constitutional-validity conclusion or Phase 2 case-law corpus is initiated by this inventory.
