## PH1-ISSUE-008, Forest Rights correction and extension

Status: OPEN, ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION.

1. The authorized next Central later-instrument dependency after Child and Adolescent Labour was the Forest Rights Act and Central Rules/instrument class.
2. New primary evidence requires correction of Extension 05. Act 34 of 2019, section 95 and Fifth Schedule removed the former Jammu and Kashmir exclusion from FRA section 1(2), effective 31-10-2019.
3. S.O. 2631(E), 07-06-2022, is a Central Article 239(1) administrative instrument directing the Administrator of Andaman and Nicobar Islands to exercise State Government powers/functions under the FRA. It is not an Act or Rules amendment.
4. G.S.R. 669(E), 06-09-2012 remains the identified Central Rules amendment endpoint in the targeted official-source search.
5. No later Central Rules amendment was retrieved in the defined official-source search. This remains retrieval silence, not legal absence.
6. The 2025 J&K Government nodal-department order is retained as corroborative implementation evidence only. It does not alter the Central instrument classification.
7. PH1-ISSUE-008 remains open because broader Central later-instrument completeness remains unresolved.

Primary research record: `research/central/PH1_FOREST_RIGHTS_EXTENSION_11_2026-09-06.md`.

## Correction trigger

The prior Extension 05 statement that no post-2018 Central amendment Act to the Forest Rights Act had been located is superseded by the verified Act 34 of 2019 amendment. The correction is limited to that proposition. The 2012 Rules endpoint remains valid.
