# Phase 1 Central Later-Instrument Decisions Addendum 07

Date: 2026-09-06

## Decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-025

The PCA section 29A Rules class remains OPEN after Extension 07. Parliamentary Digital Library/eParlib, DoPT, Legislative Department and official Gazette searches did not retrieve a qualifying Central rule expressly made under section 29A. Retrieval silence is not treated as legal absence.

## Classification control

DoPT OMs and administrative guidance, including O.M. No. 372/19/2012-AVD-III dated 03-05-2012, remain administrative guidance unless an instrument's parent-power citation establishes section 29A authority. Parliamentary answers describing those OMs as guidelines/instructions reinforce this classification.

Section 9 commercial-organisation guidance and section 19 prosecution-sanction guidance remain separate search targets. Neither is treated as a section 29A rule without an operative instrument and parent-power citation.

## Scope protection

No State/UT work, completed Central later-instrument class, section 3(2)(v) threshold matrix, BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze, website verification, drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was reopened.

## Next verification requirement

Conduct the final authoritative parliamentary laying-register/appendix and Gazette archival notification-number census. If no instrument is found, preserve the result as a qualified negative-source finding unless the authoritative repository provides a complete rules register.
