# Phase 1 Central Later-Instrument Decisions Addendum 16

Date: 2026-09-06

## Decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-034

A separate controlled Phase 1 workstream, Extension 15, is authorized for a Central-only census of post-2018 notifications under section 3 of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 appointing Special Judges, together with material amendment, supersession, continuation, withdrawal or transfer instruments affecting those appointments.

## Basis

The cumulative acceptance audit identifies Central later-instrument completeness as an unmet Phase 1 dependency. PCA Extension 14 expressly did not establish a complete Central section 3 notification register. The Extension 14 endpoint therefore requires a separate authorization before any section 3 census begins.

## Controls

- This authorization does not establish that any particular notification exists.
- It does not establish completeness of the eventual census.
- Central and State section 3 notifications must be kept distinct.
- Primary-source verification is required for material legal propositions.
- Retrieval silence must not be converted into legal absence.
- Extensions 06-08, Extension 14, and all other completed workstreams must not be repeated except for narrowly necessary cross-reference.
- PH1-ISSUE-008 remains OPEN.
- Phase 1 acceptance remains NOT YET SATISFIED.

## Status

Authorization established. No substantive Extension 15 research performed in this authorization step.
