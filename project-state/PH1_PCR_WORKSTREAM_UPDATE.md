Phase 1 update, 2026-09-05

PCR Act/Rules section-rule comparison completed in legislation/PCR_ACT_RULES_SECTION_RULE_COMPARISON.md.

Key verified findings:
- PCR ss.3-7A have substantial factual overlap with multiple SC/ST Act s.3 offences, but statutory triggers and protected-person structures differ.
- SC/ST Act s.16 expressly applies PCR Act s.10A so far as may be. PCR Rules 3-4 therefore form part of the incorporated collective-fine framework.
- PCR s.12 and SC/ST Act s.8 are distinct special presumptions.
- PCR s.10 and SC/ST Act s.4 overlap on public-servant investigative neglect but use different offence architecture.
- PCR s.15A and Rule 5 overlap administratively with SC/ST Act s.21 and SC/ST Rules reporting machinery.
- PCR s.16A and SC/ST Act s.19 use different probation-exclusion age thresholds.
