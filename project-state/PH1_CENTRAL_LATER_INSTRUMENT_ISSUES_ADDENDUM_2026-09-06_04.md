## Classification controls

- State-specific JJ Rules and State implementation notifications are not counted as amendments to the Central Model Rules.
- Delhi Gazette and other State/UT notifications that merely invoke the Model Rules are implementation instruments, not Central Model Rules amendments.
- RPwD direct-endpoint technical retrieval remains distinct from instrument identity and operative-text corroboration.
