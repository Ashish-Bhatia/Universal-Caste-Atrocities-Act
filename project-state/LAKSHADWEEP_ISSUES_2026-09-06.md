## Controlled evidence notes

1. The Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order provides the primary Lakshadweep ST beneficiary definition.
2. The current UT Administration states that there are no Scheduled Castes in Lakshadweep. This is corroborated by the current constitutional SC Order baseline reviewed.
3. Historical official Central reports establish earlier Protection Cell, Rule 9, Rule 10, Special Court and SPP arrangements, but these are not treated as current 2026 arrangements without later primary verification.
4. The current UT Administration has a functioning Social Welfare & Tribal Affairs department, Police, Kavaratti District and Sessions Court, DLSA and a Directorate of Prosecution established in May 2026. None of these current general institutions alone proves a PoA-specific designation.
5. The Central scheme records zero Lakshadweep relief beneficiaries in each of 2020-21 through 2024-25. This does not prove zero relief from UT resources.
6. NCRB-derived MHA data record zero SC atrocity/crime cases in 2021-2023 and ST figures of 0, 0, 0 cases registered in 2021-2023, with one ST case chargesheeted in 2022. These are reporting statistics, not proof of absence of unreported conduct.
7. Search non-location is not treated as legal absence.
