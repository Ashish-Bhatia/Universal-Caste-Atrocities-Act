# Phase 1 Manual Scavengers Act: BNSS Transition Verification

Date: 2026-09-06
Phase: Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION

## Scope

Targeted primary-source verification of the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013 sections 10 and 21 against the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023. Section 39 was checked for the separate exemption-instrument residual and is not closed by this record.

## Verified primary-law position

1. MS Act section 10 remains a specific statutory cognizance limitation. India Code's current Act record retains section 10, and the statutory text provides a three-month complaint period from the alleged occurrence. The existence of BNSS Chapter XXXVIII on general limitation does not by itself displace the special Act provision. BNSS section 4(2) applies BNSS procedure to offences under other laws subject to enactments regulating the manner or place of dealing with those offences, and BNSS section 5 preserves special laws and special procedure absent a specific contrary provision.

2. MS Act section 21 expressly creates a special trial mechanism: the State Government may confer on an Executive Magistrate the powers of a Judicial Magistrate of the first class for trial of MS Act offences, and the provision states that the empowered Executive Magistrate is deemed, for the purposes of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, to be a Judicial Magistrate of the first class. Section 21(2) permits summary trial.

3. BNSS section 3 is the current construction-of-references provision. It provides rules for references in any law to Magistrates and allocates functions involving adjudicative punishment/trial consequences to Judicial Magistrates, subject to the Sanhita. BNSS sections 4 and 5 preserve the special-law framework for offences under other laws and special jurisdiction/procedure.

4. The BNSS replaced the CrPC with effect from 01-07-2024, subject to its savings provision. Therefore, the MS Act's express CrPC reference requires controlled transition treatment rather than a bare numerical substitution. The repository must not state that MS Act section 21 simply becomes a particular BNSS section without qualification.

## Controlled conclusion

PH1-ISSUE-016 should be advanced from OPEN to ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION, not closed.

The current primary texts establish the operative transition framework sufficiently to remove the uncertainty that BNSS applicability was wholly unresolved. They do not, by themselves, establish a complete State-specific implementation matrix showing which Executive Magistrates have been empowered under MS Act section 21 after 01-07-2024, nor do they resolve every possible interaction between section 21's special deeming language and BNSS institutional provisions.
