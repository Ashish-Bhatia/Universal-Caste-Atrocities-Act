# Nagaland Phase 1 Decisions

Date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-NG-0001: Nagaland is treated as an ST-focused PoA jurisdiction because the Ministry's official baseline records no notified Scheduled Caste community for the State.
- DEC-NG-0002: The absence of a notified SC population is not treated as removing the PoA Act from Nagaland. Recent judicial records demonstrate use of the Act in Nagaland-related proceedings involving ST victims.
- DEC-NG-0003: Article 371A is recorded as a material constitutional/local-law interface. No conclusion is made that Article 371A exempts or invalidates the PoA Act in Nagaland.
- DEC-NG-0004: Historical NIL reports are separated from current institutional status. Historical claims that Nagaland required no special machinery are not treated as current facts.
- DEC-NG-0005: The 2024 Delhi High Court Dimapur FIR and 2025 Gauhati High Court charge-sheet record are accepted as strong current/recent corroboration that PoA offences are being invoked in Nagaland-related policing/prosecution.
- DEC-NG-0006: Current Rule 8/9/10, V&MC, court, prosecutor and relief machinery remains unresolved unless a current primary instrument is located.
- DEC-NG-0007: The official 2022 Section 21(4) report is recorded as identified but not fully extracted for Nagaland in this pass. This is a verification gap, not a reason to reopen completed jurisdictions.
- DEC-NG-0008: Nagaland is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS for Phase 1.
- DEC-NG-0009: No Bill drafting, policy-superiority conclusion, constitutional-validity conclusion or Phase 2 case-law corpus work is initiated from this inventory.
- DEC-NG-0010: The master State Implementation Inventory and master State Implementation Source Ledger remain intentionally untouched.
