## Verified findings

- BLSA Act 19 of 1976, deemed in force from 25-10-1975, with identified amending Act 73 of 1985.
- Strongest substantive overlap is BLSA ss.2(g), 4, 16 and 18 with SC/ST Act s.3(1)(h), but the ingredients differ.
- BLSA is organised around bonded labour/bonded debt and does not require SC/ST status. SC/ST s.3(1)(h) is status-conditioned and does not require a bonded-debt structure.
- BLSA ss.6-9 provide debt extinguishment, property restoration, homestead protection and post-extinguishment payment controls without direct SC/ST equivalents.
- BLSA ss.13-14 and Rule 7 substantially overlap with SC/ST vigilance, rehabilitation, monitoring and reporting structures, but their mandates differ.
- BLSA s.15 is a debt-classification burden rule, distinct from SC/ST Act s.8 presumptions and BSA general burdens.
- BLSA ss.21-22 have a materially different forum/bail structure from the SC/ST Act.
- BLSA s.23 has express company liability; no dedicated equivalent appears in the extracted SC/ST Act.
- Rules 1-7 were mapped. Rules 3-4 regulate committee membership/tenure, Rules 5-6 property-restoration procedure, and Rule 7 registers for freed bonded labour and benefits/cases.
