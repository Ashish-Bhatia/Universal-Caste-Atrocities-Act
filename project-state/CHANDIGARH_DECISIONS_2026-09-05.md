# Chandigarh Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-CH-0001: Chandigarh was processed as the next unresearched jurisdiction after Andaman and Nicobar Islands in the controlled repository sequence.
- DEC-CH-0002: Repository search found no pre-existing Chandigarh-specific inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. The master inventory hit was not treated as a jurisdiction artifact and was not modified.
- DEC-CH-0003: Chandigarh is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-CH-0004: Chandigarh has a notified Scheduled Caste population; official Ministry of Tribal Affairs material located states that no community has been specified as Scheduled Tribe in relation to Chandigarh.
- DEC-CH-0005: The 16-10-2015 Nodal Officer notification is treated as the operative Rule 9 instrument located because it expressly supersedes the 2002 order. Current incumbent verification remains open.
- DEC-CH-0006: The 17-04-2015 Rule 8 Protection Cell order is accepted as primary evidence of formal machinery and functions. Current staffing and deployment remain open.
- DEC-CH-0007: The 2021 administrative compilation's statement that no Special Police Station existed is retained as historical/current-period evidence only. It is not treated as proof of 2026 absence.
- DEC-CH-0008: No atrocity-prone area is retained as a Ministry reporting-period finding. Current 2026 Rule 3/Rule 10 status remains open.
- DEC-CH-0009: Chandigarh's State-level V&MC is documented by primary administrative instruments. Because Chandigarh is a single-district UT, district/sub-divisional machinery is not inferred without current instrument evidence.
- DEC-CH-0010: The 06-03-2024 Special Court notification is treated as the current controlling designation located. It supersedes the 2016 notification and specifies the Sessions Judge and Additional Sessions Judge-I through V structure described in the instrument.
- DEC-CH-0011: The 2015 SPP notification and 2022 report establish a Special Public Prosecutor arrangement. Current named roster, tenure and allocation remain open.
- DEC-CH-0012: The 2022 report's four victims provided relief is retained as reporting-period case-level evidence. The current central-assistance dataset is kept conceptually separate from victim-level relief counts.
- DEC-CH-0013: The 2021 and 2022 case tables are historical statistical evidence only and are not treated as a 2026 prevalence conclusion.
- DEC-CH-0014: The 2024 Dinesh Jangra decision is used only as current judicial/institutional corroboration. Phase 2 merits analysis was not commenced.
- DEC-CH-0015: PCR Act, BNS/BNSS/BSA and other related-law material is kept as an interface workstream. No transition-completeness conclusion is made.
- DEC-CH-0016: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or Phase 2 case-law corpus work was undertaken.
