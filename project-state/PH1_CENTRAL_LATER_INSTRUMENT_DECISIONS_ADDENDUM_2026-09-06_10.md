# Phase 1 Central Later-Instrument Decisions Addendum 10

Date: 2026-09-06

## Decision

DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-028

The authorized next Central later-instrument workstream after RPA Extension 09 is the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986 / Central Rules and related statutory-instrument class. The class is advanced with qualification after primary/official verification of the identified 2019, 2022 and 2026 Technical Advisory Committee constitution instruments.

## Classification control

The project records S.O. 2948(E), S.O. 4811(E) and S.O. 2284(E) as identified later statutory instruments under section 5. They are not treated as Central Rules amendments. G.S.R. 543(E), 02-06-2017 remains the identified final Central Rules amendment located in the defined search set.

Targeted searches did not retrieve a later Central Rules amendment after G.S.R. 543(E) or a post-2018 Central amendment Act. These results are qualified negative-source findings only. The 2022 TAC Schedule review and recommendations do not by themselves establish a Schedule amendment.

## Registry control

India Code remains a discovery/current-registry source and is not treated as an exhaustive Gazette register. The Act's displayed 25-03-2019 last-updated field is not used to infer current-law absence.

## Scope protection

The Child and Adolescent Labour regime remains a distinct labour/child-protection interface. No conclusion is drawn that it should be replaced, duplicated or absorbed by the proposed caste-atrocity regime.

No State/UT research, PCA Extension 06-08 searches, RPA Extension 09, BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze, section 3(2)(v) threshold work, RPwD/JJ residuals, website verification, drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was reopened.

## Acceptance effect

This disposition advances the PH1-ISSUE-008 evidence record but does not close broader Central later-instrument completeness or Phase 1 acceptance.
