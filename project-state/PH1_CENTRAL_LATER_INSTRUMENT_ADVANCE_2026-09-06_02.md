## 2. Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961

The previous PH1-ISSUE-008 advance identified amendment instruments through 17-06-2022. Primary Central Gazette evidence now establishes two additional 2024 amendment instruments:

- S.O. 995(E), 01-03-2024, Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2024. Rule 27A(e) changes the senior-citizen age reference from above 80 years to above 85 years.
- S.O. 5517(E), 20-12-2024, Conduct of Elections (Second Amendment) Rules, 2024. Rule 93(2)(a) is amended by inserting the words “as specified in these rules” after “papers”.

The Legislative Department Manual of Election Law, Volume II incorporates S.O. 995(E). Election declarations and administrative instructions citing the Rules were not counted as Rules amendments.

Primary sources:

No exhaustive 2025/2026 amendment census is asserted.
