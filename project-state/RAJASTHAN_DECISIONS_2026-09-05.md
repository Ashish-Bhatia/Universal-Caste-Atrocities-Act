# Rajasthan Phase 1 Decisions

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-RJ-0001: Rajasthan is processed as the next jurisdiction after Punjab in the repository sequence.
- DEC-RJ-0002: No Rajasthan-specific inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum was found in the repository search before research began.
- DEC-RJ-0003: Rajasthan is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS, not fully current-law closed.
- DEC-RJ-0004: The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal State implementation snapshot for committee, police, court, prosecutor, relief and training fields.
- DEC-RJ-0005: 2022 figures are historical reporting-period evidence and are not treated as 2026 compliance findings.
- DEC-RJ-0006: Rajasthan Police 2024 monthly statistics are retained as a separate State police reporting series and are not numerically merged with the Central Section 21(4) series.
- DEC-RJ-0007: The 2022 SC/ST table and Rajasthan Police series are not used to infer current incidence, effectiveness or policy superiority.
- DEC-RJ-0008: Current police district-list entries and recent officer-service records are used only as corroboration that Civil Rights/SC-ST specialist machinery continues to exist, not as a substitute for establishment orders.
- DEC-RJ-0009: Current 2026 Rule 9, Rule 10, police-cell, court, prosecutor, committee and relief instruments remain residuals because first-pass retrieval did not establish a complete current census.
- DEC-RJ-0010: Recent Rajasthan High Court Special Court postings are recorded as Phase 1 jurisdictional corroboration only. No Phase 2 case-law corpus or merits synthesis is opened.
- DEC-RJ-0011: Rajasthan State SC/ST Development Fund legislation is recorded as a related institutional interface, not as an atrocity-law measure.
- DEC-RJ-0012: Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
- DEC-RJ-0013: Master State inventory and master State source ledger are intentionally left untouched.
- DEC-RJ-0014: No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research is undertaken in this workstream.
