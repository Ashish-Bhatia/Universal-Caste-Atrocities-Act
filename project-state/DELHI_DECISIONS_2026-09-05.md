# Delhi (NCT) Phase 1 Decision Log

Research date: 2026-09-05

- DEC-DL-0001: Delhi is treated as the next unresearched jurisdiction in the repository sequence after Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.
- DEC-DL-0002: Delhi is classified PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS because core PoA machinery is evidenced, but current instrument-level verification remains incomplete.
- DEC-DL-0003: Delhi is treated as principally an SC implementation baseline because official Census/NCST material states no notified ST community under the Article 342 framework for Delhi.
- DEC-DL-0004: The Delhi Police Citizen Charter is accepted as primary evidence of a functioning SC/ST Protection Cell in the Crime Branch, while current staffing remains unresolved.
- DEC-DL-0005: Historical Ministry reporting is used for institutional baselines but is not converted into 2026 current facts without current instrument support.
- DEC-DL-0006: The Parliamentary Standing Committee's 11 Special Court reference is recorded as historical evidence only. It is not asserted as the current 2026 court count or designation.
- DEC-DL-0007: Current public-facing relief scheme pages establish an administrative relief framework but do not establish case-level payment performance or audit completeness.
- DEC-DL-0008: Recent Delhi High Court PoA proceedings are used only as current/recent institutional corroboration. No merits synthesis is undertaken.
- DEC-DL-0009: BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains a separate national workstream.
- DEC-DL-0010: No Bill drafting, policy-superiority/necessity analysis, constitutional-validity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research is permitted in this pass.
