## 1. Scope and control

Extension 16 was separately authorized after Extension 15. It is limited to Central Government post-2018 instruments materially connected to PCA sections 17A and 19 prosecution machinery, including prior-approval and prosecution-sanction processing. Section 3 Special Judge work, PCA section 29A Rules work, Extension 14 matters, and State/UT arrangements are excluded except for narrowly necessary cross-reference.

Primary search boundary: 01-01-2019 through 06-09-2026 inclusive. Earlier instruments are retained only where necessary to establish the legal or administrative chain of a post-2018 instrument.

## 2. Statutory control

Section 17A, inserted by the Prevention of Corruption (Amendment) Act, 2018, requires previous approval before enquiry, inquiry or investigation into specified acts of public servants relating to recommendations or decisions made in discharge of official functions, subject to its express exceptions and statutory timeline. Section 19 requires previous sanction before cognizance of specified PCA offences, identifies the competent sanctioning authority, provides a three-month decision period with a possible one-month extension for legal consultation, and authorises the Central Government to prescribe guidelines for prosecution sanction. [A/B]

The section 19 statutory text was checked through current legal text; the project will continue to prefer India Code/official Act text for final source freeze. The Central Government guideline power in section 19(1) is therefore a material later-instrument search target. [A/B]

## 4. Located material within scope

### E16-001: DoPT SOP, 03-09-2021, section 17A

- Identifier: No. 428/07/2021-AVD.IV(B)
- Date: 03-09-2021
- Issuing authority: Government of India, Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions, Department of Personnel and Training
- Subject: Standard Operating Procedures for processing cases under section 17A of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988
- Legal relationship: Operational administrative SOP for the statutory prior-approval process under section 17A
- Principal content verified from the reproduced instrument: stage-wise processing; police-officer rank requirements for seeking prior approval for categories of public servants; consideration by the appropriate Government/authority; single-window receipt procedure; checklist requirements; direction to administrative authorities and investigating agencies to comply.
- Legal classification: Executive/administrative instruction implementing statutory processing machinery. It is not treated as a statutory rule or amendment to the PCA.
- Evidence grade: A for the underlying Government of India instrument as reproduced in official Government material; direct DoPT document endpoint was independently identified but timed out during retrieval. B for corroborative copies where used.
- Verification status: VERIFIED AS GOVERNMENT-ISSUED INSTRUMENT, with direct DoPT retrieval limitation.
- Corroboration: A 27-12-2021 DoPT covering letter hosted by Puducherry Police records that the SOPs were circulated by the Department's 03-09-2021 letter and requests wider compliance by police authorities. [B]
- Judicial corroboration: Supreme Court/High Court records identify the 03-09-2021 DoPT SOP and use it to explain the section 17A processing framework. Judicial material is corroborative and does not substitute for the SOP.

### E16-003: CVC Master Circular No. 05/MC/2026, 27-07-2026

## 5. Operational corroboration, not counted as new statutory instruments

### E16-COR-001: DoPT Annual Report 2024-25

The DoPT Annual Report 2024-25 records 22 prosecution sanctions against 22 IAS officers under the PCA during 01-04-2023 to 13-03-2025 and notes that one order was issued withdrawing an earlier sanction order. This establishes continuing Central administrative operation of prosecution-sanction machinery but is not itself a section 19 statutory instrument and does not identify the individual orders. [A]

### E16-COR-002: DoPT Annual Report 2021-22

The DoPT Annual Report 2021-22 records the continuing use of the 2012 DoPT framework for prosecution-sanction decisions and notes that competent authorities take final decisions after considering DoPT views where CVC advice is not accepted. This is reporting-period corroboration, not a new post-2018 statutory instrument. [A]

## 6. Negative and unresolved results

1. No post-2018 Central Gazette notification or Central rule under section 17A or section 19 was located in the targeted search through 06-09-2026.
2. This does not establish that no such instrument exists.
3. The 2021 DoPT section 17A SOP is an administrative instrument and must not be described as a rule made under the PCA.
4. The 15-03-2021 DoPT Single Window OM is an administrative processing instruction and must not be described as a section 19 statutory guideline without verifying the exact legal basis and scope.
5. The 27-07-2026 CVC Master Circular is a significant later administrative instrument within the section 19 dependency, but its direct official CVC PDF remains to be retrieved and verified before final closure.
6. The 2012 DoPT prosecution-sanction framework remains historically relevant to the post-2018 administrative chain but was not counted as a post-2018 instrument. Its continuing status and relationship to the 2021 Single Window OM and 2026 CVC Master Circular require final chain verification.
