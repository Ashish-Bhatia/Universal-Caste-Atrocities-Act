# PH1 PCA Rules Residual, Extension 07

Date: 2026-09-06
Issue: PH1-ISSUE-008
Status: OPEN, PRIMARY-SOURCE SEARCH ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION
Evidence grade: A for official statutory, Gazette, DoPT and parliamentary records; historical Law Commission material retained as contextual evidence only.

## Purpose

Continue the controlled PCA section 29A primary-source search without converting retrieval silence into legal absence.

## Findings

1. Section 29A of the Prevention of Corruption Act expressly authorises Central rule-making by Official Gazette notification and requires every rule made under the Act to be laid before both Houses of Parliament. Section 29A(2) expressly identifies section 9 commercial-organisation guidelines and section 19 prosecution-sanction guidelines among possible rule-making matters.

2. Section 9(5) separately requires the Central Government to prescribe guidelines for commercial organisations. Section 19(1) separately permits Central Government guidelines for sanction of prosecution. The project continues to distinguish these guideline powers from a section 29A rule unless the instrument itself establishes the parent power.

3. Targeted Parliament Digital Library/eParlib searches for Prevention of Corruption Rules, section 29A rules, commercial-organisation guidelines, section 19 sanction guidelines and parliamentary laying did not retrieve a qualifying laying record for a Central rule expressly made under section 29A.

4. The parliamentary records retrieved instead concern DoPT administrative prosecution-sanction instructions. Lok Sabha answers identify O.M. No. 399/33/2006-AVD-III dated 06-11-2006, the 20-12-2006 instruction and O.M. No. 372/19/2012-AVD-III dated 03-05-2012 as guidelines/instructions. They do not identify these instruments as rules made under section 29A.

5. Targeted DoPT searches retrieved the 2013 Vigilance Handbook and Annual Reports for 2012-13, 2013-14, 2014-15, 2015-16 and 2017-18. These official records corroborate the administrative prosecution-sanction guidance sequence and do not provide a section 29A rule notification number.

6. Targeted Legislative Department and official Gazette searches using title variants, amendment-rule variants and post-2018 year variants did not retrieve a qualifying Central Gazette instrument expressly made under section 29A.

7. The current India Code Prevention of Corruption Act record displays section 29A but no Central Rules entry. This remains an index/retrieval result, not proof that no rules exist.

8. No qualifying Central instrument was retrieved for the section 9 commercial-organisation guidelines that establishes itself as a section 29A rule.

9. No qualifying Central instrument was retrieved for section 19 prosecution-sanction guidelines that establishes itself as a section 29A rule. The located DoPT OMs remain administrative guidance unless a parent-power citation proves otherwise.

10. Law Commission Report No. 254 is retained only as historical legislative context concerning the proposed commercial-organisation guidance model. It is not an operative post-2018 Central instrument.

## Controlled disposition

PH1-ISSUE-008 remains OPEN. The PCA Rules residual is advanced, but not closed.

The project must not state that no section 29A rules exist. The verified formulation is narrower: no qualifying section 29A Central Rules instrument was retrieved in the targeted parliamentary, DoPT, Legislative Department and Gazette searches conducted through this extension.
