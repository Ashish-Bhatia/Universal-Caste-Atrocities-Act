## Scope control

This extension addresses only the next authorized Central later-instrument dependency after RPA: the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986 and Central Rules/instrument class. It does not reopen State/UT research, PCA Extensions 06-08, RPA Extension 09, completed Central work, section 3(2)(v), the BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze, RPwD/JJ residuals, website verification, drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research.

## Baseline

India Code identifies the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986 as Act 61 of 1986 and displays the principal Central Rules dated 01-01-1988 and the Child & Adolescent Labour (Prohibition & Regulation) Amendment Rules, 2017 dated 02-06-2017. India Code also displays the Act's last-updated date as 25-03-2019. The Rules table is treated as a discovery/current-registry source, not as an exhaustive Gazette register.

The Ministry of Labour and Employment hosts the current Government text of the 1988 Rules and the primary 2017 amendment Rules. The final 2017 amendment notification is G.S.R. 543(E), dated 02-06-2017, made under section 18 of the Act. Official parliamentary records confirm that the 2017 Rules were laid under section 19 of the Act.

## Identified post-2018 Central instruments

### 1. Technical Advisory Committee, S.O. 2948(E), 14-08-2019

The Central Government constituted the Technical Advisory Committee under section 5 of the Act read with rule 3 of the Central Rules. An official Government Rules reproduction records the notification as S.O. 2948(E), dated 14-08-2019. The Ministry's later official Annual Report also identifies S.O. 2948(E) as one of the notifications constituting the TAC.

Classification: statutory instrument under the Act, not an amendment to the Central Rules.
Evidence grade: A.

### 2. Technical Advisory Committee, S.O. 4811(E), 15-09-2022

The Ministry of Labour and Employment's Government-hosted notification records S.O. 4811(E), dated 15-09-2022. It constitutes the Technical Advisory Committee under section 5 of the Act read with rule 3 of the Central Rules, with members from DGFASLI, DGMS, DGHS, ICMR, explosives, NIOH, CSIR-IITR and NIMHANS.

The Ministry's 2022-23 Annual Report independently records that S.O. 2948(E) dated 14-08-2019 and S.O. 4811(E) dated 15-09-2022 constituted the TAC, and that the fourth TAC meeting on 31-10-2022 reviewed Part A and Part B of the Schedule and sent recommendations for revised Schedule text to the Ministry.

Classification: statutory instrument under the Act, not an amendment to the Central Rules.
Evidence grade: A.

### 3. Technical Advisory Committee, S.O. 2284(E), 06-05-2026

The official Gazette records S.O. 2284(E), dated 06-05-2026. The Central Government again constituted the Technical Advisory Committee under section 5 of the Act read with rule 3 of the Central Rules. The notification lists eight members, including DGFASLI, DGMS, DGHS, ICMR, Chief Controller of Explosives, NIOH, CSIR-IITR and the Department of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry at NIMHANS.

Classification: current statutory instrument under the Act, not an amendment to the Central Rules.
Evidence grade: A.

## Other official post-2018 material located

The Ministry's current Child Labour circular archive lists a 01-08-2018 circular constituting a Task Force for implementation of the Act and a 19-03-2025 circular concerning merger of the National Child Labour Project Scheme with Samagra Shiksha Abhiyaan. These are retained as administrative/programme instruments, not treated as amendments to the Act or Central Rules.

The 2018 Task Force entry is a Ministry circular listing, not a Gazette amendment or section 18 Rules instrument. The 2025 NCLP merger entry concerns scheme administration and is not treated as an Act/Rules amendment without a qualifying statutory instrument.

## 2019-2026 schedule/rulemaking endpoint

The targeted search located the 2019, 2022 and 2026 TAC constitutions, which are material because section 5 expressly authorises Central constitution of the TAC and the Committee's statutory function connects to the hazardous-occupation/process Schedule. The 2022-23 Ministry Annual Report records that the TAC reviewed the Schedule and submitted recommendations, but the retrieved evidence does not establish a subsequent Central Gazette amendment of the Schedule through 06-09-2026.

Targeted searches did not retrieve a later Central amendment to the 1988 Rules after G.S.R. 543(E), dated 02-06-2017. This is retrieval silence, not legal absence. It does not exclude a later instrument outside the searched repositories or an instrument that does not use the Rules' short title in searchable metadata.

Targeted searches also did not retrieve a post-2018 Central amendment Act to Act 61 of 1986. India Code's 25-03-2019 last-updated field is not treated as proof of current-law absence.

## Project significance

The Child and Adolescent Labour regime is a high-interaction labour and child-protection interface identified in the Priority 2 screening. It overlaps factually with forced labour, exploitation and child victimisation, but remains a distinct statutory regime with its own protected age categories, prohibited conduct, rehabilitation fund, enforcement structure, inspection architecture and Schedule mechanism. No conclusion is drawn here about replacing, duplicating or merging the regime with caste-atrocity legislation.
