| PH1-TR-001 | PCR Rules / BNSS | PCR Rules 1977 Rule 3(4) expressly references CrPC Chapter XXI summary-trial procedure. | PH1-TR-002 | BLSA / BNS | BLSA s.2(g) explanation and s.20 use the IPC meaning of abetment. BNS s.45 supplies the current general abetment definition. S.O.2790(E) controls the reference transition. | Advanced with qualification |
| PH1-TR-003 | BLSA / BNSS | BLSA s.21(1) contains a CrPC reference concerning conferment of Judicial Magistrate powers on an Executive Magistrate; s.21(2) independently permits summary trial. BNSS Chapter XXII, ss.283-289 supplies the current general summary-trial framework, while the BLSA special-law command remains distinct. | Advanced with qualification |
| PH1-TR-004 | Central later instruments | Targeted 2026 official-source search did not identify a later Central PCR Rules amendment instrument or later BLSA amendment instrument. This is controlled search evidence only and not proof of legal absence. | PH1-ISSUE-008 remains open |
