## Controlled conclusions

- The current India Code and official departmental/NCSK indexes support a Central source set through the identified 2018 notifications.
- The 2017 Central Monitoring Committee proposal to amend the Rules does not itself establish that a later Rules amendment was enacted.
- No Central section 39 exemption order was located in the targeted official search. This remains a negative search result, not proof of absence.
- G.S.R. 376(E) and G.S.R. 726(E) are not classified as section 39 exemption orders. The Supreme Court records their withdrawal in 2023.
- PH1-ISSUE-015 and PH1-ISSUE-017 remain OPEN pending a final instrument-level Gazette/Legislative Department freeze.
