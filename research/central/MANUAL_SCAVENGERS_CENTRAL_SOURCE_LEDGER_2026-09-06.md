## Controlled conclusions

- The final targeted primary-source census identified the 2013 Act, its 01-10-2013 commencement notification, the 12-12-2013 Rules, and the Central Monitoring Committee notification chain comprising S.O. 89(E), 605(E), 2008(E), 476(E), 1389(E), 888(E), 3338(E), 4379(E), 1510(E), 93(E), 1352(E) and 3793(E).
- The earlier India Code subordinate-notification display is incomplete as a current index because it stops at S.O. 888(E), while later Gazette instruments independently establish amendments in 2023, 2024 and 2025. The Gazette instruments therefore control the instrument-level census for those later amendments.
- No amendment to the Central 2013 Rules was identified in the targeted Gazette/Legislative Department searches. The 2017 Central Monitoring Committee proposal to amend the Rules remains evidence of a proposal, not evidence of enactment.
- G.S.R. 376(E) and G.S.R. 726(E) are not classified as section 39 exemption orders. The Supreme Court records their withdrawal in 2023.
- No Central section 39 exemption order was identified in the targeted Gazette, Legislative Department, India Code and Department/NCSK search set. This is a controlled negative search result only, not proof that no such order exists anywhere.
- PH1-ISSUE-015 is eligible for controlled closure with qualification because the previously missed 2023-2025 section 29 instruments are now identified and the Rules corpus has a primary-source census, while broader Central later-instrument completeness remains under PH1-ISSUE-008.
- PH1-ISSUE-017 is eligible for controlled closure with qualification as a negative instrument-identification result, subject to the explicit no-inference rule for search silence.
