# PH1 Representation of the People / Conduct of Elections Rules, Extension 09

Date: 2026-09-06
Issue: PH1-ISSUE-008
Status: OPEN, ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION
Evidence grade: A for Government Gazette, India Code, Legislative Department and official parliamentary records; B only where explicitly identified below.

## Scope control

This extension addresses only the unresolved Central later-instrument dependency for the Representation of the People Act, 1951, principally the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961. It does not reopen State/UT research, PCA Extensions 06-08, completed Central work, section 3(2)(v), the BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze, RPwD/JJ residuals, website verification, drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research.

## Statutory and repository baseline

India Code's current Representation of the People Act, 1951 record lists the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961 and the following later Central Rules records: 22-10-2019, 19-06-2020, 06-01-2022 and 17-06-2022. It does not display the 2023 or 2024 Conduct of Elections Rules amendments in the Rules table retrieved for this research. This is a registry observation and is not treated as proof that the later Gazette instruments do not exist.

The Legislative Department's current hosted Conduct of Elections Rules PDF is an official Government source and provides the statutory text and historical amendment footnotes, but the retrieved PDF does not itself contain the 2023/2024 amendment instruments in its text. It is therefore used as a primary-text baseline, not as an exhaustive later-instrument register.

## Verified later instruments

### 1. Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2019, S.O. 1023(E), 26-02-2019

The official India Code-hosted Rules PDF records that Form 26 was amended by Notification No. S.O. 1023(E), dated 26-02-2019. The amendment concerns the candidate affidavit and includes changes concerning asset/liability and related disclosure fields. Official Lok Sabha parliamentary material records the instrument as laid before the House. This is an additional 2019 amendment instrument that was not represented by the 22-10-2019 India Code Rules-row date alone.

Evidence grade: A.

### 2. Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2019, S.O. 3786(E), 22-10-2019

The official Gazette reproduction confirms the 22-10-2019 amendment. It amended rule 18 to add notified persons/absentee voters to the postal-ballot classes and amended rule 27A, including definitions concerning absentee voters, nodal officers, persons with disabilities, poll officers and senior citizens. It also amended related postal-ballot forms and procedures.

The amendment was made under section 60(c) of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, after consultation with the Election Commission.

Evidence grade: A.

### 3. Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2020, S.O. 1964(E), 19-06-2020

The official Gazette reproduction confirms S.O. 1964(E), dated 19-06-2020. It amended rule 27A to address COVID-19 suspect or affected persons, temporarily adjusted the senior-citizen age threshold for the relevant postal-ballot provision and inserted a definition of COVID-19 suspect or affected persons.

Official Lok Sabha parliamentary records confirm the instrument was laid under section 169(3) of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 on 03-02-2021.

Evidence grade: A.

### 4. Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2020, S.O. 3667(E), 19-10-2020

Official parliamentary material records a separate 2020 amendment instrument, S.O. 3667(E), dated 19-10-2020. The instrument amended rule 90 by substituting the table of maximum election-expense limits. The parliamentary record confirms the instrument as a separate Conduct of Elections Rules amendment from S.O. 1964(E).

Evidence grade: A for the parliamentary identification; the operative Gazette text should be retained as a direct-source retrieval target if later clause-level work requires it.

### 5. Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2022, S.O. 72(E), 06-01-2022

Official India Code/parliamentary records identify S.O. 72(E), dated 06-01-2022, as the Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2022. The instrument was made under section 77(3) read with section 169 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951. The official parliamentary record confirms laying before the House.

Evidence grade: A.

### 6. Conduct of Elections (Second Amendment) Rules, 2022, S.O. 2804(E), 17-06-2022

The official Rajya Sabha parliamentary record confirms S.O. 2804(E), dated 17-06-2022, and identifies it as the Conduct of Elections (Second Amendment) Rules, 2022 under section 169(3). The Gazette text confirms that the amendment substituted “spouse” for “wife” in rule 17(b), and records S.O. 72(E) as the then-last amendment.

Evidence grade: A.

### 7. Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2023, S.O. 3778(E), 23-08-2023

The official Gazette confirms S.O. 3778(E), dated 23-08-2023. The amendment inserted rule 18A on postal voting by voters on election duty at a facilitation centre, changed rule 24(2)(c) from “any gazetted officer” to “any Group A or Group B officer”, amended rule 27A(c) to use the statutory benchmark-disability definition from the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, changed the relevant senior-citizen threshold from 65 to 80 years and amended Form 13D.

Official Lok Sabha parliamentary records identify the instrument as laid on 20-09-2023.

Evidence grade: A.

### 8. Conduct of Elections (Amendment) Rules, 2024, G.S.R. 995(E), 01-03-2024

The official Gazette confirms G.S.R. 995(E), dated 01-03-2024. It amended rule 27A(e), replacing “above 80 years” with “above 85 years” for the relevant senior-citizen postal-ballot provision. The notification was made under section 60 read with section 169 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951.

Official Lok Sabha papers-laid material confirms the instrument was laid on 26-07-2024 under section 169(3).

Evidence grade: A.

### 9. Conduct of Elections (Second Amendment) Rules, 2024, S.O. 5517(E), 20-12-2024

The official Gazette confirms S.O. 5517(E), dated 20-12-2024. It amended rule 93(2)(a) by inserting the words “as specified in these rules” after “papers”. The notification states that the principal Rules were last amended by G.S.R. 995(E), dated 01-03-2024.

Official Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha parliamentary records confirm laying on 13-02-2025. The Lok Sabha record assigns Library paper no. LT2153/18/25.

Evidence grade: A.

## 2025-2026 targeted endpoint

Targeted official Gazette and parliamentary searches were run for Conduct of Elections amendment Rules for 2025 and 2026. No qualifying 2025 or 2026 Conduct of Elections amendment Rules instrument was retrieved in the defined searches. The result is retrieval silence, not a legal-absence conclusion.

The official Gazette search also continues to retrieve 2025 instruments issued under the Representation of the People Act, including election-result declarations and other election-related orders. Those are not amendments to the Conduct of Elections Rules and are excluded from the Rules amendment sequence.

## Project significance

The RPA interface remains conditional. The project should not infer that election-law conduct is a caste-atrocity offence or that the proposed legislation should create electoral consequences. The present Phase 1 purpose is current-law mapping and preservation of the separate electoral regime. The 2023 and 2024 Rules also demonstrate cross-regime interaction with disability-accessible voting and postal-ballot procedures, but no policy conclusion is drawn here.

## Controlled result

PH1-ISSUE-008 remains OPEN.

The Representation of the People later-instrument dependency is ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION. The 2019-2024 identified amendment instruments are now materially strengthened by primary Gazette and parliamentary evidence, including the previously unrecorded S.O. 1023(E), 26-02-2019 and the separate S.O. 3667(E), 19-10-2020. The targeted 2025-2026 searches did not retrieve a qualifying Conduct of Elections Rules amendment instrument. This is a qualified retrieval result, not legal absence.
