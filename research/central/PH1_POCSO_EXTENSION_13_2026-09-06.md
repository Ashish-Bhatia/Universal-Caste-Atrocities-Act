# PH1 Protection of Children from Sexual Offences, Extension 13

Date: 2026-09-06
Issue: PH1-ISSUE-008
Status: ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION

## Scope control

This extension performs only the authorized Central later-instrument search for the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 and Central Rules/instrument class beyond the prior qualified Rules endpoint, G.S.R. 165(E), dated 09-03-2020.

No State/UT research, earlier Central extension, Bill drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research is performed. The BNS/BNSS/BSA controlled freeze is not reopened.

## Controlled baseline boundary

The prior project endpoint is G.S.R. 165(E), 09-03-2020, Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Rules, 2020. The present extension treats that instrument only as the boundary for later-instrument searching and does not repeat its substantive contents.

## 1. Post-2018 Central amendment Acts

### Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (Amendment) Act, 2019, Act 25 of 2019

Classification: pre-existing post-2018 Central amending Act, already enacted before the prior Rules endpoint.

Legal effect: substantive amendment to the POCSO Act. The Act received assent on 05-08-2019 and was brought into force on 16-08-2019 by S.O. 2957(E), dated 16-08-2019. The commencement notification is an operative commencement instrument, not a further amendment Act.

Evidence grade: A.

This is not a new Extension 13 discovery beyond the G.S.R. 165(E) boundary. It is recorded only to distinguish the statutory amendment history from genuinely later instruments and to prevent reclassification of the 2019 Act as a post-2020 instrument.

Primary evidence: Government Gazette commencement notification S.O. 2957(E), 16-08-2019; current Ministry legislation page; India Code current Act record.

### Post-2020 Central amendment Acts

Targeted searches of official Gazette, Legislative Department, Ministry of Women and Child Development, India Code and parliamentary records did not retrieve an enacted Central amendment Act to the POCSO Act after the 2019 Amendment Act.

Classification: qualified negative-source retrieval result.

Legal effect: no new enacted amendment identified from the defined search. This is retrieval silence, not a legal-absence conclusion.

Evidence grade: A for the official-source search and retrieved current registers; the conclusion remains qualified.

## 2. Central Rules after G.S.R. 165(E), 09-03-2020

### Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Rules, 2020

No later Central amendment Rules instrument or replacement Rules instrument after G.S.R. 165(E), 09-03-2020 was retrieved in the defined official-source search.

The current Ministry of Women and Child Development legislation page lists the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Rules, 2020 and the 2019 Amendment Act, without displaying a later Central POCSO Rules amendment. NCPCR's current POCSO page likewise lists the 2020 Rules and the Act. India Code's current POCSO record exposes the Rules/notifications area but the retrieved current record does not display a later Central POCSO Rules instrument.

Classification: qualified negative-source retrieval endpoint.

Legal effect: the identified Central Rules endpoint remains G.S.R. 165(E), 09-03-2020, subject to qualification. No legal-absence conclusion is made.

Evidence grade: A for Ministry/NCPCR/India Code current-source indicators and Gazette-level search; no later Gazette instrument was retrieved.

## 3. Central notifications/orders expressly under POCSO statutory powers

The targeted search for post-09-03-2020 Central notifications/orders expressly made under POCSO statutory powers did not retrieve a qualifying Central instrument that amends, replaces or otherwise changes the operative POCSO Act/Rules machinery.

Classification: qualified negative-source retrieval result.

Important boundary: State/UT notifications designating POCSO Special Courts under section 28 are not Central instruments and are outside this extension. They must not be used to establish a Central later-instrument result.

The search also did not identify a post-2020 Central notification under section 45 that changes the 2020 Rules. No section 46 removal-of-difficulties order altering the Act was retrieved.

Evidence grade: A for the defined official-source search, with the result remaining qualified.

## 4. Parliamentary legislative-history material after the prior endpoint

### Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (Amendment) Bill, 2024

A Private Member's Bill further to amend the POCSO Act was introduced in the Rajya Sabha by Dr. Fauzia Khan. The official Rajya Sabha bill text identifies it as the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (Amendment) Bill, 2024. The official Rajya Sabha debate/synopsis records show consideration in 2025, including discussion on 05-12-2025; the business remained inconclusive.

Classification: Private Member's Bill / legislative activity, not enacted law.

Legal effect: none on the current POCSO statute unless and until enacted and brought into force. It must not be incorporated into the current-law baseline as an amendment.

Evidence grade: A.

### Other post-2020 parliamentary POCSO amendment activity

Targeted official parliamentary searches did not retrieve an additional enacted Central POCSO amendment after the 2019 Act. The 2024 Private Member's Bill is retained as the material post-endpoint legislative-history item located in the defined search.

Classification: qualified parliamentary-search result.

## 5. Administrative/programme instruments encountered

Government and official institutional material after 2020 contains POCSO implementation references, training material, child-protection schemes and court-related administrative measures. These were not classified as POCSO Act/Rules amendments because the retrieved instruments do not establish exercise of the Central Government's section 45 rule-making power or another POCSO statutory power altering the Act/Rules.

In particular, the centrally sponsored Fast Track Special Courts scheme and Mission Vatsalya materials are programme/implementation instruments. They are not treated as amendments to the POCSO Act or Rules.

Evidence grade: A/B depending on source, but not relied upon as legal amendment evidence.

## 6. Current official-source indicators

The Ministry of Women and Child Development's current legislation page, last updated 09-04-2026, lists the 2019 POCSO Amendment Act and the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Rules, 2020, alongside the principal Act. It does not display a later POCSO amendment Act or later POCSO Rules instrument.

NCPCR's current POCSO page lists the 2020 Rules and the 2012 Act. It does not display a later Central POCSO Rules instrument.

India Code's current POCSO record identifies Act 32 of 2012 and exposes its Rules/Notifications/Orders categories. The retrieved record does not provide a later Central POCSO Rules instrument after G.S.R. 165(E).

These are current-source indicators, not exhaustive Gazette registers.

## Controlled conclusion

POCSO Extension 13 is ADVANCED WITH QUALIFICATION for PH1-ISSUE-008.

Controlled later-instrument result:

- No post-2020 enacted Central amendment Act to POCSO was retrieved in the defined official-source search.
- No Central POCSO Rules amendment or replacement Rules instrument after G.S.R. 165(E), 09-03-2020 was retrieved.
- No post-2020 Central notification/order expressly made under POCSO statutory powers and altering the Act/Rules machinery was retrieved.
- The Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (Amendment) Bill, 2024 is retained as legislative activity, not enacted law; official parliamentary material shows consideration in 2025 remained inconclusive.
- Administrative/programme materials are retained separately and are not classified as Act/Rules amendments.

The identified Rules endpoint therefore remains G.S.R. 165(E), 09-03-2020, with a qualified negative-source result beyond that date.

This is retrieval silence, not legal absence. The project must not state that no later instrument exists solely on this search result.
