## Scope control

This extension is restricted to the final authoritative parliamentary laying-register/appendix and Gazette archival notification-number census for Prevention of Corruption Act section 29A. It does not reopen State/UT work, Extension 06 or Extension 07 searches, completed Central workstreams, the BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze, section 3(2)(v) threshold work, website verification, drafting, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research.

## Statutory control

India Code's current Prevention of Corruption Act record identifies section 29A as the Central rule-making power. Section 29A(1) requires notification in the Official Gazette. Section 29A(2) expressly includes section 9 commercial-organisation guidelines and section 19 prosecution-sanction guidelines among matters for which rules may provide. Section 29A(3) requires every rule made under the Act to be laid before each House of Parliament for the statutory period.

India Code currently displays a Rules/Regulations area for the Act but no Central rule entry. Its Notifications area displays the 26-07-2018 commencement notification. This is a repository-index result only and is not treated as proof of legal absence.

## Parliamentary repository census

### Parliament Digital Library / eParlib

Final targeted searches were run against the official Parliament Digital Library/eParlib corpus for combinations of:

- Prevention of Corruption Rules
- Prevention of Corruption (Amendment) Rules
- section 29A rules
- rules made under the Prevention of Corruption Act
- commercial-organisation guidelines under section 9
- prosecution-sanction guidelines under section 19
- Prevention of Corruption papers laid / laying

The retrieved eParlib material included Prevention of Corruption legislation and historical parliamentary proceedings, but no qualifying laying entry identifying a Central rule as made under section 29A was retrieved.

The repository itself demonstrates that parliamentary material records papers laid and statutory material, but the targeted corpus search did not yield a section 29A rule-laying record. This remains a retrieval result, not a legal-absence finding.

### Rajya Sabha official debate / papers-laid corpus

The official Rajya Sabha debate repository was searched for the same PCA rule, section 29A, commercial-organisation and prosecution-sanction laying terms. The repository provides a dedicated Papers Laid on the Table corpus and statutory-orders functionality. The targeted search did not retrieve a qualifying section 29A PCA rules laying item.

No notification number, laying date, Library paper number or rule title was identified from the retrieved Rajya Sabha corpus that would establish a section 29A rule.

## Gazette archival notification-number census

The final notification-number search covered the known Central notification patterns and title variants for the post-2018 section 29A period, including:

- Prevention of Corruption Rules
- Prevention of Corruption (Amendment) Rules
- Prevention of Corruption Act rules
- section 29A
- section 9 commercial-organisation guidelines
- section 19 prosecution-sanction guidelines
- G.S.R. / S.O. combinations with Prevention of Corruption terminology
- year-specific searches for 2019 through 2026

No qualifying Central Gazette instrument was retrieved whose operative text states that it is made under section 29A of the Prevention of Corruption Act.

The searches did retrieve unrelated instruments that refer to the Prevention of Corruption Act, including Lokpal rules and other statutory/service instruments. Those were excluded because their parent-power citations are under other Acts or administrative powers. They do not close the PCA section 29A Rules residual.

## Important exclusion

The Lokpal (Complaint) Rules, 2020, G.S.R. 148(E), dated 02-03-2020, expressly cite section 59 of the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013 as their rule-making authority. The fact that those rules define "offence" by reference to an offence punishable under the Prevention of Corruption Act does not make them rules under PCA section 29A. They are therefore excluded from the PCA Rules census.

Similarly, DoPT O.M. No. 372/19/2012-AVD-III dated 03-05-2012 and the earlier 2006 prosecution-sanction instructions remain administrative guidance. DoPT Annual Reports describe them as departmental OMs governing sanction processing. No retrieved document supplies a section 29A parent-power citation for those OMs.

## Controlled result

No qualifying section 29A Central Rules instrument, notification number, operative Gazette text or parliamentary laying record was retrieved in the final targeted parliamentary and Gazette archival census.

This does not establish that no such instrument exists. The authoritative repositories searched do not provide a demonstrated complete negative register sufficient to convert retrieval silence into legal absence.

Accordingly, PH1-ISSUE-008 remains OPEN. The PCA Rules residual is now controlled at a qualified negative-source endpoint rather than an asserted legal absence.

## Closure formulation authorised for project use

"As of 06-09-2026, targeted searches of the official India Code record, Parliament Digital Library/eParlib, Rajya Sabha official debate/papers-laid material, DoPT records, Legislative Department material and official Gazette search/archive pathways did not retrieve a Central Prevention of Corruption Act rule expressly made under section 29A, or a parliamentary laying record for such a rule. This is a qualified negative-source finding and is not evidence that no such rule exists. DoPT prosecution-sanction OMs remain administrative guidance unless a primary instrument establishes section 29A authority."
