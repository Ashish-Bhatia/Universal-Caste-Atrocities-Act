## Scope and authorization

This record implements the separately authorized Central-only Extension 15 workstream under `project-state/PH1_CENTRAL_PCA_SECTION3_AUTHORIZATION_2026-09-06_15.md` and decision `DEC-2026-09-06-CENTRAL-034`.

Scope is limited to Central Government notifications under section 3 of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 appointing/designating Special Judges from 01-01-2019 through 06-09-2026, and material later instruments affecting those Central appointments. State/UT section 3 notifications are excluded from the census.

PCA Extensions 06-08, Extension 14, the 31-05-2025 eGazette retrieval assessment, State/UT research, BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze and other excluded workstreams were not repeated.

## Statutory control

India Code records section 3 as the provision empowering the Central Government or State Government, by notification in the Official Gazette, to appoint Special Judges for specified areas, cases or groups of cases. Section 3(2) prescribes judicial eligibility. India Code also identifies the PCA as a Central Act administered by the Department of Personnel and Training. [A]

## Verification limitations

The direct official PDF for S.O. 4645(E) returned an HTTP 502 when opened during this session, although the official eGazette search result itself exposed the notification text, number, date, authority, enabling provision, appointed court, case number, file number and signatory. The direct PDF failure is therefore recorded as a retrieval limitation, not legal absence.

The S.O. 2769(E) notification was retrieved through an indexed Gazette record reproducing the official Gazette text. Direct official PDF retrieval was not independently completed in this extension. This limitation does not justify downgrading the substantive notification text below the official indexed primary-source evidence, but it prevents a claim of direct-PDF retrieval.
