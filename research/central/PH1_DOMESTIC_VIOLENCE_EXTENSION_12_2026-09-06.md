## Scope control

This extension advances the next authorized Priority 2 Central later-instrument dependency after Forest Rights: the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 and the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Rules, 2006. It does not reopen State/UT inventories, PCA Extensions 06-08, RPA Extension 09, Child and Adolescent Labour Extension 10, Forest Rights Extension 11, section 3(2)(v), the BNS/BNSS/BSA freeze, RPwD/JJ residuals, website verification, Bill drafting, policy analysis, constitutional analysis or Phase 2 case-law research.

## Baseline statutory position

India Code identifies the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 as Act 43 of 2005, enacted on 13-09-2005 and enforced on 26-10-2006 by S.O. 1776(E), dated 17-10-2006. The Act contains the principal definitions, domestic-violence framework, Protection Officer/service-provider machinery, protection/residence/monetary/custody/compensation/interim reliefs, jurisdiction and procedure, appeal, breach offence, cognizance/proof, Protection Officer liability, non-derogation and Central rule-making power.

India Code's current Act record lists one Central Rules entry, the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Rules, 2006, dated 14-10-2006 in the displayed index, while the operative Gazette notification is G.S.R. 644(E), dated 17-10-2006. The project treats the Gazette instrument as controlling for the Rules' instrument identity and date.

## Post-2018 Central amendment identified

### Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, Act 34 of 2019

The primary India Code text of the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 records that Schedule V item 87 amended section 1(2) of the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 by omitting the words "except the State of Jammu and Kashmir". The current India Code Act text carries the corresponding footnote and states the amendment took effect on 31-10-2019.

Classification: Central amending Act affecting territorial extent.
Evidence grade: A.

This is the controlling post-2018 Central Act amendment for the PWDVA later-instrument history located in this extension. It is analogous in classification to the Forest Rights correction, but it is recorded independently for the Domestic Violence Act.

## Central Rules endpoint

The Ministry of Women and Child Development Government-hosted Gazette text identifies G.S.R. 644(E), dated 17-10-2006, as the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Rules, 2006, made under section 37 of the Act. The Rules came into force on 26-10-2006.

The current Savitribai Phule National Institute of Women and Child Development legislation page, under the Ministry of Women and Child Development, lists the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 and the Rules under that Act. It does not display a later Central amendment Rules instrument.

India Code's current PWDVA record likewise displays a single Central Rules entry and a single 17-10-2006 notification entry. The MHA current notifications register identifies S.O. 1838(E), 26-10-2007 as the Central Article 239(1) delegation instrument for the Act in all Union Territories. It is administrative/constitutional machinery, not an amendment to the Act or Rules.

Targeted official-source searches did not retrieve a later Central amendment to the PWDVA Rules after G.S.R. 644(E). This is retrieval silence, not legal absence. The controlled Rules endpoint therefore remains G.S.R. 644(E), with qualification.

## Other later legislative activity

### Protection of Women from Domestic Violence (Amendment) Bill, 2025

The Official Gazette publication of Bill No. 90 of 2025 records a Private Member's Bill proposing amendments to sections 5 and 9, including access to available self-help groups and skill-development schemes. This is a Bill, not an enacted amendment Act, and it therefore does not alter the current-law baseline.

The Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs Annual Report 2025-26 lists the Bill as introduced in Lok Sabha on 05-12-2025 by Shrimati Smita Uday Wagh and identifies its subject as amendment of sections 5 and 9. Parliamentary status evidence available for the current record classifies it as pending. It must be retained as legislative activity, not current law.

A separate Protection of Women from Domestic Violence (Amendment) Bill, 2024 was introduced in Rajya Sabha on 07-02-2025 by Shri Sandosh Kumar P. The official Rajya Sabha debate record confirms introduction. It is likewise a Bill and not an enacted amendment to the 2005 Act.

## Central administrative instrument

S.O. 1838(E), 26-10-2007 remains a verified Central Article 239(1) delegation instrument under which the Administrator of every Union Territory exercises the powers and discharges the functions of the State Government under the PWDVA within that Union Territory. It predates the later-instrument cutoff but remains relevant to the controlled instrument history and is not misclassified as a Rules amendment.
