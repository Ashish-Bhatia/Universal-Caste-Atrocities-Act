## Verification findings

- Rule 9 nodal designation is verified in the latest located State annual submission: Commissioner of Social Welfare / Director of Social Welfare.
- State Protection Cell structure is verified in the 2023 State submission: State Headquarters PCR Cell under ADGP CID, with sanctioned strength of 1 DIG, 7 DSPs, 9 Inspectors, 13 SIs, 13 HCs and 23 PCs, plus 7 regional CID offices.
- State-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee is verified as reconstituted by G.O.Ms.No.2, Social Welfare (POA), dated 01-02-2021. The 2023 State submission records its composition and nil meetings during 2023.
- Special Court operation is verified by the 2023 State submission and current Andhra Pradesh High Court judicial records. The 2023 count of 14 Special Courts plus 1 Exclusive Special Court is not treated as a 2026 statewide count.
- Prosecutor architecture is verified by the 2023 State submission. A complete current appointment register remains open.
- Relief workflow is verified through G.O.Ms.No.95, the current Social Welfare page, and the 2023 State submission's SNA/direct-credit description. No later statewide replacement rate order was located in the targeted search.
- Circular/manual/digital workflow elements are verified in the 2023 State submission, including FIR access through the AP Police citizen portal/app, victim information, direct-credit relief processing, status communication and prosecutor briefing.
- State Gazette instrument identity for the 2021 Model Contingency Plan is verified through the State's own annual submission, but the Gazette copy itself remains a primary-source retrieval target.
