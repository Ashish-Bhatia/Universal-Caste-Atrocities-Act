## Source-control notes

1. NG-STATE-001 to NG-STATE-004 are the strongest national official baseline sources located for statistical and constitutional-status screening.
2. NG-STATE-009 and NG-STATE-010 are primary judicial corroboration of current operational use of the PoA Act in Nagaland-related proceedings.
3. No current Nagaland Gazette establishing the exact Rule 8 Protection Cell, Rule 9 Nodal Officer, Rule 10 Special Officer, PoA Special Court, Exclusive Special Court, SPP/ESPP or current V&MC matrix was verified in this pass.
4. Absence from the located sources is not treated as proof of institutional absence.
5. The 2022 Ministry report was identified as an official publication, but Nagaland-specific implementation text could not be reliably extracted in this pass because direct PDF retrieval timed out. This remains an open verification item.
