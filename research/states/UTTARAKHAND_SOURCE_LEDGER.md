## Verification notes

1. Repository search before research found no Uttarakhand-specific inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. Generic/master files were not treated as jurisdiction-specific artifacts.
2. The 2022 MoSJ&E Section 21(4) report is the principal detailed institutional snapshot located for Uttarakhand.
3. Historical Rule 9 and court findings are retained as historical unless corroborated by current instruments.
4. The 2022 V&MC and charge-sheet figures are reporting-period data and are not treated as 2026 compliance findings.
5. Zero identified atrocity-prone districts in the 2022 report is not treated as a current 2026 absence conclusion.
6. Search silence for current Special Police Stations, Rule 8 staffing, current Rule 9/10 instruments and prosecutor rosters is not treated as absence.
7. The current Uttarakhand Social Welfare PoA page is evidence of a live departmental implementation page, but its displayed relief table expressly refers to the 2011 Rules and therefore requires reconciliation with later central amendments before current-rate conclusions are drawn.
8. Current judicial decisions are used only as institutional corroboration. No Phase 2 merits synthesis was undertaken.
9. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains separate and unfrozen.
10. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness remains a separate national workstream.
