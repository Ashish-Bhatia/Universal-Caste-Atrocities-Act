## Verification notes

1. Repository search found no pre-existing Andaman and Nicobar-specific jurisdiction artifact before this inventory. Generic/master files were not treated as jurisdiction-specific artifacts.
2. The 2022 MoSJ&E Section 21(4) report is the principal recent institutional snapshot located.
3. The Ministry's 2022 report records three cases completed within sixty days and none later than sixty days. This is reporting-period evidence only.
4. The 2022 report records no State-level V&MC meeting and one district-level meeting, and identifies Sub-Divisional V&MCs in four sub-divisions except South Andaman.
5. The 2022 report records a Protection Cell composed of DGP, IGP, district SPs and Director (Tribal Welfare), but does not establish current 2026 staffing or a current notification.
6. Current Rule 9 Nodal Officer appointment evidence was not independently retrieved. Historical Ministry lists do not establish current absence or current appointment.
7. Search silence for dedicated Special Police Stations is not treated as proof of absence.
8. Identified atrocity-prone areas are retained as reporting-period evidence. Current 2026 status remains open.
9. The Court of District and Session Judge, A&N Islands is treated as a documented PoA Special Court, with 2025 and 2026 judicial decisions providing current operational corroboration.
10. The 2025 and 2026 judicial materials are used only for institutional corroboration. Phase 2 merits synthesis was not undertaken.
11. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands Protection of Aboriginal Tribes Regulation, 1956 is treated as a jurisdiction-specific related-law interface because official and judicial material documents its operation alongside the PoA Act.
12. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains separate and unfrozen.
13. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness remains separate.
14. Current A&N-specific relief/payment statistics were not independently extracted in this pass. This is a retrieval limitation, not an absence finding.
