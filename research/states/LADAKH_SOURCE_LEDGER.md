## Source-control notes
- Historical 2020/2021 and Census material is separated from 2026 currentness.
- The 2021 Rule 8 Protection Cell, Rule 9 Nodal Officer and UT-level V&MC orders are primary operative instruments located in the UT repository.
- The 18-03-2026 Kargil V&MC report is treated as current evidence of local committee operation, not as proof of a complete UT-wide 2026 committee matrix.
- No current 2026 replacement/reconstitution instrument for the 2021 Rule 8, Rule 9 or UT-level V&MC orders was located in the targeted official-source search. Search non-location is not treated as legal absence.
- No current 2026 PoA-specific Special Court notification, Exclusive Special Court notification or PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment roster was located in the targeted official-source search. This remains OPEN.
- The DoSJE relief table records zero beneficiaries under the Centrally Sponsored Scheme for Ladakh for 2020-21 through 2024-25. This is not proof of zero relief from UT funds.
- The DoSJE Central Assistance table records 0.08 crore for Ladakh in 2024-25. Receipt of Central Assistance does not establish case-level expenditure or victim payment.
- BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains a separate national Phase 1 workstream.
- No D-grade source is used as the sole basis of a material conclusion.
- No Bill drafting, policy-superiority/necessity analysis, constitutional-validity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was undertaken.
