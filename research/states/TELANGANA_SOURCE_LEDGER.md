## Verification notes

1. The 2020 Ministry Telangana section is the principal directly retrievable detailed institutional snapshot.
2. The 2022 Telangana figures used for V&MCs, charge-sheets, legal aid and Exclusive Special Courts are secondary reproductions of the Ministry's 2022 report in this session and remain C grade pending direct PDF verification.
3. The 66-village identified-area list is an official 2020 reporting snapshot published by PIB in 2022. It is not treated as a 2026 current census.
4. Current 2026 district government pages confirm continuing PoA/PCR administrative functions and relief/legal-aid delivery, but do not establish the full State-wide machinery.
5. The 2024 Rachakonda police report is direct State evidence of a post-BNS offence being registered with a PoA provision. It does not establish a complete transition protocol.
6. Current named Rule 9/Rule 10 appointments, Protection Cell staffing, Special Police Station status, court/prosecutor rosters, V&MC compliance, payment workflow and later annual reports remain open.
7. Search silence is not treated as absence.
8. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remain separate national workstreams.
