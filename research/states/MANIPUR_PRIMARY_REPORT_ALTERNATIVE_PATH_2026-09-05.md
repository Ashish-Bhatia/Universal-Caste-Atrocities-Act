# Manipur, Central 2022 PoA Annual Report Alternative Authoritative Retrieval Path

Date: 2026-09-05
Phase: Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Jurisdiction: Manipur

## Objective

Obtain the complete Manipur section of the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment's 2022 report under section 21(4) of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989, without substituting secondary reproductions for the primary report.

## Primary Ministry provenance

The Ministry's current Annual Reports on Prevention Atrocity Act archive lists "Annual report under the PoA Act for the year 2022", published 17-09-2024. The previously identified primary PDF URL is:

https://socialjustice.gov.in/writereaddata/UploadFile/62901726565718.pdf

Direct retrieval of the PDF through the available web retrieval path again failed with HTTP 502. The PDF bytes were therefore not obtained.

## Alternative authoritative Government retrieval path

The official Rajya Sabha record for 07-08-2024 records the laying of the 2022 Annual Report under section 21(4) of the PoA Act. It identifies the accompanying parliamentary-library reference as L.T. 506/18/24. The official record states that the Minister of State in the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment laid the annual report for 2022, together with the statement giving reasons for delay.

Primary parliamentary source:
https://cms.rajyasabha.nic.in/UploadedFiles/Debates/OfficialDebatesDatewise/Floor/265/782024/20240807.pdf

The Rajya Sabha record is an authoritative confirmation that the 2022 report was laid before Parliament and provides the Library reference. It does not itself expose the report's contents or a downloadable copy of the report. Searches for the exact Library reference did not produce a public report file. Therefore this alternative authoritative path verifies parliamentary provenance and identifies the Library record, but it does not yet deliver the complete Manipur section.

## Primary extraction status

The project must not promote the Table 30 or Table 31 reproductions to primary evidence. The previously recorded Grade C secondary transcriptions remain secondary only.

## Secondary material located during verification

The 2026 NCDHR publication reproduces Table 30 and Table 31 and attributes them to the Ministry's 2022 section 21(4) report. Its Manipur Table 30 row is rendered as 16 districts, 2 mandatory State-level meetings, blank/zero-format conducted State meetings, 64 mandatory district-level meetings and a blank conducted-district value. Its Table 31 Manipur row is blank/hyphen for charge-sheets within 60 days, charge-sheets after 60 days, atrocity-prone districts and legal aid. These remain Grade C and are not treated as primary verification.

## Control decision

The alternative Rajya Sabha path is recorded as an authoritative provenance/retrieval lead, not as completion of the requested primary extraction. The annual-report residual therefore remains open.

Historical reporting periods remain separate from 2026 status. Manipur remains separate from Maharashtra. Central later-instrument completeness remains separate from BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification. Search or retrieval silence is not treated as institutional absence.
