The debate record exposes the Library reference but not the report bytes.

## 2. Current/recent Special Court reconciliation

A primary judicial record provides additional evidence relevant to the court workstream. A Manipur High Court order dated 26-09-2024 records that the `Court of Special Judge (SC&ST) PoA, Thoubal` existed and had heard the relevant bail proceedings. The order also records that, following the Supreme Court direction and the Gauhati High Court notification, the original records were transmitted to the Special Judge, CBI & NIA, Assam for the transferred Manipur-violence cases.

A separate 09-05-2025 judicial record also reproduces the 25-07-2023 remand order identifying the court as `Spl. Judge SC & ST POA, Thoubal` and records that the Special Judge (SC & ST POA), Thoubal handled the relevant proceedings before transfer.

These records establish a court-level historical/recent snapshot through 2025. They do NOT establish a complete 2026 statewide Special/Exclusive Special Court notification or jurisdiction matrix. The court issue therefore remains open for current statewide institutional verification.
