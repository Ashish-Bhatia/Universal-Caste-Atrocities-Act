## Source-control notes

- Currentness is separated from historical reporting. The 2015 PoA implementation report is not treated as a 2026 institutional census.
- The 2024 Lakshadweep annual-report submission is a Government document, but the accessible copy was retrieved from a secondary host. It is therefore graded B pending direct primary retrieval.
- The 2026 Protection Scheduled Tribes Regulation amendment material is a draft consultation notice. It is not treated as enacted law.
- Current UT pages establish administrative architecture but do not substitute for PoA-specific appointment, designation, notification or staffing instruments when instrument-level verification is required.
- Current Central scheme zero-relief figures are scheme-specific and do not establish zero UT-funded relief.
- NCRB figures are reported police/crime statistics. They do not establish under-reporting, legal merits or implementation effectiveness.
- Search non-location is not treated as legal absence.
- No D-grade source is used as the sole basis of a material conclusion.
- No Bill drafting, policy-superiority/necessity analysis, constitutional-validity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was undertaken.
