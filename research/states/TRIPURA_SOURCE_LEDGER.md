## Verification notes

1. The 2022 MoSJ&E Tripura report is the principal directly retrievable detailed institutional snapshot.
2. The 2022 state annual-report annexure is treated as B grade because the repository copy is hosted by a civil-society archive, although the document itself is certified by the Tripura State Public Information Officer.
3. The Protection Cell record is materially contradictory: the Ministry narrative says a Cell was constituted in the SC Welfare Department, while the certified state annexure answers No to the Rule 8 Protection Cell setup question. This is preserved as an explicit contradiction, not resolved by inference.
4. The same certified state annexure states that no Special Police Station is set up in Tripura and that registered SC/ST cases are investigated by officers not below DSP rank. This is a 2022 snapshot, not a 2026 census.
5. The 2022 report says there was no specific atrocity-prone area. This is retained as a reporting snapshot and not treated as proof of present absence.
6. Current Government of Tripura directories confirm an active SC Welfare administrative layer but do not establish the Rule 9 appointment instrument.
7. Current eCourts material confirms eight district court sites but does not itself establish PoA-specific Special Court designation.
8. The 2025 Tripura High Court PoA-related order is used only as current institutional/judicial corroboration. Phase 2 case-law research has not begun.
9. Search silence is not treated as absence.
10. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remain separate national workstreams.
