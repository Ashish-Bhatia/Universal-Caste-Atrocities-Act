## Central 2022 PoA annual-report extraction status

The official Ministry archive confirms publication of the 2022 PoA annual report on 17-09-2024. Direct machine retrieval of the report's Manipur section was not successful in this session because the Ministry archive/PDF endpoint did not expose the document contents through the available retrieval path. A secondary reproduction of one 2022 table, expressly sourced to the Ministry's Section 21(4) report, provides the following Manipur-specific entries: no reported charge-sheets within 60 days, no reported charge-sheets after 60 days, no reported atrocity-prone districts, and no reported legal-aid figure. These hyphens must be treated as non-reporting/blank data, not as zero activity. Primary extraction of the complete Manipur section remains open and high priority.

## Verification implications

1. The NCSC 2023 State report materially narrows the current-court residual by providing a primary reporting-period record for Ukhrul, and materially clarifies that the 2019 seven-jurisdiction SPP order cannot be treated as the only later reporting evidence. It does not establish what changed after 2023.
2. The NCSC 2023 report's Nil entries for the remaining districts are reporting responses and are not treated as proof that those districts lacked a Special Court or SPP before or after the reporting period.
3. The 2026-27 State budget confirms current public expenditure architecture for SC/ST/BC welfare. It does not establish a PoA victim-relief head, sanction workflow, payment performance or case-level expenditure.
4. The current Central archive establishes availability of the 2022 PoA annual report. The newly extracted secondary reproduction narrows the 2022 reporting residual but does not replace the primary report. The four reproduced Manipur fields are all blank/hyphen entries, not zero values.
5. The BPRD 01-01-2024 zero special-purpose PoA police-station figure and the 21-11-2023 Protection Cell finding remain dated snapshots. Nothing in this addendum converts them into a 2026 absence conclusion.
6. The November 2024 Chief Secretary review provides a recent corroborative lead referring to the functioning/strengthening of the Protection Cell and Special Police Stations. Because the underlying minutes/order were not retrieved, it is not treated as a primary current institutional instrument.
7. The 2021 secondary report of 10 Special Police Stations and the official 01-01-2024 BPRD figure of zero create a documented temporal/source discrepancy. It requires instrument-level reconciliation and must not be resolved by treating either figure as a 2026 census.
8. The 2024 MHA dispatch record confirms SC/ST-W correspondence addressed to the Manipur DGP, but does not itself establish a Protection Cell, Special Police Station or staffing structure.
