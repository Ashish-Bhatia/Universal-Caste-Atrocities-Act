## Status

Jharkhand is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. Current official evidence establishes dedicated SC/ST police-unit presence in multiple districts, current Special Court judicial assignments, a 2026 cabinet decision creating three District Judge-level Special Court posts, a 2023 State decision authorizing lower-rank investigation of PoA cases, current State DBT/treasury infrastructure and relevant land, local-government and labour interfaces. It does not establish a complete 2026 instrument census. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence. The 2023 investigation authorization is a high-priority verification item and is not treated as automatically displacing Rule 7.
