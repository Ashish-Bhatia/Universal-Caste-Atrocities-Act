## Verification notes

1. The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal recent implementation snapshot.
2. 2022 figures are reporting-period evidence, not 2026 compliance findings.
3. Rule 9 and Rule 10 architecture is documented, but current named appointments require later instrument verification.
4. 2022-2023 State atrocity-prone village counts differ from the 2020 PIB snapshot. They remain separate series.
5. The 2022 14-district Exclusive Special Court count and later secondary 17-district count remain separate pending direct current census verification.
6. 2025-2026 Gazette notices establish continuing appointments but not a complete statewide current roster.
7. 2026 judicial references are jurisdictional/institutional corroboration only.
8. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains a separate national residual.
