## Verification notes

1. Repository search found no pre-existing Chandigarh-specific inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. The only search hit was the untouched master State Implementation Inventory, which was not modified.
2. Chandigarh is treated as a single-district Union Territory. This is relevant when assessing the V&MC structure, but district/sub-divisional compliance is not inferred from that fact.
3. The 16 October 2015 Nodal Officer notification expressly supersedes the 2002 Personnel Department order. The 2022 report repeats the Secretary Social Welfare role.
4. The 17 April 2015 Rule 8 Protection Cell order is primary evidence of composition and functions. Later reports use DGP/IGP supervisory terminology; current 2026 staffing remains open.
5. The 2021 administrative compilation records no Special Police Station and no Exclusive Special Court/Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor at that time. This is not treated as a current 2026 absence conclusion.
6. The 2022 report states no atrocity-prone area. Earlier reports also state no atrocity-prone area. This remains historical/reporting-period evidence pending current instrument verification.
7. The 6 March 2024 judicial notification supersedes the 2016 Special Court designation and is treated as the controlling current court-designation instrument located in this pass.
8. Central assistance released and case-level relief are distinct reporting concepts. The 2024-25 central dataset records zero persons provided relief under its scheme measure for Chandigarh while the 2022 annual report records four victims provided relief during 2022. No reconciliation conclusion is drawn.
9. Search silence for current Special Police Stations, later annual reports, current prosecutor rosters or current payment records is not treated as proof of absence.
10. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains separate and unfrozen.
11. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness remains separate.
12. The 2024 Dinesh Jangra decision is used only for institutional/current judicial corroboration. Phase 2 merits synthesis was not undertaken.
