## Verification notes

1. The 2022 Section 21(4) figures are reporting-period evidence, not 2026 status.
2. Rajasthan Police monthly statistics use State police reporting categories and should not be mechanically substituted for the Central Section 21(4) series.
3. Current Rule 9 and Rule 10 orders were not located in the first-pass search.
4. Current full court/prosecutor and police-cell establishment orders were not located.
5. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
6. The source ledger does not include Phase 2 case-law research. RJ-STATE-027 is limited to recent jurisdictional corroboration.
