## Verification notes

1. Repository search found no pre-existing West Bengal-specific inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum. Generic/master files were not treated as jurisdiction-specific artifacts.
2. The 2022 MoSJ&E Section 21(4) report is the principal recent institutional snapshot.
3. Historical officer identities and instruments are not treated as current without current appointment evidence.
4. The 2022 charge-sheet, V&MC and atrocity-prone-area data are reporting-period findings only.
5. Search silence for current Special Police Stations, Rule 8 staffing, Rule 10 instruments and prosecutor rosters is not treated as absence.
6. The 22 Special Court figure is retained as a 2024 parliamentary answer using State/NCRB information, not as a complete 2026 court census.
7. Current judicial and police decisions are used only for institutional corroboration; Phase 2 merits synthesis was not undertaken.
8. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains separate and unfrozen.
9. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness remains separate.
