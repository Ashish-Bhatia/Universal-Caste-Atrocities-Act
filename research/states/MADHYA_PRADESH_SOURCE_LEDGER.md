## Status

Madhya Pradesh is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. Current official State sources establish an active PoA scheme/service layer, current Special Judge postings, current PoA-related police/prosecution training, and State legal/service repositories. Official Central State-specific reports establish a substantial implementation architecture including an ADGP-led Protection Cell, 51 Special Police Stations, State/District monitoring committees, Secretary-level Rule 9 nodal architecture and historical Rule 7 DSP investigation. Current primary appointment, court, prosecutor, committee, relief/payment, sensitive-area, SOP/digital, related-law and post-BNSS instruments remain open. Historical reporting periods are not treated as a 2026 census. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
