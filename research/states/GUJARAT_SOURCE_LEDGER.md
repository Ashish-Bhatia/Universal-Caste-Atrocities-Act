## Status

Gujarat is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. The official State source base establishes substantial administrative, police, committee, court, prosecutor, relief, awareness/statistical and related-law architecture. It does not establish a complete 2026 instrument census. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
