## Source-control notes

- No D-grade source is used as the sole basis for a material conclusion.
- Historical reporting is kept separate from 2026 status.
- Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
- The 11 Special Court figure is retained as a Parliamentary historical finding, not a current 2026 designation.
- Current Rule 9 incumbent, current Rule 10 instrument, current Special Court/ESPP corpus and case-level relief workflow remain open.
- BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains outside this jurisdiction pass.
- No Bill drafting, policy-superiority/necessity analysis, constitutional-validity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was undertaken.
