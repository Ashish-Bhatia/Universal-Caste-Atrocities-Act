## Source-control note

Currentness is assessed separately from instrument existence. The 2013/2016 instruments remain authoritative evidence of their contents, but they do not establish the identity of current office-holders in 2026. The 2022 annual report is primary for its reporting period. BPRD 2024 is dated police-organization corroboration, not a 2026 census.
