## Verification notes

1. No pre-existing Uttar Pradesh-specific inventory, source ledger, issue register or decision addendum was found in the repository before research. Generic/master files were not treated as jurisdiction-specific artifacts.
2. The 2022 MoSJ&E Section 21(4) report is the principal detailed institutional snapshot for UP.
3. The 2022 report records a State-level Special Investigation Cell and district-level Special Investigation Cells, with DySP investigation. It does not by itself establish a 2026 Special Police Station census.
4. Ministry reporting distinguishes SC/ST Protection Cells from separately enumerated Special Police Stations. Uttar Pradesh is not included in the five-State Special Police Station list in the retrieved compilation.
5. The historical Rule 9 and Rule 10 findings are retained as historical. Current named appointments and instruments remain open.
6. The 2022 V&MC figures are reporting-period data. No inference is made about 2026 meeting compliance.
7. The 65 Exclusive Special Court figure is a historical reporting snapshot. 2026 High Court notifications corroborate ongoing PoA Special Judge appointments but do not replace a complete current district census.
8. Relief and central-assistance figures are scheme-reporting data and do not establish individual statutory payment compliance.
9. Current Directorate of Prosecution material establishes an active prosecution administration and an SC/ST Act portal category, not a current named SPP/ESPP roster.
10. Search silence is not treated as absence.
11. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remain separate national workstreams.
12. No Phase 2 merits research was undertaken. Current judicial sources are used only as institutional corroboration.
