## Status

Maharashtra is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. Current/recent official State material establishes a dedicated Civil Rights Protection police function, recent establishment decisions, State PoA legislation/rules publication, prevention guidelines, victim-relief scheme architecture, current State Police statistical publication and SC/ST institutional oversight. Official Central reports provide reporting-period Rule 9, Special Court, Exclusive Special Court and atrocity-prone-area evidence, while current Central scheme data provides relief and assistance figures through 2024-25. The current 2026 instrument census remains incomplete. Historical reporting is not treated as a 2026 census, and search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
