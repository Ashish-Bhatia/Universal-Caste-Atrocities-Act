## Current status synthesis

Manipur requires unusually strict separation of reporting periods. Historical Central records establish Rule 9 and historical Special Court designations, but current State sources do not yet establish a complete 2026 PoA-specific institutional matrix. Current Police records establish that PoA provisions continue to be invoked and are being combined with BNS provisions after 01-07-2024. A 2019 primary State order establishes Special Public Prosecutor appointments for seven Sessions Court jurisdictions, but current 2026 replacement/continuation orders remain unverified. The 2025 Parliament data states that Manipur did not seek Central assistance under the PCR/PoA Centrally Sponsored Scheme in FY 2022-23 to 2024-25, so zero Central assistance must not be treated as zero State relief.

No conclusion is made about current absence of a Protection Cell, Special Police Stations, V&MCs, Rule 10 officers, Special/Exclusive Special Courts, current SPP/ESPP arrangements, PoA-specific digital systems, Manual Scavengers implementation, bonded-labour committees or post-BNS/BNSS/BSA State instructions unless supported by a primary current instrument. Search silence is not proof of absence.
