## Current status synthesis

Manipur requires unusually strict separation of reporting periods. Historical Central records establish Rule 9 and historical Special Court designations, but current State sources do not yet establish a complete 2026 PoA-specific institutional matrix. Current Police records establish that PoA provisions continue to be invoked and are being combined with BNS provisions after 01-07-2024. A 2019 primary State order establishes Special Public Prosecutor appointments for seven Sessions Court jurisdictions, but current 2026 replacement/continuation orders remain unverified.

The NCSC 2023 report adds a later reporting-period court/prosecutor snapshot for Ukhrul. The November 2024 Chief Secretary review report provides a recent corroborative lead referring to Protection Cell and Special Police Station functioning/strengthening, but its underlying official record has not been retrieved. The 2021 secondary report of 10 Special Police Stations conflicts with the official BPRD 01-01-2024 zero figure. The discrepancy is retained for instrument-level resolution and is not converted into a current absence/presence conclusion.

The 2025 Parliament data states that Manipur did not seek Central assistance under the PCR/PoA Centrally Sponsored Scheme in FY 2022-23 to 2024-25, so zero Central assistance must not be treated as zero State relief. The 2025-26 Ministry Annual Report further states that FY 2025-26 victim-relief data will be received with FY 2026-27 proposals, so a current State relief count remains unresolved.

No conclusion is made about current absence of a Protection Cell, Special Police Stations, V&MCs, Rule 10 officers, Special/Exclusive Special Courts, current SPP/ESPP arrangements, PoA-specific digital systems, Manual Scavengers implementation, bonded-labour committees or post-BNS/BNSS/BSA State instructions unless supported by a primary current instrument. Search silence is not proof of absence.
