## Verification notes

1. The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal recent State implementation snapshot located for Sikkim.
2. The 2022 figures are reporting-period evidence and are not treated as 2026 compliance findings.
3. The State Acts and Rules page establishes the existence of several State instruments, but the linked 2022 Special Court and 2020 Public Prosecutor files were not all retrievable in the first pass. Their current operative content therefore remains partly unverified.
4. Historical Rule 10 appointments are not substituted for current appointments.
5. No identified atrocity-prone area in 2022 is not treated as proof that no such area exists in 2026.
6. Sikkim Protection Cell evidence is stronger than evidence on dedicated Special Police Stations. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
7. State police/NCRB crime statistics and Section 21(4) implementation statistics are retained as separate series.
8. SK-STATE-018 is jurisdictional corroboration only and does not open Phase 2 case-law research.
9. Central later-instrument/current-law completeness and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remain separate workstreams.
