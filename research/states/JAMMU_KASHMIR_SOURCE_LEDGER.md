## Source-control notes
- Historical reporting periods are separated from 2026 status.
- The 2022 Rule 8 contradiction is preserved and treated as an open issue.
- Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.
- 2022 Special Court/SPP data are not asserted as complete 2026 rosters.
- BNS/BNSS/BSA transition verification remains a separate national workstream.
- No D-grade source is used as the sole basis for a material conclusion.
- No Bill drafting, policy-superiority/necessity analysis, constitutional-validity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was undertaken.
