## Source notes

1. MZ-STATE-005 through MZ-STATE-017 derive from one State Annual Report package for calendar year 2023. They should be cited as separate propositions but not treated as independent reporting events.
2. MZ-STATE-001 and MZ-STATE-002 are historical instruments. They establish prior State arrangements and do not prove current 2026 office-holder or committee status.
3. MZ-STATE-003 remains the operative-looking Special Court instrument because the 2023 State return repeats the same two-court structure. A later superseding notification was not located in this pass.
4. No source in this ledger establishes a complete September 2026 census of Rule 9, Rule 10, V&MC or SPP personnel.
5. The 2023 State return's NIL case/relief data must not be conflated with NCRB crime statistics or national annual-report tables that use different reporting constructs.
