## New verified risk observations

- The SC/ST Act's 2025 consolidated text still contains IPC/CrPC references, while the IPC and CrPC were repealed/re-enacted by BNS and BNSS. The Ministry of Law and Justice's 16-07-2024 notification S.O. 2790(E) is therefore a material transition instrument.
- Section 3(2)(va) continues to operate through a Schedule expressed in IPC offence terminology in the 2025 consolidation. A BNS mapping must be explicit and source-controlled.
- The Act's section 18 and section 18A use CrPC section 438 terminology, while BNSS section 482 governs anticipatory bail. This is a high-priority interface issue, not a constitutional conclusion.
- The Rules' 2018 “voluntarily” definition uses IPC section 39, creating a corresponding post-2024 transition item.
