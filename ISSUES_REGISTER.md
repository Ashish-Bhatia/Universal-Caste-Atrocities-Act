## Existing risk observations

- The SC/ST Act's 2025 consolidated text still contains IPC/CrPC references. S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, is therefore a material transition instrument.
- Section 3(2)(va) continues to operate through a Schedule expressed in IPC terminology in the 2025 consolidation. BNS mapping must be explicit.
- Section 18 and section 18A use CrPC section 438 terminology, while BNSS section 482 is the current anticipatory-bail provision. This remains a later BNSS interface issue.
- Rule 7 contains a special 60-day investigation/charge-sheet framework, requiring BNSS comparison.
- Rule 12(5) preserves other-law compensation rights, requiring later duplication analysis.
- Annexure-II contains a material source-reference discrepancy requiring primary-source resolution.
