## Newly Verified Bonded Labour Findings

- BLSA Act 19 of 1976 was enacted 09-02-1976 and is deemed in force from 25-10-1975.
- The identified amending Act is the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Amendment Act, 1985 (Act 73 of 1985), which added the Explanation to s.2(g) concerning specified contract and inter-State migrant labour situations.
- BLSA ss.2(g), 4, 16 and 18 strongly overlap with SC/ST Act s.3(1)(h) on forced/bonded labour, but the statutory ingredients differ. BLSA is organised around bonded labour/bonded debt and does not require SC/ST status. SC/ST s.3(1)(h) requires protected caste status of the victim and non-SC/ST status of the offender, but does not require a bonded-debt structure.
- BLSA ss.6-9 provide debt extinguishment, property restoration, homestead protection and post-extinguishment debt-payment controls with no direct SC/ST Act equivalent.
- BLSA ss.13-14 and Rule 7 substantially overlap with SC/ST Act s.21 and Rules 16-18 in vigilance, rehabilitation, surveys, offence monitoring and reporting, but committee mandates differ.
- BLSA s.15 is a debt-classification burden rule. It is distinct from SC/ST Act s.8 special presumptions and from BSA general burdens.
- BLSA ss.21-22 create a materially different trial/bail architecture from SC/ST law: Executive Magistrate trial after statutory conferment and summary trial, with all BLSA offences cognizable and bailable, versus the SC/ST Special/Exclusive Special Court and special bail architecture.
- BLSA s.23 contains dedicated company-liability provisions. No dedicated equivalent appears in the extracted SC/ST Act structure.
- BLSA Rules contain seven rules. Rules 3-4 regulate district/sub-divisional Vigilance Committee membership and tenure; Rules 5-6 prescribe the property-restoration application route and historical 90-day period; Rule 7 requires registers covering freed bonded labour, economic status, benefits and specified cases.
- Rules-level overlap with SC/ST Rules is substantial in committee administration, rapid response, rehabilitation, monitoring and reporting, but records and beneficiary triggers differ.
- Rules-source discrepancies are preserved: principal Rules notification appears as G.S.R. 99(E) in several Government-hosted sources and G.S.R. 9(E) in an IndiaCode/eCourts transcription; the 1983 amendment appears as S.O. 1755 in consolidated footnotes and S.O. 1775 in the Rajya Sabha laying record.
