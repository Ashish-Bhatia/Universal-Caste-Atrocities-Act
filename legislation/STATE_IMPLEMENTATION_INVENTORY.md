# State and Union Territory Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: ACTIVE
Opened: 2026-09-05

## Method

This inventory records State/UT-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and related civil-rights/atrocity interfaces. It is an official-source inventory, not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion.

For each jurisdiction, search and record, where applicable:

1. State Police Act/Police Rules and atrocity-related police orders.
2. State notifications/orders concerning SC/ST Protection Cells, Special Investigation Cells, nodal officers and special officers.
3. State relief and rehabilitation schemes/orders under the PoA Rules.
4. State implementation material under the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955.
5. Special Courts/Exclusive Special Courts and relevant court notifications.
6. Special Public Prosecutors/Public Prosecutors and prosecution machinery.
7. State/District Vigilance and Monitoring Committees.
8. SOPs, circulars, manuals, forms and digital workflows used for implementation.
9. Monitoring, reporting, grievance and inspection machinery.
10. Relevant State laws affecting land, bonded labour, public access, local government, education, employment or other atrocity-related conduct.

Searches must distinguish current material from historical reports. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## Evidence Rule

Material legal or operational claims require a traceable official source. Historical Ministry reports are retained as context and source leads but do not establish current State practice without current State-source verification.

## Gujarat Status Summary

Gujarat is sufficiently inventoried for progression, with qualifications. The current Government of Gujarat Social Justice & Empowerment publication identifies State-level nodal architecture, a State Police Protection Cell, State/District/City/Taluka vigilance structures, Special Officers and 11 sensitive districts. A current Directorate page states that 19 Special Courts are functional at identified locations. The State also publishes Special Public Prosecutor arrangements, Rule 12(4) relief norms, the Veer Megh Maya Contingency Scheme, atrocity-data/statistics functions and current/recent Manual Scavengers implementation instruments. Current individual appointments, underlying notifications, complete court/prosecutor matrices, payment workflow, PoA-specific digital workflow, current bonded-labour instruments and other primary-instrument details remain open for final Phase 1 closure.

## Goa Status Summary

Goa is sufficiently inventoried for progression, with qualifications. The 2023 Goa State Annual Report records a 05-05-2023 Rule 9 nodal-officer order, no dedicated SC/ST Protection Cell or Special Police Station at that reporting point, a State V&MC and District V&MCs for North and South Goa, and no specifically identified atrocity-prone area. Official Central reporting identifies Principal District and Sessions Courts at Panaji and Margao as PoA Special Courts and records Special Public Prosecutors. Current Goa budget documents contain a Rs.40 lakh State provision for SC/ST PoA victim compensation and a further Rs.40 lakh centrally sponsored PCR/PoA implementation component. The current Goa Commission for SC/ST expressly covers PoA/PCR complaints and monitoring. Current 2026 nodal, police-cell, Rule 10, court, prosecutor, committee, relief workflow, PoA-specific Gazette/SOP and digital workflow details remain incompletely frozen.
