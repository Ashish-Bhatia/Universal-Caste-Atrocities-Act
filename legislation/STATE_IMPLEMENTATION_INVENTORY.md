# State and Union Territory Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: ACTIVE
Opened: 2026-09-05

## Method

This inventory records State/UT-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and related civil-rights/atrocity interfaces. It is an official-source inventory, not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion.

For each jurisdiction, search and record, where applicable:

1. State Police Act/Police Rules and atrocity-related police orders.
2. State notifications/orders concerning SC/ST Protection Cells, Special Investigation Cells, nodal officers and special officers.
3. State relief and rehabilitation schemes/orders under the PoA Rules.
4. State implementation material under the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955.
5. Special Courts/Exclusive Special Courts and relevant court notifications.
6. Special Public Prosecutors/Public Prosecutors and prosecution machinery.
7. State/District Vigilance and Monitoring Committees.
8. SOPs, circulars, manuals, forms and digital workflows used for implementation.
9. Monitoring, reporting, grievance and inspection machinery.
10. Relevant State laws affecting land, bonded labour, public access, local government, education, employment or other atrocity-related conduct.

Searches must distinguish current material from historical reports. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## Coverage Status

Andhra Pradesh has completed targeted verification with qualifications. Arunachal Pradesh has received a targeted official-source inventory with qualifications. Assam has now received an independent official-source inventory with qualifications. The remaining jurisdictions are deliberately unresearched and must be processed individually. No State is treated as representative of another jurisdiction.

## Evidence Rule

Material legal or operational claims require a traceable official source. Historical Ministry reports are retained as context and source leads but do not establish current State practice without current State-source verification.
