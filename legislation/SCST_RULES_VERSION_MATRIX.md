## Current-version issues

1. India Code's displayed Rules list ends at 2018. This is not proof that no later notification, order, corrigendum or implementation instrument exists.
2. Targeted official-source searching through 05-09-2026 located no later Central amendment to the Rules after G.S.R. 588(E). This remains provisional until a final Gazette/Legislative Department index pass.
3. The 2018 “voluntarily” definition refers to IPC section 39. IPC was repealed when BNS commenced on 01-07-2024. Ministry of Law and Justice notification S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, concerning references to repealed criminal laws is therefore a required transition source.
4. Operational references to criminal procedure and substantive offences require BNS/BNSS/BSA verification. No automatic equivalence is assumed.
5. The relief Schedule is a material financial and implementation baseline. Exact amounts, contingencies and payment stages must be extracted from the consolidated Rules text before comparison or policy conclusions.
