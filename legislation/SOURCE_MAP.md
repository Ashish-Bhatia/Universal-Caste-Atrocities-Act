# Phase 1 Existing-Law Source Map

Date opened: 2026-09-05
Status: WORKING MAP

## Source hierarchy

1. Constitution and official constitutional orders.
2. India Code consolidated Acts and official legislative text.
3. Gazette notifications and amendment instruments.
4. Ministry-issued official Rules and implementation material.
5. State government gazettes/official legislative repositories.
6. Parliamentary and committee records for legislative history, when needed.

## SC/ST Act amendment chain to verify in controlled sequence

- SC/ST Act, 1989.
- Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Amendment Act, 2015, brought into force/operationalized through subsequent statutory changes.
- Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Amendment Act, 2018.
- Any later amendment, ordinance, notification or judicially required statutory change must be searched before the consolidated text is frozen.

Current India Code record for Act 33/1989 lists the consolidated Act as last updated 19-11-2018. This is a source-version fact, not a claim that no later instrument exists. A later-instrument search remains required.

## Central legislation screening queue

Priority 1, directly material:

- Constitution of India.
- Constitution (SC) Order, 1950.
- Constitution (ST) Order, 1950.
- SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989.
- SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Rules, 1995.
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
- Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023.
- Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.
- Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955.
- Protection of Civil Rights Rules, 1977.

Priority 2, potentially material to particular conduct, victim classes, remedies or administration:

- Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013.
- Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006.
- Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976.
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
- Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005.
- child-protection legislation where minors are involved.
- Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988.
- Representation of the People Act, 1951, where disqualification or electoral conduct intersects with caste/untouchability offences.
- relevant labour, land, local-government and public-service legislation.

Priority 3, contextual or implementation-dependent sources will be added only when a concrete interaction is identified.

## State source map

Coverage standard: all States and Union Territories, with direct relevance screening. No State is to be treated as representative of the Union without evidence.

Required State source classes:

1. Police Act and Police Rules.
2. Rules/orders appointing nodal officers, protection cells, special prosecutors and monitoring committees under the SC/ST Act.
3. State-level relief and rehabilitation schemes/rules implementing the SC/ST Act.
4. State rules implementing the Protection of Civil Rights Act.
5. State criminal-procedure adaptations or special-court legislation affecting the operation of the central special statutes.
6. State laws materially regulating access to public places, land, bonded labour, local government, education, employment or other domains where caste-based exclusion is regulated.

Status: NOT COMPLETE. State-by-state extraction is a remaining Phase 1 workstream.
