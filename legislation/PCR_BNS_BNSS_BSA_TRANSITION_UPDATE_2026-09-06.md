## Verified primary-source correspondence

1. PCR Rules 1977, Rule 3(4), requires the inquiry officer, when conducting the section 10A inquiry, to follow as nearly as practicable the procedure for summary trials, including recording of evidence, laid down in Chapter XXI of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973.
2. The Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 places summary trials in Chapter XXI, sections 260-265.
3. The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 places summary trials in Chapter XXII, sections 283-289.
4. Legislative Department S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, issued under section 8 of the General Clauses Act, provides the controlling construction-of-references rule for references to the CrPC in Acts, State Acts, Ordinances, regulations, Presidential orders and subordinate instruments made under them. Such references are to be read as references to the BNSS and corresponding provisions are to be construed accordingly.

## Controlled conclusion

The PCR Rules' Chapter XXI CrPC reference is a live legacy procedural reference after 01-07-2024. It is not treated as a dead citation and is not converted by arithmetic section-number substitution. For current-law description, the relevant BNSS subject matter is the summary-trial regime in Chapter XXII, sections 283-289, subject to the exact construction required by S.O. 2790(E), the PCR Rules and the BNSS transition/savings framework.

The PCR Act/Rules comparison therefore advances from an unresolved generic legacy-reference flag to a controlled subject-matter correspondence. This does not resolve every PCR procedural issue, including all former-CrPC references and the operation of pending proceedings.
