# Goa State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: INVENTORIED WITH QUALIFICATIONS
Inventory date: 2026-09-05

## Scope and evidence rule

This record maps Goa-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and related civil-rights/atrocity interfaces. It is not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion. Currentness is distinguished from historical reporting. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## 1. Administrative/nodal architecture

The 2023 Goa State Annual Report on implementation of the PoA Act records that Government Order No. 58-9-97-BC-Vol.IV/5/645 dated 05-05-2023 nominated Shri Gopal A. Parsekar, Additional Secretary to Government of Goa, as the Rule 9 nodal officer to coordinate District Magistrates, Superintendent of Police and other implementing officers. The same report records no SC/ST Protection Cell and no Special Police Station, while stating that complaints received at police stations are registered promptly and investigated by an officer of DySP rank within the stipulated period. This is a reporting-period record, not proof of the current 2026 office-holder or structure. [GOA-STATE-001]

## 2. Protection Cell and police implementation

The 2023 State report records: (a) Nil SC/ST Protection Cells under Rule 8; (b) no Special Police Station; and (c) investigation by a DySP-rank officer after registration at ordinary police stations. The current Goa Police public material located in this inventory does not establish a later PoA-specific Protection Cell or Special Police Station instrument. Therefore the 2023 position is retained as historical/current-report evidence only, and a 2026 police-unit census remains open. [GOA-STATE-001, GOA-STATE-004]

## 3. Special Officers and atrocity-prone areas

The 2023 State report states that complaints are investigated by DySP-rank officers and does not provide a current Rule 10 Special Officer appointment matrix. Its Annexure XI records that neither North Goa nor South Goa had a specifically identified atrocity-prone area in 2023 and states that police patrolling was maintained within respective police-station jurisdictions. This does not establish that no such notification exists after 2023. A current Rule 10/atrocity-prone-area primary instrument search remains required for final closure. [GOA-STATE-001]

## 4. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2023 State report records a State Level V&MC as constituted, with one meeting in 2023. It records District Level V&MCs for North Goa and South Goa, constituted respectively on 04-08-2021 and 28-06-2017, with three and two meetings during 2023. It records no Sub-Divisional V&MCs, while showing a historical constitution entry dated 17-04-2015. These are direct reported State records for 2023, not a complete 2026 committee matrix. [GOA-STATE-002]

An official Goa Government portal page currently states that the Goa Commission for SC/ST is empowered to inquire into violations of the PCR Act and PoA Act, negligence by public servants, implementation of safeguards, complaints, awareness and monitoring. This Commission is a distinct State statutory institution and should not be conflated with the Rule 16/17/17A V&MC structure. [GOA-STATE-003]

## 5. Special Courts

The official Central 2021 implementation report for Goa states that the State designated Principal District and Sessions Courts at Panaji and Margao, corresponding to North Goa and South Goa, as Special Courts for speedy trial under the PoA Act. The 2023 Goa State Annual Report should be treated as the later State reporting-period source for the implementation architecture, but the complete current 2026 notification matrix has not been independently frozen. [GOA-STATE-005, GOA-STATE-001]

A Goa Government Prosecution Citizen Charter states that Public Prosecutors are appointed as Special Public Prosecutors before specially designated courts under, among other special statutes, the Prevention of Atrocities on SC/ST. This establishes the existence of a State prosecution function for PoA matters but does not by itself establish the current appointment register or district-wise SPP/ESPP list. [GOA-STATE-006]

## 6. SPP/ESPP and legal aid

The official Central 2021 Goa section records seven Special Public Prosecutors for PoA-related cases in the North and South Goa Special Courts and states that SDPOs and police-station in-charges were sensitized. It also records a State free-legal-aid scheme for SC/ST persons without an economic criterion, with two SC/ST persons receiving legal aid during 2020. These figures and appointments are historical reporting-period evidence. Current appointment orders/registers and current legal-aid workflow remain open. [GOA-STATE-005]

The 2023 Goa Prosecution Citizen Charter independently confirms that Special Public Prosecutors are used for SC/ST PoA cases. [GOA-STATE-006]

## 7. Relief, rehabilitation and financial administration

The Goa State Annual Report for 2023 records Nil atrocity victims provided relief in its reporting tables for North Goa and South Goa. This is not evidence that the State lacks a relief mechanism. The current Goa Social Welfare budget for 2025-26 contains a specific budget head, "Compensation to victims under SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989", with Budget Estimate of Rs.40.00 lakh. The same budget material identifies a further Rs.40.00 lakh under the centrally sponsored PCR/PoA implementation component and lists protection cells/special police stations, exclusive Special Courts, relief and rehabilitation, inter-caste marriage incentives and awareness as programme purposes. The Goa Social Welfare Citizen Charter states that victims approach the respective District Magistrate for relief under the central scheme. [GOA-STATE-007, GOA-STATE-008]

The Directorate of Planning, Statistics and Evaluation Statistical Handbook records three beneficiaries and one NGO under the combined scheme description for awards for encouraging persons with disabilities and compensation to victims under the SC/ST PoA Act in 2023-24. The Economic Survey 2024-25 records 10 beneficiaries and Rs.25.00 lakh expenditure in 2023-24, and 27 beneficiaries and Rs.68.00 lakh expenditure for 2024-25 up to December 2024, under the combined centrally sponsored scheme line for inter-caste marriages and PoA victim compensation. These combined-line figures should not be treated as pure PoA victim counts without underlying scheme records. [GOA-STATE-009, GOA-STATE-010]

## 8. Awareness and training

The Goa Government published a press release dated 10-07-2025 concerning "Awareness On Laws For Prevention Of Atrocities Against SCs/STs". This establishes a recent public awareness activity but does not by itself establish the full training calendar, curriculum or institutional ownership. The 2023 State report and Central implementation material also record police sensitization. [GOA-STATE-011, GOA-STATE-005]

## 9. Goa Commission for SC/ST

The Goa Commission for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes was established under the Goa Commission for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Act, 2010. The current Goa Government page states that its functions include inquiries into violations of the PCR Act and PoA Act, negligence by public servants, monitoring safeguards, recommendations for effective implementation, complaints, awareness, research and periodical reporting. The current page identifies a Secretary/CEO and complaint procedure. This is an additional State accountability/monitoring interface. [GOA-STATE-003]

## 10. Protection of Civil Rights Act interface

An official Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment PCR implementation report states that Goa has constituted State and District Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committees. It also records, for the relevant reporting period, Principal District and Sessions Courts at North Goa and South Goa as Special Courts for PCR offences, and states that the Director of Prosecution at State level and District Magistrates at district level supervise PCR prosecutions, with investigation by DySP-rank officers. Historical Goa PCR reporting also records two district committee meetings in 2017. These records establish an interface but do not freeze current 2026 arrangements. [GOA-STATE-012, GOA-STATE-013]

## 11. Manual scavenging interface

Goa has current State notifications for State Level and District Level Survey Committees under the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013, dated 26-09-2024. A Goa Social Welfare Department guideline also records the 2024 survey process, including district-level survey actions and reporting. The State budget/administrative material separately identifies implementation of the Manual Scavengers Act. This interface should be retained for later final Phase 1 crosswalk and transition verification. [GOA-STATE-014, GOA-STATE-015]

## 12. Bonded labour interface

The current Goa Government Acts and Rules index was searched for State bonded-labour instruments. No Goa-specific bonded-labour Rule or later implementation instrument was frozen from the targeted search. This is an evidence gap, not an absence finding. Final State closure should include a focused Labour Department/Gazette search. [GOA-STATE-016]

## 13. Land and local-government interfaces

The current Goa Government Acts and Rules index lists the Goa, Daman and Diu Land Revenue Code, 1968 and Rules, the Goa, Daman and Diu Agricultural Tenancy Act, 1964, the Goa, Daman and Diu Mundkars (Protection from Eviction) Act, 1975 and Rules, and the Goa Panchayat Raj Act, 1994. The same current index records later amendments to the Land Revenue Code, including 2025 amendments. These are relevant State-law interfaces for land tenure, eviction, local government and potential caste-linked access/property disputes. No policy conclusion is drawn from their inclusion. [GOA-STATE-016]

The Goa Government has also enacted the Goa Restriction on Transfer of Agricultural Land Act, 2023, brought into force on 18-04-2023. Its interaction with SC/ST land and property protections requires separate clause-level assessment only if later research identifies a material interface. [GOA-STATE-017]

## 14. Digital/FIR workflow

The 2023 State report states that complaints are registered at police stations and investigated by DySP-rank officers. The current public State sources located for this inventory do not establish a dedicated PoA e-FIR or case-management workflow. General digital police systems should not be treated as PoA-specific without an explicit source. A current PoA-specific digital workflow search remains open. [GOA-STATE-001]

## 15. Currentness assessment

Goa is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. The evidence establishes:

- a 05-05-2023 Rule 9 nodal-officer order identified in the 2023 State report;
- a 2023 State report showing no dedicated SC/ST Protection Cell or Special Police Station at that reporting point;
- a State V&MC and two District V&MCs operating in 2023;
- North Goa and South Goa Principal District and Sessions Courts designated as PoA Special Courts in official Central reporting;
- State use of Special Public Prosecutors for PoA matters;
- a current 2025-26 Rs.40 lakh State budget provision for PoA victim compensation and a further Rs.40 lakh central-scheme component;
- current State institutional oversight through the Goa Commission for SC/ST;
- recent State awareness activity in July 2025; and
- current manual-scavenging survey committees and related administrative material.

The following remain incompletely frozen for 2026 and must be carried into final Phase 1 closure:

1. Current Rule 9 nodal appointment/order and office-holder.
2. Current Protection Cell/police-unit establishment and staffing.
3. Current Rule 10 Special Officer designations.
4. Current atrocity-prone-area notifications, if any.
5. Current State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC constitution and meetings.
6. Complete current Special/Exclusive Special Court notification matrix.
7. Current PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment register.
8. Current relief sanction, treasury/DBT and case-processing workflow.
9. Current PoA-specific Gazette/SOP/circular/manual set.
10. Current PoA-specific digital/FIR/case-management workflow.
11. Current bonded-labour implementation instruments, if any.
