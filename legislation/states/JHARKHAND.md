# Jharkhand State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: INVENTORIED WITH QUALIFICATIONS
Inventory date: 2026-09-05

## Scope and evidence rule

This record maps Jharkhand-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and related civil-rights/atrocity interfaces. It is not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion. Currentness is distinguished from historical reporting. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## 1. Departmental and Rule 9 nodal architecture

The current State Welfare Department is the principal department for SC/ST, minority and backward-class welfare. Historical official Central implementation reports identify the Secretary of the SC/ST/OBC & Minorities Welfare Department as the Rule 9 nodal officer. A current 2026 primary Rule 9 designation instrument and individual office-holder were not located in the targeted search. [JH-STATE-001, JH-STATE-003]

## 2. Protection Cell and police machinery

Historical official Central reporting described a State SC/ST Protection Cell under the Crime Investigation Branch headed by the IGP, CID, and reported Special Police Stations in all 24 districts in 2017. Current 2026 Jharkhand Police district profiles independently list SC/ST Police Stations or SC/ST units in multiple districts, including Godda, Palamu, Sahibganj, Pakur, Koderma, Jamtara, Gumla and Simdega. This establishes continuing dedicated-unit evidence but not a complete statewide census. [JH-STATE-004, JH-STATE-005, JH-STATE-021]

A material current/recent development is the State Cabinet decision of 22-11-2023 authorizing police officers below DSP rank, including Inspector/Sub-Inspector level officers, to investigate PoA cases. The statutory Rule 7 baseline ordinarily requires an investigating officer not below the rank of DSP. The legal status, scope and operative instrument behind the 2023 State authorization require separate verification and must not be treated as an automatic amendment of the Central Rules. [JH-STATE-006]

## 3. Rule 10 Special Officers and atrocity-prone areas

Historical official Central reporting identified Additional Collectors as Rule 10 Special Officers in specified districts and, in 2015, reported Additional Collector/Additional District Magistrate arrangements across all 24 districts. Current 2026 primary appointment orders were not located. A current operative atrocity-prone-area notification was also not located in the targeted search. These remain open rather than being treated as absent. [JH-STATE-003]

## 4. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

Historical official Central reporting records a State Level V&MC under the Chief Minister and District V&MCs under District Collectors/Magistrates. The 2015 report recorded 23 district-level meetings across 24 districts. Current constitution orders, membership and State/District/Sub-Divisional meeting records remain open. [JH-STATE-002]

## 5. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

Current Jharkhand High Court records show active SC/ST Act Special Court designations at multiple locations. Current records identify such functions in Daltonganj, Hazaribagh, Deoghar, Giridih, Sahibganj and other locations. A 15-04-2026 State Cabinet decision approved three District Judge-level posts for three Special Courts constituted under amended section 14(1) in the Ranchi, Dhanbad and Daltonganj judgeships. The complete current notification and jurisdiction matrix remains open and must reconcile the three 2026 posts with other current SC/ST Special Court assignments. [JH-STATE-007, JH-STATE-008, JH-STATE-009, JH-STATE-010, JH-STATE-011]

## 6. SPP/ESPP and prosecution machinery

Historical implementation material and current court records establish dedicated PoA Special Court functions, but a current statewide Special Public Prosecutor/Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor appointment register was not located in the targeted search. Individual prosecutor appointments and replacements therefore remain open.

## 7. Relief, rehabilitation, treasury and payment workflow

Current Ministry data records Jharkhand relief coverage of 607 persons in 2020-21, 1,000 in 2021-22, 556 in 2022-23, 300 in 2023-24 and 274 in 2024-25. Central assistance released was Rs.0.29 crore, Rs.3.19 crore, Rs.0.00 crore, Rs.0.81 crore and Rs.0.00 crore respectively. These figures establish reporting-period financial/physical coverage, not case-level compliance. [JH-STATE-012]

Jharkhand Finance Department maintains current DBT and electronic treasury infrastructure. The retrieved material does not establish the PoA-specific sanction, treasury withdrawal, beneficiary verification or DBT case-processing workflow. [JH-STATE-013, JH-STATE-014]

## 8. PoA-specific Gazette/SOP/circular/manual and contingency planning

Historical and current official records establish implementation architecture, but the current PoA-specific Gazette/SOP/circular/manual corpus and operative Rule 15 contingency plan were not frozen in the targeted search. This remains an open instrument-retrieval workstream.

## 9. Digital/FIR/case-management workflow

The current Jharkhand Police Citizen Charter provides general complaint/FIR registration and escalation mechanisms. It continues to cite CrPC provisions, so it is not treated as a complete post-BNSS workflow. The State UDDP provides resident/family identifiers and DBT analytics, but no PoA-specific case-management workflow was established. [JH-STATE-015, JH-STATE-016]

## 10. PCR interface

Historical official Central reporting identifies joint PCR/PoA monitoring through State and District V&MC and the State SC/ST Protection Cell. Current targeted State material did not establish a complete PCR-specific implementation order set or current case-processing interface. This remains open.

## 11. Manual scavenging interface

Current official Central records show Jharkhand beneficiaries under manual-scavenger rehabilitation programmes and current/2026 Ministry material identifies NSKFDC awareness activity in Gumla. The current State-specific implementation instruments under the 2013 Manual Scavengers Act and local enforcement structure were not located in the targeted search. [JH-STATE-018]

## 12. Bonded labour interface

The current Jharkhand Labour Department digital portal maintains a dedicated Bonded Labour section containing District/Sub-Division Vigilance Committee constitution material and nodal-officer information. The underlying instruments require extraction and current-status verification before the interface is frozen. [JH-STATE-017]

## 13. Land and local-government interfaces

The current Revenue, Registration & Land Reforms Department identifies district-level land administration, digitized revenue and registry records, online mutation, land-transfer/acquisition functions and decentralization of certain land powers. [JH-STATE-019]

Official State material identifies the Chotanagpur Tenancy Act, Santhal Pargana Tenancy Act and Panchayati Raj framework as relevant land/local-government interfaces. PESA is also relevant to Scheduled Areas. The current section-level and notification-level matrix remains open. [JH-STATE-020]

## 14. Currentness assessment

Jharkhand is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. Current official Police and High Court material establishes dedicated SC/ST Police units and active Special Court assignments. A 2026 Cabinet decision created three District Judge-level posts for Special Courts, and a 2023 Cabinet decision authorized lower-rank police investigation of PoA cases. Current financial and digital infrastructure is also documented. However, the current Rule 9 appointment, complete Rule 10/atrocity-prone-area instruments, V&MC matrix, court notification matrix, prosecutor appointments, relief workflow, PoA-specific executive instruments, digital workflow and related-law implementation instruments remain incompletely frozen.

The 2023 investigation authorization is a high-priority verification issue because it appears to interact directly with Rule 7. No conclusion is drawn here about its legal validity or effect.
