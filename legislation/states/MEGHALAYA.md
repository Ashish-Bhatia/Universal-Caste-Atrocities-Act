## Disposition

Meghalaya should PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS for Phase 1. Core implementation architecture is sufficiently established. Residuals are current Rule 9 office-holder, Rule 10 instrument, 2026 Special Court jurisdiction matrix, current SPP/ESPP register, relief/payment workflow and post-2022 annual-report submission/receipt.

Historical records are not treated as a 05-09-2026 census. Search silence is not evidence of absence. No policy, constitutional or legislative conclusion is drawn.
