# Chhattisgarh State Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Research date: 2026-09-05
Status: INVENTORIED WITH QUALIFICATIONS

## Method

Jurisdiction-specific official-source inventory. Historical records are separated from current evidence. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence. No policy, necessity, superiority or constitutional conclusion is drawn.

## Administrative and nodal machinery

The Government of Chhattisgarh Department of Tribal and Scheduled Caste Development states that its functions include review of implementation of the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and the Protection of Civil Rights Act. Its page was last updated 30-07-2024, while the department website carries 2026 material.

The official Chhattisgarh Administrative Report 2022-23 states that, in compliance with Rule 9, the Commissioner, Tribal and Scheduled Caste Development, Chhattisgarh, was declared the Nodal Officer. It also states that the State-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee was constituted under Rule 16 with the Chief Minister as Chairperson and met on 25-08-2022.

Current 2026 Rule 9 designation instrument and individual office-holder evidence remain unverified.

## Protection Cell and police machinery

The official Chhattisgarh Administrative Report 2018-19 states that 13 districts had Special Police Stations: Raipur, Durg, Rajnandgaon, Jagdalpur, Dantewada, Bilaspur, Raigarh, Surguja, Surajpur, Kabirdham, Mahasamund, Janjgir and Korba. It states that SC/ST cells operated in the remaining 14 districts.

A Government of India implementation report for 2010 records a PCR Cell at Police Headquarters under an Inspector General of Police with supporting staff and identifies Chhattisgarh among States with SC/ST Protection Cells. The 2008 report records a similar PCR Cell and twelve Special Police Thanas at that reporting point. These are historical corroboration only.

Current 2026 Protection Cell establishment, staffing and police-unit matrix remain open.

## Rule 10 and atrocity-prone areas

The 2022-23 State report establishes the Rule 9 nodal designation and committee architecture but does not provide a complete current 2026 Rule 10 office-holder matrix or current atrocity-prone-area notification list. An official 2008 Ministry report records identified atrocity-prone locations, but those records are historical and are not treated as current.

Current Rule 10 designations and atrocity-prone-area notifications remain open.

## Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2022-23 State report states that the State-level V&MC was constituted under Rule 16 with the Chief Minister as Chairperson and met on 25-08-2022. It states that District-level V&MCs had been constituted in all 28 districts and that district meetings were being held under Rule 17.

Current State public-relations records show continuing district-level activity. On 23-09-2024, the Raipur Collector chaired a district-level V&MC meeting under the Atrocities Act, including review of pending cases and relief/rehabilitation. A 24-06-2024 State public-relations notice recorded a Bilaspur district-level V&MC meeting scheduled for 25-06-2024 to review 2024-25 cases and pending court matters.

Current 2026 State, District and Sub-Divisional committee constitution and meeting matrix remain open.

## Special and Exclusive Special Courts

The 2018-19 State administrative report states that Special Courts were functioning in 11 districts: Raipur, Durg, Rajnandgaon, Jagdalpur, Bilaspur, Surguja, Janjgir, Jashpur, Korba, Koriya and Raigarh. It records sanctioned Special Judge and staff posts for Janjgir-Champa, Jashpur, Korba, Koriya (Baikunthpur) and Raigarh.

The Government of India 2023-24 PoA implementation report records Chhattisgarh as having 27 districts and 11 districts with Exclusive Special Courts. This is official Central implementation evidence, not a substitute for a current State notification matrix.

Current Chhattisgarh High Court judicial records show continuing operation. Current officer records include a Special Judge under SC/ST (P.A.) Act at Korba effective 01-04-2026 and other recent Special Judge postings. A 2025 High Court judgment records conviction by the Special Judge under the Act at Raigarh. These records establish operation at identified locations, not the complete statewide notification matrix.

Complete current State-wide Special/Exclusive Special Court notification consolidation remains open.

## Special Public Prosecutors

Historical official Ministry records establish Special Public Prosecutor machinery in connection with Special Courts. Current 2026 PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment orders or a consolidated register were not located in the targeted official-source search.

No absence conclusion is drawn. Current appointments remain open.

## Relief and rehabilitation

The Government of Chhattisgarh Administrative Report 2021-22 states that Rs.577.40 lakh allocated for relief and rehabilitation under the SC/ST PoA Act was released to districts for expenditure. It records a Chhattisgarh Finance Department letter dated 26-05-2016 directing that relief claims be drawable from the treasury where budget allocation was unavailable.

The 2022-23 State report states that relief and rehabilitation assistance was provided to 1,093 SC/ST persons in 2021-22 and that 560 victims had relief approved as of November 2022 for 2022-23. It also records increased relief rates.

An official Central implementation table records 1,115 Chhattisgarh atrocity victims provided relief in 2020-21. This is reported scheme activity, not proof of individual payment compliance.

Current 2026 relief rates, treasury/DBT processing and case-level payment workflow remain open.

## Digital and administrative workflow

A 2017 Government of Chhattisgarh page hosts implementation material concerning the SC/ST PoA Act and PCR Act through DBT and reproduces Central instructions concerning benefit delivery and the seven-day Rule 12(4) relief requirement. This is historical benefit-delivery evidence, not a current 2026 case-management system.

The current department website provides public departmental information and notices. No current PoA-specific FIR/e-complaint, case-management, relief-tracking or victim workflow was established in the targeted search.

Current PoA-specific digital workflow remains open.

## Protection of Civil Rights interface

The current department mandate includes review of both the SC/ST PoA Act and PCR Act. Historical State reports describe a Civil Rights and Protection Cell, special courts and special police machinery. Current PCR-specific operational details remain to be frozen.

## Relevant State-law interfaces

Land: the Chhattisgarh Land Revenue Code, 1959 is relevant to land possession, revenue administration and tribal land-transfer questions. Current High Court decisions continue to apply section 170-B. This is an interface source, not a PoA-specific land rule.

Panchayats: official State Finance Commission material records the Chhattisgarh Panchayat Raj Adhiniyam and the State rural local-government framework. A current clause-level atrocity-related interface remains to be checked if identified.

Bonded labour: the current Chhattisgarh Labour Department maintains an official labour-law/service portal updated in 2026. The targeted search did not establish a distinct State-specific bonded-labour implementation instrument or Rule set. No absence conclusion is drawn.

Manual scavenging: the State department publishes material concerning the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Rules, 2014, including a 2017 notice concerning amendment of the State-level survey committee structure. Current later instruments and operational status remain open.

## Evidence assessment

Chhattisgarh is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications.

Strongest evidence includes the current State department mandate, the official 2022-23 State administrative report, 2024 State district V&MC notices, current 2026 High Court judicial records and State relief-allocation records.

The record is not a complete 2026 implementation census. Current Rule 9 appointment, Protection Cell staffing, Rule 10 designations, V&MC matrix, court notification matrix, SPP/ESPP appointments, relief workflow, PoA-specific Gazette/SOP set and digital workflow remain open.
