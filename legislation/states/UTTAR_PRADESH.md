# Uttar Pradesh

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map  
Status: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS  
Research date: 2026-09-05

## Scope

This jurisdiction inventory records Uttar Pradesh-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, related PCR interfaces, State institutional arrangements, police machinery, courts/prosecutors, relief, monitoring and recent official corroboration. It is an existing-law inventory. It does not assess policy superiority, necessity or constitutional validity.

## 1. Rule 9 nodal architecture

The Ministry's implementation report records that a Rule 9 Nodal Officer had been appointed in Uttar Pradesh. [A: UP-STATE-004]

Historical State reporting identifies the Special Secretary, Social Welfare Department as the Nodal Officer coordinating District Magistrates, Superintendents of Police and other authorized officers. [A: UP-STATE-005]

The current named Rule 9 officer and current appointment order/Gazette were not independently verified in this inventory. Current administrative webpages were not treated as proof of the statutory Rule 9 appointment. Search silence is not treated as absence.

## 2. Rule 8 Protection Cell and SC/ST investigation machinery

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) report states that a Special Investigation Cell was functioning at State level. It describes a structure comprising the DGP, IGP, DIG, SP, Additional SP and nine DySPs. It also states that a Special Investigation Cell had been set up in all districts, each with one Sub-Inspector, one head constable and two constables, with the DySP as investigating officer. [A: UP-STATE-001]

Earlier Ministry reporting states that Uttar Pradesh had an SC/ST Protection Cell and distinguishes such Cells from the separately listed Special Police Stations. The same reporting lists only Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Kerala and Madhya Pradesh as States with Special Police Stations at that reporting point. [A: UP-STATE-003]

The 2022 material therefore establishes a State/district Special Investigation Cell structure but does not establish a 2026 census of dedicated Special Police Stations. Current Rule 8 staffing, command, reporting and notification-level status remain open.

## 3. Special Police Station / police machinery

The available Ministry census does not list Uttar Pradesh among the States with Special Police Stations. The 2022 Section 21(4) report instead describes State and district Special Investigation Cells and DySP investigation. [A: UP-STATE-001, UP-STATE-003]

This is not treated as a current 2026 absence finding. A current Uttar Pradesh notification/census of dedicated Special Police Stations and any later police reorganization remains unverified.

## 4. Rule 7 investigation and charge-sheet timing

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) report records 11,172 cases in which investigation and charge-sheet filing were completed within 60 days and 1,023 cases in which filing occurred later than 60 days. [A: UP-STATE-001]

The 2022 NCRB-derived police table records 12,287 SC-category cases registered during 2022, 10,314 charge-sheeted in courts, and 1,278 pending with police at year end, with 1,137 cases pending from the previous year. [A: UP-STATE-006]

These are reporting-period figures. Current Rule 7 compliance, written reasons for delayed investigation and post-BNSS transition instructions remain open.

## 5. Rule 10 / identified and atrocity-prone areas

Historical Ministry reporting records no identified atrocity-prone area in Uttar Pradesh and records District Magistrates as Special Officers in all districts in the relevant reporting snapshot. [A: UP-STATE-005]

The 2024 POA Handbook, using 2021 government reporting, likewise identifies Uttar Pradesh among States that had not identified atrocity-prone areas and among States without appointed Special Officers. [C: UP-STATE-007]

The 2022 Ministry Section 21(4) extract located for this inventory does not establish a current 2026 notification status. Accordingly, no present absence finding is made. Current Rule 3/Rule 10 surveys, notifications and Special Officer appointments remain open.

## 6. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2022 Ministry Section 21(4) report states that the State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee was constituted under the Chief Minister and that no State-level meeting was held during 2022. It states that District Level V&MCs were constituted in every district under District Magistrates and that 143 district-level meetings were held in 75 districts during 2022. [A: UP-STATE-001]

The 2022 data is a reporting-period snapshot. Current State, District and Sub-Divisional V&MC membership, Gazette notifications, meeting calendars, minutes and compliance remain unverified.

## 7. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

Ministry reporting identifies 65 of Uttar Pradesh's 75 districts as having Exclusive Special Courts in the relevant reporting dataset. The remaining districts had Special Courts through designated Additional District and Sessions Judge courts. [A: UP-STATE-002]

Historical State reporting lists 65 Exclusive Special Courts by district and states that the remaining nine districts had Additional District and Sessions Judge courts designated as Special Courts. [A: UP-STATE-005]

Current judicial corroboration exists. Allahabad High Court administrative notifications in June, August and August 2026 continue to appoint Additional District and Sessions Judges as Special Judges for Section 14 PoA Act cases in districts including Jhansi, Amroha, Bijnor, Firozabad, Saharanpur and Deoria. [A: UP-STATE-008, UP-STATE-009, UP-STATE-010]

These current notifications corroborate continuing PoA-specific Special Judge appointments but do not constitute a complete 2026 district-by-district census of all Special/Exclusive Special Courts. The current census remains open.

## 8. SPP/ESPP

The Ministry's implementation material states that States with Exclusive Special Courts and Special Courts appoint or specify Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors and Special Public Prosecutors respectively. Uttar Pradesh is included in the corresponding Special Court framework. [A: UP-STATE-002]

A current named Uttar Pradesh SPP/ESPP roster, appointment instruments, tenure and court allocation were not independently retrieved. The official Directorate of Prosecution website confirms an active State prosecution directorate and a dedicated SC/ST Act section in its legislation portal, but this does not by itself establish the current PoA-specific prosecutor roster. [A: UP-STATE-011]

## 9. Relief, rehabilitation and related assistance

The current Ministry CSS dataset records persons provided relief in Uttar Pradesh as 23,592 in 2020-21, 20,278 in 2021-22, 23,828 in 2022-23, 19,240 in 2023-24 and 20,074 in 2024-25. The same dataset records central assistance released to Uttar Pradesh as Rs. 113.03 crore, Rs. 126.72 crore, Rs. 91.54 crore, Rs. 97.95 crore and Rs. 89.49 crore respectively. [A: UP-STATE-012]

These are scheme-reporting figures. They do not establish individual statutory payment timing, the seven-day requirement, stage-wise payment, victim/witness expense compliance or the State's internal sanction workflow. Current relief rates and payment controls remain open.

The current central scheme page also records Uttar Pradesh as reporting zero persons receiving incentives for inter-caste marriages in 2021-22 through 2024-25. This is a scheme-output statistic and is not treated as evidence about the operation of the PoA Act's relief provisions. [A: UP-STATE-013]

## 10. Annual reporting and statistical context

The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the principal detailed institutional snapshot located for Uttar Pradesh. It records State/District V&MC arrangements, Special Investigation Cells, charge-sheet timing and related implementation information. [A: UP-STATE-001]

For SC-category PoA cases in 2022, the Ministry's NCRB-derived table records 12,287 cases registered during the year and 10,314 charge-sheeted in courts. [A: UP-STATE-006]

The 2022 Section 21(4) table records 11,172 investigations/charge-sheets completed within 60 days and 1,023 after 60 days. [A: UP-STATE-001]

These statistics are not used as a policy-superiority conclusion. They are retained for the existing-law baseline and later empirical work.

## 11. State PoA/PCR instruments and related-law interfaces

The located Ministry material establishes continuing State implementation machinery under the central PoA/PCR framework. The official Uttar Pradesh Directorate of Prosecution portal publishes the SC/ST Act among its legislation materials and maintains a current prosecution administration website. [A: UP-STATE-011]

A complete current Uttar Pradesh Gazette/GO set for Rule 8, Rule 9, Rule 10, V&MC composition, Special/Exclusive Special Courts, SPP/ESPP appointments, relief rates, contingency planning and PCR implementation was not independently retrieved in this first-pass inventory. This is a retrieval limitation, not an absence finding.

## 12. Current/recent judicial and police corroboration

Allahabad High Court administrative notifications issued in 2026 expressly continue or change appointments of Additional District and Sessions Judges as Special Judges for Section 14 of the PoA Act in multiple Uttar Pradesh districts. [A: UP-STATE-008, UP-STATE-009, UP-STATE-010]

The Supreme Court's 2025 decision in Manish Yadav v. State of Uttar Pradesh records proceedings before a Special Judge, SC/ST Act, Ghazipur, and an appeal arising from an Allahabad High Court order. It is used here only as current institutional corroboration, not as Phase 2 merits research. [A: UP-STATE-014]

The current Uttar Pradesh Directorate of Prosecution website confirms a dedicated prosecution administration and an SC/ST Act section within its legislation portal. [A: UP-STATE-011]

## Disposition

PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS. Uttar Pradesh has substantial documented implementation machinery: a Rule 9 Nodal Officer historically reported, a State and district Special Investigation Cell structure, State/District V&MCs, a historical framework of 65 Exclusive Special Courts and Special Courts in the remaining districts, continuing 2026 Special Judge appointments, and substantial current relief/central-assistance reporting. Current instrument-level verification remains incomplete, particularly for Rule 8/Rule 9/Rule 10 appointments, Special Police Stations, V&MC operation, prosecutor rosters, court census, payment workflow, contingency planning and BNS/BNSS/BSA transition instructions.

No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or Phase 2 case-law corpus work was undertaken.
