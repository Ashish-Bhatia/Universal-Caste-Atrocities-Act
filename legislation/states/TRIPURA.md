# Tripura

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS
Research date: 2026-09-05

## Scope

This jurisdiction inventory covers Tripura-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, related Protection of Civil Rights interfaces, State institutional arrangements, police machinery, courts/prosecutors, relief, monitoring committees and recent official corroboration. It is an existing-law inventory, not a policy-superiority or constitutional-validity conclusion.

## 1. Governing instruments and State administrative layer

Tripura has a Directorate/Department for Welfare of Scheduled Castes. A current Government of Tripura public-grievance directory identifies the Department of Welfare of Scheduled Caste and a current Special Officer, while the State RTI directory separately identifies a current Deputy Director for Welfare of Scheduled Castes. These current listings establish an active administrative layer but do not by themselves establish the current Rule 9 appointment. [A: TR-STATE-004, TR-STATE-005]

The Tripura 2025-26 State budget lists the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 and Prevention of Atrocities Act, 1989 as centrally sponsored schemes under both Tribal Welfare and SC Welfare. The older SC Welfare budget also carries a dedicated CSS head for the two Acts. [A: TR-STATE-006, TR-STATE-007]

## 2. Rule 9 nodal architecture

Central Government reporting has historically recorded Tripura as having appointed a Rule 9 Nodal Officer. The 2020-21 Ministry annual-report table records Tripura as having a Nodal Officer, while the Ministry's earlier implementation reporting likewise lists Tripura among jurisdictions with a nominated Nodal Officer. [A: TR-STATE-010, TR-STATE-011]

A 2024 citizens' audit handbook identifies the Tripura Department of Welfare of SCs, OBCs as the State nodal contact, but this is secondary corroboration and does not establish a current Gazette/order. [C: TR-STATE-012]

The current named Rule 9 officer and appointment instrument remain unverified. Current administrative directory entries are not treated as proof of Rule 9 status. Search silence is not treated as absence.

## 3. Rule 8 Protection Cell and police machinery

The Ministry's 2022 Section 21(4) Tripura report states that an SC/ST Protection Cell has been constituted in the Department for Welfare of Scheduled Castes. [A: TR-STATE-001]

There is a material source conflict. The Tripura 2022 state annual-report annexure concerning Rule 8, certified by the State Public Information Officer, records the SC/ST Protection Cell as not set up and therefore not discharging Rule 8 responsibilities. [B: TR-STATE-002]

Because these two contemporaneous 2022 records conflict, no definitive 2026 conclusion is drawn about the legal/operational status of the Protection Cell. The conflict is a controlled Phase 1 residual requiring direct State instrument/RTI reconciliation.

## 4. Special Police Station and investigation machinery

The certified Tripura 2022 state annual-report annexure records that no Special Police Station had been set up in any of the eight districts. It states instead that cases registered against SC/ST persons were investigated by officers not below the rank of Deputy Superintendent of Police. [B: TR-STATE-002]

The Ministry's national implementation material separately records Special Police Stations only in a limited set of States, excluding Tripura. [A: TR-STATE-013]

This establishes a strong historical no-special-police-station position for 2022, but a current 2026 State notification/census remains open. No conclusion of current absence is made from search silence.

## 5. Rule 7 investigation and charge-sheet timing

The Ministry's 2022 Tripura Section 21(4) report records one case in which investigation and charge-sheet filing were completed within 60 days and one case in which filing occurred later than 60 days. [A: TR-STATE-001]

A 2026 secondary analysis reproducing the Ministry's 2022 table gives the same Tripura figures, one within 60 days and one after 60 days, with no legal-aid recipient recorded in that table. The secondary reproduction is retained only as corroboration. [C: TR-STATE-014]

Current Rule 7 compliance data, written reasons for delay and post-BNSS transition instructions remain unverified.

## 6. Rule 10 / identified and atrocity-prone areas

The Ministry's 2022 Tripura report states that there was no specific atrocity-prone area in the State. [A: TR-STATE-001]

The 2020-21 Ministry implementation table also records Tripura as having no identified atrocity-prone area and no Special Officer in the historical reporting snapshot. [A: TR-STATE-010]

These are reporting snapshots. They do not establish that no current area is notified or that no Rule 10 Special Officer exists in 2026. Current notification-level verification remains open.

## 7. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The Ministry's 2022 report states that the State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee was reconstituted on 28-06-2023 under the Chairpersonship of the Chief Minister and that no State-level meeting was held during 2022. It records District Level V&MCs under District Magistrates and Sub-Divisional V&MCs in all Sub-Divisions. [A: TR-STATE-001]

The 2026 secondary reproduction of the Ministry's 2022 data records eight districts, two mandatory State-level meetings and 32 mandatory District-level meetings, with no District-level meetings reported in the reproduced table. [C: TR-STATE-014]

The 2022 state report's statement that the State committee was reconstituted in 2023, combined with its statement about no meeting during 2022, indicates reporting-period separation. Current 2026 membership, calendars, minutes and compliance remain open.

## 8. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

The Ministry's 2022 Tripura report states that the State Government, with concurrence of the Chief Justice of the Guwahati High Court, specified the District & Sessions Judges of West Tripura Judicial District at Agartala, Gomati at Udaipur, South Tripura at Belonia, Unakoti at Kailashahar, North Tripura at Dharmanagar, and Additional District & Sessions Judges at Khowai, Sonamura and Kamalpur under Unakoti as Special Courts for PoA Act trials. [A: TR-STATE-003]

The 2020-21 Ministry table also recorded Special Courts for Tripura but no Exclusive Special Courts. [A: TR-STATE-010]

Current district-level notifications and whether any court has subsequently been designated as an Exclusive Special Court remain unverified. The current eCourts portal confirms eight Tripura district court sites, but its directory alone does not establish PoA-specific designation. [A: TR-STATE-009]

## 9. SPP/ESPP

The Ministry's 2022 report records eight Special Public Prosecutors specified to conduct concerned cases in the designated Special Courts. [A: TR-STATE-003]

The 2020-21 Ministry table records Tripura as having Special Public Prosecutors but no Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors, consistent with the absence of Exclusive Special Courts in that historical table. [A: TR-STATE-010]

Current Gazette notifications, names, tenure, court allocation and exclusivity status remain open. The 2025-26 State notice board shows general appointments of Public Prosecutors and Additional Public Prosecutors, but this does not establish PoA-specific appointment. [B: TR-STATE-008]

## 10. Relief, rehabilitation and victim/witness expenses

The Ministry's 2022 Tripura report records relief to two atrocity victims during 2022 and legal aid to seven persons under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987. [A: TR-STATE-003]

The current central scheme table records Tripura relief recipients as NA for 2020-21, 3 in 2021-22, 9 in 2022-23, 0 in 2023-24 and 0 in 2024-25. The same table records central assistance to Tripura of Rs. 0.00 crore, Rs. 0.20 crore, Rs. 0.00 crore, Rs. 0.11 crore and Rs. 0.20 crore respectively for those years. These are scheme-reporting figures and are not proof of statutory seven-day payment compliance. [A: TR-STATE-015]

The 2025-26 State budget confirms the continuing CSS instrument for PCR/PoA implementation. Current relief rates, sanction/payment workflow, seven-day compliance, travel/maintenance/diet allowances and victim/witness support workflow remain unverified. [A: TR-STATE-006]

## 11. State PoA/PCR instruments and related-law interfaces

The located Tripura budget documents establish continuing State participation in the Centrally Sponsored Scheme for PCR/PoA implementation. The 2022 state annual-report material provides certified operational data for Special Courts and police investigation arrangements. [A: TR-STATE-002, TR-STATE-006]

No complete current Tripura Gazette/departmental instrument set for Rule 8, Rule 9, Rule 10, court designations, prosecutor appointments, relief rates, contingency planning or related PCR implementation was independently retrieved in this first-pass inventory. This is a document-retrieval limitation, not an absence finding.

## 12. Annual reporting and statistics

The 2022 Ministry Section 21(4) report is the principal detailed Tripura institutional snapshot located. It records State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC architecture, one timely and one delayed charge-sheet, a Protection Cell according to the narrative section, no specific atrocity-prone area, specified Special Courts, eight SPPs, two victims receiving relief and seven legal-aid recipients. [A: TR-STATE-001, TR-STATE-003]

The certified state annual-report annexure creates a material contradiction on the Rule 8 Protection Cell and states that no Special Police Station exists, with DSP-or-above investigation. [B: TR-STATE-002]

The 2020-21 Ministry report records Tripura with one SC case and zero ST cases in the 2019 SC/ST PoA court table, with one case pending at year end in the SC table. [A: TR-STATE-010]

The 2025-26 Ministry annual report records current central-assistance release through the CSS at the national level and the Tripura state budget continues to list the scheme. [A: TR-STATE-006, TR-STATE-016]

## 13. Current/recent judicial and police corroboration

A 2 July 2025 Tripura High Court order in The State of Tripura v. Sri Saikat Talapatra records a Special Judge, Gomati Judicial District, Udaipur, and addresses the victim's right to be heard in bail proceedings under Section 15A of the SC/ST PoA Act. The High Court cancelled the underlying bail order because the victim/informant had not been given the required opportunity of hearing. This is current judicial corroboration of active PoA-specific Special Judge proceedings and application of Section 15A. [A/C: TR-STATE-017]

A November 2025 Tripura High Court order in the same litigation records prosecution representation by the Public Prosecutor and continued judicial supervision of criminal proceedings. It is retained as institutional corroboration, not as a Phase 2 case-law merits analysis. [A/C: TR-STATE-018]

The current eCourts directory confirms eight Tripura district court sites and was updated 11-04-2026. It does not itself establish current PoA Special Court designation. [A: TR-STATE-009]

No current Tripura Police annual report or police-specific statewide PoA implementation report was located that independently resolves the Rule 8/Special Police Station conflict. Search silence is not treated as absence.

## 14. Currentness limitations

- current Rule 10 Special Officer appointment and any identified/atrocity-prone area notifications;
- current State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC membership, meeting calendars, minutes and compliance;
- current district-by-district Special/Exclusive Special Court notifications and census;
- current named SPP/ESPP roster, tenure, court allocation and exclusivity;
- current relief rates, sanction/payment workflow and seven-day compliance;
- current victim/witness travel, maintenance and dietary allowance workflow;
- current contingency plan and prevention measures under the amended Rules;
- direct retrieval of current Tripura PoA/PCR Government Orders, Gazette notifications and departmental circulars;
- later Section 21(4) reports after 2022 as directly verified primary material;
- current post-BNS/BNSS/BSA police/prosecution transition instructions;
- current PoA-specific legal-aid and victim/witness referral workflow;
- reconciliation of the 2022 eight-district court structure with current 2026 district judiciary arrangements.

## Disposition

PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS. Tripura has documented State/District/Sub-Divisional V&MC architecture, Special Courts, eight Special Public Prosecutors, historical Rule 9 Nodal Officer reporting, a 2022 report describing a Protection Cell, and continuing PCR/PoA budgetary and administrative machinery. The certified 2022 state annexure materially conflicts with the Ministry narrative on the Protection Cell and records no Special Police Stations, while current named appointments, instruments, court/prosecutor rosters, relief workflow and transition instructions remain incompletely verified.

No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or Phase 2 case-law corpus work was undertaken.
