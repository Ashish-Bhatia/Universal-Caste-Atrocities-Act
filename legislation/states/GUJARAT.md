# Gujarat State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: INVENTORIED WITH QUALIFICATIONS
Inventory date: 2026-09-05

## Scope and evidence rule

This record maps Gujarat-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and related civil-rights/atrocity interfaces. It is not a policy assessment or constitutional conclusion. Currentness is distinguished from historical reporting. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## 1. Administrative and nodal architecture

The current Government of Gujarat Department of Social Justice & Empowerment PoA page states that the State has appointed the Special Inspector General of Police, Scheduled Caste/Scheduled Tribe, Gandhinagar as Nodal Officer and the Additional Chief Secretary/Principal Secretary/Secretary, Social Justice & Empowerment, as ex-officio Nodal Officer. It also identifies State Review Committee arrangements. The page is current as an official State publication, but it does not provide a current individual appointment order or office-holder name for the police post. [GUJ-STATE-001]

## 2. Protection Cell and police implementation

The same State page states that a State-level Police Protection Cell exists under the Additional Director General of Police at Gandhinagar under Rule 12, for analysis and control of offences against SC/ST members. It also records a 03-10-1991 General Administration Department resolution sanctioning 15 Deputy Superintendent of Police posts at district level for atrocity investigation and supervision. These are strong official State records, but the current 2026 staffing/unit matrix and underlying primary orders should be separately frozen. [GUJ-STATE-005]

## 3. Special Officers and sensitive/atrocity-prone areas

The State PoA page states that Vigilance Officers of Ahmedabad, Vadodara and Rajkot were appointed as Special Officers and identifies 11 sensitive districts: Mehsana, Ahmedabad (Rural), Junagadh, Banaskantha, Kheda, Amreli, Rajkot (Rural), Kutch, Surendranagar, Vadodara (Rural) and Bharuch. The page does not itself provide the operative notification dates or later amendments. Therefore these are recorded as current State-published arrangements with an open primary-instrument verification item. [GUJ-STATE-001, GUJ-STATE-011]

## 4. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The current State PoA page records a State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee chaired by the Chief Minister, a State Level Review Committee chaired by the Secretary, Social Justice & Empowerment, District Level V&MCs chaired by Collectors, City Vigilance Committees chaired by Police Commissioners or Collectors, and Taluka Vigilance Committees chaired by Mamlatdars. Current committee constitution orders, meeting frequency and minutes require separate retrieval for a complete 2026 census. [GUJ-STATE-001]

## 5. Special Courts

The current Gujarat Directorate page for BCK-64 states that 19 Special Courts are functional at Kachchh, Palanpur, Ahmedabad (Rural), Amreli, Vadodara, Surat, Junagadh, Panchmahal, Rajkot, Surendranagar, Bhavnagar, Jamnagar, Mehsana, Himmatnagar, Navsari, Valsad, Dahod, Narmada and Patan. The broader PoA page contains an older 18-court description followed by a 19-court sanctioned/functional list, so the current BCK-64 page is preferred for the present functional count while the underlying notification matrix remains an open verification item. [GUJ-STATE-002]

## 6. SPP/ESPP and legal aid

The current State PoA page states that District Government Pleaders have been designated as Special Public Prosecutors for atrocity cases. A primary Gujarat Legal Department notification dated 31-01-1990 specifies Public Prosecutors for named Special Courts as Special Public Prosecutors under section 15. The current individual appointment register and any post-1990 replacement/additional appointments are not frozen. [GUJ-STATE-003, GUJ-STATE-004]

## 7. Relief, rehabilitation and financial administration

The State PoA page publishes State relief norms under Rule 12(4), including offence-specific compensation and staged payment arrangements. It separately records the Veer Megh Maya Contingency Scheme under Rule 15, with assistance for migration, housing, education, food, medical expenses, agricultural land, reconstruction and other rehabilitation measures. The current scheme page also states financial assistance is provided to SC victims and dependants for registered PoA offences. These State schedules are official evidence of the published scheme architecture, but current operative resolutions, rates, treasury/DBT workflow and case-level payment controls remain to be frozen. [GUJ-STATE-006, GUJ-STATE-007, GUJ-STATE-012]

## 8. Awareness, administration and statistics

The Directorate's current N-1 branch page states that it collects atrocity data, prepares annual reports, handles Central assistance and statistical work. The Department's Direction and Administration page identifies continuing schemes for Special Courts, administrative strengthening and computerisation. These establish current institutional functions but do not establish performance outcomes. [GUJ-STATE-009, GUJ-STATE-010]

## 9. Protection of Civil Rights Act interface

The Gujarat Directorate's current administration pages separately identify staff for enforcement of the Protection of Civil Rights Act and the PoA Special Court machinery. The project retains this as an implementation interface requiring final crosswalk with the already-completed Central PCR comparison. [GUJ-STATE-010]

## 10. Manual scavenging interface

The Gujarat Safai Kamdar Vikas Nigam's current publication set includes the Manual Scavengers Act and Rules. A 24-06-2024 State resolution constitutes State Level, District Level and Municipal Corporation Level Survey Committees under the Manual Scavengers Rules. A 29-02-2020 State notification empowers local authorities and District Magistrates under sections 18 and 19 of the 2013 Act to enforce prohibition, rehabilitation, investigation and prosecution duties. These are current/recent State implementation instruments. [GUJ-STATE-013, GUJ-STATE-014]

## 11. Bonded labour interface

Official Gujarat administrative material identifies the Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976 as applicable to contractor/labour engagement and administered through labour authorities. A complete Gujarat-specific current bonded-labour notification/rules set has not yet been frozen. This is an evidence gap, not an absence finding. [GUJ-STATE-015]

## 12. Land and local-government interfaces

The Gujarat State legal publication system contains State legislation and rules affecting local administration, caste-certificate issuance/verification and related welfare administration. The Gujarat Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Classes (Issuance and Verification of Caste Certificates) Act, 2018 is expressly listed in the current State Acts and Rules index. Detailed clause-level interaction is deferred to later crosswalk work if material. [GUJ-STATE-008]

## 13. Digital/FIR workflow

The located current Gujarat State PoA pages establish administration, computerisation as a Directorate function and a State Police Protection Cell, but do not establish a dedicated current PoA e-FIR or case-management workflow. General police digital systems should not be treated as PoA-specific without an explicit source. A focused digital-workflow search remains open. [GUJ-STATE-010]

## 14. Currentness assessment

Gujarat is sufficiently inventoried for progression with qualifications. The current State publication layer establishes:

- State-level nodal architecture and Police Protection Cell;
- Special Officer and sensitive-district arrangements as currently published;
- State, District, City and Taluka vigilance/monitoring structures;
- 19 currently stated functional Special Courts;
- Special Public Prosecutor arrangements;
- published State relief and Veer Megh Maya rehabilitation architecture;
- current atrocity data/statistics and computerisation functions; and
- current/recent Manual Scavengers implementation committees and enforcement notification.

The following remain open for final Phase 1 closure:

1. Current primary Rule 9 designation/appointment instrument and individual office-holder.
2. Current Protection Cell establishment/order, staffing and district police-unit matrix.
3. Current Rule 10 Special Officer orders.
4. Primary notifications underlying the 11 sensitive-district list and any later atrocity-prone-area notifications.
5. Complete Special/Exclusive Special Court notification matrix.
6. Current PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointment register.
7. Current relief-rate, treasury/DBT and case-processing workflow.
8. Current PoA-specific Gazette/SOP/circular/manual set.
9. Current PoA-specific digital/FIR/case-management workflow.
10. Current V&MC constitution orders and meeting records.
11. Current bonded-labour implementation instruments.
12. Current Manual Scavengers implementation instruments beyond the 2020 and 2024 records.
