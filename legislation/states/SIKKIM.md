# Sikkim

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS
Research date: 2026-09-05

## Scope

This jurisdiction inventory covers Sikkim-specific implementation material for the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, related Protection of Civil Rights interfaces, State institutional arrangements, police machinery, courts/prosecutors, relief, monitoring committees and recent official corroboration. It is an existing-law inventory, not a policy-superiority or constitutional-validity conclusion.

## 1. Governing instruments and State publication

The Sikkim Social Welfare Department's current Acts and Rules page lists the Prevention of Atrocities Act, 1989, a 2014 SC/ST Act amendment document, 2014 Vigilance Committee Notification, 2014 State Monitoring Committee, a 2022 Special Courts notification, a 2020 Public Prosecutors notification, a 2016 inter-caste marriage incentive notification, a 2014 Special Courts notification and a 2006 Vigilance and Monitoring Committee instrument. The page was crawled in 2026 and establishes a current State publication layer, although the underlying linked files were not all retrievable in this pass. [A: SK-STATE-001]

The current Sikkim Social Welfare Department describes the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 and SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 as flagship programmes of its Social Justice Division and states that the Department implements the relevant Central and State-supported activities. [A: SK-STATE-002]

## 2. Rule 9 nodal architecture

Historical Ministry reporting establishes that Sikkim had not nominated a Rule 9 Nodal Officer in the 2012 status material. [B/C: SK-STATE-003]

The current Sikkim Social Welfare Department's RTI disclosure identifies the Secretary as head of the Department and records a current departmental "Nodal Officer" entry for another subject area, but it does not establish that the named officer is the Rule 9 PoA Nodal Officer. [A: SK-STATE-004]

Accordingly, a current Rule 9 PoA appointment order and named Nodal Officer remain unverified. No inference is made from the generic departmental nodal-officer entry.

## 3. Rule 8 Protection Cell and police machinery

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records a Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Protection Cell in Sikkim under the supervision of SSP/CID. [A: SK-STATE-005]

The Sikkim Police Manual describes the SC/ST PoA Rules and states that a PCR Cell is under the charge of SP, CID, while setting out Rule 8 functions for the Protection Cell. The manual separately lists caste atrocities involving serious violence among matters requiring special reporting. [A: SK-STATE-006, SK-STATE-007]

A Central implementation report also lists Sikkim among States/UTs with an SC/ST Protection Cell. The same source lists only Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Kerala and Madhya Pradesh as States with dedicated Special Police Stations for registration of SC/ST offence complaints in that reporting context. Sikkim is not in that five-State list. This is historical corroboration and is not treated as proof of current absence of a dedicated Special Police Station. [A: SK-STATE-008]

Current complete Protection Cell establishment, staffing, reporting line and any dedicated Special Police Station remain open.

## 4. Rule 7 investigation and charge-sheet timing

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records one Sikkim case charge-sheeted within 60 days and no case charge-sheeted later than 60 days. These are 2022 reporting-period figures, not a 2026 compliance rate. [A: SK-STATE-005]

The Sikkim Police Manual states that PoA cases are to be investigated by the prescribed police machinery and identifies Rule 7 as the governing investigation provision. [A: SK-STATE-006]

Current investigation orders, current case-level compliance and post-BNS/BNSS/BSA transition instructions remain open and separate.

## 5. Rule 10 / atrocity-prone areas

The 2022 Section 21(4) report states that no atrocity-prone area was identified in Sikkim. [A: SK-STATE-005]

Historical Ministry reports provide a more detailed implementation snapshot. In the 2014 report, despite there being no identified atrocity-prone area, Additional District Magistrates of East, West, North and South districts were appointed as Special Officers under Rule 10. [A: SK-STATE-009]

The Sikkim Police Manual also explains the statutory preventive mechanism for areas prone to atrocities under the PoA framework. [A: SK-STATE-006]

No current 2026 Rule 10 appointment order or current identified-area notification was located. The historical four-ADM arrangement must not be treated as current without a current instrument.

## 6. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records that a State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee had been constituted, but no State-level meeting was held during 2022. It records that District Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committees had been constituted and that five meetings were held in six districts during 2022. [A: SK-STATE-005]

The current State Acts and Rules page lists a 2014 Vigilance Committee Notification, a 2014 State Monitoring Committee instrument and a 2006 Vigilance and Monitoring Committee instrument. [A: SK-STATE-001]

The current six-district administrative structure is corroborated by the Social Welfare Department's current departmental description, which states that welfare offices operate in all six districts. [A: SK-STATE-010]

Current 2023-2026 State, District and any Sub-Divisional V&MC orders, calendars and minutes were not fully established in this pass. The 2022 meeting figures therefore remain historical compliance evidence only.

## 7. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

The 2022 Section 21(4) report states that District and Session Courts in Sikkim had been designated as Special Courts for PoA cases. It records eight Special Public Prosecutors for the designated Special Courts. [A: SK-STATE-005]

The State Acts and Rules page currently lists a Special Courts notification dated 2022 and an earlier Special Courts notification dated 2014. [A: SK-STATE-001]

An official Sikkim government reporting document records a 2020 Home Department notification, with concurrence of the High Court of Sikkim, specifying the Courts of Session at East/Gangtok, North/Mangan, West/Gyalshing and South/Namchi as Special Courts for the relevant special-law jurisdiction. The source located through official-government reporting is retained as corroboration, but the underlying 2022 PoA notification itself was not retrievable in this pass. [B: SK-STATE-011]

Current 2026 court notifications and a district-by-district current PoA Special Court census remain open. No Exclusive Special Court is established by the located 2022 Section 21(4) report.

## 8. SPP/ESPP

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records eight Special Public Prosecutors for designated Special Courts in Sikkim. [A: SK-STATE-005]

The current State Acts and Rules page separately lists a 2020 Public Prosecutors notification, establishing that a State instrument exists in the publication record. [A: SK-STATE-001]

The current named SPP roster, appointment orders, whether any prosecutor is an Exclusive Special Public Prosecutor, and current allocation across courts remain unverified.

## 9. Relief, rehabilitation and victim/witness expenses

The Ministry's Social Welfare Statistics handbook records Sikkim with zero persons receiving relief to atrocity victims in FY 2021-22 and zero in FY 2022-23. The same table records 17 and 16 inter-caste marriage incentive beneficiaries respectively. These are financial/physical achievement series, not proof that no qualifying atrocity occurred. [A: SK-STATE-012]

The 2022 Section 21(4) report does not, in the located extract, provide a positive Sikkim relief count. A separate 2022 source table records one charge-sheeted case and no legal-aid count for Sikkim. [A: SK-STATE-005, SK-STATE-013]

The current State Social Welfare Department confirms the PoA/PCR programme and awareness work, but a current case-level relief sanction/payment workflow, Rule 12 seven-day compliance, travel/maintenance payment process and victim/witness support protocol were not independently established. [A: SK-STATE-002]

## 10. State PoA/PCR instruments and related-law interfaces

The current State publication page lists the PoA Act, 2014 SC/ST amendment material, Special Courts notifications, Public Prosecutors notification, Vigilance Committee instruments and inter-caste marriage incentive notification. [A: SK-STATE-001]

The current Social Welfare Department states that both PCR and PoA are implemented through its Social Justice Division and describes central assistance purposes including Protection Cells/Special Police Stations, Special Courts, relief and rehabilitation, inter-caste marriage incentives and awareness. [A: SK-STATE-002]

The current RTI disclosure identifies the PoA Act and PCR-related material among the Department's records and identifies the Social Welfare Department as document custodian. [A: SK-STATE-004]

No separate Sikkim substantive anti-discrimination offence code was established in this first-pass inventory. The PCR interface remains a Central-law interface administered through the State.

## 11. Annual reporting and statistics

The 2022 Section 21(4) report is the strongest located recent State implementation snapshot. It records: constituted State V&MC with no State meeting in 2022; five District V&MC meetings across six districts; a State Protection Cell under SSP/CID; no identified atrocity-prone area; District and Session Courts designated as Special Courts; and eight SPPs. [A: SK-STATE-005]

A later Central parliamentary answer reproduces 2018-2022 crime data. For Sikkim, SC cases were 5 in 2018, 4 in 2019, 0 in 2020, 22 in 2021 and 3 in 2022; ST cases were 1, 2, 1, 0 and 4 respectively. These are NCRB/state crime statistics, not the Section 21(4) implementation series. [A/B: SK-STATE-014]

A 2025 parliamentary answer reports Sikkim ST PoA cases registered/disposed/pending for 2019-2023. It records 2, 0, 1, 4 and 2 cases registered in 2019-2023, with corresponding court-disposal figures of 1, 0, 1, 1 and 2 and pending-for-trial figures of 3, 3, 3, 3 and 1. This is a separate court-statistics series and should not be merged with the 2022 Section 21(4) implementation report. [A/B: SK-STATE-015]

Later annual Section 21(4) reports after 2022 were not located and independently verified in this pass.

## 12. Legal-aid and victim-support interface

Historical Section 21(4) reporting states that Sikkim implemented the Legal Services Authorities Act to provide legal aid to victims of offences including atrocities against SC/ST members. [A: SK-STATE-016]

The Sikkim State Legal Services Authority published a 2023 legal-awareness activity report showing a dedicated September 2023 programme on the SC/ST PoA Act delivered through Doordarshan/All India Radio outreach. [A: SK-STATE-017]

Current PoA-specific referral, victim-assistance, counsel-selection and Section 15A workflow were not independently verified. Current legal-aid infrastructure should therefore be treated as corroborated at programme level, not as proof of a complete PoA case-management workflow.

## 13. Current/recent judicial and police corroboration

The Sikkim High Court's 2016 decision in Naresh Kumar Agarwal v. State of Sikkim arose from an SC/ST PoA Special Court case in East Sikkim. The High Court set aside the conviction under the then Section 3(1)(x) while maintaining the conviction under Section 353 IPC, illustrating that PoA matters have been processed through the State's Special Judge structure. This is recorded only as jurisdictional corroboration, not as Phase 2 merits research. [A: SK-STATE-018]

The current Sikkim Police Manual, published in the recent official police document set, continues to contain a dedicated SC/ST PoA implementation section and describes Rule 8 Protection Cell functions and preventive powers. [A: SK-STATE-006]

The High Court of Sikkim's current public judicial material also identifies judges who have held Special Judge assignments under the SC/ST PoA Act in Sikkim, corroborating the continuing institutional role of Special Judges, though the biographical records do not establish a current 2026 posting roster. [A: SK-STATE-019]

No sufficiently reliable 2025-2026 Sikkim High Court merits judgment under the PoA Act was located in the first-pass search. The 2016 judgment is therefore retained as historical jurisdictional corroboration only.

## Disposition

PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS. Sikkim has a documented State implementation structure, including a Protection Cell under SSP/CID, State and District V&MC machinery, designated District and Session Courts, SPPs, historical Rule 10 Special Officers and current State publication of key PoA instruments. The 2022 implementation snapshot is comparatively small in absolute numbers, with one charge-sheet within 60 days, no charge-sheet reported after 60 days, no identified atrocity-prone area and five district-level V&MC meetings across six districts. However, the current 2026 instrument census and compliance position remain incomplete.

No Bill drafting, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority/necessity analysis or Phase 2 case-law corpus work was undertaken.
