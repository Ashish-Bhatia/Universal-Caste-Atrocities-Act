# Bihar State/UT Implementation Inventory

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map  
Status: TARGETED OFFICIAL-SOURCE INVENTORY COMPLETE WITH QUALIFICATIONS  
Research date: 2026-09-05

## Scope and source discipline

This is an existing-law inventory only. It does not assess necessity, superiority, desirability, constitutionality, or policy merits of any proposed replacement. Bihar was researched independently. No implementation arrangement is inferred from Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh or Assam.

Primary Bihar State sources were preferred. Official Central Government implementation reports are used as historical corroboration or source leads. Currentness is qualified where a current State-wide instrument or census was not located. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## 1. State legislation, Rules and implementation repositories

The current Government of Bihar SC & ST Welfare Department site expressly publishes and maintains material concerning the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989. The department's current site was last updated in September 2026 and identifies the Act among its highlighted resources. The Bihar Digital Repository separately lists the SC/ST PoA Act amendments, a Gazette Rules record, a 2020 "Margdarshika" and a 2023 "SC&ST (POA) ACT 1989 - SANKALP" under the SC & ST Welfare Department. [A]

The Digital Repository also lists Bihar's Manual Scavengers Acts & Rules material and Forest Rights Act/Rules/Guidelines material under the SC & ST Welfare Department. These are relevant interface sources, but their individual operative provisions require separate clause-level review if they become material to the national baseline. [A]

No separate Bihar statute materially amending the central SC/ST PoA Act was established in this targeted pass. The Bihar repository entry titled "THE SCHEDULED CASTES AND THE SCHEDULED TRIBES (PREVENTION OF ATROCITIES) ACT, 1989 -Amendments" is recorded as an implementation/repository lead, not as proof of a State amendment Act.

## 3. State SC/ST Protection Cell and special investigation machinery

Official Central implementation records historically report a State-level SC/ST Protection Cell in Bihar. The 2007 official report states that a Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Cell functioned under the Inspector General of Police (Weaker Section), with a State-level SC/ST police station at CID Headquarters and nine district-level special police stations at Nalanda, Bhojpur, Rohtas, Gaya, Vaishali, Samastipur, Begusarai, Bhagalpur and Munger. [A]

The 2017 implementation report records a Civil Rights Cell at Police Headquarters headed by an SP under the supervision of the ADG (Civil Rights), with DIG (Civil Rights) and other staff, and 36 district SC/ST Cells headed by DSPs. [A]

These records establish substantial historical special-investigation architecture. A current 2026 Bihar Police/State primary order establishing the present Protection Cell structure and staffing was not located in the targeted search. Current staffing therefore remains UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Rule 9 Nodal Officer

The 2007 and 2013 official Bihar implementation records identify the Secretary, Home Department as the State Nodal Officer, responsible for convening review meetings. [A]

The 2019 Bihar State Annual Report, available through a third-party mirror of the underlying State report, repeats the Secretary, Home Department as Nodal Officer and records the Additional District Magistrate in each district as Special Officer. [A for underlying State report; host provenance C]

The current 2026 SC & ST Welfare Department website does not itself identify the present Rule 9 office-holder. Accordingly, Secretary, Home Department is retained as the latest directly located State-reported Rule 9 designation, but current 2026 designation/office-holder is QUALIFIED and requires primary appointment/designation verification.

## 5. Rule 10 Special Officers and atrocity-prone areas

Official State/central implementation records identify the Additional District Magistrate in each district as the Rule 10 Special Officer. The 2019 State Annual Report records this designation and describes the role as coordinating with the District Magistrate, Superintendent of Police and other implementing officers. [A for underlying State report]

Historical official records identify 33 Bihar districts as sensitive/atrocity-prone from the perspective of atrocities against SC/ST members. The list includes Patna, Nalanda, Rohtas, Kaimur/Bhabhua, Bhojpur, Buxar, Gaya, Jehanabad, Nawada, Aurangabad, Saran, Siwan, Gopalganj, Muzaffarpur, Sitamarhi, West Champaran, East Champaran, Vaishali, Darbhanga, Madhubani, Samastipur, Saharsa, Madhepura, Purnia, Bhagalpur, Banka, Munger, Sheikhpura, Begusarai, Khagaria, Katihar, Jamui and Araria. [A]

The 2019 State Annual Report retains the 33-district identification. [A for underlying State report]

No current 2026 State notification was located in the targeted search confirming whether the same list remains operative, has been modified, or has been replaced by district/area-specific designations. Current Rule 10 and atrocity-prone-area status therefore remains QUALIFIED.

## 6. Special Courts and current judicial operation

Historical official Bihar implementation reports record Special Courts across the State. The 2007 report states that the Court of Additional Sessions Judge was the Special Court under the Act in each district and also records Exclusive Special Courts at nine divisional places and at Bhojpur and East Champaran. [A]

The 2017 official report records an Additional District and Sessions Judge as Special Court in each district and five Exclusive Special Courts at Patna, Gaya, Bhagalpur, Muzaffarpur and Begusarai. [A]

Current official Patna High Court records demonstrate continuing Exclusive Special Court under SC/ST Act posts. The current judicial officer directory identifies Exclusive Special Court under SC/ST Act postings at Samastipur, Nawada, Biharsharif, Muzaffarpur and other locations, while individual officer records show postings beginning in 2025. [A]

The current Patna High Court case record also demonstrates operation of an Exclusive Special Court (SC/ST Act), Patna, in a 2025-26 matter. A 16-04-2026 High Court order records an appeal from an order of the Exclusive Special Judge SC/ST, Patna, under section 14A(2) of the Act. [A]

Current judicial operation is therefore VERIFIED at multiple locations, but a complete 2026 State-wide court notification/matrix was not consolidated in this pass. Historical counts are not treated as current census figures.

## 7. Special Public Prosecutors and prosecution machinery

Official Bihar implementation reports historically record Special Public Prosecutors appointed for PoA Special Courts. The 2007 report states that Special Public Prosecutors had been appointed in the Special Courts. The 2017 report records Special Public Prosecutors in 31 Special Courts and Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors in five Exclusive Special Courts. [A]

The current Bihar Law Department maintains a current Special Public Prosecutor category within its law-officer administration. [A]

A current 2026 PoA-specific statewide appointment register or consolidated appointment order was not located in the targeted search. Current PoA-specific SPP/ESPP appointments remain UNVERIFIED as a complete census.

## 8. State and District Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The Government of Bihar published a notice dated 13-08-2026 for reconstitution of the State-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee for effective implementation of the SC/ST PoA Act and Rules. The notice appears on the current SC & ST Welfare Department official notice page, which was last updated 04-09-2026. This is direct current State evidence of a State-level V&MC reconstitution process in 2026. [A]

Historical official reports record the State-level V&MC under the chairpersonship of the Chief Minister and District-level V&MCs under district administration. The 2013 report records two State-level meetings and 114 district-level meetings across 38 districts during that reporting year. [A]

The 2017 report records 149 district-level V&MC meetings in 33 districts. [A]

The current 2026 State notice establishes the State-level committee reconstitution, but the full text/annexure of the 13-08-2026 order was not retrievable through the public search result. Current committee composition, District/Sub-Divisional V&MC constitution and meeting frequency therefore require document-level retrieval before being frozen.

## 9. Relief and rehabilitation and payment evidence

The Government of India Department of Social Justice and Empowerment's current scheme page states that the centrally sponsored scheme supports relief and rehabilitation of atrocity victims, and its State/UT table records Bihar persons provided relief under the scheme at 9,825 in 2021-22, 7,243 in 2022-23, 14,795 in 2023-24 and 15,443 in 2024-25. [A]

The same official source records central assistance released to Bihar of Rs.35.00 crore in 2021-22, Rs.44.89 crore in 2022-23, Rs.37.63 crore in 2023-24 and Rs.46.34 crore in 2024-25 under the combined PCR/PoA implementation scheme. [A]

These figures establish reported scheme-level relief activity and central assistance. They do not, by themselves, establish the current Bihar victim-level payment workflow, payment timing, State share, treasury/DBT mechanism, or case-level compliance with the Rules' relief schedule.

The Bihar SC & ST Welfare Department's current website exposes sanction/allotment letters and PoA resources, indicating an administrative publication channel for financial implementation. [A]

Current Bihar relief-rate orders, payment workflow and case-processing instruments were not fully frozen in this pass.

## 10. PoA-specific SOPs, circulars, manuals and digital/public-access workflows

The Bihar Digital Repository currently lists a 2020 "Margdarshika" and a 2023 "SC&ST (POA) ACT 1989 - SANKALP" under the SC & ST Welfare Department. These records establish the existence of State implementation guidance/instruction artifacts. The operative contents and legal status of each document require direct document retrieval before clause-level reliance. [A]

The current SC & ST Welfare Department site provides PoA resources, meetings/proceedings, office orders, guidelines and sanction/allotment letters. [A]

The current evidence therefore supports a State administrative publication and guidance layer, but a consolidated 2026 PoA SOP/manual and a PoA-specific digital case-tracking workflow were not established from the targeted public-source search.

## 11. State Gazette and official publication material

## 12. PCR and related atrocity-interface legislation

The current Bihar SC & ST Welfare Department maintains PoA-related resources and the State Digital Repository lists the central PoA Act/Rules and Manual Scavengers material. The targeted search did not establish a separate Bihar statute materially amending the central Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955. [A]

The Bihar State implementation architecture historically combines PoA and civil-rights functions. The 2017 report's Civil Rights Cell and historical Protection Cell evidence are retained as implementation context, not as proof of current organizational identity.

## 13. Land, bonded labour, manual scavenging, local government and related State interfaces

Land and tenancy: the Bihar Digital Repository currently lists the Bihar Land Reforms Act, 1950, Bihar Land Reforms Rules, 1951, Bihar Tenancy-related material and the Bihar Special Survey and Settlement Rules, among other land-administration instruments. These are relevant to land-dispossession and title/tenancy interfaces but were not clause-mapped in this State inventory. [A]

Local government: the Bihar Panchayat Raj Act, 2006 and multiple amendments are maintained by the Bihar State Election Commission and the State Digital Repository. The official Panchayat Raj Act establishes Gram Katchahry and district/panchayat administrative structures. [A]

Bonded labour: the targeted Bihar official-source search did not establish a separate State bonded-labour statute or PoA-specific bonded-labour instrument. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence. The central Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act remains the principal identified labour interface pending any later State instrument verification.

Manual scavenging: the Bihar Digital Repository explicitly lists "Manual Scavengers- Acts & Rules" and "Manual Scavengers" under the SC & ST Welfare Department. This is direct State repository evidence of an implementation-document layer. The operative contents and current notifications were not separately extracted because the central Manual Scavengers Act/Rules comparison is already complete at Phase 1 level and this State pass is limited to identifying State-specific instruments. [A]
