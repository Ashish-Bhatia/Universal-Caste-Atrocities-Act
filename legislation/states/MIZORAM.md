# Mizoram

## Phase 1 State/UT Inventory

Date: 05-09-2026
Status: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS
Phase: 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map

## Scope and method

This inventory is jurisdiction-specific. It covers the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, as implemented/reported for Mizoram. Primary Government and Gazette material is preferred. Historical reports are separated from the latest verified State Annual Report material. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## Executive position

Mizoram has a State-level SC/ST Protection Cell and two notified Special Courts covering the State's judicial districts. The latest located Mizoram State Annual Report, for calendar year 2023 and transmitted to the Union Government on 19 March 2024, reports no registered PoA cases, no identified atrocity-prone areas, no State-level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee, no Sub-Divisional V&MCs, no Special Officers for identified areas, no specified Special Public Prosecutors, no Special Police Stations, no relief/legal-aid payments, no periodic survey, and no State plan under Rule 15. It also reports that the SC/ST Protection Cell exists, with an ADGP/IGP as Chair and senior CID/Aizawl police members, but the report does not identify a current named Rule 9 Nodal Officer. These findings are evidence of the position reported for 2023, not a verified census of every arrangement in September 2026.

The 2013 Gazette notification remains the identified primary instrument for the two Special Courts. The 2000 Gazette instrument identified the Additional Secretary, Home Department as Rule 9 Nodal Officer, but no later authoritative appointment instrument was located in the present research pass. Accordingly, the current office-holder remains UNVERIFIED.

## Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2023 State Annual Report states that the State Level Vigilance and Monitoring Committee was not constituted and held no meetings. The Sub-Divisional Level Committee table reports NIL. The District Level table does not provide a reliable complete constitution-date matrix in the retrieved copy and records no meaningful meeting activity. Therefore the existence and operational status of each District V&MC should be treated as unresolved rather than inferred.

The 2012 Mizoram Gazette separately records constitution of a State Level V&MC under Rule 16, chaired by the Chief Minister, with Ministers, MPs/MLAs, Chief Secretary, Home Secretary, DGP and Social Welfare Secretary among the members. The 2016 Rules changed the statutory Rule 16 composition and meeting requirement. The 2023 State Annual Report's statement that the State Level Committee was not constituted therefore establishes a later reported implementation position, not that the 2012 notification never existed.

- State meetings: NIL reported in 2023.
- District V&MCs: current constitution/meeting matrix not adequately verified from the 2023 return.

## Special / Exclusive Special Courts

Primary Gazette Notification No. A.45011/1/2008-LJE dated 29-01-2013, published 08-02-2013, specifies two Courts of Sessions as Special Courts under section 14 of the PoA Act:

1. 3rd Court of Additional District and Sessions Judge, Aizawl, for the Aizawl Judicial District comprising Aizawl, Kolasib, Mamit, Champhai and Serchhip administrative districts.
2. Court of Additional District and Sessions Judge, Lunglei, for Lunglei, Lawngtlai and Saiha.

The 2023 State Annual Report repeats the two-court structure and reports no Exclusive Special Court.

- Special Courts: 2.
- Exclusive Special Courts: NONE reported.
- Territorial coverage: all 11 districts through two judicial districts.
- Current 2026 continuation of the 2013 notification: strongly supported by the 2023 State return, but a later superseding Gazette instrument was not located in this research pass.

## Relief and payment workflow

The 2023 Mizoram State Annual Report reports NIL relief payments and NIL travelling/maintenance expenses because no PoA cases were reported. It also reports no State plan under Rule 15.

## Related-law interfaces

1. Community-status verification: The Mizoram Scheduled Tribes and Scheduled Castes (Regulation of Issuance and Verification of) Community Certificates Act, 2014 regulates issuance and verification of SC/ST community certificates. This is relevant where an accused disputes the victim's statutory community status.
2. Manual scavenging: Mizoram republished the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013 in the State Gazette in 2014. The SC/ST PoA Act also contains an offence concerning manual scavenging, creating an overlapping enforcement interface.
3. PCR law: the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 remains a separate central statute. The State administrative allocation records enforcement responsibility for SC/ST protection and related welfare functions.
4. General criminal law transition: PoA offences interact with the general criminal law framework. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition is a separate national Phase 1 closure workstream and is not re-researched here.

## Evidence limitations

- The strongest current Mizoram-specific implementation source located is the State's 2023 annual return, transmitted in March 2024 and disclosed under RTI in September 2024. It is not a September 2026 census.
- The current Rule 9 Nodal Officer name/designation after the 2000 appointment was not verified.
- Current 2026 Special Public Prosecutor appointments were not verified.
- Current 2026 Rule 10 Special Officer status and identified-area status were not verified beyond the 2023 return.
- District and Sub-Divisional V&MC constitution/meeting records require further primary retrieval.
- No current Rule 15 plan was located; the 2023 return states none existed for that reporting year.
- The 2023 State return reports no PoA cases, while national crime statistics/search extracts show some SC/ST crime figures for Mizoram in other contexts/years. Those datasets must not be conflated. The State return is an implementation-reporting source; NCRB crime tables require separate reconciliation of offence definitions, year and reporting basis.
