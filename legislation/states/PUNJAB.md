# Punjab: Phase 1 Existing-Law Baseline

Date: 2026-09-05
Phase: 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Disposition: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS

## Scope

This jurisdiction pass inventories Punjab-specific implementation of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989 and Rules, using primary or official sources where available. It does not assess the desirability, necessity or constitutional validity of a proposed Universal Caste Atrocities Act.

## 1. State institutional machinery

### Rule 8 Protection Cell

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records a special SC/ST Cell headed by a Superintendent of Police, with supporting staff, under the Director, Bureau of Investigation, Punjab, described as the Additional Director General of Police (Crime), at Police Headquarters. District SC/ST Cells are recorded under the control of Senior Superintendents of Police. A Punjab Police RTI/organisation document also identifies an SC&ST Cell with responsibility for enquiries of complaints relating to SC&ST matters. Evidence: A for the 2022 report; A for the Punjab Police organisational material. Current 2026 command/order details require verification.

### Rule 9 Nodal Officer

The 2020 official Section 21(4) report identifies the Secretary/Principal Secretary, Department of Welfare of Scheduled Castes & Backward Classes and Minorities, as the Rule 9 Nodal Officer. The 2017 report similarly identifies the Secretary/Principal Secretary of the welfare department. The specific current 2026 appointment order and present officer are not established in this pass. Evidence: A, historical/currentness limited.

### Rule 10 Special Officer / atrocity-prone areas

The 2022 Section 21(4) report states that there is no atrocity-prone area in Punjab. Earlier official reports also recorded no identified atrocity-prone area. This does not establish that no area can be identified in 2026 without a current survey/notification. The current Rule 10 Special Officer appointment arrangement therefore remains unverified. Evidence: A for reported position; currentness limitation.

### Special police stations / investigation

The 2022 report documents the State and district SC/ST Protection Cells but does not establish a current statewide dedicated SC/ST special-police-station matrix. A Punjab Police RTI document confirms an SC&ST Cell handling complaints. Search silence is not treated as proof that no dedicated/special police station exists. The 2022 report records 17 cases in which investigation and charge-sheet filing occurred within 60 days and 28 cases later than 60 days. Current investigation orders and the post-BNS/BNSS position remain open. Evidence: A.

## 2. Vigilance and Monitoring Committees

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records:
- State Level V&MC under the Chief Minister. No State-level meeting was held during 2022.
- District V&MCs. Five meetings were held in 23 districts during 2022.
- Sub-Divisional V&MCs constituted in all districts. No Sub-Divisional meeting was held during 2022.

Older official reports record the same three-level structure with varying historical meeting counts. Current meeting compliance after 2022 requires verification. Evidence: A.

## 3. Special and Exclusive Special Courts

The 2022 Section 21(4) report records 27 Special Courts functioning in all districts and two Exclusive Special Courts at Ludhiana and Fatehgarh Sahib. It also records 26 Special Public Prosecutors and two Exclusive Special Public Prosecutors. The 2020 report recorded 26 Special Courts in all districts, one Exclusive Special Court at Ludhiana, 38 SPPs and one ESPP, showing that the judicial/prosecution arrangement changed over time. A 2017 report records 17 Special Courts and all Public Prosecutors specified as SPPs. These historical changes must not be flattened into a single static number.

The Punjab and Haryana High Court has an active 2026 district judiciary website and current district judicial administration, but a current Punjab-wide PoA Special/Exclusive Special Court notification matrix was not established in this pass. Current 2026 court notifications, operational status and prosecutor rosters remain open. Evidence: A for the Government reports; A for current High Court administrative material as corroboration of current district judiciary infrastructure.

## 4. Relief, rehabilitation and legal aid

The 2022 report states that relief and rehabilitation were provided under Rules 12(4) and 12(4A) to no persons within seven days and to 9 persons after seven days. It separately records relief amounts provided to 26 atrocity victims during 2022. These figures arise from different reporting fields and should not be assumed to be directly additive or equivalent. The report states that free legal aid is provided to Scheduled Castes under the Punjab Legal Services framework and that travelling and maintenance expenses are provided under the PoA Rules.

Current Punjab State Legal Services Authority infrastructure is confirmed through Punjab Government and Punjab and Haryana High Court materials. Current district-level legal-aid arrangements are therefore institutionally corroborated, but the current PoA-specific workflow, payment authority, district allocation and 2026 compliance are not fully verified. Evidence: A.

## 5. Annual reporting and statistics

The Ministry's official 2022 Section 21(4) report records Punjab-specific implementation data. For Scheduled Caste PoA cases, Punjab had 166 cases pending from the previous year, 274 cases registered during 2022, 62 cases charge-sheeted in courts, 24 final reports and 158 cases pending with police at the end of 2022. These are PoA Act police-disposal figures and must be distinguished from broader NCRB crime-against-SC statistics.

The same 2022 report records 17 cases charge-sheeted within 60 days and 28 after 60 days, 0 State V&MC meetings, 5 District V&MC meetings in 23 districts and 0 Sub-Divisional meetings. It records 0 persons receiving relief within seven days and 9 after seven days, while a separate section records relief to 26 atrocity victims during the year. No atrocity-prone area was reported.

The 2022 report also records 3 acquittals and no appeals against acquittals in that reporting field. These figures are historical reporting-period evidence, not 2026 compliance findings.

## 6. State-specific related legislation and institutions

Punjab has the Punjab State Commission for Scheduled Castes Act, 2004, which establishes a State Commission for Scheduled Castes with functions directed toward protecting and safeguarding SC interests and recommending remedial measures. Punjab also maintains a Department of Social Justice, Empowerment and Minorities and a Punjab State Scheduled Castes Commission as a current departmental organisation. The Punjab Scheduled Castes and Backward Classes (Reservation in Services) Act, 2006 and Punjab Scheduled Castes Land Development and Finance Corporation framework are additional SC-related State instruments relevant to later crosswalk work.

These instruments are recorded for later duplication, institutional-overlap and consequential-amendment analysis. No conflict or superiority conclusion is made in Phase 1. Evidence: A.

## 7. Currentness and data-integrity limitations

The strongest Punjab-specific implementation snapshot located is the Ministry's Section 21(4) report for calendar year 2022, supplemented by Punjab Government, Punjab Police and Punjab and Haryana High Court materials. Current 2026 Rule 9 and Rule 10 appointment orders, current atrocity-prone-area survey/list, current dedicated special-police-station matrix, current Protection Cell order, current V&MC compliance, current Special/Exclusive Special Court notifications, current SPP/ESPP roster, current relief workflow and post-BNS/BNSS/BSA State instructions remain open.

The 2022 report contains a separate ST table whose Punjab row presents internally inconsistent fields in the extracted/searchable version. It is not used for a substantive Punjab ST statistical conclusion in this inventory. This is recorded as a source/data-integrity residual rather than silently corrected.

Search silence is not treated as evidence of absence.

## Disposition

PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS. Punjab is sufficiently documented for the Phase 1 inventory, with a strong official 2022 implementation snapshot and corroborating State/current institutional material. Currentness and instrument-level verification residuals remain open. No Phase 2 case-law corpus, constitutional-validity analysis, policy-superiority analysis or Bill drafting is initiated by this file.
