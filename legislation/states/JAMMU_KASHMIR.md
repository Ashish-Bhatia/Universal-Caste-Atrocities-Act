# Jammu and Kashmir

Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map  
Status: PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS  
Research date: 2026-09-05

## 1. Post-2019 transition
The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 created the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir with legislature and the Union territory of Ladakh from the former State, with appointed day 31 October 2019. It contains provisions on continuation/adaptation of laws, SC/ST orders, courts and administration. [A: JK-001, JK-002]

The consolidated PoA Act records removal of the former Jammu and Kashmir territorial exception by section 95 and the Fifth Schedule to the Reorganisation Act, effective 31 October 2019. [A: JK-003]

The J&K 2022 Annual Report states that until 2019 incidents were dealt with under the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 and thereafter the PoA Act applied to the UT. This is retained as the UT administration's reporting-period account. [A: JK-004]

The J&K and Ladakh High Court currently publishes the Reorganisation Act, central Acts extended by it, criminal/civil rules and vulnerable-witness protocols. [A: JK-005]

## 2. Beneficiary scope
The 2024 Constitution (Jammu and Kashmir) Scheduled Tribes Order (Amendment) Act lists J&K STs including Bakarwal, Balti, Beda, Bot/Boto, Brokpa/Drokpa/Dard/Shin, Changpa, Gadda Brahmin, Gaddi, Garra, Gujjar, Koli, Mon, Paddari Tribe, Pahari Ethnic Group, Purigpa and Sippi. [A: JK-006, JK-007]

This is a material post-2024 beneficiary-scope fact. No policy inference is made. Historical 2011 ST population and 2018-2020 ST crime data in the Ministry of Tribal Affairs annual report are historical only. [A: JK-008]

## 3. Rule 8 Protection Cell and Special Police Station
The 2022 J&K Annual Report says district-level Special Cells existed in all 20 districts under DySP Headquarters officers designated as Nodal Officers. It separately states that the UT-level Rule 8 SC/ST Protection Cell was not set up and was under consideration with Home Department. Annexure XVIII repeats the latter position. [A: JK-004]

A 2022 PIB response states an SC/ST Protection Cell had been set up in J&K. [A: JK-009]

This is an unresolved source contradiction. The controlled conclusion is that district-level/special nodal cells were reported as functioning in 2022, while the formal UT-level Rule 8 architecture requires current instrument-level verification. Search silence is not treated as absence.

The 2022 Annual Report states no dedicated Special Police Station had been set up and that PCR-related Nodal Cells monitored investigation of atrocity cases. [A: JK-004]

## 4. Rule 9 Nodal Officer
The 2022 Annual Report identifies Government Order No. 20-JK(SWO) of 2021 dated 15 February 2021, nominating the Secretary, J&K Advisory Board for Welfare and Development of Scheduled Castes as Rule 9 Nodal Officer under the supervision of the Administrative Secretary, Social Welfare Department. [A: JK-004]

## 5. Rule 10 and identified areas
The 2022 Annual Report records no identified atrocity-prone area and therefore no Rule 10 Special Officer for that reporting period. It also states that survey/identification work was in process and areas were being considered according to reported atrocity frequency. [A: JK-004]

A current 2026 Rule 3 identification record and Rule 10 instrument remain open.

## 6. V&MCs
Government Order No. 1111-JK (GAD) of 2020 dated 15 December 2020 constituted a High Power V&MC chaired by the Lieutenant Governor, with senior Home, Social Welfare, Finance and Police representation and Social Welfare as Convener. It required meetings at least twice yearly in January and July. [A: JK-010]

The 2022 report records district V&MCs in all 20 districts and Sub-Divisional V&MCs in all sub-divisions. It records 23 district-level meetings in 2022 and no UT-level meeting. [A: JK-004, JK-011]

The J&K General Administration Department records a 4 June 2025 order reconstituting the High Power V&MC for PoA implementation. The official listing establishes the reconstitution event, while the full order text was not independently retrieved. [A: JK-012]

A contemporaneous 2025 report describes the reconstituted committee and twice-yearly meeting requirement. [B: JK-013]

A January 2026 Jammu district V&MC meeting reviewed 11 cases and approved compensation in two FIR-registered cases. This is local corroboration only. [B: JK-014]

## 7. Investigation
The 2022 report records four cases investigated and charge-sheeted within 60 days and two later than 60 days. It records DySP-level investigation and monitoring through Special/Nodal Cells. [A: JK-004]

Current 2026 investigating-officer roster and compliance audit remain open. BNS/BNSS/BSA transition is a separate national workstream.

## 8. Special Courts
S.O. 422 dated 2 September 2022, issued by J&K Law and Parliamentary Affairs with High Court concurrence, designated the Principal District and Sessions Judge Court in each of the 20 J&K districts as a section 14 Special Court. [A: JK-015]

The 2022 report records 20 Special Courts and no Exclusive Special Courts. [A: JK-004, JK-015]

Current 2026 court-wise designation and later Exclusive Special Court notifications remain open.

## 9. SPP/ESPP
S.O. 584 dated 7 November 2022 designated Special Public Prosecutors for the Special Courts. The 2022 report records 20 sanctioned SPP posts, one for each district, with supervision by the Director General Prosecution, District Prosecuting Officers and District Magistrates. It records no SPP/ESPP performance review for 2022. [A: JK-004, JK-016]

Current 2026 named SPP/ESPP roster, allocation, tenure and performance records remain open.

## 10. Relief and payment
The 2022 report records relief to 14 victims in Jammu, Kathua and Udhampur, all recorded as SC victims. It states relief was authorized through District Magistrates and District Social Welfare Officers, with PMFS transfer arrangements. [A: JK-004]

The relief annexure records 14 cases paid within the Rule 12(4) seven-day framework. Current case-level payment audit, rates, rehabilitation outcomes and expenditure reconciliation remain open. [A: JK-004]

## 11. Victim/witness and legal aid
The 2022 report states the specific section 15A(11) scheme was still under consideration, while asserting case-level compliance. It records 15 volunteer Legal Advisors and 23 Field Level Counsellors assisting with FIRs, compensation, relief, rehabilitation and travel/maintenance. [A: JK-004]

J&K Legal Services Authority provided free legal aid to 572 SC persons and 433 ST persons in 2022. The report separately states no Centrally Sponsored Scheme legal-aid funds were provided for that period. [A: JK-004]

The 2022 travel/maintenance annexure records no persons receiving such payments, while stating a PMFS mechanism had been placed with district welfare officers. This requires audit, not inference. [A: JK-004]

The 2025 J&K and Ladakh High Court vulnerable-witness protocol expressly recognizes PoA section 15A notice and hearing rights for vulnerable victims and dependants. [A: JK-018]

## 12. Section 21(4) reporting
The J&K Social Welfare Department confirmed the 2022 Annual Report was forwarded to the Ministry on 31 May 2023. The national repository identifies J&K reports for 2019-2022. Later J&K-specific reporting was not independently retrieved. [A: JK-004, JK-019]

Historical reporting remains separate from 2026 status.

## 13. PoA/PCR instruments
The J&K 2022 report records pre-2019 reliance on PCR and post-2019 PoA applicability. District PCR-related Nodal Cells are described as monitoring atrocity investigations. This is a related-law interface, not a substitute for Rule 8 PoA machinery. [A: JK-004]

Complete current J&K Gazette/notification/circular corpus remains open.

## 14. Related-law interfaces
The 2024 ST Order amendment is the principal jurisdiction-specific beneficiary-scope interface. [A: JK-006]

The J&K Agrarian Reforms Act expressly refers to Gujjar, Bakarwal and Gaddi households and land rights, creating a land-law interface relevant to notified J&K ST communities. [A: JK-021]

The Forest Rights Act, 2006 became applicable to J&K after 31 October 2019 and is relevant to forest-dwelling ST land/forest-rights issues. Implementation effectiveness is outside this Phase 1 pass. [B: JK-022]

No Fifth Schedule Scheduled Area regime or comparable territorial protective regime was located in this pass. Search silence is not treated as proof of absence.

## 15. Current/recent corroboration
The 2022 PIB response provides police/administrative corroboration of an SC/ST Protection Cell, while the 2022 J&K report supplies the conflicting granular account. [A: JK-009, JK-004]

The January 2026 Jammu district V&MC meeting corroborates continuing local PoA review and compensation activity. [B: JK-014]

The 2025 High Court vulnerable-witness protocol corroborates continuing judicial recognition of PoA victim/dependant rights. [A: JK-018]

## Disposition
PROCEED/CLOSE WITH LIMITATIONS. J&K has substantial evidence of post-2019 PoA implementation, including Rule 9 appointment, district monitoring, Special Courts, SPP designation, investigation reporting, relief/payment and legal-aid arrangements. The principal unresolved issue is the Rule 8 Protection Cell architecture. The 2022 contradiction is preserved rather than silently resolved.

No Bill drafting, policy-superiority/necessity analysis, constitutional-validity analysis or Phase 2 case-law research was undertaken.
