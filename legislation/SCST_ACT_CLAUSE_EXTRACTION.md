# SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989, Clause-Level Extraction

Phase: 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: WORKING, current-law verification substantially advanced but not frozen
Extraction date: 2026-09-05

## Primary current-text source

India Code, Act No. 33 of 1989, PDF headed "As on the 21st September, 2025". The PDF identifies the amending Acts as the 2015 Amendment Act (1 of 2016), 2018 Amendment Act (27 of 2018), and Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 (34 of 2019). Evidence grade A. The India Code metadata page separately reports a last-updated indicator of 19-11-2018. The 2025 consolidated PDF demonstrates why the metadata date must not be treated as a completeness cutoff.

| 4 | Penalises wilful neglect by a non-SC/ST public servant of duties under the Act/Rules. Duties include reading back oral information, FIR registration and correct sectioning, furnishing copies, recording victim/witness statements, investigating and filing charge sheet within 60 days with written explanation for delay, correct preparation/translation of documents/electronic records, and other prescribed duties. Administrative inquiry recommendation is required for booking; Special/Exclusive Special Court takes cognizance and directs penal proceedings. | Direct overlap with BNSS investigation/reporting duties and public-servant accountability. |
| 5 | Enhanced punishment for subsequent conviction under Chapter II, minimum 1 year up to punishment for the offence. | Special recidivist layer. |

| 8 | Presumptions concerning financial assistance, group offences following existing disputes, and accused knowledge of victim/family. | Direct BSA burden/presumption crosswalk required. |
| 9 | State Government may confer specified police powers, including arrest, investigation and prosecution before Special Court, on State officers by Gazette notification; other officers must assist; Code applies so far as may be. | BNSS police-power interface. |
| 10 | Special Court may order externment of person likely to commit Chapter II offence in Scheduled/tribal or identified areas, up to three years, with grounds and representation/revocation mechanism. | Preventive power. | 17 | District Magistrate, SDM, other Executive Magistrate or police officer not below DSP may declare an area atrocity-prone and take preventive action after specified information/inquiry/reason to believe; specified CrPC chapters apply; State may issue preventive schemes. | BNSS transition and preventive-procedure interface. |

| 19 | CrPC section 360 and Probation of Offenders Act, 1958 do not apply to persons over 18 convicted under Act. | BNSS sentencing/probation interface requires mapping. |

## Amendment and current-version findings

1. The 2025 India Code consolidated PDF expressly lists the 2015 Amendment Act (1 of 2016), 2018 Amendment Act (27 of 2018), and Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 (34 of 2019).
2. Relevant 2015 Amendment Act provisions are shown as effective from 26-01-2016.
3. Section 18A was inserted by the 2018 Amendment Act and is shown as effective from 20-08-2018.
4. The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 removed the words excluding Jammu and Kashmir from section 1(2), effective 31-10-2019.
5. Targeted official-source searching located the India Code consolidation as on 21-09-2025 and did not locate a later Central amendment Act through 05-09-2026. This is not a completeness declaration. A final Gazette/Legislative Department index verification remains open.
6. Ministry of Law and Justice notification S.O. 2790(E), dated 16-07-2024, issued under section 8 of the General Clauses Act, provides that references in Acts, rules, notifications and similar instruments to IPC, CrPC and Indian Evidence Act provisions are to be read as references to BNS, BNSS and BSA respectively, with corresponding provisions construed accordingly. This is material to the Act's legacy references in sections 2, 6, 12, 17, 18, 19 and the Schedule.
