# SC/ST Act Section 3(2)(v) BNS Punishment-Threshold Matrix

Date: 2026-09-06
Phase: Phase 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: CONTROLLED PRIMARY-TEXT MATRIX WITH QUALIFICATION

## Control rule

Section 3(2)(v) is a punishment-threshold provision, not a fixed list of offences. For a post-01-07-2024 offence, identify the applicable BNS offence and exact subsection first. Then test the enacted punishment against the statutory ten-year threshold. Do not substitute a BNS section number for an IPC number arithmetically.

For pre-01-07-2024 conduct, BNS repeal/savings controls remain applicable and the conduct is not retrospectively converted into a BNS offence.

## Verification rules

1. The threshold is determined from the applicable BNS subsection, not the BNS section heading alone.
2. Merged BNS provisions must be separated by subsection where the punishment differs.
3. A Schedule correspondence does not itself establish that section 3(2)(v) is satisfied.
4. Section 3(2)(va) Schedule operation remains a separate special-law route and is not collapsed into section 3(2)(v).
5. Judicial interpretation of section 3(2)(v), including how courts treat alternative punishment structures and corresponding-offence construction, is deferred to Phase 2.
6. BNS text and S.O. 2790(E) control transition. No numerical renumbering is used as a substitute for subject-matter correspondence.
