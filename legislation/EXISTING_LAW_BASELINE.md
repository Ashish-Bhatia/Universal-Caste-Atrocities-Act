# Existing-Law Baseline

Phase: 1, Existing-Law Baseline and Source Map
Status: WORKING BASELINE, NOT COMPLETE
Baseline date: 2026-09-05
Method: primary-source first, evidence-graded, non-presumptive

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the first verified map of the existing legal framework relevant to caste-based discrimination, untouchability, atrocities against Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, criminal liability, criminal procedure, evidence, victim/witness protections, and legislative competence.

It is a research baseline, not a recommendation for repeal, replacement, or amendment.

## 2. Evidence grading

A = primary authoritative source.
B = strong official/credible source used where the primary text is not directly retrievable in the current tool pass.
C = reliable secondary source.
D = weak or unverified source.

No major policy or drafting conclusion is drawn solely from C/D evidence.

## 11. Other central legislation requiring source-map review

The following are placed in the Phase 1 source map because their subject matter is materially capable of intersecting with caste-discrimination/atrocity enforcement. Inclusion does not mean each will be amended or relied upon:

- Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950.
- Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950.
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, where disability/caste intersection and non-discrimination duties overlap.
- Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 and relevant sexual-offence provisions where victim-protection architecture intersects.
- Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act, 2006, where land/forest rights and ST protections intersect.
- Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act, 2013, where caste-linked occupational exploitation intersects.
- Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976, where forced/compulsory labour intersects with caste-based exploitation.
- Child protection legislation where offences involve children and caste status.
- Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988 and public-servant accountability provisions where neglect or misuse of public office overlaps with caste-atrocity enforcement.

Each item remains to be verified at clause level before being treated as a material drafting dependency.

## 12. State legislation and rules

Phase 1 will not treat one State's framework as representative of India. The state-law map must be built state-by-state for material areas, with priority given to:

1. State police legislation and police rules.
2. State rules/orders establishing SC/ST protection cells, nodal officers, special prosecutors and monitoring committees.
3. State rules governing relief and rehabilitation under the SC/ST Act.
4. State rules implementing the Protection of Civil Rights Act where separately issued.
5. State laws on social disabilities, public access, land/tenancy, bonded labour, manual scavenging, local government and preventive powers where caste-based enforcement intersects.
6. State-specific amendments or adaptations affecting criminal procedure or special courts.

The current pass has not yet established an exhaustive all-State inventory. No conclusion about State-level uniformity is made.

## 14. Current baseline limitations

- This is the first substantive repository research pass.
- The repository had no prior substantive legal research to reconcile.
- The India Code records for the 1989 Act and 1955 Act provide consolidated metadata and section structures, but detailed clause-level extraction remains necessary.
- The 1995 Rules require a controlled extraction of the consolidated amended text and each material amendment.
- The three new criminal codes require offence/procedure/evidence crosswalks, not merely section inventories.
- State legislation has not yet been exhaustively mapped.
- No constitutional case-law conclusions are imported into this baseline. Case-law is Phase 2.
- No policy conclusion follows from this baseline.
